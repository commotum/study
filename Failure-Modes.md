## FM-DB-MCQ: Double-Barreled MCQ

**Double-Barreled MCQ:** A multiple-choice question that combines more than one target skill into a single item, testing multiple concepts, procedures, or misconceptions at once instead of maintaining singular focus. Despite the term “double-barreled,” it can include two or more embedded tasks or decision points. The issue is that a learner’s answer may not clearly reveal which specific skill they understood or misunderstood.

## FM-SPF: Split Plotting Failure

**Split Plotting Failure:** A pedagogical plotting failure where directly related functions, signals, or transformations are shown on separate plots even though a shared coordinate grid would make the comparison clearer. This occurs when the instructional goal depends on comparing shape, position, shift, scale, or alignment, but the split layout forces learners to reconcile separate axes instead of seeing the relationship directly.

## FM-UCL: Unclear Content Language

**Unclear Content Language:** A wording failure where the prompt, explanation, answer choice, instruction, or feedback is ambiguous, imprecise, or difficult to interpret. This occurs when the language does not clearly identify the intended task, target concept, required action, or meaning of key terms.

## FM-MSL: Misleading Content Language

**Misleading Content Language:** A wording failure where the language unintentionally suggests a different task, quantity, relationship, or interpretation than the one being assessed. This occurs when the content appears to ask for one thing while intending another, causing the learner to infer the target from context rather than from clear wording.
