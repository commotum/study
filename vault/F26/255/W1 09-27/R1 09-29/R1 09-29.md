## Problem 1

The electrical voltage in some 3D region is given by

$$
V(x,y,z)=x^3-5xy+xyz.
$$

Consider $P(2,8,5)$ and $\mathbf v=\langle 1,1,-1\rangle$.

**(a)** Find the rate of change of the voltage at $P$ in the direction of $\mathbf v$.

**(b)** What is the maximum rate of change of the voltage at $P$?

**(c)** In which direction does the voltage increase/decrease most rapidly at $P$?

**(d)** Give two orthogonal directions in which the rate of change of the voltage at $P$ is $0$.

## Problem 2

Consider the space curve

$$
\mathbf r(t)=\langle x(t),y(t),z(t)\rangle
=\langle 3t,4t,5\ln\sec t\rangle.
$$

**(a)** Find

$$
\frac{d\mathbf r}{dt}=\mathbf r'(t)=\langle x'(t),y'(t),z'(t)\rangle.
$$

**(b)** Find $d\mathbf r=\langle dx,dy,dz\rangle$ (in terms of $dt$).

**(c)** Find the differential arc length $ds=|d\mathbf r|$ (in terms of $dt$).

**(d)** Find the arc length from $t=0$ to $t=\frac{\pi}{3}$.

## Problem 3

**(a)** Use polar coordinates to evaluate

$$
\int_0^{\sqrt{3}}\int_1^{\sqrt{4-y^2}}
\sqrt{x^2+y^2-1}\,dx\,dy.
$$

**(b)** Use polar coordinates to evaluate

$$
\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2-y^2}\,dy\,dx.
$$

What does this say about $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx$? How about $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx$?

## Problem 4

The temperature in a room is given by the function

$$
w=f(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2+z^2.
$$

**(a)** There is a helix in the room with parameterization

$$
\langle x,y,z\rangle=\langle\cos t,\sin t,t\rangle.
$$

Use chain rule to find $\dfrac{dw}{dt}$. Reflect on whether your answer makes sense.

**(b)** There is a cone in the room with parameterization

$$
\langle x,y,z\rangle=\langle r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,r\rangle.
$$

Use chain rule to find $\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial r}$ and $\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial\theta}$. Reflect on whether your answers make sense.

## Problem 5

The temperature in a room is given by the function

$$
w=f(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2+z^2.
$$

There is a sphere in the room with parameterization

$$
\langle x,y,z\rangle
=\langle\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\rho\cos\phi\rangle.
$$

Use chain rule to find $\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial\rho}$, $\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial\phi}$, and $\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial\theta}$. Reflect on whether your answers make sense.

## Problem 6

Let $0\le a\le b$ and let $f(x)\ge g(x)$ on $[a,b]$.

Let $R$ be the region on the $xz$-plane between $z=f(x)$ and $z=g(x)$ over $[a,b]$, and let $D$ be the solid region obtained by rotating $R$ around the $z$-axis.

**(a)** Give $D$ as a set of triples in cylindrical coordinates:

$$
D=\{(r,\theta,z)\mid {?}\le r\le {?},\;{?}\le\theta\le {?},\;{?}\le z\le {?}\}.
$$

**(b)** Let $V$ be the volume of $D$. Prove the shell formula:

$$
V=\iiint_D dV=2\pi\int_a^b\bigl[xf(x)-xg(x)\bigr]\,dx.
$$
