## Problem 1

This problem will review the product and chain rule for derivatives, focusing on aspects that will be useful in this class.

**(a)** Compute the derivative of $f(x)=e^x\sin(x)$.

**(b)** Express the following in terms of $y(x)$ and $y'(x)$:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\bigl(\sin(x)\,y(x)\bigr).
$$

**(c)** Compute the following derivative ($\exp(x)=e^x$):

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\exp(x^2).
$$

**(d)** Express the following derivative in terms of $p(x)$ and an antiderivative:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\exp\left(\int p(x)\,dx\right).
$$

## Problem 2

Solving initial value problems was an important application of integration from MTH 252. Recall that the function $y(x)$ satisfying

$$
y'(x)=f(x),\qquad y(x_0)=a
$$

is

$$
y(x)=a+\int_{x_0}^x f(s)\,ds.
$$

The function $y(x)$ is known as the solution to the initial value problem. Solve the following initial value problems:

**(a)** $y'(x)=x^4,\quad y(0)=5$.

**(b)** $y'(x)=\sqrt{x},\quad y(0)=4$.

**(c)** $y'(x)=5\sin(x),\quad y(0)=10$.

**(d)** $y'(x)=\dfrac{1}{x},\quad y(1)=3$.

## Problem 3

This problem will take you through the process of solving the initial value problem for the differential equation

$$
e^xy'+e^xy=0,\qquad y(0)=y_0.
$$

**(a)** Use the product rule to express the following in terms of $e^x$, $y$, and $y'$:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}(e^xy).
$$

If $e^xy'+e^xy=0$, what must be true about $\dfrac{d}{dx}(e^xy)$?

**(b)** What does part (a) imply about $e^xy$?

**(c)** If $y(0)=y_0$, what is $e^0y(0)$ equal to?

**(d)** Use the above to determine what $y(x)$ must be equal to.
