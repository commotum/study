Set up, but do not evaluate, the exact volume integral when slices perpendicular to the $x$-axis are semicircles whose diameters lie in $R$.

- $\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{2}\pi\left(\frac{3-\frac{x^2}{2}-\sin(\pi x)-1}{2}\right)^2\,dx$
- $\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{2}\left[\pi\left(3-\frac{x^2}{2}\right)^2-\pi\left(\sin(\pi x)+1\right)^2\right]dx$
- $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{1}\frac{\pi}{2}\left(\frac{3-\frac{x^2}{2}-\sin(\pi x)-1}{2}\right)^2\,dx$
