## Problem 1

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-1
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 1**

  Find the exact area enclosed by

  $$
  y=x,\qquad y=4x,\qquad x=7.
  $$

  Set up the area using vertical slices, then enter its value.

  $$
  A= ==147/2==
  $$
feedback: |
  On $0\le x\le 7$, the upper curve is $4x$ and the lower curve is $x$. Therefore

  $$
  A=\int_0^7(4x-x)\,dx
   =\int_0^7 3x\,dx
   =\frac{3}{2}(7^2)
   =\frac{147}{2}.
  $$
```

## Problem 2

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-2
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 2**

  Find the exact area enclosed by

  $$
  f(x)=16e^x,\qquad g(x)=-x+16,\qquad x=16.
  $$

  $$
  A= ==16e^(16)-144==
  $$
feedback: |
  The curves meet at $x=0$, and $16e^x$ is above $-x+16$ on $0\le x\le16$. Thus

  $$
  A=\int_0^{16}\left(16e^x+x-16\right)dx
   =\left[16e^x+\frac{x^2}{2}-16x\right]_0^{16}
   =16e^{16}-144.
  $$
```

## Problem 3

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-3
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 3**

  The graphs $f(x)=x$ and $g(x)=x^9$ enclose two bounded regions. Find their total exact area.

  $$
  A= ==4/5==
  $$
feedback: |
  The curves meet at $x=-1,0,1$. Symmetry lets us double the area on $[0,1]$, where $x$ is above $x^9$:

  $$
  A=2\int_0^1(x-x^9)\,dx
   =2\left(\frac12-\frac1{10}\right)
   =\frac45.
  $$
```

## Problem 4

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-4
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 4**

  Find the exact area enclosed by

  $$
  x=y^2,\qquad x=6+5y.
  $$

  Integrate with respect to $y$.

  $$
  A= ==343/6==
  $$
feedback: |
  Solving $y^2=6+5y$ gives $y=-1$ and $y=6$. The line is the right boundary and the parabola is the left boundary:

  $$
  A=\int_{-1}^{6}(6+5y-y^2)\,dy
   =\left[6y+\frac52y^2-\frac13y^3\right]_{-1}^{6}
   =\frac{343}{6}.
  $$
```

## Problem 5

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-5
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 5**

  Find the exact area enclosed by

  $$
  x=y^2+3,\qquad 2y=27-x.
  $$

  Integrate with respect to $y$.

  $$
  A= ==500/3==
  $$
feedback: |
  Rewrite the line as $x=27-2y$. The intersections satisfy $y^2+3=27-2y$, so $y=-6$ and $y=4$. Therefore

  $$
  A=\int_{-6}^{4}\left[(27-2y)-(y^2+3)\right]dy
   =\int_{-6}^{4}(24-2y-y^2)\,dy
   =\frac{500}{3}.
  $$
```

## Problem 6

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-6
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 6**

  Find the exact area enclosed by

  $$
  y=6-x,\qquad y=\ln(x-5),\qquad y=6.
  $$

  Integrate with respect to $y$.

  $$
  A= ==e^6+11==
  $$
feedback: |
  Write the side boundaries as $x=6-y$ and $x=e^y+5$. They meet when $y=0$, and the top boundary is $y=6$. Hence

  $$
  A=\int_0^6\left[(e^y+5)-(6-y)\right]dy
   =\left[e^y-y+\frac{y^2}{2}\right]_0^6
   =e^6+11.
  $$
```

## Problem 7

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-7
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 7**

  Find the exact area enclosed by

  $$
  y=e^{3x},\qquad x=4,\qquad y=1.
  $$

  $$
  A= ==(e^12-13)/3==
  $$
feedback: |
  The exponential meets $y=1$ at $x=0$. Using vertical slices,

  $$
  A=\int_0^4(e^{3x}-1)\,dx
   =\left[\frac13e^{3x}-x\right]_0^4
   =\frac{e^{12}-13}{3}.
  $$
```
