## Problem 1

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-1
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 1**

  Use the arc-length formula to find the exact length of the line

  $$
  y=-9x+2
  $$

  from $(-1,11)$ to $(1,-7)$.

  $$
  s= ==2sqrt(82)==
  $$
feedback: |-
  Since $dy/dx=-9$ and the $x$-interval has length $2$,

  $$
  s=\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2}\,dx
   =\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{82}\,dx
   =2\sqrt{82}.
  $$
```

## Problem 2

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-2
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 2**

  Use the arc-length formula to find the exact length of

  $$
  y=x^{3/2}+1
  $$

  from $x=2$ to $x=7$.

  $$
  s= ==(67sqrt(67)-22sqrt(22))/27==
  $$
feedback: |-
  Here $dy/dx=\frac32\sqrt{x}$, so

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  s
  &=\int_2^7\sqrt{1+\frac94x}\,dx\\
  &=\frac12\int_2^7\sqrt{9x+4}\,dx\\
  &=\frac1{27}\left[(9x+4)^{3/2}\right]_2^7\\
  &=\frac{67\sqrt{67}-22\sqrt{22}}{27}.
  \end{aligned}
  $$
```

```quiz
feedback: |-
  For $x$ as a function of $y$, the arc-length integrand is

  $$
  \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{dx}{dy}\right)^2}.
  $$

  $$
  s=\int_5^7\sqrt{1+64y^{14}}\,dy.
  $$
```

## Problem 4

A ski slope rises from ground level to a height of $h=144\ \mathrm{m}$ over a horizontal base distance of $l=240\ \mathrm{m}$. Its profile is

$$
y=Ax^{3/2},
$$

where $x$ is horizontal distance from the bottom and $y$ is vertical height.

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-4a
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 4 — Part A**

  Find $A$ exactly.

  $$
  A= ==sqrt(15)/100==
  $$
feedback: |-
  The top of the slope is $(240,144)$, so

  $$
  144=A(240)^{3/2}.
  $$

  Therefore

  $$
  A=\frac{144}{240^{3/2}}
   =\frac{3}{20\sqrt{15}}
   =\frac{\sqrt{15}}{100}.
  $$
```

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-4b
input_mode: math
require_exact: true
content: |-
  **Part B**

  A skier travels directly along the slope from the top to the bottom. Find the distance traveled, rounded to one decimal place. Enter the number only.

  $$
  s\approx ==283.5==\ \mathrm{m}
  $$
feedback: |-
  With $A=\sqrt{15}/100$,

  $$
  \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{3\sqrt{15}}{200}\sqrt{x}.
  $$

  Thus

  $$
  s=\int_0^{240}\sqrt{1+\frac{27}{8000}x}\,dx
   \approx 283.478\ \mathrm{m}.
  $$

  Rounded to one decimal place, the skier travels $283.5\ \mathrm{m}$.
```

## Problem 5

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-5
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 5**

  Evaluate.

  $$
  \int_{1/3}^{5/2}(3x+2)\,dx= ==325/24==
  $$
feedback: |-
  An antiderivative is $\frac32x^2+2x$. Therefore

  $$
  \int_{1/3}^{5/2}(3x+2)\,dx
   =\left[\frac32x^2+2x\right]_{1/3}^{5/2}
   =\frac{115}{8}-\frac56
   =\frac{325}{24}.
  $$
```

## Problem 6

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-6
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 6**

  Use integration by parts to evaluate

  $$
  \int \sqrt{x}\ln(4x)\,dx.
  $$

  Use $C$ for the arbitrary constant and absorb constant terms into $C$ whenever possible.

  Answer: ==(2/3)x^(3/2)ln(4x)-(4/9)x^(3/2)+C==
feedback: |-
  Let $u=\ln(4x)$ and $dv=x^{1/2}\,dx$. Then $du=dx/x$ and $v=\frac23x^{3/2}$:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \int x^{1/2}\ln(4x)\,dx
  &=\frac23x^{3/2}\ln(4x)
    -\frac23\int x^{1/2}\,dx\\
  &=\frac23x^{3/2}\ln(4x)-\frac49x^{3/2}+C.
  \end{aligned}
  $$
```

## Problem 7

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-7
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 7**

  Evaluate exactly.

  $$
  \int\frac{9\cos x}{1+\sin^2 x}\,dx
  $$

  Use $C$ for the arbitrary constant and absorb constant terms into $C$ whenever possible.

  Answer: ==9arctan(sin(x))+C==
feedback: |-
  Let $u=\sin x$, so $du=\cos x\,dx$. Then

  $$
  \int\frac{9\cos x}{1+\sin^2x}\,dx
   =9\int\frac{du}{1+u^2}
   =9\tan^{-1}(u)+C
   =9\tan^{-1}(\sin x)+C.
  $$
```
