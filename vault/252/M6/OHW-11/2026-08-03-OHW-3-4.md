## Problem 1

Rewrite the improper rational function as a polynomial plus a proper rational function.

$$
f(x)=\frac{x^2+9}{x+4}
$$

Choose the correct equation:

- $f(x)=x+\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $f(x)=x+4+\dfrac{25}{x+4}$
- $f(x)=x+5-\dfrac{20}{x+4}$
- $f(x)=x-4+\dfrac{25}{x+4}$
