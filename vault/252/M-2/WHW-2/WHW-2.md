## Problem 6

Section 1.2, Problem 91:

Suppose that

$$
\int_0^4 f(x)\,dx=5, \qquad \int_0^2 f(x)\,dx=-3,
$$

and

$$
\int_0^4 g(x)\,dx=-1, \qquad \int_0^2 g(x)\,dx=2.
$$

Compute the integral.

$$
\int_2^4 \left(f(x)-g(x)\right)\,dx
$$
