## Problem 3

Section 1.4, Problem 223:

A particle moves with velocity

$$
v(t)=4-2t, \qquad 0\le t\le 2,
$$

in meters per second. Find the displacement at time $t$ and the total distance traveled up to $t=2$.

## Problem 4

Section 1.4, Problem 226:

A particle moves with acceleration

$$
a(t)=t-3, \qquad 0\le t\le 6,
$$

in meters per second. Find the velocity and displacement at time $t$ and the total distance traveled up to $t=6$, given $v(0)=3$ and $d(0)=0$.

## Problem 7

Section 1.5, Problem 316:

The area of a semicircle of radius $1$ can be expressed as

$$
\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx.
$$
