## Introduction

Suppose we have the graph of a function $y=f(x)$ to which we apply the following transformations in the order listed:

1. translation by $\color{blue}5$ units up
2. translation by $\color{red}4$ units left
3. stretch by a scale factor of $\color{purple}3$ parallel to the $y$-axis

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

To find the final expression, we apply each transformation in turn.

1. Translating the graph of $y=f(x)$ up by $\color{blue}5$ units is equivalent to adding $\color{blue}5$ to the function expression. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y=f(x)+{\color{blue}5}$.
2. Translating the graph of $y=f(x)+5$ by $\color{red}4$ units to the left is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ with $(x+{\color{red}{4}})$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y =f(x+{\color{red}4})+{\color{blue}5}$.
3. Stretching the graph of $y=f(x+4)+5$ by a scale factor of $\color{purple}3$ parallel to the $y$-axis is equivalent to multiplying the function expression by $3$. Applying this transformation, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 3 \cdot (f(x + 4) + 5) \\
&= 3 \cdot f(x + 4) + 3 \cdot 5 \\
&= 3f(x + 4) + 15
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the final function expression is

$$
y=3f(x+4)+15
$$

## Applying Several Transformations: Horizontal Shift Only Inside Parentheses

**Example:** Given the graph of $y=f(x)$, we apply the following transformations in the order given:

1. Translate it up by $2$ units
2. Stretch it by a scale factor of $5$ parallel to the $y$-axis
3. Translate it to the right by $4$ units

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

**Explanation**

To find the expression, let's apply each of the transformations in turn.

1. Translating the graph of $y=f(x)$ up by $2$ units is equivalent to adding $2$ to the function expression. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y=f(x)+2$.
2. Stretching the graph of $y=f(x)+2$ by a scale factor of $5$ parallel to the $y$-axis is equivalent to multiplying the function expression by $5$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y=5\big(f(x)+2 \big) = 5f(x)+10$.
3. Translating the graph of $y=5f(x)+10$ by $4$ units to the right is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ with $(x-4)$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y =5f(x-4)+10$.

Therefore, the final function expression is

$$
y=5f(x-4)+10
$$

## Applying Several Transformations: Horizontal Stretch Only Inside Parentheses

**Example:** Given the graph of $y=f(x)$, we apply the following transformations in the order given:

1. Stretch it by a scale factor of $\dfrac 12$ parallel to the $x$-axis
2. Translate it down by $4$ units
3. Stretch it by a scale factor of $\dfrac13$ parallel to the $y$-axis

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

**Explanation**

To find the expression, let's apply each of the transformations in turn.

1. Stretching the graph of $y=f(x)$ by a scale factor of $\dfrac 12$ parallel to the $x$-axis is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ with $2x$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y = f\left(2x \right)$.
2. Translating the graph of $y=f\left(2x\right)$ down by $4$ units is equivalent to subtracting $4$ from $f\left(2x \right)$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y =f\left(2x \right)-4$.
3. Stretching the graph of $y=f\left(2x \right)-4$ by a scale factor of $\dfrac13$ parallel to the $y$-axis is equivalent to multiplying the function expression by $\dfrac13$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y=\dfrac13(f\left(2x\right)-4) = \dfrac13f(2x)-\dfrac43$.

Therefore, the final function expression is

$$
y= \dfrac13f(2x)-\dfrac43
$$

## Applying Several Transformations: Horizontal Shift Followed By Horizontal Stretch Inside Parentheses

**Example:** Given the graph of $y=f(x)$, we apply the following transformations in the order given:

1. Stretch it by a scale factor of $3$ parallel to the $y$-axis
2. Translate it to the right by $2$ units
3. Stretch it by a scale factor of $\dfrac 15$ parallel to the $x$-axis

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

**Explanation**

To find the expression, let's apply each of the transformations in turn.

1. Stretching the graph of $y=f\left(x \right)$ by a scale factor of $3$ parallel to the $y$-axis is equivalent to multiplying the function expression by $3$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y=3f\left(x \right)$.
2. Translating the graph of $y=3f(x)$ by $2$ units to the right is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ with $(x-2)$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y =3f(x-2)$.
3. Stretching the graph of $y=3f\left(x-2 \right)$ by a scale factor of $\dfrac 15$ parallel to the $x$-axis is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ with $5x$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y = 3f\left((5x)-2 \right) =3f\left(5x-2 \right)$.

Therefore, the final function expression is

$$
y=3f\left(5x-2\right)
$$

## Applying Several Transformations: Horizontal Stretch Followed By Horizontal Shift Inside Parentheses

**Example:** Given the graph of $y=f(x)$, we apply the following transformations in the order given:

1. Translate it down by $2$ units
2. Stretch it by a scale factor of $\dfrac 16$ parallel to the $x$-axis
3. Translate it to the left by $\dfrac12$ units

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

**Explanation**

To find the expression, let's apply each of the transformations in turn.

1. Translating the graph of $y=f(x)$ down by $2$ units is equivalent to subtracting $2$ from $f(x)$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y =f(x)-2$.
2. Stretching the graph of $y=f(x)-2$ by a scale factor of $\dfrac 16$ parallel to the $x$-axis is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ with $6x$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y = f\left(6x \right)-2$.
3. Translating the graph of $y=f\left(6x\right)-2$ by $\dfrac12$ units to the left is equivalent to replacing every instance of $x$ by $\left(x+\dfrac12\right)$. Applying this transformation, we obtain $y =f\left(6\left(x+\dfrac12\right) \right)-2 = f\left(6x+3 \right)-2$.

Therefore, the final function expression is

$$
y=f\left(6x+3 \right)-2
$$
