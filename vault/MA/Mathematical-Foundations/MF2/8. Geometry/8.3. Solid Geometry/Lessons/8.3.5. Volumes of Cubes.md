## Introduction

Suppose that we have a cube whose edges are $2\,\textrm{cm}$ in length, like the one shown below.

![](<../Source/8.3.5. Volumes of Cubes/Images/d04832ca9d8be3328d8c1b27f8076fa5.png>)

The formula for the volume $V$ of a cube with sides of length $s$ is given by

$$
V = s^3
$$

To find the volume of this cube, we substitute

$$
s=2 \: \textrm{cm}
$$

into the formula. This gives

$$
V = (2\, \textrm{cm})^3 = 8 \text{cm}^3
$$

So the volume of the cube is $8\,\textrm{cm}^3$.

## Calculating the Volume of a Cube

**Example:** What is the volume of the cube?

![](<../Source/8.3.5. Volumes of Cubes/Images/1dd283e56ed97a6cd063f6eefa842991.png>)

**Explanation**

The volume $V$ of a cube can be found using the formula

$$
V = s^3
$$

where $s$ is the side length of the cube.

Substituting

$$
s=4\,\textrm{cm}
$$

into the formula, we get

$$
V = (4\, \textrm{cm})^3 = 64\, \textrm{cm}^3
$$

## Finding a Side Length of a Cube Given Its Volume

**Example:** The volume of a cube is $729\, \textrm{cm}^3$. Find the length of the edges of the cube.

**Explanation**

Since the volume of a cube is

$$
V = s^3
$$

where $s$ is the length of an edge, we can find $s$ as follows:

$$
s = \sqrt[3]{V} = \sqrt[3]{729} = 9
$$

Therefore, the length of the edges is $9\,\textrm{cm}$.

## The Volume of a Cube: Word Problems

**Example:** Six identical plastic cubic containers have a combined volume of $48 \: \textrm{ft}^3$. Find the side length of a single container.

**Explanation**

First, we find the volume of a single container:

$$
\dfrac{48 \: \textrm{ft}^3}{6} = 8 \: \textrm{ft}^3
$$

Now, we need to find the side length of one container.

Since the volume of a cube is

$$
V = s^3
$$

where $s$ is the length of a side, we can find $s$ as follows:

$$
s = \sqrt[3]{V} = \sqrt[3]{8} = 2
$$

Therefore, the length of a side of a single container is $2\,\textrm{ft}$.
