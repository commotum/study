## Introduction

To add complex numbers, we just add the real parts and add the imaginary parts separately. For example,

$$
\begin{aligned}
(3 + 5\textrm{i}) + (2 - 3\textrm{i}) &= (3 + 5\textrm{i}) + (2 - 3\textrm{i}) \\
&= (3 + 2) + (5 + (-3))\textrm{i} \\
&= (3 + 2) + (5 - 3)\textrm{i} \\
&= 5 + 2\textrm{i}
\end{aligned}
$$

In general, we write

$$
(a + b\textrm{i}) + (c + d\textrm{i}) = (a + c) + (b + d)\textrm{i}
$$

Subtracting complex numbers works in the same way: we just subtract the real parts and subtract the imaginary parts separately. For example,

$$
\begin{aligned}
(3 + 5\textrm{i}) - (2 - 3\textrm{i}) &= (3 + 5\textrm{i}) - (2 - 3\textrm{i}) \\
&= (3 - 2) + (5 - (-3))\textrm{i} \\
&= (3 - 2) + (5 + 3)\textrm{i} \\
&= 1 + 8\textrm{i}
\end{aligned}
$$

In general, we write

$$
(a + b\textrm{i}) - (c + d\textrm{i}) = (a - c) + (b - d)\textrm{i}
$$

## Adding Complex Numbers

**Example:** Evaluate $(4 + 3\,\textrm{i}) + (1 + 5\,\textrm{i})$.

**Explanation**

To add complex numbers, we just add the real parts and add the imaginary parts separately.

For the given problem, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4 + 3\textrm{i}) + (1 + 5\textrm{i}) &= (4 + 3\textrm{i}) + (1 + 5\textrm{i}) \\
&= (4 + 1) + (3 + 5)\textrm{i} \\
&= 5 + 8\textrm{i}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Subtracting Complex Numbers

**Example:** Find the value of $(5 + 4\,\textrm{i}) - (1 + 2\,\textrm{i})$.

**Explanation**

To subtract complex numbers, we just subtract the real parts and subtract the imaginary parts separately.

For the given problem, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
(5 + 4\textrm{i}) - (1 + 2\textrm{i}) &= (5 + 4\textrm{i}) - (1 + 2\textrm{i}) \\
&= (5 - 1) + (4 - 2)\textrm{i} \\
&= 4 + 2\textrm{i}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Adding and Subtracting Complex Numbers Written in Nonstandard Form

**Example:** Evaluate $\left(\dfrac{1}{2} + \sqrt{-9} \right) + (3 - \sqrt{-16})$.

**Explanation**

First, we simplify the two complex numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\frac{1}{2} + \sqrt{-9}) + (3 - \sqrt{-16}) &= (\frac{1}{2} + 3\textrm{i}) + (3 - 4\textrm{i})
\end{aligned}
$$

To add these two complex numbers, we just add the real parts and add the imaginary parts separately:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\frac{1}{2} + 3\textrm{i}) + (3 - 4\textrm{i}) &= (\frac{1}{2} + 3) + (3 + (-4))\textrm{i} \\
&= (\frac{1}{2} + 3) + (3 - 4)\textrm{i} \\
&= \frac{7}{2} - 1\textrm{i} \\
&= \frac{7}{2} - \textrm{i}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Adding Purely Imaginary Numbers

**Example:** Find the value of $5\,\textrm{i} - 8\,\textrm{i}$.

**Explanation**

To subtract two complex numbers, we just subtract the real parts and subtract the imaginary parts separately.

In this case, $5\,\textrm{i}$ and $8\,\textrm{i}$ are purely imaginary numbers. Therefore, we only need to subtract their imaginary parts, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
5\textrm{i} - 8\textrm{i} = (5 - 8)\textrm{i} &= -3\textrm{i}
\end{aligned}
$$
