## Introduction

Suppose we want to solve the following system of equations:

$$
{x + y = 4; x - 2 = y
$$

To solve systems of equations *graphically*, we first draw each of the equations in the same coordinate system. Then, the solution of the system is given by the common points where the curves intersect.

Let's see how this method works for the system above. To plot the functions given by the equations, we first rewrite them by making $y$ the subject in each equation.

$$
{y =-x + 4; y = x - 2
$$

Then, we draw the curves in the same coordinate system:

![](<../Source/6.3.8. Solving Systems of Nonlinear Equations Using Graphs/Images/3ed80dfc58c44c92b5cdc0e1fcdd372e.png>)

The lines intersect at the point

$$
(x,y) = (3,1)
$$

, which is the solution to our system.

![](<../Source/6.3.8. Solving Systems of Nonlinear Equations Using Graphs/Images/f54b402e99bd1124d7e679500e95c100.png>)

We can also use the graphical method to solve systems of equations where the corresponding functions are not straight lines. These types of systems are called **nonlinear systems**. Let's consider such an example.

## Solving Systems of Equations Containing Quadratic Functions

**Example:** Using the graphical method, find the solution(s) of the following systems of equations:
${y =-x^{2} + 2; y =-2$

![](<../Source/6.3.8. Solving Systems of Nonlinear Equations Using Graphs/Images/bab7313133f7fa636b6874c76a12f2d1.png>)

**Explanation**

The solutions $(x,y)$ of the system correspond to the intersection points of the two curves.

![](<../Source/6.3.8. Solving Systems of Nonlinear Equations Using Graphs/Images/0663135e2029167ec3135606efa51fde.png>)

From the graph, the curves intersect at

$\qquad$ $(-2,-2)\quad$ and $\quad(2,-2)$.

Therefore, these are the solutions of the corresponding system.

## Solving System of Equations Graphically

**Example:** Using the graphical method, find the solutions $(x,y)$ of the following system of equations:
${y = f(x); y = g(x)$

![](<../Source/6.3.8. Solving Systems of Nonlinear Equations Using Graphs/Images/e9816b2e7ee5e46f76ec314bb7edfea8.png>)

**Explanation**

The solutions $(x,y)$ of the system correspond to the intersection points of the two curves.

![](<../Source/6.3.8. Solving Systems of Nonlinear Equations Using Graphs/Images/33e35594313c35fe0c7b4c434fac6333.png>)

From the graph, the curves intersect at

$\qquad$ $(-1,-1)\quad$ and $\quad(0,-1)$.

Therefore, these are the solutions of the corresponding system.
