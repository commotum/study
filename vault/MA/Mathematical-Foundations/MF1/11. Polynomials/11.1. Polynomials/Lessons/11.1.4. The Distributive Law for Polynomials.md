## Introduction

To expand an expression like

$$
2x^2 (4x+3)
$$

we can use the distributive law. We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x^{2}(4x + 3) &=  \\
2x^{2} \cdot (4x) + 2x^{2} \cdot (3) &=  \\
(2 \cdot 4)(x^{2} \cdot x) + (2 \cdot 3)x^{2} &=  \\
8 \cdot x^{2 + 1} + 6 \cdot x^{2} &=  \\
&= 8x^{3} + 6x^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="multiplying-polynomials"></a>
## Multiplying Polynomials

**Example:** Simplify $-3t^3\left(3t^2+2t\right)$.

**Explanation**

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-3t^{3}(3t^{2} + 2t) &=  \\
(-3t^{3}) \cdot (3t^{2}) + (-3t^{3}) \cdot (2t) &=  \\
((-3) \cdot 3)(t^{3} \cdot t^{2}) + ((-3) \cdot 2)(t^{3} \cdot t) &=  \\
(-9)(t^{3 + 2}) + (-6)(t^{3 + 1}) &=  \\
(-9)(t^{5}) + (-6)(t^{4}) &=  \\
&= - 9t^{5} - 6t^{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Cases Containing Two Negative Signs

**Example:** Simplify $-5x\left(x^3-4\right)$.

**Explanation**

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-5x(x^{3} - 4) &=  \\
(-5x)(x^{3}) + (-5x)(-4) &=  \\
(-5)(x \cdot x^{3}) + ((-5) \cdot (-4))(x) &=  \\
(-5)x^{1 + 3} + (20)x &=  \\
&= - 5x^{4} + 20x
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="multiplying-polynomials-containing-fractions"></a>
## Multiplying Polynomials Containing Fractions

**Example:** Simplify $-6x^2\left(\dfrac{x^3}{2}+3x^2\right)$.

**Explanation**

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-6x^{2}(\frac{x^{3}}{2} + 3x^{2}) &=  \\
(-6x^{2})(\frac{x^{3}}{2}) + (-6x^{2})(3x^{2}) &=  \\
(-6x^{2})(\frac{1}{2}x^{3}) + (-6x^{2})(3x^{2}) &=  \\
((-6) \cdot \frac{1}{2})(x^{3} \cdot x^{2}) + ((-6) \cdot 3)(x^{2} \cdot x^{2}) &=  \\
(-3)(x^{3 + 2}) + (-18)(x^{2 + 2}) &=  \\
&= - 3x^{5} - 18x^{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Note:** We provide the answer in *standard form*, with the terms arranged by degree in descending order.

<a id="multiplying-multivariable-polynomials"></a>
## Multiplying Multivariable Polynomials

**Example:** Simplify $xy\left(3x^2+xy+4\right)$.

**Explanation**

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$
\begin{aligned}
xy(3x^{2} + xy + 4) &=  \\
(xy) \cdot (3x^{2}) + (xy) \cdot (xy) + (xy) \cdot 4 &=  \\
3(x \cdot x^{2})(y) + (x \cdot x)(y \cdot y) + 4(xy) &=  \\
3(x^{3})(y) + (x^{2})(y^{2}) + 4xy &=  \\
&= 3x^{3}y + x^{2}y^{2} + 4xy
\end{aligned}
$$
