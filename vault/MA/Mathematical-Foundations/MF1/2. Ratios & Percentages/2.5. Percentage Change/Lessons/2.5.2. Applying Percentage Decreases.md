## Introduction

In a previous lesson, we spent some time learning how to find a number that is a certain percentage *greater than* a given amount. This is known as finding a *percentage increase.*

We can apply similar reasoning to find a number that is a certain percentage *less than* a given amount. This corresponds to applying a **percentage decrease.**

To apply a percentage decrease, we do the following:

1. Subtract the decrease from $100\%$.
2. Convert the result to a decimal multiplier.
3. Find the product of the multiplier and the original number.

Let's take a look at a worked example.

---

<a id="a-worked-example"></a>
## A Worked Example

Consider the following problem.

> What number is $20\%$ less than $250$?

The key words here are "$20\%$ *less than.*" This means we need to apply a percentage decrease, decreasing the original number by $20\%$.

To solve this problem, we follow three steps.

- **Step 1.** Subtract the decrease from $100\%$.To find a number $20\%$ *less than* $250$, we apply a new percentage of
$100\% - 20\% = 80\%$.
This means that our new number is $80\%$ of $250$.

- **Step 2.** Convert the result to a decimal multiplier.Now, we convert the percentage to a decimal:
$80\% = \dfrac{80}{100} = 0.8$
- **Step 3.** Find the product of the multiplier and the original number.So, to find a number that is $20\%$ less than $250$, we multiply $250$ by $0.8{:}$
$0.8 \cdot 250 = 200$

Therefore, the number that is $20\%$ less than $250$ is $200$.

<a id="applying-a-percentage-decrease"></a>
## Applying a Percentage Decrease

**Example:** What number is $30\%$ less than $120$?

**Explanation**

To find a number $30\%$ *less than* $120$, we apply a new percentage of

$$
100\% - 30\% = 70\%
$$

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

$$
70\% = \dfrac{70}{100} = 0.7
$$

So, to find a number that is $70\%$ of $120$, we multiply $120$ by $0.7{:}$

$$
0.7 \cdot 120 = 84
$$

## Applying a Percentage Decrease: Word Problems

**Example:** A discount of $14\%$ is offered on all hiking boots. What is the sale price of a pair of boots originally priced at $\[math]78$?

**Explanation**

To find an amount that's $14\%$ *less than* $\[math]78$, we apply a new percentage of

$$
100\% - 14\% = 86\%
$$

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

$$
86\% = \dfrac{86}{100} = 0.86
$$

So, to find $86\%$ of $\[/math]78$, we multiply $\[math]78$ by $0.86{:}$

$$
0.86 \cdot \[/math]78 = \[math]67.08
$$

Therefore, the sale price of the hiking boots is $\[/math]67.08$.
