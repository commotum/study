## Introduction

Recall the sum formula for cosine:

$$
\cos(x+y) = \cos x \cos y - \sin x \sin y
$$

Substituting

$$
y={\color{blue}x}
$$

into the above gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos (x + x) &= \cos x\cos x - \sin x\sin x
\end{aligned}
$$

which simplifies to

$$
\cos 2x = \cos^2 {x} - \sin^2 {x}
$$

This is called the **double-angle formula for cosine**.

<a id="using-the-double-angle-formula-to-simplify-expressions"></a>
## Using the Double-Angle Formula to Simplify Expressions

**Example:** Express $\cos^2{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{24}\right)} - \sin^2{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{24}\right)}$ as a single trigonometric ratio.

**Explanation**

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$
\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x = \cos 2x
$$

Substituting

$$
x={\dfrac{\pi}{24}}
$$

into the above, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos^{2} (\frac{π}{24}) - \sin^{2} (\frac{π}{24}) &= \cos (2 \cdot \frac{π}{24}) \\
&= \cos (\frac{π}{12})
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="alternative-forms-of-the-double-angle-formula-for-cosine"></a>
## Alternative Forms of the Double-Angle Formula for Cosine

It's possible to write the double angle formula for cosine in two alternative forms.

From the Pythagorean identity

$$
\sin^2{x}+\cos^2{x}=1
$$

we obtain

$$
\cos^2{x}=1-\sin^2{x}
$$

Substituting this into the double-angle formula for cosine, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos 2x &= \cos^{2} x - \sin^{2} x \\
&= (1 - \sin^{2} x) - \sin^{2} x \\
&= 1 - 2\sin^{2} x
\end{aligned}
$$

This gives an alternative form of the double-angle formula for cosine. We'll restate it below:

$$
\cos{2x} =1 - 2\sin^2{x}
$$

Similarly, from the same Pythagorean identity, we obtain that

$$
\sin^2{x}=1-\cos^2{x}
$$

Substituting into the double-angle formula for cosine, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos 2x &= \cos^{2} x - \sin^{2} x \\
&= \cos^{2} x - (1 - \cos^{2} x) \\
&= 2\cos^{2} x - 1
\end{aligned}
$$

This is another alternative form of the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$
\cos{2x} =2\cos^2{x}-1
$$

## Using Alternative Forms of Double-Angle Formula to Simplify Expressions

**Example:** Express $2\cos^2{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)}-1$ as a single trigonometric ratio.

**Explanation**

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$
2 \cos^2 x - 1 = \cos 2x
$$

Substituting

$$
x={\dfrac{\pi}{3}}
$$

into the above, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
2\cos^{2} (\frac{π}{3}) - 1 &= \cos (2 \cdot \frac{π}{3}) \\
&= \cos (\frac{2π}{3})
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding Exact Values of Trigonometric Expressions Using the Double-Angle Formula

**Example:** Without using a calculator, find the exact value of $\cos^2\left(22.5^\circ\right) - \sin^2\left(22.5^\circ\right)$.

**Explanation**

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$
\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x = \cos 2x
$$

Substituting

$$
x={22.5^\circ}
$$

into the above, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos^{2} (22.5^{∘}) - \sin^{2} (22.5^{∘}) &= \cos (2 \cdot 22.5^{∘}) \\
&= \cos 45^{∘} \\
&= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
