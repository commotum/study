## Introduction

The **minor** of the entry $a_{ij}$ in a matrix $A$ is the determinant of the remaining entries when the row and column containing $a_{ij}$ are removed.

For instance, suppose we want to find the minor of

$$
a_{{\color{red}1}{\color{blue}2}} = {\bbox[lightgray, 2pt]{-1}}
$$

(row ${\color{red}1}$, column ${\color{blue}2}$) in the matrix $A$ given by

$$
\begin{bmatrix}3 & -1 & 6 \\ 5 & 2 & 7 \\ 8 & 9 & 4\end{bmatrix}
$$

To find the minor, we exclude all entries in row $1$ and all entries in column $2$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}3 & -1 & 6 \\ 5 & 2 & 7 \\ 8 & 9 & 4\end{bmatrix}
$$

This leaves us with the $2\times 2$ determinant given by

$\begin{bmatrix}5 & 7 \\ 8 & 4\end{bmatrix}$
We call it $M_{{\color{red}{1}}{\color{blue}{2}}}$ to denote the fact that it was obtained by deleting row $\color{red}1$ and column $\color{blue}2$ of the matrix $A$.

Finally, we compute the above determinant:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{vmatrix}5 & 7 \\ 8 & 4\end{vmatrix} &= 5 \cdot 4 - 7 \cdot 8 \\
&= 20 - 56 \\
&=-36
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating a Minor in a 3x3 Matrix With Integer Entries

**Example:** Calculate the minor of the entry $b_{21}$ in the matrix
$\begin{bmatrix}10 & 1 & 5 \\ 3 & 6 & 8 \\ 4 & 7 & 2\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

The minor of $b_{21} = 3$ (second row, first column) is the determinant of the remaining entries when the column and row containing $3$ are removed:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}∗ & 1 & 5 \\ 3 & ∗ & ∗ \\ ∗ & 7 & 2\end{bmatrix}
$$

So, the minor of $b_{21}=3$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
det\begin{bmatrix}1 & 5 \\ 7 & 2\end{bmatrix} &= \begin{vmatrix}1 & 5 \\ 7 & 2\end{vmatrix} \\
&= 1 \cdot 2 - 5 \cdot 7 \\
&= 2 - 35 \\
&=-33
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating a Minor in a 3x3 Matrix With Variable Entries

**Example:** What is the minor of $c_{22}$ in the matrix
$\begin{bmatrix}7x & 0 & -3y \\ 1 & x + 2y & 4 \\ -y & 0 & 5\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

First, we remove the second row and second column:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}7x & ∗ & -3y \\ ∗ & x + 2 y & ∗ \\ -y & ∗ & 5\end{bmatrix}
$$

Next, we calculate the determinant:

$$
\begin{aligned}
det\begin{bmatrix}7x & -3y \\ -y & 5\end{bmatrix} &= \begin{vmatrix}7x & -3y \\ -y & 5\end{vmatrix} \\
&= 7x \cdot 5 - (-3y) \cdot (-y) \\
&= 35x - 3y^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving for an Unknown Given the Value of a Minor

**Example:** Given that the minor of $a_{23}$ is $5$, find all the possible values of $k$ in the matrix
$\begin{bmatrix}k & 3 & -1 \\ -5 & 2 & 7 \\ -1 & 2k & -6\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

First, we remove the second row and third column:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}k & 3 & ∗ \\ ∗ & ∗ & 7 \\ -1 & 2k & ∗\end{bmatrix}
$$

The minor of $a_{23}$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
det\begin{bmatrix}k & 3 \\ -1 & 2k\end{bmatrix} &= \begin{vmatrix}k & 3 \\ -1 & 2k\end{vmatrix} \\
&= k \cdot 2k - 3 \cdot (-1) \\
&= 2k^{2} + 3
\end{aligned}
$$

We're told that the minor of $a_{23}$ is $5$, so we must have:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2k^{2} + 3 &= 5 \\
2k^{2} &= 2 \\
k^{2} &= 1 \\
k &= ± 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the solutions are $k=-1$ and $k=1$.
