## Introduction

If we want to add (or subtract) three or more matrices, all operations should be done elementwise. For example, suppose

$$
\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 1 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}
$$

Then we can calculate ${A}+{B}-{C}$ by performing the operations on each corresponding entry, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
A + B - C &= \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 1 \\ -1\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}-1 \\ 2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 5 \\ -2\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}1 + (-1) - 0 \\ 1 + 2 - 5 \\ -1 + 0 - (-2)\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}0 \\ -2 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="calculating-the-sum-or-difference-of-three-or-more-matrices"></a>
## Calculating the Sum or Difference of Three or More Matrices

**Example:** Calculate $A-(B-C)$, where

$\begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0 \\ 2 & -2\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

Performing the operations on each corresponding entry, we get:

$$
\begin{aligned}
A - (B - C) &= \begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0 \\ 2 & -2\end{bmatrix} - (\begin{bmatrix}-1 & -1 \\ 2 & 0\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}0 & -5 \\ 1 & 1\end{bmatrix}) \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0 \\ 2 & -2\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-1 - 0 & -1 - (-5) \\ 2 - 1 & 0 - 1\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0 \\ 2 & -2\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-1 & 4 \\ 1 & -1\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}-1 - (-1) & 0 - 4 \\ 2 - 1 & -2 - (-1)\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}0 & -4 \\ 1 & -1\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Properties of Matrix Addition and Subtraction

Since addition and subtraction of matrices are performed elementwise, they have the same properties as addition and subtraction of real numbers.

For example, if $A$, $B$, and $C$ are three $m \times n$ matrices then we can add them in any order:

$$
(A+B)+C = A+(B+C) = A+B+C
$$

Moreover, we can distribute a subtraction over matrices in parentheses:

$$
A - (B + C) = A - B - C
$$

<a id="calculating-the-sum-or-difference-of-three-or-more-matrices-using-distributivity"></a>
## Calculating the Sum or Difference of Three or More Matrices Using Distributivity

**Example:** Find $A+C-(B+A)$, if

$\begin{bmatrix}1 & -2 \\ \sqrt{2} & 0 \\ 0 & -2\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

First, we simplify the expression by distributing the subtraction over the matrices in the parentheses:

$$
\begin{aligned}
A + C - (B + A) &= A + C - B - A \\
&= A + C - B - A \\
&= C - B
\end{aligned}
$$

We are left with a single subtraction. So, we subtract the corresponding entries of the two matrices:

$$
\begin{aligned}
C - B &= \begin{bmatrix}3 & -1 \\ 1 & 2 \\ -6 & 1\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}3 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 \\ -4 & 1\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}3 - 3 & -1 - (-1) \\ 1 - 2 & 2 - 0 \\ -6 - (-4) & 1 - 1\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}0 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 \\ -2 & 0\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$
