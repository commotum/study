## Introduction

The **circumference** of a circle is the length of the path that goes around the circle.

![](<../Source/12.4.3. The Circumference of a Circle/Images/f1e77c0cd2f2322435cd574b1b205c04.png>)

The circumference is usually denoted by the letter $C$.

One important fact about the circumference is that, for *any* circle, no matter how small or large it is, the ratio of circumference $C$ to diameter $d$ is a fixed number! Specifically,

$$
\dfrac{C}{d} = {\color{blue}\boldsymbol{\pi}} \approx 3.141\,592\,654..
$$

This important irrational number is called **pi** (pronounced "pie") and we use the Greek letter $\pi$ to express it. So, we can calculate the circumference by using the following formula:

$$
C = πd
$$

Here, our circle has diameter $4\,\textrm{cm}$, so its circumference is

$$
C = \pi \cdot 4 = 4\pi \approx 12.566\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

rounded to three decimal digits.

Since the diameter is twice as long as the radius ($d=2r$), we can also write the formula for the circumference as

$$
C = \pi d = 2\pi r
$$

## Calculating the Circumference of a Circle Given the Diameter

**Example:** What is the circumference $C$ of a circle with a diameter of $d=10$?

![](<../Source/12.4.3. The Circumference of a Circle/Images/e905a27afe42f9707299ac454ba7cfe8.png>)

**Explanation**

We use the formula $C=\pi d$ and get

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= πd \\
&= π \cdot 10 \\
&= 10π
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the exact answer. If we want an approximate answer, say rounded to three decimal places, we can use the approximation

$$
\pi\approx 3.14\,159
$$

and we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 10 \cdot π \\
&\approx 10 \cdot 3.14159 \\
&\approx 31.416
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating the Circumference of a Circle Given the Radius

**Example:** What is the circumference $C$ of a circle with a radius of $6$?

![](<../Source/12.4.3. The Circumference of a Circle/Images/d289f1df9b1d446f7c85673d5b3028b4.png>)

**Explanation**

We use the formula $C=2\pi r$ to get

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 2πr \\
&= 2π \cdot 6 \\
&= 12π
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding the Radius or Diameter of a Circle Given the Circumference

**Example:** What is the radius $r$ of a circle with a circumference of $C=17\pi$?

![](<../Source/12.4.3. The Circumference of a Circle/Images/34d0843c453ef6ff61bed0b6c45586fa.png>)

**Explanation**

We use the formula for the circumference $C=2\pi r$ and solve for $r$. So, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 2πr \\
17π &= 2πr \\
r &= \frac{17π}{2π} \\
&= \frac{17}{2} \\
&= 8.5
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating the Circumference of a Circle: Word Problem

**Example:** Carol has to wrap a rope around a circular column whose diameter is $20\,\textrm{cm}$. What length of the rope will wrap the column perfectly?

**Explanation**

The length of the rope needed to wrap around the column is its circumference.

The formula for the circumference of a circle is $C = \pi d$, where

$$
d=20\,\textrm{cm}
$$

is the diameter.

Therefore, we have

$$
C = \pi\cdot 20 = 20\pi \,\textrm{cm}
$$
