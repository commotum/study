## Introduction

Some angles have special names.

- A **right angle** has a measure of $90^\circ$. It corresponds to one-quarter of a full turn.![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/0729ecdd0e50e50ae481185840af19a7.png>)
- A **straight angle**, which is just a straight line, has a measure of $180^\circ$. It corresponds to one-half of a full turn.![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/cc5d4e4f0eebd08cb156839b6e212a3c.png>)

- A **null angle** has a measure of $0^\circ$. You can imagine "closing" the angle until the two sides of the angle overlap. In this case, we don't have any turn, so its measure is zero!![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/d2a63c2f2b942f09af2cd7367e0505a0.png>)
- A **full angle** has a measure of $360^\circ$. You can imagine "opening" the angle until one of the sides goes all the way around. So, this is the angle between a ray and itself after it has made one full turn.![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/7ba2fb4b247374848dbc4b7c8d7fcf85.png>)

On a diagram, null and full angles can look the same, so it is up to us what we choose to call it.

<a id="identifying-null-and-full-angles"></a>
## Identifying Null and Full Angles

**Example:** What type of angle is $\angle RST$, shown in the figure above?

![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/7ba2e95bc60239428277d91cdce52d7a.png>)

**Explanation**

Notice that the vertex of $\angle RST$ is point $S$, not point $T$. So $\angle RST$ is *not* a straight angle.

As a result, the two rays $\overset{\longrightarrow}{SR}$ and $\overset{\longrightarrow}{ST}$ of our angle coincide. So, we can think of $\angle RST$ as:

- A *null* angle, assuming it doesn't turn at all.

![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/f106ef923fbb8853e6a5c55fb69aa5cb.png>)

- A *full* angle, assuming it makes one full turn.

![](<../Source/12.2.4. Right, Straight, Full, and Null Angles/Images/704abb17e8bb0827d712d21d1ea5afa1.png>)

So, the answer is "Null" or "Full."
