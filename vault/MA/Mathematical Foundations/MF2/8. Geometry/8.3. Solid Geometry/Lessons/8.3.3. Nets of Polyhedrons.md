## Introduction

We can represent $3$-dimensional shapes in $2$-dimensions using **nets**.
To make a polyhedron net, we take its faces and lay them out as if they were lying flat and open.

Below, we have the net of a polyhedron, but which one?

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/1e30374749e77694003a7249124c1b52.png>)

We can get back the original polyhedron by folding the net along its edges to form a solid shape. The way we fold the net is shown below:

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/de95e6d4f0d42485d8cb0f9f3a2a9c30.png>)

As a result, we get a cube:

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/7ec9e28d21eb3383cabba19770c52bcc.png>)

## Identifying the Net of a Polyhedron

**Example:** Which of the nets above is the net of a rectangular prism?

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/6f6ec633437508eae898016db3599a8e.png>)

**Explanation**

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/576da1bf73d721f27a4eca6ea7968c5f.png>)

If we were to fold the net above along its edges, then we'd get a rectangular prism, as shown below:

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/29330498723fb2c3c02be93f0f252798.png>)

Notice that the other two nets have triangular faces, so they cannot be the nets of a rectangular prism.

Therefore, the correct answer is I.

<a id="identifying-true-statements-given-some-polyhedra-nets"></a>
## Identifying True Statements Given Some Polyhedra Nets

**Example:** Which of the statements is true about the solids shown above?

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/aafce7a138e2f96659ed04b96aad967b.png>)

1. The net of solid I consists of triangles only.
2. The net of solid II consists of $4$ triangles and $2$ rectangles.
3. The net of solid III consists of triangles only.

**Explanation**

Let's sketch the net of each solid.

![](<../Source/8.3.3. Nets of Polyhedrons/Images/fa108ab6eab5252c07853259b613e0ab.png>)

Now, let's go through the statements one by one.

- Statement I is false because the net of solid I consists of rectangles.
- Statement II is false because the net of solid II consists of $2$ triangles and $3$ rectangles.
- Statement III is true.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
