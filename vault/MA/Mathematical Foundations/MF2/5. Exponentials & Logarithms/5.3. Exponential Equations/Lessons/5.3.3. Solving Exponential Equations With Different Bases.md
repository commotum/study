## Introduction

Suppose we are given the equation

$$
3^{3x} = 81^{x-1}
$$

which has two exponential terms. How do we solve it?

To solve this equation, we need both exponential terms to have a common base. To do this, we rewrite the equation using the fact that

$$
81 = {\color{red}3}^4
$$

as follows:

$$
{\color{red}3}^{3x}=\left({\color{red}3}^4\right)^{x-1}
$$

Then, we apply the power rule for exponents:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3^{3x} &= 3^{4(x - 1)}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, since both sides of the equation share the same base $({\color{red}3})$, we equate the exponents and solve for $x$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x &= 4(x - 1) \\
3x &= 4x - 4 \\
x &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

Whenever we can rewrite an equation so that both sides have a common base, we can solve the equation by equating the exponents.

## Solving Exponential Equations Containing Different Bases by Rewriting One Exponent

**Example:** Solve for $x$ where $3^{3x-3}=9^x$.

**Explanation**

To solve this equation, we need both exponential terms to have a common base.

To do this, we write $9$ as ${\color{red}3}^2$, and then rewrite the right-hand side using the power rule for exponents:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3^{3x - 3} &= 9^{x} \\
3^{3x - 3} &= (3^{2})^{x} \\
3^{3x - 3} &= 3^{2x}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, since both sides of the equation share the same base $({\color{red}3})$, we equate the exponents and solve for $x$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x - 3 &= 2x \\
x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving Exponential Equations Containing Different Bases by Rewriting Two Exponents

**Example:** Solve for $x$ where $9^{3x}=27^{x+2}$.

**Explanation**

To solve this equation, we need both exponential terms to have a common base. To do this, we rewrite the equation using

$$
9={\color{red}3}^2
$$

and

$$
27 = {\color{red}3}^3
$$

as follows:

$$
\left({\color{red}3}^2\right)^{3x}=\left({\color{red}3}^3\right)^{x+2}
$$

Then, we apply the power rule for exponents:

$$
3^{2(3x)} = 3^{3(x + 2)}
$$

Now, since both sides of the equation share the same base $({\color{red}3})$, we equate the exponents and solve for $x$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(3x) &= 3(x + 2) \\
6x &= 3x + 6 \\
3x &= 6 \\
x &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$
