## Introduction

Previously, we considered the differential equation

$$
4xy'' + 2y' - y = 0
$$

and, using the Frobenius trial solution

$$
y=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^{\lambda+n}
$$

we found that the possible values of $\lambda$ are $\lambda=0$ and

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

Since $\lambda\in(0,1)$, we take

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

Let's now determine a **recurrence relation** for the coefficients of a Frobenius series solution of our previous example, for the case $\lambda \in (0,1)$.

Rewriting each term of the ODE as a power series (with the

$$
\color{blue}n=0
$$

term isolated), we obtained

$$
\begin{aligned}
4xy^{″} &= 4(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ - 1} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)4((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n}x^{λ + n - 1}, \\
2y^{′} &= 2λa_{0}x^{λ - 1} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)2(λ + n)a_{n}x^{λ + n - 1}, \\
- y &= - ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)a_{n - 1}x^{λ + n - 1}
\end{aligned}
$$

We continue from this point.

**Step 4**: Consolidate all the series into a single series that is equal to $0$.

Since we already handled the

$$
{\color{blue}n=0}
$$

terms in the previous slide to obtain the indicial equation, we focus only on the coefficients of the

$$
n\geq 1
$$

terms here.

For

$$
n\geq 1
$$

collecting like powers of $x$ gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[4((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n} + 2(λ + n)a_{n} - a_{n - 1}]x^{λ + n - 1} &=  \\
x^{λ - 1}∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[4((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n} + 2(λ + n)a_{n} - a_{n - 1}]x^{n} &=  \\
&= x^{λ - 1}∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(4(λ + n)^{2} - 2(λ + n))a_{n} - a_{n - 1}]x^{n} \mid
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 5**: Set the coefficients of the series equal to $0$.

For each

$$
n\geq 1
$$

the coefficient of $x^n$ must be $0$, so

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4(λ + n)^{2} - 2(λ + n))a_{n} - a_{n - 1} &= 0 \\
(λ + n)(4(λ + n) - 2)a_{n} - a_{n - 1} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, since we are working in the case $\lambda\in(0,1)$, we substitute

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

and solve for $a_n{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\frac{1}{2} + n)(4(\frac{1}{2} + n) - 2)a_{n} &= a_{n - 1} \\
4n(n + \frac{1}{2})a_{n} &= a_{n - 1} \\
2n(2n + 1)a_{n} &= a_{n - 1} \\
a_{n} &= (a_{n - 1})/(2n(2n + 1)), n \ge 1
\end{aligned}
$$

## Determining a Recurrence Formula for the Coefficients of a Frobenius Series Solution: Constant Coefficients of y and y'

**Example:** $10x^2y'' + 7xy' + (3x-1)y = 0$

Let $\displaystyle y = x^\lambda \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ be a Frobenius series solution about the regular singular point $x=0$ of the differential equation above.
Find $f(n)$, given that $\lambda\in(0,1)$ and the recurrence relation for $a_n$ can be written as
$a_n = -\dfrac{3a_{n-1}}{f(n)}, \qquad n \geq 1$.

*Hint: Substituting the Frobenius series solution into each term of the ODE, we obtain
$10x^{2}y^{″}|= 10(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)10((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n}x^{λ + n},; 7xy^{′}|= 7λa_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)7(λ + n)a_{n}x^{λ + n},; (3x - 1)y|=-a_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)(3a_{n - 1} - a_{n})x^{λ + n}$.*

**Explanation**

If $x=0$ is a regular singular point of a second-order homogeneous linear differential equation, then the method of Frobenius states that there is at least one solution of the form

$$
y = x^\lambda \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n
$$

where $\lambda$ and $a_n$ for

$$
n=0,1,2,\ldots
$$

are constants.

Substituting this solution and its derivatives into the ODE, collecting like terms, and setting coefficients equal to zero, we obtain:

- the indicial equation from the coefficients of the $n=0$ term for arbitrary $a_0$, and
- a recurrence formula from the coefficients of the $n \geq 1$ terms.

We are given the three terms of the ODE as power series with the

$$
\color{blue}n=0
$$

term isolated in each.

$$
\begin{aligned}
10x^{2}y^{″} &= 10(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)10((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n}x^{λ + n}, \\
7xy^{′} &= 7λa_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)7(λ + n)a_{n}x^{λ + n}, \\
(3x - 1)y &= -a_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)(3a_{n - 1} - a_{n})x^{λ + n}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we continue from this point.

**Step 4**: Consolidate all the series into a single series that is equal to $0$.

Collecting the terms for

$$
{\color{blue}n=0}
$$

we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
10(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ} + 7λa_{0}x^{λ} - a_{0}x^{λ} &=  \\
(10λ^{2} - 10λ + 7λ - 1)a_{0}x^{λ} &=  \\
&= (10λ^{2} - 3λ - 1)a_{0}x^{λ}
\end{aligned}
$$

Similarly, collecting terms for

$$
n\geq 1
$$

we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[10((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n} + 7(λ + n)a_{n} + (3a_{n - 1} - a_{n})]x^{λ + n} &=  \\
∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[10(λ + n)^{2}a_{n} - 3(λ + n)a_{n} - a_{n} + 3a_{n - 1}]x^{λ + n} &=  \\
&= x^{λ}∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(10(λ + n)^{2} - 3(λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + 3a_{n - 1}]x^{n} \mid
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
(10λ^{2} - 3λ - 1)a_{0}x^{λ} + x^{λ}∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(10(λ + n)^{2} - 3(λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + 3a_{n - 1}]x^{n} &= 0 \\
(10λ^{2} - 3λ - 1)a_{0} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(10(λ + n)^{2} - 3(λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + 3a_{n - 1}]x^{n} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 5**: Set the coefficients of the series equal to $0$.

Setting the coefficients of the $n=0$ term equal to $0$ with arbitrary

$$
a_0\neq0
$$

we obtain the indicial equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(10λ^{2} - 3λ - 1)a_{0} &= 0 \\
10λ^{2} - 3λ - 1 &= 0 \\
(2λ - 1)(5λ + 1) &= 0 \\
λ &= -\frac{1}{5}, \frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since we're interested in $\lambda\in(0,1)$, we take

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

Similarly, setting the coefficients of $x^n$ for

$$
n \geq 1
$$

equal to $0$, we obtain the following recurrence relation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(10(λ + n)^{2} - 3(λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + 3a_{n - 1} &= 0 \\
(2(λ + n) - 1)(5(λ + n) + 1)a_{n} + 3a_{n - 1} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we substitute

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

into the above and solve for $a_n{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2(\frac{1}{2} + n) - 1)(5(\frac{1}{2} + n) + 1)a_{n} &= -3a_{n - 1} \\
2n(5n + \frac{7}{2})a_{n} &= -3a_{n - 1} \\
n(10n + 7)a_{n} &= -3a_{n - 1} \\
a_{n} &= - (3a_{n - 1})/(n(10n + 7))
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
f(n)=n(10n+7)
$$

## Determining a Recurrence Formula for the Coefficients of a Frobenius Series Solution: Binomial Coefficient of y or y'

**Example:** $4x^2y'' + 3x(x+2)y' - 2y = 0$

Let $\displaystyle y = x^\lambda \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ be a Frobenius series solution about the regular singular point $x=0$ of the differential equation above.
Find $f(n)$, given that $\lambda\in(0,1)$ and the recurrence relation for $a_n$ can be written as
$a_n = \dfrac{3(1-2n)}{f(n)}\,a_{n-1}, \qquad n \geq 1$.

*Hint: Substituting the Frobenius series solution into each term of the ODE, we obtain
$4x^{2}y^{″}|= 4(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)4((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n}x^{λ + n},; 3x(x + 2)y^{′}|= 6λa_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[3(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1} + 6(λ + n)a_{n}]x^{λ + n},; - 2y|=-2a_{0}x^{λ} - ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)2a_{n}x^{λ + n}$.*

**Explanation**

If $x=0$ is a regular singular point of a second-order homogeneous linear differential equation, then the method of Frobenius states that there is at least one solution of the form

$$
y = x^\lambda \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n
$$

where $\lambda$ and $a_n$ for

$$
n=0,1,2,\ldots
$$

are constants.

Substituting this solution and its derivatives into the ODE, collecting like terms, and setting coefficients equal to zero, we obtain:

- the indicial equation from the coefficients of the $n=0$ term for arbitrary $a_0$, and
- a recurrence formula from the coefficients of the $n \geq 1$ terms.

We are given the three terms of the ODE as a power series with the

$$
\color{blue}n=0
$$

term isolated in each.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x^{2}y^{″} &= 4(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)4((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n))a_{n}x^{λ + n}, \\
3x(x + 2)y^{′} &= 6λa_{0}x^{λ} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[3(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1} + 6(λ + n)a_{n}]x^{λ + n}, \\
- 2y &= -2a_{0}x^{λ} - ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)2a_{n}x^{λ + n}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we continue from this point.

**Step 1**: Consolidate all the series into a single series that is equal to $0$.

Collecting the terms for

$$
{\color{blue}n=0}
$$

we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
4(λ^{2} - λ)a_{0}x^{λ} + 6λa_{0}x^{λ} - 2a_{0}x^{λ} &=  \\
(4λ^{2} - 4λ + 6λ - 2)a_{0}x^{λ} &=  \\
&= 2(2λ^{2} + λ - 1)a_{0}x^{λ}
\end{aligned}
$$

Similarly, collecting terms for

$$
n\geq 1
$$

we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[4((λ + n)^{2} - (λ + n)) + 3(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1} + 6(λ + n)a_{n} - 2a_{n}]x^{λ + n} &=  \\
∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[4(λ + n)^{2} + 2(λ + n)a_{n} - 2a_{n} + 3(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1}]x^{λ + n} &=  \\
&= 2x^{λ}∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(2(λ + n)^{2} + (λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + \frac{3}{2}(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1}]x^{n} \mid
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(2λ^{2} + λ - 1)a_{0}x^{λ} + 2x^{λ}∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(2(λ + n)^{2} + (λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + \frac{3}{2}(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1}]x^{n} &= 0 \\
(2λ^{2} + λ - 1)a_{0} + ∑_(n = 1)^(∞)[(2(λ + n)^{2} + (λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + \frac{3}{2}(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1}]x^{n} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2**: Set the coefficients of the series equal to $0$.

Setting the coefficient of the $n=0$ term equal to $0$ with arbitrary

$$
a_0\neq0
$$

we obtain the indicial equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2λ^{2} + λ - 1 &= 0 \\
(λ + 1)(2λ - 1) &= 0 \\
λ &= -1, \frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since we're interested in $\lambda\in(0,1)$, we take

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

Similarly, setting the coefficients of $x^n$ for

$$
n\geq1
$$

equal to $0$, we obtain the following recurrence relation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2(λ + n)^{2} + (λ + n) - 1)a_{n} + \frac{3}{2}(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1} &= 0 \\
((λ + n) + 1)(2(λ + n) - 1)a_{n} &= -\frac{3}{2}(λ + n - 1)a_{n - 1}
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting

$$
\lambda=\dfrac12
$$

and solving for $a_n$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
((\frac{1}{2} + n) + 1)(2(\frac{1}{2} + n) - 1)a_{n} &= -\frac{3}{2}(\frac{1}{2} + n - 1)a_{n - 1} \\
(n + \frac{3}{2})(2n)a_{n} &= -\frac{3}{2}(n - \frac{1}{2})a_{n - 1} \\
n(2n + 3)a_{n} &= \frac{3}{4}(1 - 2n)a_{n - 1} \\
a_{n} &= (3(1 - 2n))/(4n(2n + 3))a_{n - 1}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
f(n) = 4n(2n+3)
$$
