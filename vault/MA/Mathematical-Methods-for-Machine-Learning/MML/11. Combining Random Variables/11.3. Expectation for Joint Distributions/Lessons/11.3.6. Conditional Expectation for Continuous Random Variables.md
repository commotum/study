## Introduction

For continuous random variables $X$ and $Y$, the **conditional expectation of $X$ given $Y=y$** is defined as

$$
\textrm E\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix} =\int\limits_{\mathbb R} x \, f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y)\;\textrm dx
$$

where $f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y)$ is the conditional probability density function of $X$ given $Y$.

This definition is similar to that of $\textrm{E}[X]$. However, we now use the conditional PDF $f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y)$ instead of the marginal PDF $f_X(x)$ in our integral.

Note the following:

- When we select a particular value of $y$, the conditional expected value of $X$ given $Y=y$ returns a real number. However, if $y$ is not specified, i.e., $\textrm{E}\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix}$ *depends* on $y$, we have that $\textrm{E}\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix}$ gives us a *function* of $y$.
- If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, then $\textrm E\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix} = \textrm E[X]$ for all $y$.
- We sometimes use the notation $\mu_{X \mid y}$ to denote $E\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix}$.
- We have an analogous definition for $\textrm E\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = x\end{bmatrix}$:
$\textrm E\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = x\end{bmatrix} =\int\limits_{\mathbb R} y \, f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)\;\textrm dy$,
where $f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)$ is the conditional probability density function of $Y$ given $X$.

<a id="a-worked-example"></a>
## A Worked Example

Let's find the conditional expectation

$$
\textrm{E}\big\begin{bmatrix}X \, & \, Y=1\big\end{bmatrix}
$$

given that the conditional probability density function $f_{X\mid Y}(x \, \mid \, 1)$ is given by

$$
{2(x - 1),\begin{vmatrix}1 \le x \le 2 \\ 0,\end{vmatrix}otherwise
$$

Applying the formula for the conditional expectation of $X$ given $Y=y$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
E\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = 1\end{bmatrix} &= ∫_{- ∞}^{∞}xf_{X\mid Y}(x \mid 1)dx \\
&= ∫_{1}^{2}x \cdot 2(x - 1)dx \\
&= ∫_{1}^{2}2x(x - 1)dx \\
&= ∫_{1}^{2}2x^{2} - 2xdx \\
&= [\frac{2x^{3}}{3} - x^{2}]_{1}^{2} \\
&= [(2(2)^{3})/(3) - (2)^{2}] - [(2(1)^{3})/(3) - (1)^{2}] \\
&= [\frac{16}{3} - 4] - [\frac{2}{3} - 1] \\
&= [\frac{16}{3} - \frac{12}{3}] - [\frac{2}{3} - \frac{3}{3}] \\
&= \frac{4}{3} - (-\frac{1}{3}) \\
&= \frac{5}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="calculating-a-conditional-expectation-from-a-conditional-pdf"></a>
## Calculating a Conditional Expectation From a Conditional PDF

**Example:** ${(3(1 + y^{2}))/(4),\begin{vmatrix}0 \le y \le 1 \\ 0,\end{vmatrix}otherwise$

Let $X$ and $Y$ be continuous random variables. Find the conditional expected value $\textrm{E}\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X=1\big\end{bmatrix}$ given that the conditional probability density function $f_{Y\mid X}(y \, \mid \, 1)$ is shown above.

**Explanation**

Recall that the conditional expected value of $Y$ given $X=x$ is defined by

$$
\textrm E\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X = x \big\end{bmatrix} = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} y \, f_{Y\mid X}(y \, \mid \, x) \; \textrm{d}y
$$

Therefore, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
E\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = 1\end{bmatrix} &= ∫_{- ∞}^{∞}yf_{Y\mid X}(y \mid 1)dy \\
&= ∫_{0}^{1}y \cdot (3(1 + y^{2}))/(4)dy \\
&= \frac{3}{4}∫_{0}^{1}(y + y^{3})dy \\
&= \frac{3}{4}[\frac{y^{2}}{2} + \frac{y^{4}}{4}]_{0}^{1} \\
&= \frac{3}{4}(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} - 0) \\
&= \frac{9}{16}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="calculating-conditional-expectations-given-joint-and-marginal-pdfs"></a>
## Calculating Conditional Expectations Given Joint and Marginal PDFs

**Example:** $f(x, y) = {y - x,\begin{vmatrix}-1 \le x \le 0, 0 \le y \le 1, \\ 0,\end{vmatrix}otherwisef_{Y}(y) = {\frac{1}{2} + y,\begin{vmatrix}0 \le y \le 1, \\ 0,\end{vmatrix}otherwise$

Let $X$ and $Y$ be continuous random variables. Their joint probability density function and the marginal probability density function of $Y$ are shown above. Find the conditional expected value $\textrm{E}\bigg\begin{bmatrix}X \,\bigg & \, Y=\dfrac{1}{2} \bigg\end{bmatrix}$.

**Explanation**

Recall that the conditional expected value of $X$ given $Y=y$ is defined by

$$
\textrm E\big\begin{bmatrix}X \, & \, Y = y \big\end{bmatrix} = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x \, f_{X\mid Y}(x \, \mid \, y) \; \textrm{d}x
$$

Note that

$$
\textrm E\big\begin{bmatrix}X \, & \, Y = y \big\end{bmatrix}
$$

is a function of $y$.

The conditional density function of $X$ given $Y=y$ can be computed as

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y) &= (f(x, y))/(f_{Y}(y)) \\
&= \frac{y - x}{\frac{1}{2} + y} \\
&= (2(y - x))/(1 + 2y)
\end{aligned}
$$

where

$$
-1 \leq x \leq 0, \: 0 \leq y \leq 1
$$

Outside this domain, we have

$$
f_{X \mid Y} (x \, \mid \, y) = 0
$$

Now, substituting

$$
y=\dfrac{1}{2}
$$

into the expression for $f_{X \mid Y} (x \, \mid \, y)$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid \frac{1}{2}) &= (2(\frac{1}{2} - x))/(1 + 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}) \\
&= \frac{1 - 2x}{2} \\
&= \frac{1}{2} - x
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, using the definition of the conditional expectation above, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
E\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = \frac{1}{2}\end{bmatrix} &= ∫_{- ∞}^{∞}xf_{X\mid Y}(x \mid \frac{1}{2})dx \\
&= ∫_{-1}^{0}x(\frac{1}{2} - x)dx \\
&= ∫_{-1}^{0}(\frac{x}{2} - x^{2})dx \\
&= [\frac{x^{2}}{4} - \frac{x^{3}}{3}]_{-1}^{0} \\
&= 0 - (\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{3}) \\
&=-\frac{7}{12}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="conditional-expectation-as-a-function"></a>
## Conditional Expectation as a Function

**Example:** $f(x, y) = {\frac{x + y}{4},\begin{vmatrix}0 \le y \le x, 0 < x < 2 \\ 0,\end{vmatrix}otherwisef_{X}(x) = {\frac{3x^{2}}{8},\begin{vmatrix}0 < x < 2 \\ 0,\end{vmatrix}otherwise$

Let $X$ and $Y$ be continuous random variables. Their joint probability density function and the marginal probability density function of $X$ are shown above. Find the expression for the conditional expected value $\textrm{E}\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X=x\big\end{bmatrix}$ for $0 < x < 2$.

**Explanation**

Recall that the conditional expected value of $Y$ given $X=x$ is defined by

$$
\textrm E\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X = x \big\end{bmatrix} = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} y \, f_{Y\mid X}(y \, \mid \, x) \; \textrm{d}y
$$

Note that

$$
\textrm E\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X = x \big\end{bmatrix}
$$

is a function of $x$.

The conditional density function of $Y$ given $X=x$ can be computed as

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x) &= (f(x, y))/(f_{X}(x)) \\
&= ((\frac{x + y}{4}))/((\frac{3x^{2}}{8})) \\
&= (2(x + y))/(3x^{2})
\end{aligned}
$$

where

$$
0 \leq y \leq x,\; 0 < x < 2
$$

Outside this domain, we have

$$
f_{Y \mid X} (y \, \mid \, x) = 0
$$

Therefore, using the definition of the conditional expectation above, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
E\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = x\end{bmatrix} &= ∫_{- ∞}^{∞}yf_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)dy \\
&= ∫_{0}^{x}y \cdot (2(x + y))/(3x^{2})dy \\
&= \frac{2}{3x^{2}}∫_{0}^{x}(xy + y^{2})dy \\
&= \frac{2}{3x^{2}}[\frac{xy^{2}}{2} + \frac{y^{3}}{3}]_{0}^{x} \\
&= \frac{2}{3x^{2}}[\frac{x^{3}}{2} + \frac{x^{3}}{3} - 0] \\
&= \frac{2}{3x^{2}} \cdot \frac{5x^{3}}{6} \\
&= \frac{1}{3x^{2}} \cdot \frac{5x^{2} \cdot x}{3} \\
&= \frac{1}{3x^{2}} \cdot \frac{5x^{2} \cdot x}{3} \\
&= \frac{5x}{9}
\end{aligned}
$$
