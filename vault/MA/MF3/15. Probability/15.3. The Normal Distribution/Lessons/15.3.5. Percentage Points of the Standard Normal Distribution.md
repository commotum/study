## Introduction

When working with normally distributed random variables, we often want to find a value of $z$ that gives rise to a specific probability.

For example, suppose that $Z\sim N(0,1)$, and we wish to find the value of $z$ such that

$$
P(Z\leq z) = 0.85
$$

Therefore, our problem is to find a value of $z$ such that

$$
\Phi(z) = 0.85
$$

![](<../Source/15.3.5. Percentage Points of the Standard Normal Distribution/Images/528d1d80711b2d7aa5487fa26144131b.png>)

A good place to start is the CDF table for $\Phi(z)$ around the $z$-values where

$$
\Phi(z)\approx 0.85
$$

This part of the table is shown below.

| $z$ | $\, 0.03 \,$ | $\, 0.04 \,$ | $\, 0.05 \,$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $\, 1.0 \,$ | $\, 0.8485 \,$ | $\, 0.8508 \,$ | $\, 0.8531 \,$ |
| $\, 1.1 \,$ | $\, 0.8708 \,$ | $\, 0.8729 \,$ | $\, 0.8749 \,$ |
| $\, 1.2 \,$ | $\, 0.8907 \,$ | $\, 0.8925 \,$ | $\, 0.8944 \,$ |

There is no listed value of $z$ where

$$
\Phi(z) = 0.85
$$

exactly. However, notice that there are values where $\Phi(z)$ is slightly below and slightly above $0.85{:}$

- $\Phi(1.03) = 0.8485 < 0.85$
- $\Phi(1.04) = 0.8508 > 0.85$

Therefore, the required value of $z$ must lie between $1.03$ and $1.04$. To approximate this value of $z$, we take the average of these two numbers:

$$
z = \dfrac{1.03 + 1.04}{2} = 1.035
$$

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
\Phi(1.035) \approx 0.85
$$

Taking the average of two numbers on an interval to find an improved approximation is known as **interval bisection**.

## Estimating the Z-Score for a Given Probability Using Interval Bisection

**Example:** Approximate the value of $z$ such that $P(Z \leq z) = 0.1450$ using interval bisection.

| $z$ | $\, 0.05 \,$ | $\, 0.06 \,$ | $\, 0.07 \,$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $\, -1.0 \,$ | $\, 0.1469 \,$ | $\, 0.1446 \,$ | $\, 0.1423 \,$ |
| $\, -0.9 \,$ | $\, 0.1711 \,$ | $\, 0.1685 \,$ | $\, 0.1660 \,$ |
| $\, -0.8 \,$ | $\, 0.1977 \,$ | $\, 0.1949 \,$ | $\, 0.1922 \,$ |

**Explanation**

First, we find the two numbers in the table that $0.1450$ lies between. These numbers are $0.1446$ and $0.1469$. So, we have the following inequality:

$$
0.1446 \mid < 0.1450 < 0.1469
$$

Writing the above inequality in terms of $\Phi$, we have

$$
Φ(-1.06) \mid < Φ(z) < Φ(-1.05)
$$

So, using interval bisection, we can approximate $z$ as

$$
z \approx \dfrac{-1.06 + (-1.05)}{2} = -1.055
$$

## Using a Percentage Table To Find the Z-Score for a Given Probability

One final way to find the $z$-score for a given probability is to simply look it up in a **percentage points table**, such as the one below.

| $\quad p \quad$ | $0.55$ | $0.60$ | $0.65$ | $0.70$ | $0.75$ | $0.80$ | $0.85$ | $0.90$ | $0.95$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $\Phi^{-1}(p)$ | $\,0.1257\,$ | $\,0.2533\,$ | $\,0.3853\,$ | $\,0.5244\,$ | $\,0.6745\,$ | $\,0.8416\,$ | $\,1.0364\,$ | $\,1.2816\,$ | $\,1.6449\,$ |

This table gives the values of $\Phi^{-1}(p)$ for different values of $p$, where $p = \Phi(z)$ is the cumulative distribution function for the standard normal distribution.

Let's use the table above to find $z$ such that

$$
P(Z \leq z) = 0.75
$$

First, we express the given information in terms of $\Phi{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(Z \le z) &= 0.75 \\
Φ(z) &= 0.75
\end{aligned}
$$

So, we are looking for the value of $z$ such that

$$
z = \Phi^{-1}(0.75)
$$

We look for the column in the table that corresponds to $p=0.75$, and we find that

$$
p = 0.75 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \Phi^{-1}(p) = 0.6745
$$

Therefore, we have

$$
z = \Phi^{-1}(0.75) = 0.6745
$$

## Using a Percentage Points Table and Symmetry To Find the Z-Score for a Given Probability

**Example:** The table below gives the values of $\Phi^{-1}(p)$ for different values of $p$, where $p = \Phi(z)$ is the cumulative distribution function for the standard normal distribution. Find the value of $z$ such that $P(Z \leq z) = 0.20$.

| $\quad p \quad$ | $0.65$ | $0.70$ | $0.75$ | $0.80$ | $0.85$ | $0.90$ | $0.95$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $\Phi^{-1}(p)$ | $\,0.3853\,$ | $\,0.5244\,$ | $\,0.6745\,$ | $\,0.8416\,$ | $\,1.0364\,$ | $\,1.2816\,$ | $\,1.6449\,$ |

**Explanation**

From the table, notice that

$$
P(Z\leq 0.8416) = 0.80
$$

Therefore, we have

$$
P(Z\geq 0.8416) = 1 - 0.80 = 0.20
$$

By the symmetry of the standard normal distribution, we have

$$
\Phi(-0.8416) = 0.20
$$

![](<../Source/15.3.5. Percentage Points of the Standard Normal Distribution/Images/c5f6a1ed04af60c2c69b10c15f148f83.png>)

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
\begin{aligned}
Φ(-0.8416) &= 0.20 \\
P(Z \le - 0.8416) &= 0.20
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, the required value of $z$ is $-0.8416$.
