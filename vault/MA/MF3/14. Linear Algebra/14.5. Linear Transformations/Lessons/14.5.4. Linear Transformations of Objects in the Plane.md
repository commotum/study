## Introduction

Consider a line segment $\overline{AB}$ whose endpoints are located at $A(1,1)$ and $B(2,-1)$. Let's denote $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ as the position vectors of the points $A$ and $B$ respectively, as shown below.

![](<../Source/14.5.4. Linear Transformations of Objects in the Plane/Images/7d833ce29abadb3a44d5b19a8b787fa5.png>)

In the plane, we know that a non-singular linear transformation maps a straight line onto a straight line. Similarly, a linear transformation maps a line segment onto another line segment.

Suppose that $\overline{A'B'}$ is the image of $\overline{AB}$ under the action of the linear transformation $\mathbf{T}$ represented by the matrix
$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 1 & -2\end{bmatrix}$

How do we compute $\overline{A'B'}$?

To find the coordinates of $A'$ and $B'$ simultaneously, we first create a matrix $X$ containing the coordinates of both ends $A({\color{red}1},{\color{red}1})$ and $B({\color{blue}2},{\color{blue}-1})$:
$\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 1 & -1\end{bmatrix}$

Next, we compute the product $TX$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 1 & -2\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore, $A'({\color{red}3},{\color{red}-1})$ and $B'({\color{blue}3},{\color{blue}4})$. Our result is shown below.

![](<../Source/14.5.4. Linear Transformations of Objects in the Plane/Images/c5a63a567b83d3b0612a243d69b8be39.png>)

## Computing the Image of a Line Segment Under the Action of a Linear Transformation

**Example:** Consider the linear transformation $\mathbf T$ with matrix representation $T$, given by

$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 1 & -1\end{bmatrix}$

The line segment $\overline{AB}$ is shown below. Find $\overline{A'B'}$, the image of $\overline{AB}$ under the action of $\mathbf T$.

![](<../Source/14.5.4. Linear Transformations of Objects in the Plane/Images/99de657ac0df287926da7644ba8c7907.png>)

**Explanation**

To find the image of $\overline{AB}$ under the action of $\mathbf T$, we first create a matrix $X$ containing the coordinates of the endpoints of $\overline{AB}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2 & -1 \\ -1 & 1\end{bmatrix}
$$

Now, we compute the image of $X$ under the action of $\mathbf T$ by calculating the matrix product $TX$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
TX &= \begin{bmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 1 & -1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}2 & -1 \\ -1 & 1\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 3 & -2\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the coordinates of the respective endpoints of the image are $A'(1,3)$ and $B'(1,-2)$, as shown below.

![](<../Source/14.5.4. Linear Transformations of Objects in the Plane/Images/105a1915ca20d2babc4684b44cd6620a.png>)

## Computing the Image of a Polygon Under the Action of a Linear Transformation

**Example:** Consider the linear transformation $\mathbf T$ with matrix representation $T$, given by
$\begin{bmatrix}1 & -1 \\ 0 & -2\end{bmatrix}$

The triangle $\triangle ABC$ is shown below. Find $\triangle A'B'C'$, the image of $\triangle ABC$ under the action of $\mathbf T$.

![](<../Source/14.5.4. Linear Transformations of Objects in the Plane/Images/8d714d1a8dad0fec63191b436f75f1f2.png>)

**Explanation**

To find the image of $T$ under the action of $\mathbf T$, we first create a matrix $X$ containing all of the vertices of $\triangle ABC$:
$\begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 & 1\end{bmatrix}$

Now, we compute the image of $X$ under the action of $\mathbf T$ by calculating the matrix product $TX$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
TX &= \begin{bmatrix}1 & -1 \\ 0 & -2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}-1 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 & 1\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 0 & 1 \\ -4 & 0 & -2\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the coordinates of the respective vertices of $\triangle A'B'C'$ are $(-3,-4), (0,0), (1,-2)$, as shown below.

![](<../Source/14.5.4. Linear Transformations of Objects in the Plane/Images/3ac3afc1cb31f4c0e1cf319acf62a417.png>)
