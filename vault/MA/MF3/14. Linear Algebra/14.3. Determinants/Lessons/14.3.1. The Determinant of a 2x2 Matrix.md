## Introduction

The **determinant** of a general $2 \times 2$ matrix
$\begin{bmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{bmatrix}$
is denoted $\text {det} ({A})$ and computed as
$\begin{bmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{bmatrix}$

For example, given the matrix
$\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 5 & 3\end{bmatrix}$
the determinant is

$$
\det(A) = {\color{blue}{2}} \cdot {\color{blue}{3}} - {\color{red}{1}} \cdot {\color{red}{5}} = 1
$$

To remember how to compute the determinant of a $2 \times 2$ matrix, you can imagine taking the product of the diagonal of the matrix, and then subtracting the product of the off-diagonal terms, as follows:

![](<../Source/14.3.1. The Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix/Images/fa64b649b848e4196e7a4763e7a9e1f6.png>)

Later, we will see that the determinant is related to the "volume" spanned by the row vectors or column vectors in a matrix. We will also see that the determinant can tell us about many other properties of a matrix.

However, in this lesson, we will just focus on computing the determinant.

## Calculating the Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix With Integer Entries

**Example:** Calculate $\det(A)$, if $\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 5 & 6\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

For a $2 \times 2$ matrix, the determinant is given by the formula

$$
\begin{vmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{vmatrix}
$$

Using the formula, we have:

$$
\begin{aligned}
det(A) &= \begin{vmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 5 & 6\end{vmatrix} \\
&= 3 \cdot 6 - 2 \cdot 5 \\
&= 18 - 10 \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating the Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix With Non-Integer Entries

**Example:** Calculate $\det(A)$, if $\begin{bmatrix}\sqrt{2} & -2 \\ 0 & \sqrt{3}\end{bmatrix}$

**Explanation**

For a $2 \times 2$ matrix, the determinant is given by the formula

$$
\begin{vmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{vmatrix}
$$

Using the formula, we have:

$$
\begin{aligned}
det(A) &= \begin{vmatrix}\sqrt{2} & -2 \\ 0 & \sqrt{3}\end{vmatrix} \\
&= \sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{3} - (-2) \cdot 0 \\
&= \sqrt{6}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving for an Unknown in an Expression Involving the Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

**Example:** If
${A} = \left\lbrack \matrix {2 & -1 \ -3 & k} \right\rbrack$
and
$\det({A}) = 5$, what is the value of $k$?

**Explanation**

For a $2 \times 2$ matrix, the determinant is given by the formula

$$
\begin{vmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{vmatrix}
$$

Using the formula, we have:

$$
\begin{aligned}
det(A) &= \begin{vmatrix}2 & -1 \\ -3 & k\end{vmatrix} \\
&= 2 \cdot k - (-1) \cdot (-3) \\
&= 2k - 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $\det(A)=5$, we obtain:

$$
\begin{aligned}
det(A) &= 5 \\
2k - 3 &= 5 \\
2k &= 8 \\
k &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$
