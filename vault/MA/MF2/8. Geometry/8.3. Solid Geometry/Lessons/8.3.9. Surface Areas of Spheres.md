## Introduction

Given a sphere with radius

$$
r=4\:\textrm{cm}
$$

how do we calculate its surface area?

![](<../Source/8.3.9. Surface Areas of Spheres/Images/0c6d897fee982484b63a7aec75ec89d1.png>)

We can calculate the surface area $S$ of a sphere using the formula

$$
S = 4\pi r^2
$$

where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.

Substituting

$$
r=4\:\textrm{cm}
$$

into the formula, we obtain

$$
S = 4 \pi (4)^2 = 64\pi \:\textrm{cm}^2
$$

We often express the surface area of a sphere as a multiple of $\pi$. We can also get a numerical approximation using a calculator. In this case, we have

$$
S \approx 201.06 \:\textrm{cm}^2
$$

rounded to two decimal places.

## Finding the Surface Area of a Sphere Given Its Radius or Diameter

**Example:** A sphere has a diameter of $3 \: \textrm{in}$. Find the surface area of the sphere.

**Explanation**

We can calculate the surface area of a sphere using the formula

$$
S = 4 \pi r^2
$$

where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.

Substituting

$$
r=\dfrac{3}{2} \: \textrm{in}
$$

into the formula, we obtain

$$
S = 4 \pi \left(\dfrac {3} {2} \right)^2 = 9\pi \: \textrm{in}^2
$$

## Finding the Radius or Diameter of a Sphere Given Its Surface Area

**Example:** Calculate the radius of a sphere given that its surface area is $50\pi \: \textrm{cm}^2$.

![](<../Source/8.3.9. Surface Areas of Spheres/Images/7bc0cfbe0e49c296a4e7f8289375faa5.png>)

**Explanation**

We can calculate the surface area of a sphere using the formula

$$
S = 4 \pi r^2
$$

where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.

Substituting

$$
S=50\pi \: \textrm{cm}^2
$$

into the formula, we can solve for $r$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
S &= 4πr^{2} \\
50π &= 4πr^{2} \\
\frac{50π}{4π} &= r^{2} \\
r^{2} &= \frac{25}{2} \\
r &= \sqrt{\frac{25}{2}} \\
&= \frac{5}{\sqrt{2}} \\
&= \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}cm
\end{aligned}
$$

## Relating the Surface Area and Volume of a Sphere

**Example:** The volume of the sphere is $\dfrac{500}{3}\pi \: \textrm{in}^3$. Calculate the surface area of the sphere.

**Explanation**

We can find the volume of a sphere using the formula

$$
V = \dfrac 4 3 \pi r^3
$$

where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.

Substituting

$$
V=\dfrac{500}{3}\pi \: \textrm{in}^3
$$

into the formula, we can solve for $r$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= \frac{4}{3}πr^{3} \\
\frac{500}{3}π &= \frac{4}{3}πr^{3} \\
\frac{500π \cdot 3}{3 \cdot 4π} &= r^{3} \\
r^{3} &= 125 \\
r &= 5in
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, the surface area of ​​the sphere is

$$
\begin{aligned}
S &= 4πr^{2} \\
&= 4π(5)^{2} \\
&= 100πin^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
