## Introduction

Suppose we want to find all of the solutions to the equation

$$
x \cdot 3^x - 2 \cdot 3^x = 0
$$

First, we factor the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x \cdot 3^{x} - 2 \cdot 3^{x} &= 0 \\
(x - 2) \cdot 3^{x} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that $ab=0$ if and only if $a=0$ or $b=0$. This gives us two equations,

$$
x-2 = 0 \quad \textrm{and} \quad 3^x=0
$$

We now solve each of these equations separately.

- The equation $x-2=0$ has the solution $x=2$.
- The equation $3^x=0$ has no real solution because $3^x$ is positive for every real number $x$.

Therefore, the original equation has a single solution, $x=2$.

## Solving an Exponential Equation by Factoring

**Example:** What is the product of solutions of the equation $x^2e^{x-1} - 2x e^{x-1} -15 e^{x-1} = 0$?

**Explanation**

First, we factor the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}e^{x - 1} - 2xe^{x - 1} - 15e^{x - 1} &= 0 \\
(x^{2} - 2x - 15)e^{x - 1} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that $ab=0$ if and only if $a=0$ or $b=0$. This gives us two equations,

$$
x^2 - 2x - 15 = 0 \quad \textrm{and} \quad e^{x-1}=0
$$

We now solve each of these equations separately.

- The equation $x^2 - 2x - 15 = 0$ can be factored as
$(x+3)(x-5)=0$,
which leads to the solutions $x=-3,5$.
- The equation $e^{x-1}=0$ has no solutions because $e^{x-1}$ is positive for every real number $x$.

Therefore, the original equation has the solutions $x=-3,5$, and their product is

$$
(-3) \cdot 5 = -15
$$

## Solving an Exponential Equation by Rearranging the Equation and Then Factoring

**Example:** What is the product of solutions of the equation $9x^2\cdot 3^{x+1} = 16\cdot 3^{x+1}$?

**Explanation**

First, we factor the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
9x^{2} \cdot 3^{x + 1} &= 16 \cdot 3^{x + 1} \\
9x^{2} \cdot 3^{x + 1} - 16 \cdot 3^{x + 1} &= 0 \\
(9x^{2} - 16)3^{x + 1} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that $ab=0$ if and only if $a=0$ or $b=0$. This gives us two equations,

$$
9x^2 - 16 = 0 \quad \textrm{and} \quad 3^{x+1}=0
$$

We now solve each of these equations separately.

- The equation $9x^2 - 16=0$ can be factored as
$(3x+4)(3x-4) = 0$,
which leads to the solutions $x=-\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{4}{3}$.
- The equation $3^{x+1}=0$ has no solutions because $3^{x+1}$ is positive for every real number $x$.

Therefore, the original equation has the solutions

$$
x=-\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{4}{3}
$$

and their product is

$$
\left(-\dfrac{4}{3} \right) \cdot \dfrac{4}{3} = -\dfrac{16}{9}
$$
