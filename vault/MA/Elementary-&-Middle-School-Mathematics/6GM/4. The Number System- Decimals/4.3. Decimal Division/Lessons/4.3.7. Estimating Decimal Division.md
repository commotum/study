## Introduction

We can estimate decimal division by rounding each decimal to the nearest whole number and dividing the resulting numbers.

For example, suppose we wish to estimate the quotient of the following division problem.
$19.7 \div 5.3$

One way to proceed is first to round both quantities to the nearest whole number:

$$
19.7\begin{vmatrix}\div & 5.3 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 20 & \div\end{vmatrix}5
$$

So, we can form an estimate of $19.7 \div 5.3$ by evaluating $20 \div 5$.

Computing the quotient of this simplified problem gives

$$
20 \div 5 = 4
$$

Therefore, $19.7 \div 5.3$ is approximately equal to $4$. We can write this symbolically as follows:

$$
19.7 \div 5.3 \approx 4
$$

The symbol

$$
\approx
$$

means "is approximately equal to."

## Rounding to the Nearest Whole Number

**Example:** By rounding the dividend to the nearest whole number, estimate the value of $94.8 \div 5$.

**Explanation**

First, we round the dividend to the nearest whole number:
$94.8 \rightarrow 95$

Hence, we need to find the value of $95 \div 5$. Computing the quotient, we get:

| $1$$9$$5$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 5}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$5$$4$$5$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$4$$5$$0$ |  |
| --- | ---: |

Hence,

$$
95 \div 5 = 19
$$

Therefore, $94.8 \div 5$ is approximately equal to $19$.

## Rounding to the Nearest Ten, Hundred or Thousand

**Example:** Estimate $12.3 \div 106.8$ by rounding the divisor to the nearest hundred.

**Explanation**

First, we round the divisor to the nearest hundred:
$106.8 \rightarrow 100$

Now, we need to compute

$$
12.3 \div 100
$$

There are two zeros in $100$, so we move the decimal point two places to the left:

$$
12.3 \rightarrow 0.123
$$

Therefore, $12.3 \div 106.8$ is approximately equal to $0.123 \,$.

## Rounding Decimals With Two Decimal Places

**Example:** Estimate the value of $206.6 \div 3.43$ by rounding each number to the nearest whole number.

**Explanation**

We round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$206.6\begin{vmatrix}\div & 3.43 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 207 & \div\end{vmatrix}3$

Now, we need to find the value of

$$
207 \div 3
$$

So, we go through the long division procedure as usual:

| $6$$9$$3$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7}$$-\!\!\!\!$$1$$8$$2$$7$$-\!\!\!\!$$2$$7$$\fbox{0}$ |
| --- |

Therefore, $206.6 \div 3.43$ is approximately equal to $69$.

<a id="cases-with-remainders"></a>
## Cases With Remainders

**Example:** Estimate the value of $116.8 \div 5.15$ by rounding each number to the nearest whole number. Round your final answer to the nearest whole number.

**Explanation**

We round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$116.8\begin{vmatrix}\div & 5.15 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 117 & \div\end{vmatrix}5$

Now, we need to find the value of
$117\div 5$.

So, we go through the long division procedure as usual:

|  |  | $2$ | $3$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $5$ | $\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7}$ |  |  |
| $-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$ | $1$ | $0$ |  |
|  |  | $1$ | $7$ |
|  | $-\!\!\!\!\!$ | $1$ | $5$ |
|  |  |  | $\fbox{2}$ |

Therefore,

$$
117 \div 5 = 23\,\text{R}2
$$

The remainder $2$ is closer to $0$ than to the divisor $5$. So, to round to the nearest whole number, we must round down:

$$
117 \div 5 \rightarrow 23
$$

Therefore, $116.8 \div 5.15$ is approximately equal to $23$.
