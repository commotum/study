## Introduction

When using long division to divide whole numbers, it's often appropriate to round the final answer.

For instance, let's find the value of $58 \div 7$, rounded to the nearest tenth. We start by going through the long division procedure as usual:

| $\,\, \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8 {\,. \,} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} {\color{black}8} \: \ldots$$7$$\phantom{\mid}\!\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8 {\,. \,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!$$5$$6$$2$$0$$-\!\!\!$$1$$4$$6$$0$$-\!\!\!$$5$$6$$4$$\vdots$ |
| --- |

We could divide further, but since we want to round to $1$ decimal place (the nearest tenth), we stop after finding the quotient to $2$ decimal places:

$$
58 \div 7 = 8.28 \ldots
$$

Finally, we round the quotient to the nearest tenth. The hundredths place is $8$, which is greater than $5$, so we round up:
$8.28 \ldots \rightarrow 8.3$

## Rounding to One Decimal Place

**Example:** Round the value of $137 \div 8$ to $1$ decimal place.

**Explanation**

We start by going through the long division procedure as usual:

| $\,\, \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\,. \,} 1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} {\color{black}2} \: \ldots$$8$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\,. \,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$8$$5$$7$$-\!\!\!$$5$$6$$1$$0$$-\!\!\!$$8$$2$$0$$-\!\!\!$$1$$6$$4$$\vdots$ |
| --- |

We could divide further, but since we want to round to $1$ decimal place, we stop after finding the quotient to $2$ decimal places:

$$
137 \div 8 = 17.12\ldots
$$

Finally, we round the quotient to the nearest tenth ($1$ decimal place). The hundredths place is $2$, which is less than $5$, so we round down:
$17.12\ldots \rightarrow 17.1\,$.

## Rounding to One Decimal Place: Word Problems

**Example:** Abby wants to cut a $63$ inch piece of wire into four smaller pieces of equal size. How long should each piece be? Round your answer to $1$ decimal place.

**Explanation**

To determine the length of each piece, we need to divide $63$ by $4$.

We start by going through the long division procedure as usual:

| $\, 1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 5 {\,. \,} 7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} {\color{blue}5} \:$$4$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\,. \,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!$$4$$2$$3$$-\!\!\!$$2$$0$$3$$0$$-\!\!\!$$2$$8$$2$$0$$-\!\!\!$$2$$0$$0$ |
| --- |

We went through all the digits and reached a remainder of $0$, so the division is done.

Therefore,

$$
63 \div 4= 15.75
$$

Finally, we round the quotient to the nearest tenth ($1$ decimal place). The hundredths place is $5$, which is greater than or equal to $5$, so we round up:

$$
15.75 \rightarrow 15.8
$$

We conclude that each piece would be about $15.8$ inches long.

## Rounding to Two Decimal Places

**Example:** Round the value of $66 \div 9$ to the nearest hundredth.

**Explanation**

We start by going through the long division procedure as usual:

| $\,\, \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\,. \,} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} {\color{blue}3}\: \ldots$$9$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\,. \,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$3$$3$$0$$-\!\!\!$$2$$7$$3$$0$$-$$2$$7$$3$$0$$-\!\!$$2$$7$$3$$\vdots$ |
| --- |

We could divide further, but since we want to round to $2$ decimal places (the nearest hundredth), we stop after finding the quotient to $3$ decimal places:

$$
66 \div 9 = 7.333\ldots
$$

Finally, we round the quotient to the nearest hundredth. The thousandths place is $3$, which is less than $5$, so we round down:
$7.333\ldots \rightarrow 7.33\,$.
