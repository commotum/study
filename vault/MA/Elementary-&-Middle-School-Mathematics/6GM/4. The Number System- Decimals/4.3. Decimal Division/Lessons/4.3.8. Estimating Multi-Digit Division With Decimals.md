## Introduction

To estimate the result of dividing two decimals, we can round both decimals before we divide.

For example, let's estimate the value of $60.1 \div 15.4$ by rounding each decimal to the nearest whole number.

First, we round both decimals to the nearest whole number:
$60.1\begin{vmatrix}\div & 15.4 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 60 & \div\end{vmatrix}15$

So, we need to find the value of $60 \div 15$. Computing the quotient, we get:

$$
60 \div 15 = 4
$$

Therefore, $60.1 \div 15.4$ is approximately $4$.

## Dividing a Whole Number by a Decimal

**Example:** By rounding the divisor to the nearest whole number, estimate the value of $736 \div 22.5$.

**Explanation**

First, we round the divisor to the nearest whole number:
$22.5 \rightarrow {\color{blue}23}$

So, we need to find the value of $736 \div 23$. Using long division, we get:

| $3$$2$$23$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!$$6$$9$$4$$6$$-\!\!\!\!$$4$$6$$0$ |
| --- |

That is,

$$
736 \div 23 = 32
$$

Therefore, $736 \div 22.5$ is approximately $32$.

**Explanation**

$539.5\begin{vmatrix}\div & 18.2 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 540 & \div\end{vmatrix}18$
So, we need to find the value of $540 \div 18$. Using long division, we get:

| $3$$0$$18$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4 {\;\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!\!$$5$$4$$0$$0$ |
| --- |

That is,

$$

$$

Therefore, $539.5 \div 18.2$ is approximately $30$.
