## Introduction

A **line of symmetry** for a figure is a line such that folding the figure along the line produces two matching halves.

For example, let's consider the polygon below.

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/ba6c6d5e3a16c54883423b62e46e5472.png>)

This polygon has a line of symmetry $l$, shown below.

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/b5637fcbe492f56615c1ccde2453fad4.png>)

Notice how folding the figure along $l$ produces two perfectly matching halves.

## Identifying Lines That Reflect a Polygon to Itself

**Example:** Which of the pictures below shows a line of symmetry for the figure?

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/e6c470c824274be93bcd09bea9aff69c.png>)

**Explanation**

A line of symmetry for a figure is a line such that folding the figure along the line produces two matching halves.

Let's consider each of the lines in turn.

- By folding the polygon along line I, we do *not* map the polygon onto itself.![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/e335502ad46a2dac71a66f4d4debc0b7.png>)

- By folding the polygon along line II, we map the polygon onto itself.![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/b3bd8ad60479ae2660cbde8901342826.png>)

- By folding the polygon along line III, we do *not* map the polygon onto itself.![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/3464d4f1d615c24e8f573122f8b6e484.png>)

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."

## Finding Lines of Symmetry

Some polygons don't have any lines of symmetry. For example, let's find how many lines of symmetry the polygon shown below has.

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/627ea9b537d042421e0ba4d39dd50d1d.png>)

Recall that a *line of symmetry* for a figure is a line such that folding the figure along the line produces two matching halves.

No matter which lines we try, it is not possible to fold this polygon into matching parts. Therefore, the given polygon has no lines of symmetry.

To demonstrate, we can't consider all possible lines, but three attempts are shown below.

- Folding along a horizontal line does not map the polygon onto itself.

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/466b7448b890d5fbb68357a4e461584a.png>)

- Folding along a sloped line does not map the polygon onto itself either.

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/f7eb4722f08ba57315c266cfa7d01846.png>)

- Folding along a vertical line does not map the trapezoid onto itself either.

![](<../Source/7.1.8. Lines of Symmetry/Images/9c5d71d830b2e1a0177ec76daf5127bf.png>)

We could try other lines, but none of them will work.
