## Introduction

Let's look at the following right triangle.

![](<../Source/7.1.3. Areas of Right Triangles/Images/770b5cc7e4cb05d7adb9af31ca46ee3d.png>)

How can we find its area?

It turns out that:

> The area of a right triangle is half the area of the corresponding rectangle:
> $\text{Area of Triangle} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \text{(Area of Rectangle)}$.

In our case, the corresponding rectangle has dimensions $8\:\text{m}$ by $6\:\text{m}$.

![](<../Source/7.1.3. Areas of Right Triangles/Images/aa33a5d61f1c0e2955f6c2099a8fa035.png>)

The area of the rectangle is

$$
\begin{aligned}
Area of Rectangle &= 8m \cdot 6m \\
&= (8 \cdot 6)(m \cdot m) \\
&= 48m^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the area of the triangle is

$$
\textrm{Area of Triangle} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 48 = 24 \: \text{m}^2
$$

Let’s see more examples.

## Finding the Area of a Right Triangle

**Example:** Find the area of the triangle.

![](<../Source/7.1.3. Areas of Right Triangles/Images/c6ab8226fdf11cf5a359079e603d8dc5.png>)

**Explanation**

The area of a right triangle is half the area of the corresponding rectangle:

$$
\text{Area of Triangle} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \text{(Area of Rectangle)}
$$

In our case, the corresponding rectangle has dimensions $10\:\text{m}$ by $6\:\text{m}$.

![](<../Source/7.1.3. Areas of Right Triangles/Images/cb2a9d7c204bf0709679b890dd3bc9cf.png>)

The area of the rectangle is

$$
\text{Area of Rectangle} = 10 \cdot 6 = 60 \: \text{m}^2
$$

Therefore, the area of the triangle is

$$
Area of Triangle = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 60 = 30m^{2}
$$

## Finding the Area of a Right Triangle From a Description

**Example:** A flowerbed in the shape of a right triangle has a base length of $10\,\textrm{ft}$ and a width of $7\,\textrm{ft}$. What is the area of the flowerbed?

**Explanation**

Let's sketch our flowerbed based on the description.

![](<../Source/7.1.3. Areas of Right Triangles/Images/dc4cc6ff06859d512a372588869d07ca.png>)

The area of a right triangle is half the area of the corresponding rectangle:

$$
\text{Area of Triangle} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \text{(Area of Rectangle)}
$$

In our case, the corresponding rectangle has dimensions $10\:\text{ft}$ by $7\:\text{ft}$.
So, the area of the rectangle is

$$
\text{Area of Rectangle} = 10 \cdot 7 = 70 \: \text{ft}^2
$$

Therefore, the area of the triangle is

$$
Area of Triangle = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 70 = 35ft^{2}
$$
