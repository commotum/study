## Introduction

The volume of a shape is the amount of $3$-dimensional space inside it. We can calculate the volume of a rectangular prism with the following formula:

$$
V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}
$$

Let's use this to calculate the volume of the rectangular prism shown below.

![](<../Source/7.2.7. Volumes of Rectangular Prisms With Fractional Edge Lengths/Images/0f8dfac8c54f990fac9201168ac41b51.png>)

This rectangular prism has a length of $2\,\text{km}$, a width of

$$
\dfrac{3}{2} \,\text{km}
$$

, and a height of

$$
\dfrac{5}{2}\,\text{km}
$$

So, the volume of the rectangular prism is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= length \cdot width \cdot height \\
&= 2 \cdot \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{5}{2} \\
&= \frac{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 2} \\
&= \frac{30}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

We can simplify the fraction above by dividing the numerator and denominator by $2$:

$$
\dfrac{30 \div 2}{4 \div 2}= \dfrac{15}{2}
$$

Finally, we write the resulting improper fraction as a mixed number:

$$
\dfrac{15}{2} = 7 \, \textrm{R} \, 1 = 7 \, \dfrac{1}{2}
$$

Therefore, the volume is

$$
7 \, \dfrac{1}{2}\,\text{km}^3
$$

## Calculating the Volume of a Rectangular Prism

**Example:** What is the volume of the rectangular prism shown below?

![](<../Source/7.2.7. Volumes of Rectangular Prisms With Fractional Edge Lengths/Images/4d8a7338f61c3708dfd6f6bee57432c3.png>)

**Explanation**

The rectangular prism has a length of $2\,\text{cm}$, a width of

$$
\dfrac{3}{4}\,\text{cm}
$$

, and a height of

$$
\dfrac{2}{3}\,\text{cm}
$$

So, the volume of the rectangular prism is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= length \cdot width \cdot height \\
&= 2 \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \\
&= \frac{2}{1} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \\
&= \frac{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2}{1 \cdot 4 \cdot 3} \\
&= \frac{12}{12}
\end{aligned}
$$

We now simplify the above fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by $12$:

$$
\dfrac{12 \div 12}{12 \div 12}= \dfrac{1}{1} = 1
$$

Therefore, the volume of the rectangular prism is $1 \,\text{cm}^3$.

## Calculating the Volume of a Rectangular Prism Given Mixed Number Edge Lengths

**Example:** Determine the volume of the figure shown above.

![](<../Source/7.2.7. Volumes of Rectangular Prisms With Fractional Edge Lengths/Images/fc10a0783650ba75c24d6e36933ed609.png>)

**Explanation**

The rectangular prism has a length of $3 \, \text{in}$, a width of

$$
1\,\dfrac{1}{3} \,\text{in}
$$

, and a height of $2\,\text{in}$.

So, the volume of the rectangular prism is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= length \cdot width \cdot height \\
&= 3 \cdot 1\frac{1}{3} \cdot 2
\end{aligned}
$$

We write

$$
1 \, \dfrac{1}{3}
$$

as an improper fraction:

$$
1 \, \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{(1 \times 3) + 1}{3} = \dfrac{4}{3}
$$

Now, we can multiply the numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= 3 \cdot \frac{4}{3} \cdot 2 \\
&= \frac{3 \cdot 4 \cdot 2}{3} \\
&= \frac{24}{3} \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the volume is $8 \,\text{in}^3$.

## Volumes of Rectangular Prisms Given the Height and Base Area

We can also calculate the volume of a rectangular prism if we know the height and the area of the base. All we have to do is multiply these quantities together:

$$
V = \text{base} \times \text{height}
$$

Let's apply this formula to find the volume of the rectangular prism shown below.

![](<../Source/7.2.7. Volumes of Rectangular Prisms With Fractional Edge Lengths/Images/252349f874fb661528fb6bc2c6e6f0ee.png>)

For this rectangular prism, the area of the base is $8 \, \text{cm}^2$ and the height is

$$
\dfrac{3}{4} \, \text{cm}
$$

So, the volume of the rectangular prism is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= base \cdot height \\
&= 8 \cdot \frac{3}{4} \\
&= \frac{8 \cdot 3}{4} \\
&= \frac{24}{4} \\
&= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the volume is $6 \, \text{cm}^3$.

**Note:** Where does this formula come from? The key is to realize that the area of the base of a rectangular prism is equal to the length multiplied by the width:

$$
\text{base} = \text{length} \times \text{width}
$$

Looking at the original formula for calculating the volume of a rectangular prism, we have:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= length \cdot width \cdot height \\
&= (length \cdot width) \cdot height \\
&= base \cdot height
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating the Volume of a Rectangular Prism Given the Height and Base Area

**Example:** Find the volume of the figure shown below.

![](<../Source/7.2.7. Volumes of Rectangular Prisms With Fractional Edge Lengths/Images/97574b2a0c54164f12e8c897f6f772bc.png>)

**Explanation**

The picture shows a rectangular prism, where the area of the base is $4 \, \text{m}^2$ and the height is

$$
\dfrac{4}{3} \, \text{m}
$$

So, the volume of the rectangular prism is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= base \cdot height \\
&= 4 \cdot \frac{4}{3} \\
&= \frac{4 \cdot 4}{3} \\
&= \frac{16}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the volume is

$$
\dfrac{16}{3} \, \text{m}^3
$$
