## Introduction

In previous lessons, we explored how to convert larger metric units of length to smaller ones. In this lesson, we will learn how to convert smaller metric units of length to larger ones.

To convert between metric units of length, we use the following unit conversions:

- $1$ centimeter $= 10$ millimeters
- $1$ meter $=100$ centimeters
- $1$ kilometer $=1,000$ meters

We can visualize equivalent units of length using measuring instruments. For example, the meterstick below shows the equivalence between $1$ meter and $100$ centimeters.

![](<../Source/1.6.1. Converting Metric Units of Length to Larger Units/Images/a8480ad1707bfe0411bc779b6cc04d23.png>)

Let's get some practice at converting between different units of length.

<a id="a-worked-example"></a>
## A Worked Example

Suppose we want to convert $420$ millimeters to centimeters. We start with the unit conversion between centimeters and millimeters:

$$
1 \,\textrm{cm} = 10 \,\textrm{mm}
$$

Since we are converting *from* millimeters *to* centimeters, we divide both sides of the above equation by $10$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{10} \,\textrm{cm} = 1 \,\textrm{mm}
$$

The right-hand side reads $1\,\textrm{mm}$, and we want to know how many centimeters are in $\color{blue}420$ millimeters. So, we multiply *both* sides of this equation by ${\color{blue}{420}}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
420 \cdot \frac{1}{10}cm &= 420 \cdot 1mm \\
(420 \div 10)cm &= 420mm \\
42cm &= 420mm
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $420$ millimeters equals $42$ centimeters.

<a id="converting-millimeters-to-centimeters"></a>
## Converting Millimeters to Centimeters

**Example:** What is $14\,\textrm{mm}$ in centimeters?

**Explanation**

We need to convert $14$ millimeters to centimeters.

We start with the unit conversion between centimeters and millimeters:

$$
1 \,\textrm{cm} = 10 \,\textrm{mm}
$$

Since we are converting from millimeters to centimeters, we divide both sides of the above equation by $10$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{10} \,\textrm{cm} = 1 \,\textrm{mm}
$$

We want to determine how many centimeters are in $14$ millimeters. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $14{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
14 \cdot \frac{1}{10}cm &= 14 \cdot 1mm \\
(14 \div 10)cm &= 14mm \\
1.4cm &= 14mm
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $14\,\textrm{mm}$ equals $1.4\,\textrm{cm}$.

## Converting Centimeters to Meters

**Example:** What is $363$ centimeters in meters?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between meters and centimeters:

$$
1 \,\textrm{m} = 100 \,\textrm{cm}
$$

Since we are converting from centimeters to meters, we divide both sides of the above equation by $100$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{100} \,\textrm{m} = 1 \,\textrm{cm}
$$

We want to determine how many meters are in $363$ centimeters. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $363{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
363 \cdot \frac{1}{100}m &= 363 \cdot 1cm \\
(363 \div 100)m &= 363cm \\
3.63m &= 363cm
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $363\,\textrm{cm}$ equals $3.63\,\textrm{m}$.

## Converting Meters to Kilometers

**Example:** How many kilometers are in $8,375$ meters?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between kilometers and meters:

$$
1 \,\textrm{km} = 1,000 \,\textrm{m}
$$

Since we are converting from meters to kilometers, we divide both sides of the above equation by $1,000$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{1,000} \,\textrm{km} = 1 \,\textrm{m}
$$

We want to determine how many kilometers are in $8,375$ meters. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $8,375{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
8, 375 \cdot \frac{1}{1, 000}km &= 8, 375 \cdot 1m \\
(8, 375 \div 1, 000)km &= 8, 375m \\
8.375km &= 8, 375m
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $8,375$ meters equals $8.375$ kilometers.
