## Introduction

Recall the following length conversions:

- $1\,\textrm{ft} = 12\,\textrm{in}$
- $1\,\textrm{yd} = 3\,\textrm{ft}$

When we convert from a smaller unit to a larger unit, we may need to use long division to divide by the number of smaller units in one larger unit.

For example, suppose we want to convert $84$ feet into yards.

We start with the unit conversion between yards and feet:

$$
1\,\textrm{yd} = 3\,\textrm{ft}
$$

Since we are converting from feet to yards, we divide both sides of the equation by $3$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{3}\,\textrm{yd} = 1\,\textrm{ft}
$$

We want to determine how many yards are in $84$ feet. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $84{:}$

Finally, we simplify this fraction by interpreting

$$
\dfrac{84}{3}
$$

as the division $84 \div 3$. We can calculate this using long division:

|  | $2$ | $8$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $3$ | $\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$ |  |
| $-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$ | $6$ |  |
|  | $2$ | $4$ |
| $-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$ | $2$ | $4$ |
|  |  | $\fbox{0}$ |

Therefore,

$$
84\,\textrm{ft} = 28\,\textrm{yd}
$$

In this lesson, we will use long division to simplify the quotients that appear when converting measurements to larger units.

## Converting Lengths to Larger Units

**Example:** How many feet are in $216$ inches?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between feet and inches:

$$
1 \,\textrm{ft} = 12 \,\textrm{in}
$$

Since we are converting from inches to feet, we divide both sides of the above equation by $12$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{12} \,\textrm{ft} = 1 \,\textrm{in}
$$

We want to determine how many feet are in $216$ inches. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $216{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
216 \cdot \frac{1}{12}ft &= 216 \cdot 1in \\
\frac{216}{12}ft &= 216in
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we simplify this fraction by interpreting

$$
\dfrac{216}{12}
$$

as the division $216 \div 12$. We can calculate this using long division:

|  |  | $1$ | $8$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $12$ | $\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$ |  |  |
| $-\!\!\!\!$ | $1$ | $2$ |  |
|  |  | $9$ | $6$ |
|  | $-\!\!\!\!$ | $9$ | $6$ |
|  |  | $\phantom{0}$ | $0$ |

Therefore, $216\,\textrm{in}$ equals $18\,\textrm{ft}$.

## Two-Step Length Conversions

Sometimes we need two conversions to change a measurement into a larger unit.

For example, to convert inches to yards, we can convert

$$
\textrm{inches} \rightarrow \textrm{feet} \rightarrow \textrm{yards}
$$

For example, suppose we want to convert $360$ inches into yards. We first convert $360$ inches into feet, and then convert the result from feet to yards.

- To convert from $360$ inches into feet, we start with the unit conversion$1\,\textrm{ft} = 12\,\textrm{in}$.Now, we divide both sides of the above equation by $12$ and obtain the following:$\dfrac{1}{12}\,\textrm{ft} = 1\,\textrm{in}$We want to determine how many feet are in $360$ inches. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $360{:}$$360 \cdot \frac{1}{12}ft|= 360 \cdot 1in; \frac{360}{12}ft|= 360in$Now, we simplify this fraction by interpreting $\dfrac{360}{12}$ as the division $360 \div 12$. We can calculate this using long division:$3$$0$$12$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!\!$$3$$6$$0$$0$Therefore,$360\,\textrm{in} = 30\,\textrm{ft}$.
- Next, to convert from $30$ feet into yards, we start with the unit conversion$1\,\textrm{yd} = 3\,\textrm{ft}$.Now, we divide both sides of the above equation by $3$ and obtain the following:$\dfrac{1}{3}\,\textrm{yd} = 1\,\textrm{ft}$We want to determine how many yards are in $30$ feet. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $30{:}$$30 \cdot \frac{1}{3}yd|= 30 \cdot 1ft; \frac{30}{3}yd|= 30ft; 10yd|= 30ft$Therefore,$30\,\textrm{ft} = 10\,\textrm{yd}$.

In conclusion,

$$
360\,\textrm{in} = 10\,\textrm{yd}
$$

To convert inches to yards, we convert inches to feet first, then convert feet to yards.

## Converting Lengths From Inches to Yards

**Example:** How many yards are in $408$ inches?

**Explanation**

To convert $408$ inches into yards, we convert $408$ inches into feet, and then convert the result from feet to yards.

- To convert from $408$ inches into feet, we start with the unit conversion
$1\, \textrm{ft} =12 \, \textrm{in}$.Now, we divide both sides of the above equation by $12$ and obtain the following:$\dfrac{1}{12}\, \textrm{ft} =1 \, \textrm{in}$We want to determine how many feet are in $408$ inches. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $408{:}$$408 \cdot \frac{1}{12}ft|= 408 \cdot 1in; \frac{408}{12}ft|= 408in$Now, we simplify this fraction by interpreting $\dfrac{408}{12}$ as the division $408 \div 12$. - Next, to convert from $34$ feet into yards, we start with the unit conversion
