## Introduction

In previous lessons, we explored converting larger customary units of length to smaller ones. In this lesson, we will learn how to convert smaller customary units of length to larger ones.

To convert between customary units of length, we use the following unit conversions:

- $1 \,\textrm{yd} = 3 \,\textrm{ft}$
- $1 \,\textrm{ft} = 12 \,\textrm{in}$

We can visualize equivalent units of length using measuring devices. For example, the instrument below shows the equivalence between $1$ foot and $12$ inches.

![](<../Source/1.6.2. Converting Customary Units of Length to Larger Units/Images/f8aa4ce290669fea65c1680625276d85.png>)

Let's get some practice at converting between different units of length.

---

<a id="a-worked-example"></a>
## A Worked Example

Suppose we want to determine how many yards are in $34$ feet.

To do this, we start with the unit conversion between yards and feet:

$$
1 \,\textrm{yd} = 3 \,\textrm{ft}
$$

Since we are converting *from* feet *to* yards, we divide both sides of the above equation by $3$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{3} \,\textrm{yd} = 1 \,\textrm{ft}
$$

The right-hand side reads $1\,\textrm{ft}$, and we want to know how many yards are in $\color{blue}34$ feet. So, we multiply *both* sides of this equation by ${\color{blue}{34}}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
34 \cdot \frac{1}{3}yd &= 34 \cdot 1ft \\
\frac{34}{3}yd &= 34ft \\
(34 \div 3)yd &= 34ft \\
11\frac{1}{3}yd &= 34ft
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $34\,\textrm{ft}$ equals

$$
11\,\dfrac13\,\textrm{yd}
$$

Sometimes we need to use long division when converting between different units. Let's see an example.

## Converting Lengths From Feet to Yards

**Example:** How many yards are in $63$ feet?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between yards and feet:

$$
1 \,\textrm{yd} = 3 \,\textrm{ft}
$$

Since we are converting from feet to yards, we divide both sides of the above equation by $3$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac13 \,\textrm{yd} = 1 \,\textrm{ft}
$$

We want to determine how many yards are in $63$ feet. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $63{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
63 \cdot \frac{1}{3}yd &= 63 \cdot 1ft \\
\frac{63}{3}yd &= 63ft
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we simplify this fraction by interpreting

$$
\dfrac{63}{3}
$$

as the division $63 \div 3$.
We can calculate this using long division:

|  | $2$ | $1$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $3$ | $\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3}$ |  |
| $-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$ | $6$ |  |
|  | $0$ | $3$ |
| $-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$ |  | $3$ |
|  |  | $\fbox{0}$ |

Therefore, $63\,\textrm{ft}$ equals $21\,\textrm{yd}$.

## Converting Lengths From Inches to Feet

**Example:** How many feet are in $40$ inches?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between feet and inches:

$$
1 \,\textrm{ft} = 12 \,\textrm{in}
$$

Since we are converting from inches to feet, we divide both sides of the above equation by $12$ and obtain the following:

$$
\dfrac{1}{12} \,\textrm{ft} = 1 \,\textrm{in}
$$

We want to determine how many feet are in $40$ inches. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation $40{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
40 \cdot \frac{1}{12}ft &= 40 \cdot 1in \\
\frac{40}{12}ft &= 40in \\
\frac{40 \div 4}{12 \div 4}ft &= 40in \\
\frac{10}{3}ft &= 40in \\
3\frac{1}{3}ft &= 40in
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $40\,\textrm{in}$ equals

$$
3\,\dfrac13\,\textrm{ft}
$$

## Two-Step Conversions

**Example:** How many yards are in $168$ inches?

**Explanation**

To convert $168$ inches into yards, we convert $168$ inches into feet, and then convert the result from feet to yards.

- To convert from $168$ inches into feet, we start with the unit conversion
$1\, \textrm{ft} =12 \, \textrm{in}$.
Now, we divide both sides of the above equation by $12$ and obtain the following:
$\dfrac{1}{12}\, \textrm{ft} =1 \, \textrm{in}$
We want to determine how many feet are in $168$ inches. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation $168{:}$
$168 \cdot \frac{1}{12}ft|= 168 \cdot 1in; \frac{168}{12}ft|= 168in$
Now, we simplify this fraction by interpreting $\dfrac{168}{12}$ as the division $168 \div 12$. We can calculate this using long division:
$1$$4$$12$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8}$$-\!\!\!\!$$1$$2$$4$$8$$-\!\!\!\!$$4$$8$$\phantom{0}$$0$
Therefore, $168\,\textrm{in}$ equals $14\,\textrm{ft}$.

- Next, to convert from $14$ feet into yards, we start with the unit conversion
$1\, \textrm{yd} =3 \, \textrm{ft}$.
Now, we divide both sides of the above equation by $3$ and obtain the following:
$\dfrac{1}{3}\, \textrm{yd} =1 \, \textrm{ft}$
We want to determine how many yards are in $14$ feet. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation $17{:}$
$14 \cdot \frac{1}{3}yd|= 14 \cdot 1ft; \frac{14}{3}yd|= 14ft; 4\frac{2}{3}yd|= 14ft$
Therefore, $14\,\textrm{ft}$ equals $4\,\dfrac{2}{3}\,\textrm{yd}$.

In conclusion, $168\,\textrm{in}$ equals

$$
4\,\dfrac{2}{3}\,\textrm{yd}
$$
