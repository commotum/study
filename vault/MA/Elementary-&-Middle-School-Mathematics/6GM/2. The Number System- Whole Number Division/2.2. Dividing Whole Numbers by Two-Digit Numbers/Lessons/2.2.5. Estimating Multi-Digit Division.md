## Introduction

We can use our knowledge of rounding to estimate multi-digit division. To illustrate, let's estimate the value of

$$
960 \div 64
$$

by rounding the divisor to the nearest ten.

The divisor is $64$, and rounding to the nearest ten we have

$$
64 \rightarrow 60 \,
$$

So, we need to find the value of

$$
960 \div 60
$$

which we write as the following fraction:

$$
\dfrac{960}{60}
$$

To simplify the fraction, we can divide the numerator and denominator by $10$ to remove the zeros from the end of both numbers:

$$
\dfrac{960 \div 10}{60 \div 10} = \dfrac{96}{6}
$$

So, we just need to compute

$$
96 \div 6 \,
$$

We use long division, as follows:

| $\,1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!$$6$$3$$6$$-\!\!\!$$3$$6$$0$ |
| --- |

Therefore, $960 \div 64$ is approximately $16$.

## Rounding Both Numbers to the Nearest Ten

**Example:** By rounding both numbers to the nearest ten, estimate the value of $727 \div 22$.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest ten:
$727\begin{vmatrix}\div & 22 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 730 & \div\end{vmatrix}20$

So we need to find the value of $730 \div 20$, which we write as the following fraction:

$$
\dfrac{730}{20}
$$

Dividing the numerator and denominator by $10$, we get

$$
\dfrac{730 \div 10}{20 \div 10} = \dfrac{73}{2}
$$

which is the same as $73 \div 2$.

Computing the quotient, we obtain:

| $\,\,3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\,. \,} 5$$2$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\,. \,} 0}$$-\!\!\!$$6$$1$$3$$-\!\!\!$$1$$2$$1$$0$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$0$$0$ |
| --- |

Therefore, $727 \div 22$ is approximately $36.5 \,$.

## Rounding Both Numbers to the Nearest Ten: Word Problems

**Example:** A paddling pool with a capacity of $997$ gallons is filled through a pump that provides $19$ gallons of water every minute. By rounding each number to the nearest ten, estimate the number of minutes needed to fill the pool.

**Explanation**

Here, we need to estimate the value of $997 \div 19$.

First, we round both numbers to the nearest ten:
$997\begin{vmatrix}\div & 19 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 1, 000 & \div\end{vmatrix}20$

So we need to find the value of $1,000 \div 20$, which can be written as the following fraction:

$$
\dfrac{1,000}{20}
$$

By dividing the numerator and denominator by $10$, we get

$$
\dfrac{1,000 \div 10}{\, \, \, \, \,\, \, \, 20 \div 10} = \dfrac{100}{2}
$$

which is the same as

$$
100 \div 2 = 50
$$

Therefore, $997 \div 19$ is about $50$.

So, it will take about $50$ minutes to fill the pool.

## Rounding Both Numbers to the Nearest Hundred

**Example:** By rounding both numbers to the nearest hundred, estimate the value of $142,773 \div 398$.

**Explanation**

We round both numbers to the nearest hundred:
$142, 773\begin{vmatrix}\div & 398 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 142, 800 & \div\end{vmatrix}400$

So we need to find the value of $142,800 \div 400$, which we can write as the following fraction:

$$
\dfrac{142,800}{400}
$$

Dividing the numerator and denominator by $100$, we get

$$
\dfrac{142,800 \div 100}{400 \div 100} = \dfrac{1,428}{4}
$$

which is the same as $1,428 \div 4$.

Computing the quotient, we obtain:

| $\,\, {\:\phantom{\mid} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7}$$4$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8}$$-\!\!\!$$1$$2$$2$$2$$-\!\!\!$$2$$0$$2$$8$$-\!\!\!$$2$$8$$0$ |
| --- |

Therefore, $142,773 \div 398$ is approximately $357$.

## Rounding the Dividends and Divisors to Different Amounts

**Example:** Estimate the value of $52,284 \div 841$ by rounding the dividend to the nearest thousand and rounding the divisor to the nearest hundred.

**Explanation**

We round the dividend to the nearest thousand:
$52,284 \rightarrow 52,000$

Also, we round the divisor to the nearest hundred:
$841 \rightarrow 800$

So we need to find the value of $52,000 \div 800$, which can be written as the following fraction:

$$
\dfrac{52,000}{800}
$$

Dividing the numerator and denominator by $100$, we get

$$
\dfrac{52,000 \div 100}{800 \div 100} = \dfrac{520}{8}
$$

which is the same as $520 \div 8$.

Computing the quotient, we obtain:

| $\,\,\phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 5$$8\,$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!$$4$$8$$4$$0$$-\!\!\!$$4$$0$$0$ |
| --- |

Therefore, $52,284 \div 841$ is approximately $65$.
