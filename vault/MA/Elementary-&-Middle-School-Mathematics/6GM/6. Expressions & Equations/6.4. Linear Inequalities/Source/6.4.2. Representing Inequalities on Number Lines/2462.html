In general, there are four types of inequalities:

  • greater than or equal

    This is represented by a filled circle and an arrow that goes to the right

  • greater than

    This is represented by an empty circle and an arrow that goes to the right

  • less than or equal

    This is represented by a filled circle and an arrow that goes to the left

  • less than

    This is represented by an empty circle and an arrow that goes to the left
