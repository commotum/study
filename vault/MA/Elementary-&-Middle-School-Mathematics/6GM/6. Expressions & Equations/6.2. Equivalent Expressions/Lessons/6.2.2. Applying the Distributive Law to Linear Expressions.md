## Introduction

The **distributive law** describes how multiplication works when it is applied to a sum inside parentheses.

When a number is multiplied by a sum, the multiplication is **distributed** to each term in the sum. In other words, the number outside the parentheses multiplies every term inside the parentheses.

For example, consider the expression $3(2x + 5)$. The number $3$ multiplies both terms inside the parentheses:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(2x + 5) &=  \\
3 \cdot 2x + 3 \cdot 5 &=  \\
&= 6x + 15
\end{aligned}
$$

After applying the distributive law, the parentheses are removed, and the expression is written as a sum of the resulting terms.

In this lesson, you will learn how to apply the distributive law to different linear expressions.

<a id="distributing-a-number-over-the-sum-of-two-terms"></a>
## Distributing a Number Over the Sum of Two Terms

**Example:** $(7 + 4y)\cdot 6=$

**Explanation**

Using the distributive law, we multiply each term inside the parentheses by the number $6$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(7 + 4y) \cdot 6 &=  \\
7 \cdot 6 + 4y \cdot 6 &=  \\
&= 42 + 24y
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="dealing-with-a-minus-inside-the-parentheses"></a>
## Dealing With a Minus Inside the Parentheses

The distributive law can also be used when the expression inside the parentheses is a *difference*.

When a number is multiplied by an expression with a minus sign, the number outside the parentheses still multiplies every term inside the parentheses. The subtraction sign stays between the terms.

For example, consider the expression $5(4x - 3)$. The number $5$ multiplies both $4x$ and $3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5(4x - 3) &=  \\
5 \cdot 4x - 5 \cdot 3 &=  \\
&= 20x - 15
\end{aligned}
$$

After distributing, the parentheses are removed, and the expression is written as the difference of the resulting terms.

<a id="distributing-a-number-over-the-difference-of-two-terms"></a>
## Distributing a Number Over the Difference of Two Terms

**Example:** $(9x - 4)\cdot 5=$

**Explanation**

Using the distributive law, we multiply each term inside the parentheses by the number $5$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(9x - 4) \cdot 5 &=  \\
9x \cdot 5 - 4 \cdot 5 &=  \\
&= 45x - 20
\end{aligned}
$$

## Distributing a Number Over the Difference of Two Variable Terms

**Example:** $6(2p - 5q)=$

**Explanation**

Using the distributive law, we multiply each term inside the parentheses by the number $6$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6(2p - 5q) &=  \\
6 \cdot 2p - 6 \cdot 5q &=  \\
&= 12p - 30q
\end{aligned}
$$
