## Introduction

A **solution** to a linear equation is a value (or set of values) that makes the equation true when you substitute it in place of the variable.

To check whether a value is a solution:

- Step 1. Substitute the value for the variable.
- Step 2. Simplify both sides of the equation.
- Step 3. If the result is a true statement, then the value is a solution.

For example, consider the equation $x-4=9$.

If we check $x=13$, we substitute

$$
{\color{blue}{x}}={\color{blue}{13}}{:}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x - 4 &= 9 \\
13 - 4 &= 9 \\
9 &= 9✓
\end{aligned}
$$

Since the equation becomes a true statement, $x=13$ is a solution.

Now, let's check $x=10{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x - 4 &= 9 \\
10 - 4 &= 9 \\
6 &= 9\cdot
\end{aligned}
$$

Since the equation becomes a false statement, $x=10$ is not a solution.

## Identifying Solutions to Linear Equations Involving Addition

**Example:** Which value is a solution to the equation $m + 6 = 14$?

1. $m=6$
2. $m=7$
3. $m=8$
4. $m=9$

**Explanation**

A solution to a linear equation is a value (or set of values) that makes the equation true when you substitute it in place of the variable.

Notice that if we substitute

$$
{\color{blue}{m}}={\color{blue}{8}}
$$

into the given equation, we get a true statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m + 6 &= 14 \\
8 + 6 &= 14 \\
14 &= 14✓
\end{aligned}
$$

All other options result in a false statement. Therefore, the solution is $m = 8$.

**Example:** Which value is a solution to the equation $5p = 35$?

## Identifying Solutions to Linear Equations Involving Division

**Example:** What is the solution of the equation $\dfrac{s}{5} = 9$?

1. $s=45$
2. $s=50$
3. $s=55$
4. $s=60$

**Explanation**

A solution to a linear equation is a value (or set of values) that makes the equation true when you substitute it in place of the variable.

Notice that if we substitute

$$
{\color{blue}{s}}={\color{blue}{45}}
$$

into the given equation, we get a true statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{s}{5} &= 9 \\
\frac{45}{5} &= 9 \\
9 &= 9✓
\end{aligned}
$$

No other values result in a true statement. Therefore, the solution is

$$
s = \bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{45}
$$
