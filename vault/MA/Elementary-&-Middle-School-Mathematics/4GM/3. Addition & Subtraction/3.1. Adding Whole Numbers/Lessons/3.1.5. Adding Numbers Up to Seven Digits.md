## Introduction

Let's compute the following sum:

$$
4,745,136 + 25,985
$$

We proceed, as usual, using the standard algorithm.

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}7 & 4 & 5 & 1 & 3\end{vmatrix}6; +\begin{vmatrix}2 & 5 & 9 & 8\end{vmatrix}5
$$

Then, we add the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 & 1 \\ 4 & 7 & 4\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}1 & 3 & 6 \\ + & 2 & 5\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}8 & 5 \\ 4 & 7\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix}1
$$

Therefore,

$$
4,745,136 + 25,985 = 4,771,121
$$

## Adding Numbers Up to Seven Digits Without Carrying

**Example:** Calculate the sum $5,326,143 + 271,612$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}5 & 3 & 2 & 6 & 1 & 4 & 3 \\ + & 2 & 7 & 1 & 6 & 1 & 2\end{bmatrix}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
\begin{bmatrix}5 & 3 & 2 & 6 & 1 & 4 & 3 \\ + & 2 & 7 & 1 & 6 & 1 & 2 \\ 5 & 5 & 9 & 7 & 7 & 5 & 5\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore,

$$
5,326,143 + 271,612 = 5,597,755
$$

## Adding Numbers Up to Seven Digits With Carrying

**Example:** What is $3,246,341+ 71,769$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}2 & 4 & 6 & 3 & 4\end{vmatrix}1; +\begin{vmatrix}7 & 1 & 7 & 6\end{vmatrix}9
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 & 4\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}3 & 4 & 1 \\ + & 7 & 1\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}6 & 9 \\ 3 & 3\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}8 & 1 & 1\end{vmatrix}0
$$

Therefore,

$$
3,246,341+ 71,769 = 3,318,110
$$

## Adding Numbers Up to Seven Digits: Word Problems

**Example:** In $2020$, a certain country had an elderly population of $2,079,439$ and a non-elderly population of $8,019,826$. What was the total population of the country?

**Explanation**

To find the total population of the country, we need to calculate the value of

$$
2,079,439 + 8,019,826
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}0 & 7 & 9 & 4 & 3 & 9 \\ + & 8 & 0 & 1 & 9 & 8\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 6
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 7\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}4 & 3 & 9 \\ + & 8 & 0\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}9 & 8 & 2 & 6 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 & 9\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}2 & 6\end{vmatrix}5
$$

So,

$$
2,079,439 + 8,019,826 = 10,099,265
$$

Therefore, the total population of the country was $10,099,265$.
