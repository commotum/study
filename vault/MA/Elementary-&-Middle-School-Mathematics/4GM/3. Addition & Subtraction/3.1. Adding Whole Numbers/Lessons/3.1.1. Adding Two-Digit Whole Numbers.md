## Introduction

We can use place value to add two-digit whole numbers easily.

As an example, let's find the following sum:

$$
24+35
$$

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

| **tens** | **ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}4$ |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}5$ |

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left) and writing the results below:

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}4} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}9}{:}
$$

| **tens** | **ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}4$ |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}5$ |
|  | $\color{blue}9$ |

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get

$$
{\color{red}2} + {\color{red}3} = {\color{red}5}{:}
$$

| **tens** | **ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}4$ |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}5$ |
| $\color{red}5$ | $\color{blue}9$ |

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
24+35 = 59
$$

---

<a id="adding-using-the-standard-algorithm"></a>
## Adding Using the Standard Algorithm

Drawing place value charts each time we want to add a pair of numbers isn't very convenient. For this reason, we usually follow a simplified process called the **standard algorithm**.

Let's once again consider the following sum:

$$
24+35
$$

First, we line up our numbers as follows: (ones over ones, and tens over tens):

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 5
$$

Notice that we've also drawn a horizontal line. This is where we'll write our answer.

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}4} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}9}{:}
$$

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 5; 9
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get

$$
{\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}3} = {\color{blue}5}{:}
$$

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}9
$$

Therefore,

$$
24 + 35 = 59
$$

## Adding Two-Digit Numbers to Two-Digit Numbers

**Example:** Robert weighs $37$ pounds, and Milly weighs $41$ pounds. What is the combined weight of these two children?

**Explanation**

To find the combined weight, we need to calculate the value of

$$
37+41
$$

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 1
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}1} = {\color{blue}8}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 1; 8
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}7}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}8
$$

Therefore,

$$
37 + 41 = 78
$$

So, the combined weight of the children is $78$ pounds.

## Adding With Carrying

Let's now calculate the following sum using place value charts:

$$
37+26
$$

First, we line up our numbers (ones over ones, and tens over tens), as before:

| **tens** | **ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}7$ |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}6$ |

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}3}
$$

Now, notice that

$$
1
$$

So, we write $\color{blue}3$ below the *ones* and **carry** $\color{red}1$ to the *tens*. Let's write this additional $\color{red}1$ over the *tens* column.

| $\color{red}1$ |  |
| --- | ---: |
| **tens** | **ones** |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}7$ |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}6$ |
|  | $\color{blue}3$ |

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get

$$
{\color{red}1} + {\color{red}3} + {\color{red}2} = {\color{red}6}
$$

(notice that we also add the $\color{red}1$ carried from the previous step):

| $\color{red}1$ |  |
| --- | ---: |
| **tens** | **ones** |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}7$ |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}6$ |
| $\color{red}6$ | $\color{blue}3$ |

Therefore,

$$
37+26 = 63
$$

Let's now solve the same problem using the standard algorithm.

---

<a id="using-the-standard-algorithm-with-carrying"></a>
## Using the Standard Algorithm With Carrying

Once again, we consider the following sum:

$$
37+26
$$

To use the standard algorithm, we first line up our numbers (ones over ones, and tens over tens):

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 6
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}3}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}3}$ below the *ones* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *tens*:

$$
1; 3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 6; 3
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}6}{:}
$$

$$
1; 3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 6\end{vmatrix}3
$$

Therefore,

$$
37 + 26 = 63
$$

<a id="adding-two-digit-numbers-to-two-digit-numbers-with-carrying"></a>
## Adding Two-Digit Numbers to Two-Digit Numbers With Carrying

**Example:** The regular time of a particular soccer game lasted $92$ minutes, and extra time lasted $33$ minutes. How long did the game last in total?

**Explanation**

To find the combined time, we need to calculate the value of

$$
92 + 33
$$

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
9\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 3
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}3} = {\color{blue}5}{:}
$$

$$
9\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 3; 5
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}9} + {\color{blue}3} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}2}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}2}$ below the *tens* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *hundreds*:

$$
1; 9\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}5
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds,

$$
{\color{blue}1}+{\color{blue}0}+{\color{blue}0}={\color{blue}1}{:}
$$

$$
1; 0\begin{vmatrix}9 & 2 \\ + & 0\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 5
$$

Therefore, the game lasted $125$ minutes.

## Adding Two-Digit Numbers to Two-Digit Numbers: Carrying Twice

**Example:** What is $58+55$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ +\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 5
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}3}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}3}$ below the *ones* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *tens*:

$$
1; 5\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ +\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 5; 3
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}5} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}1}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}1}$ below the *tens* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *hundreds*:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}8; + \mid 5\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}3
$$

**Step 3.** Notice that neither $58$ nor $55$ have a hundreds digit. Therefore, we place $\color{red}0$ in their hundreds places:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 0\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ +\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 5\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}3
$$

Then, adding the hundreds, we get

$$
{\color{blue}1}+{\color{blue}0}+{\color{blue}0}={\color{blue}1}{:}
$$

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 0\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ +\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}5 & 5 \\ 1 & 1\end{vmatrix}3
$$

Therefore,

$$
58 + 55 = 113
$$
