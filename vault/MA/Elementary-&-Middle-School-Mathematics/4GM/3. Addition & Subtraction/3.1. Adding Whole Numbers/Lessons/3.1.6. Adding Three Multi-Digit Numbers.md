## Introduction

We can use the standard algorithm to add multiple multi-digit whole numbers.

For example, let's use the standard algorithm to compute the following sum:

$$
801 + 523 + 198
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1 \\ + & 5\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ +\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 9 \mid 8
$$

Next, we add the numbers in each place value (from right to left). However, this time, we need to sum three numbers in each place value, as well as any carried digits:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 8 & 0\end{vmatrix}1; +\begin{vmatrix}5 & 2 & 3 \\ + & 1 & 9\end{vmatrix}8; 1\begin{vmatrix}5 & 2\end{vmatrix}2
$$

Therefore,

$$
801 + 523 + 198 = 1,522
$$

## Adding Three Numbers Up to Three Digits

**Example:** Lauren is listening to some songs while waiting for a train. The duration of the first song is $123$ seconds, the second is $182$ seconds, and the third is $157$ seconds. What is the total duration of all three songs?

**Explanation**

To find the total duration of all three tracks, we need to compute the sum

$$
123 + 182 + 157
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 3 \\ + & 1\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ +\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 5 \mid 7
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers using the standard algorithm:
$1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ +\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}8 & 2 \\ + & 1\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 2$

So, we have

$$
123 + 182 + 157 = 462
$$

Therefore, the total duration of all three songs is $462$ seconds.

## Adding Three Numbers Up to Five Digits

**Example:** What is $31,252 + 12,892 + 25,034$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}1 & 2 & 5 & 2 \\ + & 1 & 2 & 8\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}5 & 0 & 3\end{vmatrix}4
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers using the standard algorithm:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 5 & 2 \\ + & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}9 & 2 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 4 \\ 6 & 9 & 1\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 8
$$

Therefore,

$$
31,252 + 12,892 + 25,034= 69,178
$$

## Adding Three Numbers Up to Seven Digits

**Example:** $2,078,503 + 148,450 + 1,885,047 =$

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}0 & 7 & 8 & 5 & 0 & 3 \\ + & 1 & 4 & 8 & 4 & 5\end{vmatrix}0; +\begin{vmatrix}1 & 8 & 8 & 5 & 0 & 4\end{vmatrix}7
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers using the standard algorithm:

$1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 2 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 7 & 8 & 5\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ +\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}4 & 8 & 4 & 5 & 0 \\ + & 1 & 8 & 8 & 5\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}4 & 7 \\ 4 & 1\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 0 & 0\end{vmatrix}0$
Therefore,

$$
2,078,503 + 148,450 + 1,885,047 = 4,112,000
$$
