## Introduction

Adding large numbers without using a calculator takes some effort. However, in some circumstances, it is sufficient to find an **estimate** for a particular sum. Computing an estimate is usually faster and easier than solving the original problem.

To estimate the answer to an addition problem, we round the numbers *first* and *then* add them.

To illustrate this, let's find an estimate for the value of

$$
382+567
$$

Let's start by rounding both numbers to the nearest ten:

$$
382\begin{vmatrix}+ & 567 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 380 & +\end{vmatrix}570
$$

Then, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{380}} + {\color{blue}{570}}$:

$$
1; 3\begin{vmatrix}8 & 0 \\ + & 5\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 9\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 0
$$

Therefore,

$$
382+567 \approx 950
$$

The symbol

$$
\approx
$$

means "is approximately equal to."

The estimate we've obtained is pretty close to the actual value of the sum. In fact,

$$
382+567=949
$$

In this example, we rounded to the nearest ten. In the next example, we'll estimate a sum by rounding both numbers to the nearest hundred.

## Estimating Sums Containing Numbers Up to Three Digits

**Example:** There are currently $243$ trees in the park. Workers will plant $158$ more trees this week. By rounding each value to the nearest hundred, estimate how many trees will be in the park when the workers are finished.

**Explanation**

To estimate how many trees will be in the park when the workers are finished, we need to find an approximation for the sum

$$
243+158
$$

First, we round both numbers to the nearest hundred:

$$
243\begin{vmatrix}+ & 158 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 200 & +\end{vmatrix}200
$$

Next, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{200}} + {\color{blue}{200}}$:
$2\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 0$

So, we have

$$
243 + 158 \approx 400
$$

Therefore, there will be approximately $400$ trees in the park when the workers are finished.

## Estimating Sums Containing Numbers Up to Five Digits

**Example:** Estimate the sum $28,170 + 3,802$ by rounding each number to the nearest thousand.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest thousand:

$$
28, 170\begin{vmatrix}+ & 3, 802 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 28, 000 & +\end{vmatrix}4, 000
$$

Next, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{28,000}} + {\color{blue}{4,000}}$:
$1; 2\begin{vmatrix}8 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ + & 4 & 0 & 0\end{vmatrix}0; 3\begin{vmatrix}2 & 0 & 0\end{vmatrix}0$

Therefore, $28,170 + 3,802$ is approximately $32,000$.

## Estimating Sums Containing Numbers Up to Seven Digits

**Example:** Estimate the sum $788,134 + 2,531,765$ by rounding each number to the nearest hundred thousand.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest hundred- thousand:

$$
788, 134\begin{vmatrix}+ & 2, 531, 765 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 800, 000 & +\end{vmatrix}2, 500, 000
$$

Next, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{800,000}} + {\color{blue}{2,500,000}}$:
$1; 8\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ + & 2 & 5 & 0 & 0\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 \\ 3 & 3\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 & 0\end{vmatrix}0$
Therefore, $788,134 + 2,531,765$ is approximately $3,300,000$.
