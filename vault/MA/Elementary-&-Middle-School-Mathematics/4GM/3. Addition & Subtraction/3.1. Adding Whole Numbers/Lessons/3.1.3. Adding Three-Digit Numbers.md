## Introduction

Let's calculate the following sum:

$$
410 + 352
$$

We proceed using the standard algorithm.

First, we line up our numbers (ones over ones, and tens over tens):

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}1 & 0 \\ + & 3\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 2
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}2}{:}
$$

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}1 & 0 \\ + & 3\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 2; 2
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}6}{:}
$$

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}1 & 0 \\ + & 3\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 6\end{vmatrix}2
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds, we get

$$
{\color{blue}4} + {\color{blue}3} = {\color{blue}7}{:}
$$

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}1 & 0 \\ + & 3\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 2
$$

Therefore,

$$
410 + 352 = 762
$$

## Adding Three-Digit Numbers Without Carrying

**Example:** There are $373$ boys and $415$ girls in a particular elementary school. What is the total number of students in the school?

**Explanation**

To find the total number of students, we need to calculate the value of
$373+415$.

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 & 3 \\ + & 4\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 5
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}8}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 & 3 \\ + & 4\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 5; 8
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}1} = {\color{blue}8}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 & 3 \\ + & 4\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}8
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds,

$$
{\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}7}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 & 3 \\ + & 4\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 8
$$

So,

$$
373 + 415 = 788
$$

Therefore, there are a total of $788$ students in the school.

## Addition With Carrying

Let's calculate the following sum:

$$
128 + 249
$$

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 8 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 9
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}9} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}7}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}7}$ below the *ones* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *tens*:

$$
1; 1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 8 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 9; 7
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}7}{:}
$$

$$
1; 1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 8 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}9 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}7
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds, we get

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}
$$

$$
1; 1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 8 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}9 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 7
$$

Therefore,

$$
128 + 249 = 377
$$

## Adding Three-Digit Numbers With Carrying

**Example:** What is $850 + 645$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}5 & 0 \\ + & 6\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 5
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}5}{:}
$$

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}5 & 0 \\ + & 6\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 5; 5
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}5} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}9}{:}
$$

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}5 & 0 \\ + & 6\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 9\end{vmatrix}5
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds,

$$
{\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}4}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}4}$ below the *hundreds* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *thousands*:

$$
1; 8\begin{vmatrix}5 & 0 \\ + & 6\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}9 \mid 5
$$

**Step 4.** Adding the thousands,

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}1}{:}
$$

$$
1; 0\begin{vmatrix}8 & 5 & 0 \\ + & 0 & 6\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 9 \mid 5
$$

Therefore,

$$
850 + 645 = 1,495
$$

## Adding Three-Digit Numbers: Carrying Multiple Times

**Example:** Calculate $457 + 789$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}5 & 7 \\ + & 7\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 9
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}9} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}6}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}6}$ below the *ones* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *tens*:

$$
1; 4\begin{vmatrix}5 & 7 \\ + & 7\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 9; 6
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}5} + {\color{blue}8} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}4}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}4}$ below the *tens* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *hundreds*:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 8\begin{vmatrix}9 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}6
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds,

$$
{\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}4} + {\color{blue}7} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}2}
$$

We write ${\color{blue}2}$ below the *hundreds* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *thousands*:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 4 & 5\end{vmatrix}7; +\begin{vmatrix}7 & 8\end{vmatrix}9; 2 \mid 4 \mid 6
$$

**Step 4.** Adding the thousands,

$$
{\color{blue}1}+{\color{blue}0}+{\color{blue}0}={\color{blue}1}{:}
$$

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 0 & 4\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ +\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}7 & 8 & 9 \\ 1 & 2 & 4\end{vmatrix}6
$$

Therefore,

$$
457 + 789 = 1,246
$$
