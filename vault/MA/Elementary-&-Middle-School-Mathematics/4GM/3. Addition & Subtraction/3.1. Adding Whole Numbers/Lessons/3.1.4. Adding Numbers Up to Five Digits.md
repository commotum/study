## Introduction

Using the standard algorithm, we can calculate the sum of two numbers containing any number of digits.

Let's use the standard algorithm to compute the following sum:

$$
32,810+8,921
$$

The procedure is the same as the one we've seen before. We start by lining up our numbers:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 & 8 & 1 & 0 \\ + & 8 & 9 & 2 & 1\end{bmatrix}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}8 & 1 & 0 \\ + & 8 & 9\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}7 & 3\end{vmatrix}1
$$

Therefore,

$$
32,810+8,921 = 41,731
$$

## Adding Numbers Up to Five Digits

**Example:** What is $23,861+5,026$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2 & 3 & 8 & 6 & 1 \\ + & 5 & 0 & 2 & 6\end{bmatrix}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2 & 3 & 8 & 6 & 1 \\ + & 5 & 0 & 2 & 6 \\ 2 & 8 & 8 & 8 & 7\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore,

$$
23,861+5,026 = 28,887
$$

## Adding Numbers Up to Five Digits With Carrying

**Example:** Compute the value of $89,743+32,876$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}9 & 7 & 4 & 3 \\ + & 3 & 2 & 8\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 6
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 & 1 \\ 8 & 9 & 7\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ +\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}2 & 8 & 7 & 6 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 & 6\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 9
$$

Therefore,

$$
89,723+32,876 = 122,619
$$

## Adding Numbers Up to Five Digits: Word Problems

**Example:** Mike wrote two essays. There are $11,391$ words in the first essay and $7,942$ words in the second essay. How many words did Mike write altogether?

**Explanation**

To find how many words Mike wrote altogether, we need to calculate the value of

$$
11,391 + 7,942
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}1 & 1 & 3 & 9 & 1 \\ + & 7 & 9 & 4 & 2\end{bmatrix}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}3 & 9 & 1 \\ + & 7 & 9\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}3 & 3\end{vmatrix}3
$$

So,

$$
11,391 + 7,942 = 19,333
$$

Therefore, Mike wrote $19,333$ words in total.
