## Introduction

Using the standard algorithm, we can calculate the difference between two numbers containing any number of digits.

Let's use the standard algorithm to compute the following difference:

$$
42,345 - 3,124
$$

First, we line up our numbers (ones over ones, tens over tens, hundreds over hundreds, and so on):

$$
\begin{bmatrix}4 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 \\ - & 3 & 1 & 2 & 4\end{bmatrix}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}3 & 4 & 5 \\ - & 3 & 1\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}2 & 2\end{vmatrix}1
$$

Therefore,

$$
42,345 - 3,124 = 39,221
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Up to Five Digits Without Borrowing

**Example:** Compute the value of $57,458 - 25,242$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}7 & 4 & 5 & 8 \\ - & 2 & 5 & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 2
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left:

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}7 & 4 & 5 & 8 \\ - & 2 & 5 & 2\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}2 & 1\end{vmatrix}6
$$

Therefore,

$$
57,458 - 25,242 = 32,216
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Up to Five Digits With Borrowing

**Example:** What is $83,005 - 52,009$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}3 & 0 & 0 & 5 \\ - & 5 & 2 & 0\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 9
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$2\begin{vmatrix}9 & 9 & 15 \\ 8 & 3 & 0\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}2 & 0 & 0 & 9 \\ 3 & 0 & 9 & 9\end{vmatrix}6$
Therefore,

$$
83,005 - 52,009 = 30,996
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Up to Five Digits: Word Problems

**Example:** There are $32,355$ bees in a hive. On a particular day, $12, 247$ of the bees leave to find pollen. How many bees are left in the hive?

**Explanation**

To determine how many bees are left in the hive, we need to calculate the value of

$$
32, 355 - 12, 247
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}2 & 3 & 5 & 5 \\ - & 1 & 2 & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 7
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}15 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}3 & 5 & 5 \\ - & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}4 & 7 \\ 2 & 0\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 0 \mid 8
$$

Therefore, there are $20,108$ bees left in the hive.
