## Introduction

Let's calculate the following difference:

$$
225 - 116
$$

We proceed using the standard algorithm.

The first step is to write the numbers in the usual way, where ones are aligned over ones, tens over tens, and hundreds over hundreds:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}2 & 5 \\ - & 1\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 6
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $5 - 6$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place. This leaves

$$
2 - 1 = {\color{red}{1}}
$$

tens and $\color{red}15$ ones:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}15 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 1 \mid 6
$$

**Step 2.** Then, we subtract ones from ones, tens from tens, and hundreds from hundreds:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}15 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}1 & 6 \\ 1 & 0\end{vmatrix}9
$$

Therefore,

$$
225 - 116 = 109
$$

## Subtracting Three-Digit Numbers From Three-Digit Numbers

**Example:** What is $578 - 246$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones*, *tens* over *tens*, and *hundreds* over *hundreds*):

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}7 & 8 \\ - & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 6
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Subtracting the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}8} - {\color{blue}6} = {\color{blue}2}{:}
$$

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}7 & 8 \\ - & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 6; 2
$$

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}7} - {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}3}{:}
$$

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}7 & 8 \\ - & 2\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}2
$$

**Step 3.** Subtracting the hundreds,

$$
{\color{blue}5} - {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}
$$

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}7 & 8 \\ - & 2\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 2
$$

Therefore,

$$
578 - 246 = 332
$$

## Subtracting Three-Digit Numbers From Three-Digit Numbers: Borrowing Once

**Example:** What is $272 - 190$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}7 & 2 \\ - & 1\end{vmatrix}9 \mid 0
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Subtracting the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}2} - {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}2}{:}
$$

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}7 & 2 \\ - & 1\end{vmatrix}9 \mid 0; 2
$$

**Step 2.** We cannot compute $7 - 9$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *hundreds* place:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}17 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 9 \mid 0; 2
$$

**Step 3:** Then, we subtract tens and hundreds:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}17 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}9 & 0 \\ 0 & 8\end{vmatrix}2
$$

Therefore,

$$
272 - 190 = 82
$$

## Subtracting Three-Digit Numbers From Three-Digit Numbers: Borrowing Twice

**Example:** What is $332 - 174$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}3 & 2 \\ - & 1\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 4
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $2 - 4$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place.
Then, subtracting the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}12} - {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}8}{:}
$$

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 7 \mid 4; 8
$$

**Step 2.** We cannot compute $2 - 7$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *hundreds* place.
Then, we subtract tens and hundreds:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}12 & 12 \\ 3 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; -\begin{vmatrix}1 & 7\end{vmatrix}4; 1 \mid 5 \mid 8
$$

Therefore,

$$
332 - 174 = 158
$$

## Borrowing From Zero

**Example:** What is $208 - 169$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}0 & 8 \\ - & 1\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 9
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $8 - 9$, and there are $0$ tens to borrow from. So, we borrow $1$ from the *hundreds* place first:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}10 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 6 \mid 9
$$

Then, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place:

$$
9\begin{vmatrix}18 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}10\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 6 \mid 9
$$

**Step 2.** Then, we subtract ones, tens, and hundreds:

$$
9\begin{vmatrix}18 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}10\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}6 & 9 \\ 0 & 3\end{vmatrix}9
$$

Therefore,

$$
208 - 169 = 39
$$
