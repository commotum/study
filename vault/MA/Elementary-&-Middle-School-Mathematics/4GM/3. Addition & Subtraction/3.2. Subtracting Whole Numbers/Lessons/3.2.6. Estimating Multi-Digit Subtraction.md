## Introduction

Subtracting large numbers without using a calculator takes some effort. However, in some circumstances, it is sufficient to find an **estimate** for a particular difference. Computing an estimate is usually faster and easier than solving the original problem.

To estimate the answer to a subtraction problem, we round the numbers *first* and *then* subtract them.

To illustrate this, let's find an estimate for the value of

$$
481 - 73
$$

Let's start by rounding both numbers to the nearest ten:

$$
481\begin{vmatrix}- & 73 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 480 & -\end{vmatrix}70
$$

Next, we calculate the difference ${\color{blue}{480}} - {\color{blue}{70}}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}4 & 8 & 0 \\ - & 7 & 0 \\ 4 & 1 & 0\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore,

$$
481 - 73 \approx 410
$$

The symbol

$$
\approx
$$

means "is approximately equal to."

The estimate we've obtained is pretty close to the actual value of the difference. In fact,

$$
481 - 73=408
$$

In this example, we rounded to the nearest ten. In the next example, we'll estimate a difference by rounding both numbers to the nearest hundred.

## Estimating Differences Containing Numbers Up to Three Digits

**Example:** Sandy bought $432$ grams of chocolate chips and used $302$ grams to make cookies. By rounding each value to the nearest hundred, estimate the weight of the remaining chocolate chips.

**Explanation**

To estimate the weight of the remaining chocolate chips, we need to approximate the difference

$$
432 - 302
$$

First, we round both numbers to the nearest hundred:

$$
432\begin{vmatrix}- & 302 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 400 & -\end{vmatrix}300
$$

Next, we calculate the difference ${\color{blue}{400}} - {\color{blue}{300}}$:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 \\ - & 3\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 0
$$

So, we have

$$
432 - 302 \approx 100
$$

Therefore, Sandy has approximately $100$ grams of chocolate chips remaining.

## Estimating Differences Containing Numbers Up to Five Digits

**Example:** Estimate the difference $83,250 - 15,845$ by rounding each number to the nearest thousand.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest thousand:

$$
83, 250\begin{vmatrix}- & 15, 845 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 83, 000 & -\end{vmatrix}16, 000
$$

Next, we calculate the difference ${\color{blue}{83,000}} - {\color{blue}{16,000}}$:

$$
7\begin{vmatrix}13 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 & 0 \\ - & 1 & 6\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 \\ 6 & 7\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 0 \mid 0
$$

Therefore, $83,250 - 15,845$ is approximately $67,000$.

## Estimating Differences Containing Numbers Up to Seven Digits

**Example:** Estimate the difference $4,755,450 - 804,001$ by rounding each number to the nearest hundred thousand.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest hundred thousand:

$$
4, 755, 450\begin{vmatrix}- & 804, 001 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 4, 800, 000 & -\end{vmatrix}800, 000
$$

Next, we calculate the difference ${\color{blue}{4,800,000}} - {\color{blue}{800,000}}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}4 & 8 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ - & 8 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 4 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore, $4,755,450 - 804,001$ is approximately $4,000,000$.
