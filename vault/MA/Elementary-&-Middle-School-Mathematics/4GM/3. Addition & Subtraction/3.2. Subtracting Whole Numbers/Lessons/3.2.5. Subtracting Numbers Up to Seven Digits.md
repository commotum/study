## Introduction

Let's compute the following difference:

$$
1,456,546 - 1,224,292
$$

As always, the first step is to line up our numbers according to their place value (ones over ones, tens over tens, hundreds over hundreds, and so on):

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}4 & 5 & 6 & 5 & 4 & 6 \\ - & 1 & 2 & 2 & 4 & 2\end{vmatrix}9 \mid 2
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}14 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}5 & 6 & 5 & 4 & 6 \\ - & 1 & 2 & 2 & 4\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}9 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}2 & 5\end{vmatrix}4
$$

Therefore,

$$
1,456,546 - 1,224,292 = 232,254
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Up to Seven Digits Without Borrowing

**Example:** Compute the value of $1,487,645 - 45,214$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}4 & 8 & 7 & 6 & 4\end{vmatrix}5; -\begin{vmatrix}4 & 5 & 2 & 1\end{vmatrix}4
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}4 & 8 & 7 & 6 & 4 & 5 \\ - & 4 & 5 & 2 & 1 & 4 \\ 1 & 4 & 4 & 2 & 4 & 3\end{vmatrix}1
$$

Therefore,

$$
1,487,645 - 45,214 = 1,442,431
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Up to Seven Digits With Borrowing

**Example:** What is $2,354,702 - 1,446,254$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers:

$2\begin{vmatrix}3 & 5 & 4 & 7 & 0 & 2 \\ - & 1 & 4 & 4 & 6 & 2\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 4$
Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$1\begin{vmatrix}13 & 4 & 14 & 6 & 9 & 12 \\ 2 & 3 & 5 & 4 & 7 & 0\end{vmatrix}2; -\begin{vmatrix}1 & 4 & 4 & 6 & 2 & 5\end{vmatrix}4; 9\begin{vmatrix}0 & 8 & 4 & 4\end{vmatrix}8$
Therefore,

$$
2,354,702 - 1,446,254 = 908,448
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Up to Seven Digits: Word Problems

**Example:** Mike, a baseball player, earns $\[math]1, 245,800$ per year, while Randy, a football player, makes $\[/math]1,227,900$ per year. How much more does Mike earn per year compared to Randy?

**Explanation**

To find out how much more Mike earns compared to Randy, we need to calculate the value of

$$
1, 245,800 - 1,227,900
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 4 & 5 & 8 & 0 & 0 \\ - & 1 & 2 & 2 & 7 & 9\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 0
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}14 & 18 \\ 1 & 2\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}5 & 8 & 0 & 0 \\ - & 1 & 2 & 2\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}9 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 7 & 9\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 0
$$

Therefore, Mike earns $\[math]17,900$ more per year.
