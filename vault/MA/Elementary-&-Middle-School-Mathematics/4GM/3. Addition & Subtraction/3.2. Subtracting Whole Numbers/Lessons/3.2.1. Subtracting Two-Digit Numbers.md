## Introduction

We can use place value to subtract two-digit whole numbers easily.

As an example, let's find the following difference:

$$
65-42
$$

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}6$ | $\color{blue}5$ |
| $\color{red}4$ | $\color{blue}2$ |

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left) and writing the results below:

**Step 1.** Subtracting the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}5} - {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}
$$

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}6$ | $\color{blue}5$ |
| $\color{red}4$ | $\color{blue}2$ |
|  | $\color{blue}3$ |

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens, we get

$$
{\color{red}6} - {\color{red}4} = {\color{red}2}{:}
$$

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}6$ | $\color{blue}5$ |
| $\color{red}4$ | $\color{blue}2$ |
| $\color{red}2$ | $\color{blue}3$ |

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
65-42 = 23
$$

---

<a id="subtraction-using-the-standard-algorithm"></a>
## Subtraction Using the Standard Algorithm

Drawing place value charts each time we want to subtract a pair of numbers isn't very convenient. For this reason, we usually follow a simplified process called the **standard algorithm**.

Let's once again consider the following difference:

$$
65-42
$$

First, we line up our numbers as follows: (ones over ones, and tens over tens):

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 2
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Subtracting the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}5} - {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}
$$

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 2; 3
$$

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens, we get

$$
{\color{red}6} - {\color{red}4} = {\color{red}2}{:}
$$

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}3
$$

Therefore,

$$
65 - 42 = 23
$$

## Subtracting Two Numbers

**Example:** Find the value of $84 - 31$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ -\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 1
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Subtracting the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}4} - {\color{blue}1} = {\color{blue}3}{:}
$$

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ -\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 1; 3
$$

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}8} - {\color{blue}3} = {\color{blue}5}{:}
$$

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ -\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}3
$$

Therefore,

$$
84 - 31 = 53
$$

<a id="subtraction-with-borrowing"></a>
## Subtraction With Borrowing

Let's consider the following subtraction problem:

$$
45-17
$$

First, we line up our numbers (ones over ones and tens over tens), as before:

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}4$ | $\color{blue}5$ |
| $1$ | $7$ |

We have a problem. Since $5$ is smaller than $7$, we cannot calculate $5-7$. So what do we do?

The answer is to take the first number $({\color{red}{4}}{\color{blue}{5}})$, "borrow" a ten from the tens place and put it in the ones place, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
4tens + 5ones &= 3tens + 15ones
\end{aligned}
$$

Updating the first row in our table gives the following:

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}15$ |
| $1$ | $7$ |

Now, we subtract as usual!

**Step 1.** Subtracting the ones, we get $15-7=8{:}$

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}15$ |
| $1$ | $7$ |
|  | $8$ |

**Step 1.** Subtracting the tens, we get $3-1=2{:}$

| **Tens** | **Ones** |
| --- | ---: |
| $\color{red}3$ | $\color{blue}15$ |
| $1$ | $7$ |
| $2$ | $8$ |

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
45-17 = 28
$$

Let's now solve the same problem using the standard algorithm.

<a id="using-the-standard-algorithm-with-borrowing"></a>
## Using the Standard Algorithm With Borrowing

Once again, we consider the following difference:

$$
45 - 17
$$

To use the standard algorithm, we first line up our numbers (ones over ones and tens over tens):

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 7
$$

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $5-7$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place. Then we have

$$
4 - 1 = {\color{red}3}
$$

tens and $\color{red}15$ ones:

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}15 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}5; - \mid 1 \mid 7
$$

Subtracting the ones, we get

$$
{\color{blue}15} - {\color{blue}7} = {\color{blue}8}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}15 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}5; - \mid 1 \mid 7; 8
$$

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens, we get

$$
{\color{blue}3} - {\color{blue}1} = {\color{blue}2}{:}
$$

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}15 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}5; - \mid 1\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}8
$$

Therefore,

$$
45 - 17 = 28
$$

<a id="subtracting-two-numbers-with-borrowing"></a>
## Subtracting Two Numbers With Borrowing

**Example:** Find the value of $72 - 56$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
7\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ -\end{vmatrix}5 \mid 6
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $2 - 6$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place. Then we have

$$
7 - 1 = {\color{red}6}
$$

tens and $\color{red}12$ ones:

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}2; - \mid 5 \mid 6
$$

Now, subtracting the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}12} - {\color{blue}6} = {\color{blue}6}{:}
$$

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}2; - \mid 5 \mid 6; 6
$$

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}6} - {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}1}{:}
$$

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}2; - \mid 5\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}6
$$

Therefore,

$$
72 - 56 = 16
$$

## Word Problems

**Example:** A college basketball team scored $25$ fewer points this month compared to last month. If they scored $82$ points last month, how many points did they score this month?

**Explanation**

To find the number of points the team scored this month, we need to find $82-25$.

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ -\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 5
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $2 - 5$. So, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place. Then we have

$$
8 - 1 = {\color{red}7}
$$

tens and $\color{red}12$ ones:

$$
7\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}2; - \mid 2 \mid 5
$$

Now, subtracting the ones,

$$
{\color{blue}12} - {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}7}{:}
$$

$$
7\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}2; - \mid 2 \mid 5; 7
$$

**Step 2.** Subtracting the tens,

$$
{\color{blue}7} - {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}5}{:}
$$

$$
7\begin{vmatrix}12 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}2; - \mid 2\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}7
$$

Therefore, the team scored $57$ points last month.
