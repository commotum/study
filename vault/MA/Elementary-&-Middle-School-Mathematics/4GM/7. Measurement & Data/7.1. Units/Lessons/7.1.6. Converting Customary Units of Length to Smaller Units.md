## Introduction

When solving problems with units, we often want to convert from one unit of measurement to another.

To convert between customary units of length, we use the following unit conversions:

- $1$ yard $=3$ feet
- $1$ foot $=12$ inches

We can visualize equivalent units of length using measuring devices. For example, the instrument below shows the equivalence between $1$ foot and $12$ inches.

![](<../Source/7.1.6. Converting Customary Units of Length to Smaller Units/Images/1c5dc64416eb2f3223df829c0643e534.png>)

Let's get some practice at converting between different units of length.

<a id="converting-lengths-from-yards-to-feet"></a>
## Converting Lengths From Yards to Feet

**Example:** How many feet are in $14$ yards?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between yards and feet:

$$
1\, \textrm{yard} =3 \, \textrm{feet}
$$

We want to figure out how many feet are in ${\color{blue}{14}}$ yards. So, we multiply *both* sides of the above equation by ${\color{blue}{14}}$:

$$
14 \cdot 1yard = 14 \cdot 3feet; 14yards = 14 \cdot 3feet
$$

We can calculate $14\times 3$ using an area model:

![](<../Source/7.1.6. Converting Customary Units of Length to Smaller Units/Images/67f21718db46180b89a106d5324f0da4.png>)

According to our model,

$$
14\times 3 = 30+12 = 42
$$

Therefore, $14$ yards equals $42$ feet.

## Converting Lengths From Feet to Inches

**Example:** What is $45 \, \textrm{ft}$ expressed in inches?

**Explanation**

We start with the unit conversion between feet and inches:

$$
1 \,\textrm{ft} = 12 \,\textrm{in}
$$

We want to figure out how many inches are in $45$ feet. So, we multiply both sides of the above equation by $45$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
45 \cdot 1ft &= 45 \cdot 12in \\
45ft &= 45 \cdot 12in
\end{aligned}
$$

We can calculate $45 \times 12$ using an area model:

![](<../Source/7.1.6. Converting Customary Units of Length to Smaller Units/Images/209308346daa472dd19a983b5df26438.png>)

According to our model,

$$
45 \times 12 = 400+50+80+10=540
$$

Therefore, $45 \, \textrm{ft}$ equals $540 \, \textrm{in}$.
