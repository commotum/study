## Introduction

We can extend our understanding of place value to multiply three-digit numbers by one-digit numbers.

As an example, let's consider the following multiplication problem:

$$
145 \times {\color{red}{4}}
$$

First, let's write the three-digit number $(145)$ in expanded form:

$$
145 = {\color{blue}{100}} + {\color{blue}{40}} + {\color{blue}{5}}
$$

We now multiply all numbers in our expanded form by ${\color{red}{4}}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
100 \cdot 4 &= 400 \\
40 \cdot 4 &= 160 \\
5 \cdot 4 &= 20
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's now add these results:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 \\ + & 1\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 0
$$

Therefore,

$$
145 \times 4 = 580
$$

## Multiplying a Three-Digit Number by a One-Digit Number Given a Decomposition

**Example:** Mary wishes to find the value of $4\times748$. She proceeds as follows:

$4 \cdot 700|= [math]\phantom{0000}[/math]; 4 \cdot 40|= [math]\phantom{000}[/math]; 4 \cdot 8|= [math]\phantom{00}[/math]$

Find the missing numbers, and use your answers to find the value of $4\times748$.

**Explanation**

Notice that $748$ in expanded form is

$$
748 = 700+40+8
$$

So, we can compute $4\times 748$ by filling in the missing numbers and adding the results.

First, let's find the missing values:

$$
\begin{aligned}
4 \cdot 700 &= [math]\,2,800\,[/math] \\
4 \cdot 40 &= [math]\,160\,[/math] \\
4 \cdot 8 &= [math]\,32\,[/math]
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's now add these results:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}8 & 0 & 0 \\ + & 1 & 6\end{vmatrix}0; +\begin{vmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 2 & 9\end{vmatrix}9 \mid 2
$$

Therefore,

$$
4\times748 = 2,992
$$

## Multiplying a Three-Digit Number by a One-Digit Number

**Example:** What is the value of $745 \times 8$?

**Explanation**

First, let's write the three-digit number $(745)$ in expanded form:

$$
745 = {\color{blue}{700}} + {\color{blue}{40}} + {\color{blue}{5}}
$$

We now multiply all numbers in our expanded form by ${\color{red}{8}}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
700 \cdot 8 &= 5, 600 \\
40 \cdot 8 &= 320 \\
5 \cdot 8 &= 40
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's now add these results:

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}6 & 0 & 0 \\ + & 3 & 2\end{vmatrix}0; +\begin{vmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 5 & 9\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 0
$$

Therefore,

$$
745 \times 8 = 5,960
$$

## Multiplying a Three-Digit Number by a One-Digit Number: Word Problems

**Example:** An adhesive tape has a length of $164$ feet. What is the total length of $6$ adhesive tapes?

**Explanation**

To figure out the total length of $6$ tapes, we need to compute the value of

$$
{\color{red}{6}} \times 164
$$

First, let's write the three-digit number $(164)$ in expanded form:

$$
164 = {\color{blue}{100}} + {\color{blue}{60}} + {\color{blue}{4}}
$$

We now multiply all numbers in our expanded form by ${\color{red}{6}}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6 \cdot 100 &= 600 \\
6 \cdot 60 &= 360 \\
6 \cdot 4 &= 24
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's now add these results:

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 \\ + & 3\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ 9\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 4
$$

Therefore, six tapes cover $984$ feet.
