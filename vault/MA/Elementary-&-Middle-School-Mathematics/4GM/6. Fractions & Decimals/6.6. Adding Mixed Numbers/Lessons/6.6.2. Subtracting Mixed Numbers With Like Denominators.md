## Introduction

To subtract mixed numbers with like denominators, we subtract

- whole numbers from whole numbers, and
- fractions from fractions.

For example, let's find the value of

$$
{\color{red}9} \,{\color{blue}\dfrac{3}{5}} - {\color{red}6} \,{\color{blue}\dfrac{2}{5}}
$$

- Subtracting the whole numbers, we get
${\color{red}9} - {\color{red}6} = {\color{red}{3}}$.
- Subtracting the fractions, we get
${\color{blue}\dfrac{3}{5}} - {\color{blue}\dfrac{2}{5}} = \dfrac{3 - 2}{5} = {\color{blue}{\dfrac{1}{5}}}$.

Finally, we combine our results:

$$
9 \,\dfrac{3}{5} - 6 \,\dfrac{2}{5} = {\color{red}{3}}{\color{blue}{\dfrac{1}{5}}}
$$

## Subtracting Mixed Numbers With Like Denominators

**Example:** Find the value of $12 \,\dfrac{4}{6} - 4 \,\dfrac{3}{6}$.

**Explanation**

To subtract mixed numbers with like denominators, we subtract

- whole numbers from whole numbers, and
- fractions from fractions.

With that in mind, let's subtract our mixed numbers.

- Subtracting the whole numbers, we get
$12 - 4 = {\color{red}{8}}$.
- Subtracting the fractions, we get
$\dfrac{4}{6} - \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{4 - 3}{6} = {\color{blue}{\dfrac{1}{6}}}$.

Finally, we combine the resulting whole number and fraction to create a mixed number:

$$
12 \,\dfrac{4}{6} - 4 \,\dfrac{3}{6} = {\color{red}{8}}\,{\color{blue}{\dfrac{1}{6}}}
$$

## Subtracting Mixed Numbers With Equal Whole Parts

**Example:** Expressed as a fraction in its simplest form, $5\,\dfrac 37 - 5 \,\dfrac 17 =$

**Explanation**

- whole numbers from whole numbers, and
- fractions from fractions.

With that in mind, let's subtract our mixed numbers.

- Subtracting the whole numbers, we get
$5-5 = {\color{red}{0}}$.
- Subtracting the fractions, we get
$\dfrac 37 - \dfrac 17 = \dfrac{3-1}{7} = {\color{blue}{\dfrac 27}}$.

## Subtracting Mixed Numbers With Equal Fractional Parts

**Example:** What is the value of $5 \,\dfrac{2}{5} - 3 \,\dfrac{2}{5} \,$?

**Explanation**

To subtract mixed numbers with like denominators, we subtract

- whole numbers from whole numbers, and
- fractions from fractions.

With that in mind, let's subtract our mixed numbers.

- Subtracting the whole numbers, we get
$5 - 3 = {\color{red}{2}}$.
- Subtracting the fractions, we get
$\dfrac{2}{5} - \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{2 - 2}{5} = \dfrac{0}{5} = {\color{blue}0}$.
So, the fractional part is zero. This is because zero divided by any number gives zero.

Finally, we combine the resulting whole number and fraction to create a mixed number:

$$
5 \,\dfrac{2}{5} - 3 \,\dfrac{2}{5} = {\color{red}{2}}+ {\color{blue}0} = {\color{red}2}
$$

## Subtracting Mixed Numbers When Simplification Is Needed

**Example:** What is the value of $8 \,\dfrac{7}{9} - 4 \,\dfrac{4}{9} \,$?

**Explanation**

To subtract mixed numbers with like denominators, we subtract

- whole numbers from whole numbers, and
- fractions from fractions.

With that in mind, let's subtract our mixed numbers.

- Subtracting the whole numbers, we get
$8 - 4 = {\color{red}{4}}$.
- Subtracting the fractions, we get
$\dfrac{7}{9} - \dfrac{4}{9} = \dfrac{7 - 4}{9} = \dfrac{3}{9}$.
We can simplify this fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by $3{:}$
$\dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{3 \div 3}{9 \div 3} = {\color{blue}{\dfrac{1}{3}}}$

Finally, we combine the resulting whole number and fraction to create a mixed number:

$$
8 \,\dfrac{7}{9} - 4 \,\dfrac{4}{9} = {\color{red}{4}}\,{\color{blue}{\dfrac{1}{3}}}
$$
