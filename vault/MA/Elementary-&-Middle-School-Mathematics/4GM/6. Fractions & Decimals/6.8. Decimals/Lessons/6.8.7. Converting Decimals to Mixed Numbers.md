## Introduction

Whenever we write a decimal as a mixed number, our mixed number will have

- a **whole number part** corresponding to the decimal's whole number part, and
- a **fractional part** corresponding to the decimal part.

For example, let's write the following decimal as a mixed number:

$$
{\color{blue}{5.}}{\color{red}{7}}
$$

To express this decimal as a mixed number, we look at its whole number and decimal parts:

- The whole number part is ${\color{blue}5}$.
- The decimal part has $1$ decimal place. Therefore, we can write it as a decimal fraction as follows:
$0.{\color{red}7} = \dfrac{\color{red}7}{10}$

Therefore, we express our decimal as a mixed number in the following way:

$$
{\color{blue}5}\,\dfrac{\color{red}7}{10}
$$

## Converting Decimals With One Decimal Place

**Example:** Which mixed number is equivalent to $1.9$?

**Explanation**

Whenever we write a decimal as a mixed number, our mixed number will have

- a *whole number part* that corresponds to the whole number to the *left* of the decimal point, and
- a *fractional part* that corresponds to the decimal part to the *right* of the decimal point.

With that in mind, let's write ${\color{blue}1}.\!{\color{red}9}$ as a mixed number.

- The whole number part is ${\color{blue}1}$.
- The decimal part has $1$ decimal place, so we can write it as a fraction in the following way:
$0.9 = {\color{red}\dfrac{9}{10}}$

Therefore, we can write the given decimal as the following mixed number:

$$
1.9 = 1\,\dfrac{9}{10}
$$

<a id="simplifying-mixed-numbers"></a>
## Simplifying Mixed Numbers

In some cases, we may be required to simplify the fractional part of the mixed number.

To demonstrate, let's convert ${\color{blue}4}.\!{\color{red}6}$ to a mixed number using the normal methods:

- The whole number part is ${\color{blue}4}$.
- The decimal part has $1$ decimal place. Therefore, we can write it as a fraction in the following way:
$0.6 = {\color{red}\dfrac{6}{10}}$
Notice that we can simplify this fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by $2{:}$
$\frac{6}{10}|= \frac{6 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{3}{5}$

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
4.6 = 4\,\dfrac{3}{5}
$$

## Converting Decimals to Mixed Numbers With Simplification

**Example:** Which mixed number is equivalent to $51.4$?

**Explanation**

Whenever we write a decimal as a mixed number, our mixed number will have

- a *whole number part* that corresponds to the whole number to the left of the decimal point, and
- a *fractional part* that corresponds to the decimal part to the right of the decimal point.

With that in mind, let's write ${\color{blue}51}.\!{\color{red}4}$ as a mixed number.

- The whole number part is ${\color{blue}51}$.
- The decimal part has $1$ decimal place, so we can write it as a fraction in the following way:
$0.4= \dfrac{4}{10}$
Now we reduce the fraction to its simplest form by dividing the numerator and denominator by $2{:}$
$\dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{4 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = {\color{red}\dfrac{2}{5}}$

Therefore, we can write the given decimal as the following mixed number:

$$
51.4 = 51 \, \dfrac{2}{5}
$$
