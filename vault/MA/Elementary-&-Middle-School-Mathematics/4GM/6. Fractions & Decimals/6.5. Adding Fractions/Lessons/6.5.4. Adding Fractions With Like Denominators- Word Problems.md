## Introduction

Fraction addition often arises in real-world situations.

For example, suppose a cable is cut into two pieces. One piece is of length

$$
\dfrac{2}{7}\,\textrm{m}
$$

and the other of length

$$
\dfrac{4}{7}\,\textrm{m}
$$

What was the original length of the cable?

To find the original length of the cable, we need to calculate the sum

$$
\dfrac{2}{7} + \dfrac{4}{7}
$$

To add two fractions with like denominators, we add the numerators and keep the denominators the same. So,

$$
\dfrac{2}{7} + \dfrac{4}{7}= \dfrac{2+4}{7} = \dfrac{6}{7}
$$

Therefore, the original length of the cable was

$$
\dfrac{6}{7}\,\textrm{m}
$$

## Adding Fractions

**Example:** Grace downloaded two files. The size of the first file was $\dfrac{7}{5}\,\textrm{MB}$ and the size of the second was $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\textrm{MB}$. What is the combined size of the files?

**Explanation**

To find the combined size of the files, we need to calculate the sum

$$
\dfrac{7}{5} + \dfrac{2}{5}
$$

To add two fractions with like denominators, we add the numerators and keep the denominators the same. So,

$$
\dfrac{7}{5} + \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{7+2}{5} = \dfrac{9}{5}
$$

Therefore, the combined size of the files is

$$
\dfrac{9}{5}\,\textrm{MB}
$$

## Adding Fractions and Simplifying the Result

**Example:** Claire has made a scarf using $\dfrac{1}{10}\, \mathrm{yd}$ of one fabric and $\dfrac{3}{10}\, \mathrm{yd}$ of another fabric. How many yards of fabric did she use in total?

**Explanation**

To find how many yards of fabric Claire used in total, we need to calculate the sum

$$
\dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{3}{10}
$$

To add two fractions with like denominators, we add the numerators and keep the denominators the same. So,

$$
\dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{3}{10}= \dfrac{1+3}{10} = \dfrac{4}{10}
$$

We can simplify this fraction by dividing the numerator and the denominator by $2{:}$

$$
\dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{4 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \dfrac{2}{5}
$$

Therefore, Claire used

$$
\dfrac{2}{5}\, \mathrm{yd}
$$

of fabric.

## Adding Fractions and Converting the Result to a Mixed Number

**Example:** Emily carries some books and other supplies in her backpack. If her books weigh $\dfrac{74}{8}\,\textrm{lb}$ and her other supplies weigh $\dfrac{25}{8}\,\textrm{lb}$, what is the combined weight of the contents of Emily's backpack? Express your final answer as a mixed number.

**Explanation**

To find the weight of the contents of Emily's backpack, we need to calculate the sum

$$
\dfrac{74}{8} + \dfrac{25}{8}
$$

To add two fractions with like denominators, we add the numerators and keep the denominators the same. So,

$$
\dfrac{74}{8} + \dfrac{25}{8} = \dfrac{74+25}{8} = \dfrac{99}{8}
$$

We then convert

$$
\dfrac{99}{8}
$$

to a mixed number:

$$
99 \div 8 = 12\,\textrm{R}3 = 12\,\dfrac38
$$

Therefore, the contents Emily's backpack weigh

$$
12\,\dfrac{3}{8}\,\textrm{lb}
$$
