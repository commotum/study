## Introduction

The method of subtracting numbers in scientific notation with different powers of ten is similar to adding them. We follow the usual three steps:

- **Step 1**: Identify the smaller number.
- **Step 2**: Rewrite the smaller number to have the same power of ten as the larger number.
- **Step 3**: Subtract the two numbers as usual.

For instance, let's compute the following difference:

$$
\left(9 \times 10^{7} \right) - \left(8 \times 10^{6} \right)
$$

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
8 \cdot 10^{6} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{7 - 1} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-1 + 7} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{7} &=  \\
&= 0.8 \cdot 10^{7}
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we can carry out the subtraction as usual:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(9 \cdot 10^{7}) - (8 \cdot 10^{6}) &=  \\
(9 \cdot 10^{7}) - (0.8 \cdot 10^{7}) &=  \\
(9 - 0.8) \cdot 10^{7} &=  \\
&= 8.2 \cdot 10^{7}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Subtracting Numbers With Different Positive Powers of Ten

**Example:** Express $\left(6.2 \times 10^{13} \right) - \left(8.1 \times 10^{12} \right)$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
8.1 \cdot 10^{12} &=  \\
8.1 \cdot 10^{13 - 1} &=  \\
8.1 \cdot 10^{-1 + 13} &=  \\
8.1 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{13} &=  \\
&= 0.81 \cdot 10^{13}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(6.2 \cdot 10^{13}) - (8.1 \cdot 10^{12}) &=  \\
(6.2 \cdot 10^{13}) - (0.81 \cdot 10^{13}) &=  \\
(6.2 - 0.81) \cdot 10^{13} &=  \\
&= 5.39 \cdot 10^{13}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Subtracting Numbers With Different Negative Powers of Ten

**Example:** Express $\left(3 \times 10^{-9} \right) - \left(6 \times 10^{-10} \right)$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6 \cdot 10^{-10} &=  \\
6 \cdot 10^{-9 - 1} &=  \\
6 \cdot 10^{-1 - 9} &=  \\
6 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{-9} &=  \\
&= 0.6 \cdot 10^{-9}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(3 \cdot 10^{-9}) - (6 \cdot 10^{-10}) &=  \\
(3 \cdot 10^{-9}) - (0.6 \cdot 10^{-9}) &=  \\
(3 - 0.6) \cdot 10^{-9} &=  \\
&= 2.4 \cdot 10^{-9}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Subtracting Numbers Where the Powers Differ by More Than One

**Example:** Express $\left(4 \times 10^{5} \right) - \left(8 \times 10^{3}\right)$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
8 \cdot 10^{3} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{5 - 2} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-2 + 5} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-2} \cdot 10^{5} &=  \\
&= 0.08 \cdot 10^{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4 \cdot 10^{5}) - (8 \cdot 10^{3}) &=  \\
(4 \cdot 10^{5}) - (0.08 \cdot 10^{5}) &=  \\
(4 - 0.08) \cdot 10^{5} &=  \\
&= 3.92 \cdot 10^{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Word Problems

**Example:** The flow rate of the Amazon River is $2.8 \times 10^{8}$ liters per second, while the flow rate of the Orinoco River is $3.3 \times 10^{7}$ liters per second. How much greater is the flow in the Amazon river compared to the Orinoco river?

**Explanation**

We need to write the following expression in scientific notation:
$2.8 \times 10^{8} - 3.3 \times 10^{7}$.

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3.3 \cdot 10^{7} &=  \\
3.3 \cdot 10^{8 - 1} &=  \\
3.3 \cdot 10^{-1 + 8} &=  \\
3.3 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{8} &=  \\
&= 0.33 \cdot 10^{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get
$(2.8 \cdot 10^{8}) - (3.3 \cdot 10^{7}); (2.8 \cdot 10^{8}) - (0.33 \cdot 10^{8}); (2.8 - 0.33) \cdot 10^{8}; 2.47 \cdot 10^{8}$.

Therefore, the Amazon river flow is $2.47 \times 10^{8}$ liters per second more than the Orinoco.
