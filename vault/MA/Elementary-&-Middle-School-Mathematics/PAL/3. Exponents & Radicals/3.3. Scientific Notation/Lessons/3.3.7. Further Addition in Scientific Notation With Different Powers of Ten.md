## Introduction

Sometimes, when adding two numbers in scientific notation, we get a number that's not in scientific notation. In such cases, we need to rewrite the final result.

To demonstrate, let's calculate the following sum:

$$
\left(9 \times 10^{7}\right) + \left(9.3 \times 10^{8}\right)
$$

The first number in the sum is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the second number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
9 \cdot 10^{7} &=  \\
9 \cdot 10^{8 - 1} &=  \\
9 \cdot 10^{-1 + 8} &=  \\
&= 9 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{8} \\
&= 0.9 \cdot 10^{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we can carry out the addition as usual:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(9 \cdot 10^{7}) + (9.3 \cdot 10^{8}) &=  \\
(0.9 \cdot 10^{8}) + (9.3 \cdot 10^{8}) &=  \\
(0.9 + 9.3) \cdot 10^{8} &=  \\
&= 10.2 \cdot 10^{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

The above number is not in scientific notation because $10.2 \gt 10$. So, we need to write $10.2$ in scientific notation, as follows:

$$
10.2 = 1.02\times 10^{1}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}
10.2 \cdot 10^{8} &=  \\
(1.02 \cdot 10^{1}) \cdot 10^{8} &=  \\
1.02 \cdot (10^{1} \cdot 10^{8}) &=  \\
1.02 \cdot 10^{1 + 8} &=  \\
&= 1.02 \cdot 10^{9}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, our final answer is $1.02 \times 10^{9}$.

## Adding Numbers in Scientific Notation

**Example:** Express $\left(8 \times 10^{-4}\right) + \left(9.5 \times 10^{-3}\right)$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

The first number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the second number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
8 \cdot 10^{-4} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-3 - 1} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-1 - 3} &=  \\
8 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
&= 0.8 \cdot 10^{-3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the addition, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(8 \cdot 10^{-4}) + (9.5 \cdot 10^{-3}) &=  \\
(0.8 \cdot 10^{-3}) + (9.5 \cdot 10^{-3}) &=  \\
(0.8 + 9.5) \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
&= 10.3 \cdot 10^{-3}
\end{aligned}
$$

The above number is not in scientific notation because $10.3 \gt 10$. So, we need to write $10.3$ in scientific notation, as follows:

$$
10.3 = 1.03\times 10^{1}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}
10.3 \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
(1.03 \cdot 10^{1}) \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
1.03 \cdot (10^{1} \cdot 10^{-3}) &=  \\
1.03 \cdot 10^{1 - 3} &=  \\
&= 1.03 \cdot 10^{-2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, our final answer is $1.03 \times 10^{-2}$.

## Subtracting Numbers in Scientific Notation

**Example:** Express $\left(7 \times 10^{7}\right) - \left(1 \times 10^{8}\right)$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

The first number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the second number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
7 \cdot 10^{7} &=  \\
7 \cdot 10^{8 - 1} &=  \\
7 \cdot 10^{-1 + 8} &=  \\
7 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{8} &=  \\
&= 0.7 \cdot 10^{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(7 \cdot 10^{7}) - (1 \cdot 10^{8}) &=  \\
(0.7 \cdot 10^{8}) - (1 \cdot 10^{8}) &=  \\
(0.7 - 1) \cdot 10^{8} &=  \\
&= - 0.3 \cdot 10^{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

The above number is not in scientific notation because $0.3 < 1$. So, we need to write $0.3$ in scientific notation, as follows:

$$
0.3 = 3\times 10^{-1}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}
-0.3 \cdot 10^{8} &= - (3 \cdot 10^{-1}) \cdot 10^{8} \\
&=-3 \cdot (10^{-1} \cdot 10^{8}) \\
&=-3 \cdot 10^{-1 + 8} \\
&=-3 \cdot 10^{7}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, our final answer is $-3 \times 10^{7}$.

## Word Problems

**Example:** The maximum acceptable concentration for nitrate in drinking water is $1 \times 10^{-3} \, \mathrm{g/L}$.
Laboratory analysis established that the concentration of nitrate in the water of a particular mountain river is $7 \times 10^{-4} \, \mathrm{g/L}$ less than the maximum acceptable concentration for drinking water. What is the concentration of nitrate in this river?

**Explanation**

We need to write the following expression in scientific notation:
$1 \times 10^{-3} - 7 \times 10^{-4}$.

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
7 \cdot 10^{-4} &=  \\
7 \cdot 10^{-3 - 1} &=  \\
7 \cdot 10^{-1 - 3} &=  \\
7 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
&= 0.7 \cdot 10^{-3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1 \cdot 10^{-3}) - (7 \cdot 10^{-4}) &=  \\
(1 \cdot 10^{-3}) - (0.7 \cdot 10^{-3}) &=  \\
(1 - 0.7) \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
&= 0.3 \cdot 10^{-3}
\end{aligned}
$$

The above number is not in scientific notation because $0.3 \lt 1$. So, we need to write $0.3$ in scientific notation, as follows:

$$
0.3 = 3\times 10^{-1}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.3 \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
(3 \cdot 10^{-1}) \cdot 10^{-3} &=  \\
3 \cdot (10^{-1} \cdot 10^{-3}) &=  \\
&= 3 \cdot 10^{-4}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the concentration of nitrate in the river is $3 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{g/L}$.
