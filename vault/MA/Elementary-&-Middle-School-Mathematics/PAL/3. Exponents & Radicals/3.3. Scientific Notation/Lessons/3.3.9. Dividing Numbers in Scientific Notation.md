## Introduction

Suppose that we have two numbers in scientific notation, like $4 \times 10^5$ and $2\times 10^3$. How can we divide one by the other?

To divide $4 \times 10^{5}$ by $2 \times 10^{3}$, we apply the following three steps:

- **Step 1:** Group the decimal parts and the powers of $10$ separately.
$\frac{4 \cdot 10^{5}}{2 \cdot 10^{3}}|= \frac{4}{2} \cdot \frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}}$
- **Step 2:** Divide the decimal parts.

$$
\frac{4}{2} \cdot \frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}} = 2 \cdot \frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}}
$$

- **Step 3:** Divide the powers of $10$ using the quotient rule for exponents.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2 \cdot \frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}} &= 2 \cdot 10^{5 - 3} \\
&= 2 \cdot 10^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the final answer is $2\times 10^2$.

## Dividing Numbers With Positive Powers

**Example:** What is $\dfrac{7.2 \times 10^{5}}{1.8 \times 10}$ written in scientific notation?

**Explanation**

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the coefficients from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{7.2 \cdot 10^{5}}{1.8 \cdot 10} &= \frac{7.2}{1.8} \cdot \frac{10^{5}}{10^{1}} \\
&= 4 \cdot 10^{5 - 1} \\
&= 4 \cdot 10^{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Dividing Numbers With Negative Powers

**Example:** Rounding your answer to three decimal places where appropriate, write $\dfrac{6.32 \times 10^{-4}}{2.4 \times 10^{6}}$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the decimal part from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{6.32 \cdot 10^{-4}}{2.4 \cdot 10^{6}} &= \frac{6.32}{2.4} \cdot \frac{10^{-4}}{10^{6}} \\
&\approx 2.6333 \cdot 10^{-4 - 6} \\
&\approx 2.633 \cdot 10^{-10}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Dividing With Negative Numbers

**Example:** Express $\dfrac{8.6 \times 10^{7}}{(-2 \times 10^{5})}$ in scientific notation.

**Explanation**

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the decimal part from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately.
We also remember that a positive number divided by a negative number gives a negative number:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(8.6 \cdot 10^{7})/((-2 \cdot 10^{5})) &= -\frac{8.6 \cdot 10^{7}}{2 \cdot 10^{5}} \\
&=-\frac{8.6}{2} \cdot \frac{10^{7}}{10^{5}} \\
&=-4.3 \cdot 10^{7 - 5} \\
&=-4.3 \cdot 10^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Dividing Numbers and Rewriting the Result

**Example:** What is $\dfrac{2\times 10^{3}}{8 \times 10^{7}}$ written in scientific notation?

**Explanation**

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the decimal part from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{2 \cdot 10^{3}}{8 \cdot 10^{7}} &= \frac{2}{8} \cdot \frac{10^{3}}{10^{7}} \\
&= 0.25 \cdot 10^{3 - 7} \\
&= 0.25 \cdot 10^{-4}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is not in scientific notation since $0.25 < 1$. Writing the decimal part in scientific notation, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.25 \cdot 10^{-4} &= (2.5 \cdot 10^{-1}) \cdot 10^{-4} \\
&= 2.5 \cdot (10^{-1} \cdot 10^{-4}) \\
&= 2.5 \cdot (10^{-1 - 4}) \\
&= 2.5 \cdot 10^{-5}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the final answer is $2.5 \times 10^{-5}$.
