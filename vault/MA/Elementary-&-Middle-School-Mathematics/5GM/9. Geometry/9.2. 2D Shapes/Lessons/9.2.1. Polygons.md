## Introduction

A polygon is any shape where all the sides are straight. Here are some examples of polygons:

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/a76f6b5b12beb2d969b3a77b3f60ba58.png>)

However, the following shape is *not* a polygon, since it doesn't have all straight sides:

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/50a805e984f3cdec0df4d502f03a6d28.png>)

The corners of a polygon are called **vertices**, and the sides are called **edges**.
For a polygon, the number of vertices always equals the number of edges.

Now, how many vertices does the polygon shown below have?

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/67d2e6f0816ca1db28cda9c40d99e295.png>)

The vertices are the corners. The polygon has $5$ vertices in total, as shown below.

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/46a055c0e0b85d0c7b3ccc4037fbda18.png>)

## Types of Polygons

Polygons with a specific number of sides, or vertices, have special names.

| Number of sides (or vertices) | Name | Shape |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $3$ | Triangle | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/0574e2687231713abede2e6b091186ca.png>) |
| $4$ | Quadrilateral | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/9062123aaffec9867e55ed1498896b5a.png>) |
| $5$ | Pentagon | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/11892def5eee8ec601387c577e91f292.png>) |
| $6$ | Hexagon | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/54d77350e3b0b1133a68eb39e07c15b7.png>) |
| $7$ | Heptagon | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/64217007f97a2dfcb41597a8499605e6.png>) |
| $8$ | Octagon | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/4ed45c3dff02c28c79bea0241c1c1c3c.png>) |
| $9$ | Nonagon | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/41b40c0ff647cad45e10a4ddb3cd9c8b.png>) |
| $10$ | Decagon | ![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/b4f3661435f0eda25f0502433f37e364.png>) |

Only one of the following is a **heptagon**, but which? Let's find out.

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/3819bd3888011699233c85a2120a89ed.png>)

A heptagon has $7$ vertices and $7$ straight sides. Therefore, the answer is III.

Notice that shape I is not a polygon since it has a curved side. Shape II has $8$ vertices, making it an **octagon**.

## Classifying Polygons

**Example:** What is the name of the polygon shown below?

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/fd1e221a3b1f3821d6fb429ea33e77b8.png>)

**Explanation**

The polygon has $4$ vertices, as shown below.

![](<../Source/9.2.1. Polygons/Images/1c006818f2bd9771772a936a005efa62.png>)

The polygon also has $4$ straight sides connecting its vertices.

Therefore, it's a **quadrilateral**.
