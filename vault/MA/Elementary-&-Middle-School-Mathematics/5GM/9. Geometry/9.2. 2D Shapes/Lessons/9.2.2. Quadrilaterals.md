## Completing the Sentence: Polygons and Quadrilaterals

**Example:** Place the following words into the sentence below.

$\qquad\qquad$ (*Polygon*, *Quadrilateral*)

$\qquad$ A $\text{___________}$ is a $\text{___________}$ that has exactly $4$ sides.

**Explanation**

By the definition of a quadrilateral, the correct answer is

$\qquad$ A **quadrilateral** is a **polygon** that has exactly $4$ sides.

## Trapezoids and Parallelograms

There are a few special types of quadrilaterals.

A **trapezoid** is a quadrilateral with *exactly one* pair of opposite sides parallel to each other:

![](<../Source/9.2.2. Quadrilaterals/Images/6f976f60e50d94f06e54642979734189.png>)

A **parallelogram** is a quadrilateral with *exactly* two pairs of opposite sides parallel to each other:

![](<../Source/9.2.2. Quadrilaterals/Images/1ca445189316ff501f7ccb656a76ebea.png>)

## Identifying Shapes: Trapezoids and Parallelograms

**Example:** Which of the shapes below is a parallelogram?

![](<../Source/9.2.2. Quadrilaterals/Images/727fc44df6eaf0319f52c84c47a7583e.png>)

**Explanation**

Let's examine the shapes one by one:

- Shape I is a triangle since it has only $3$ sides.
- Shape II is a trapezoid. It has $4$ sides and only one pair of parallel sides.
- Shape III represents a parallelogram since it has $4$ sides and $2$ pairs of parallel sides.

![](<../Source/9.2.2. Quadrilaterals/Images/d0369c30be176335c1f7ea23ce88cb80.png>)

Therefore, only III is a parallelogram.

<a id="identifying-types-of-quadrilaterals-given-information-about-their-sides"></a>
## Identifying Types of Quadrilaterals Given Information About Their Sides

**Example:** What word is missing from the sentence below?

$\qquad$ *Any $\text{__________}$ has exactly $1$ pair of parallel sides.*

1. parallelogram
2. trapezoid
3. quadrilateral

**Explanation**

The correct answer is II: **trapezoid**. The correct sentence is:

$\qquad$ *Any **trapezoid** has exactly $1$ pair of parallel sides.*

![](<../Source/9.2.2. Quadrilaterals/Images/c86570f76560026aac4b90319eba1859.png>)

By contrast, any parallelogram has exactly $2$ pairs of parallel sides, and a quadrilateral can have $0$, $1$, or $2$ pairs of parallel sides.
