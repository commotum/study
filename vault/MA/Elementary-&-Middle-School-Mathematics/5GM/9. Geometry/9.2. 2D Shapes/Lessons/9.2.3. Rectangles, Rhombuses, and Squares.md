## Introduction

There are even more special types of quadrilaterals.

A **rhombus** is a quadrilateral with $2$ pairs of parallel sides, where all $4$ sides are equal:

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/2900c609261a6e3de97de040563ca4a7.png>)

A **rectangle** is a quadrilateral with $2$ pairs of parallel sides and $4$ right angles:

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/aa192555a5247956fdbbfd0327ec4121.png>)

A **square** is a quadrilateral with $2$ pairs of parallel sides, where all $4$ sides are equal, and also all $4$ angles are right angles:

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/ab0e4acff2efd2dd6d88fb57f1bccff1.png>)

## Identifying Shapes: Rhombuses and Rectangles

**Example:** Which of the shapes below is a rhombus?

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/c46f81c73031f9029b32520fc089ec35.png>)

**Explanation**

Let's examine each of the shapes one by one:

- Shape I is a *rhombus*. It has $4$ equal sides and $2$ pairs of parallel sides (but no right angle).

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/5fefa16505ecd2301f94fb0013542890.png>)

- Shape II shows a trapezoid (not a rhombus) since it has $4$ sides but only $1$ pair of parallel sides.
- Shape III represents a polygon with $5$ sides, so it's not a rhombus.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only".

## Reasoning About Rhombuses and Rectangles

**Example:** The shape above is a square because

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/e82a109d13d12804fcc138e7e1a64436.png>)

1. it has $4$ sides and $4$ right angles
2. it has $4$ sides
3. it has $4$ equal sides and $4$ equal angles

**Explanation**

The correct answer is that "*it has $4$ equal sides and $4$ equal angles*".

The description "*it has $4$ sides and $4$ right angles*" is not enough because it describes a rectangle, where not all the sides are necessarily equal:

![](<../Source/9.2.3. Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares/Images/12a717d9ea93173995dd7999e699da90.png>)

Likewise, having $4$ sides is not enough to be considered a square. The following quadrilateral is not a square:
