## Introduction

To multiply a two-digit number by a one-digit number, we can use the **standard algorithm**.

For example, to multiply $23$ by $3$, we write the two-digit number above the one-digit number, lining up the place values:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 48 + 720 = 76800000000
$$

First, we multiply the ones, placing the answer in the ones column under the line:

$$
_(0); 2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}3; 9 \mid 3 \cdot 3 = 90000000000000000; Write:9
$$

Then, we multiply by the value in the tens place, putting the answer in the tens column under the line:

$$
_(0); 2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}3; 6 \mid 9 \mid 2 \cdot 3 = 60000000000000000; Write:6
$$

Therefore,

$$
23 \times 3 = 69
$$

## Two-Digit by One-Digit Multiplication

**Example:** What is $4$ multiplied by $21$?

**Explanation**

We write our multiplication this way:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 48 + 720 = 76800000000
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
_(0); 2\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4; 4 \mid 1 \cdot 4 = 40000000000000000; Write:4
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens:

$$
_(0); 2\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4; 8 \mid 4 \mid 2 \cdot 4 = 80000000000000000; Write:8
$$

Therefore,

$$
21 \times 4 = 84
$$

## Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers By One-Digit Numbers With Carries

Sometimes when multiplying the ones, the result is two digits long.

In this case, we write down the digit in the ones place, and we **carry** the digit in the tens place. We then add the carried digit to the result of the multiplication of the tens.

For example, let's multiply $26$ by $6$.

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 14and3 + 14 = 170000
$$

First, we multiply the ones. We have

$$
6\times6= {\color{blue}3}{\color{red}6}
$$

, so we write the ones place value, $\color{red}6$, and carry the tens place value, ${\color{blue}3}$:

$$
_(3); 2\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}6; 6 \mid 6 \cdot 6 = 3600000000000000000; Carry:3; Write:6
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens. We have

$$
2\times 6 = 12
$$

, to which we add the carry of ${\color{blue}3}$:

$$
_(3); 2\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}6; 1\begin{vmatrix}5 & 6\end{vmatrix}2 \cdot 6 = 12and3 + 12 = 15; Carry:-; Write:15
$$

When we reach the tens multiplication, we do not carry anything. Instead, we write down the complete result.

Therefore,

$$
26 \times 6 = 156
$$

## Two-Digit by One-Digit Multiplication With Carries

**Example:** Calculate $37\times 4$.

**Explanation**

We write it this way:

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 3 \cdot 4 = 12and2 + 12 = 14
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
_(2); 3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4; 8 \mid 7 \cdot 4 = 28and2 + 12 = 14; Carry:2; Write:8
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens:

$$
_(2); 3\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4; 1\begin{vmatrix}4 & 8\end{vmatrix}3 \cdot 4 = 12and2 + 12 = 14; Carry:-; Write:14
$$

Therefore,

$$
37 \times 4 = 148
$$

## Word Problems

**Example:** As part of a school assignment, each student in a class must interview $25$ people about their sports preferences. If there are $7$ students in the class, how many interviews will they need to conduct in total?

**Explanation**

To calculate the total number of interviews, we need to multiply $25$ by $7$.

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 14and3 + 14 = 170000
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
_(3); 2\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}7; 5 \mid 5 \cdot 7 = 3500000000000000000; Carry:3; Write:5
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens:

$$
_(3); 2\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}7; 1\begin{vmatrix}7 & 5\end{vmatrix}2 \cdot 7 = 14and3 + 14 = 17; Carry:-; Write:17
$$

Therefore, there will be a total of $175$ interviews.
