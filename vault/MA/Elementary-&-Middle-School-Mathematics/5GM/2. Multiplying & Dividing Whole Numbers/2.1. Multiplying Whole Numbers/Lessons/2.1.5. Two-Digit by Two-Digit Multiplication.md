## Introduction

We can use the standard algorithm to multiply two-digit numbers.

To illustrate, let's use the standard algorithm to compute $12\times 13$.

Recall that ${\color{blue}1}{\color{red}3}$ in expanded form is ${\color{blue}10} + {\color{red}3}$. The idea is to first multiply $12$ by ${\color{red}3}$, then by ${\color{blue}10}$, and add the results.

We write one number above the other as follows:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1 & 3\end{vmatrix}36 + 120 = 156
$$

First, we multiply the top number ($12$) by the value in the ones place of the second number ($\bbox[2px, lightgray]{3}$), writing the result under the line:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1\end{vmatrix}3; 3 \mid 6 \mid 12 \cdot 3 = 36000
$$

Next, we multiply $12$ by $\bbox[2px, lightgray]{1}0$. Let's do this in steps:

**Step 1**: Any number multiplied by $10$ always gives a zero at the end. Therefore, we place a zero in the ones column underneath our first result.

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1\end{vmatrix}3; 3\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 0\end{vmatrix}12 \cdot 10 = 1200
$$

**Step 2**: Now, we work out $\bbox[2px, lightgray]{2}\times \bbox[2px, lightgray]{1}$. We place the answer next to our zero:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1\end{vmatrix}3; 3\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 2 \cdot 1 = 20
$$

**Step 3**: Next, we we work out $\bbox[2px, lightgray]{1}\times \bbox[2px, lightgray]{1}$:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1\end{vmatrix}3; 3\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 0 \mid 1 \cdot 1 = 10
$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 1\end{vmatrix}3; 3\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ +\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 0 \\ 1 & 5\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 36 + 120 = 156
$$

Therefore,

$$
12 \times 13 = 156
$$

## Multiplying a Two-Digit Number by a Two-Digit Number

**Example:** What is $23$ multiplied by $22$?

**Explanation**

We write it this way:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2 & 2\end{vmatrix}46 + 460 = 506
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2\end{vmatrix}2; 4 \mid 6 \mid 23 \cdot 2 = 46000
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2\end{vmatrix}2; 4\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 0 \mid 23 \cdot 20 = 4600
$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}3 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2\end{vmatrix}2; 4\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ +\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}6 & 0 \\ 5 & 0\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 46 + 460 = 506
$$

Therefore,

$$
23 \times 22 = 506
$$

## Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers by Two-Digit Numbers with Carries

Just like when multiplying a number by a one-digit number, we will sometimes have carries. Let's see how to deal with this by multiplying $24$ by $32$.

We write it this way:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 2\end{vmatrix}48 + 720 = 768
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
0; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; 2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; 4 \mid 8 \mid 24 \cdot 2 = 48000
$$

On this occasion, there were no carries when multiplying the ones, and so the carry box $0$ remains empty.

Next, we multiply the tens. Let's do this in steps.

**Step 1**: We start by writing a zero:

$$
0; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; 2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; 4\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ 0\end{vmatrix}24 \cdot 30 = 7200
$$

**Step 2**: We now work out

$$
\bbox[2px, lightgray]{4}\times \bbox[2px, lightgray]{3} = {\color{blue}1}{\color{red}2}
$$

We carry the ${\color{blue}1}$ and write down $\color{red}2$:

$$
[math]\color{blue}1[/math]; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; 2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; 4\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 4 \cdot 3 = 12; Carry:1; Write:2
$$

**Step 3**: We multiply

$$
\bbox[2px, lightgray]{2}\times \bbox[2px, lightgray]{3} = 6
$$

and we add the carry from the previous multiplication to make

$$
6+{\color{blue}{1}} = {\color{red}{7}}
$$

$$
[math]\color{blue}1[/math]; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; 2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; 4\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 0 \mid 2 \cdot 3 = 6and6 + 1 = 7; Carry: -; Write:7
$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$
[math]\color{blue}1[/math]; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; 2\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; 4\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ +\end{vmatrix}7\begin{vmatrix}2 & 0 \\ 7 & 6\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 48 + 720 = 768
$$

Therefore,

$$
24 \times 32 = 768
$$

## Multiplying a Two-Digit Number by a Two-Digit Number With Carried Digits

**Example:** Calculate $47 \times 35$.

**Explanation**

As seen before, we start by writing one number on top of the other:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 5\end{vmatrix}00000000000000000
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
[math]\color{blue}3[/math]; 4\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 5 \\ 2 & 3 & 5\end{vmatrix}47 \cdot 5 = 23500000
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
[math]\color{blue}2[/math]; [math]\color{blue}3[/math]; 4\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 5 \\ 2 & 3 & 5 \\ 1 & 4 & 1\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 47 \cdot 30 = 1, 41000
$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$
[math]\color{blue}2[/math]; [math]\color{blue}3[/math]; 4\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 5 \\ 2 & 3 & 5 \\ + & 1 & 4\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 1\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}4 & 5\end{vmatrix}235 + 1410 = 1, 645
$$

Therefore,

$$
47 \times 35 = 1, 645
$$

## Multiplying a Two-Digit Number by a Two-Digit Number: Word Problems

**Example:** Jessica runs $12$ kilometers every morning. How many kilometers does Jessica run in $28$ days?

**Explanation**

To calculate the total number of kilometers that Jessica runs in $28$ days, we need to multiply $12$ by $28$.

So, we write it this way:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2 & 8\end{vmatrix}12 \cdot 20 = 240
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
[math]\color{blue}1[/math]; 1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2\end{vmatrix}8; 9 \mid 6 \mid 12 \cdot 8 = 96000
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
[math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; [math]\color{blue}1[/math]; 1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2\end{vmatrix}8; 9\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 0 \mid 12 \cdot 20 = 2400
$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$
[math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; [math]\color{blue}1[/math]; 1\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2\end{vmatrix}8; 9\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ +\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 3 & 3\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 96 + 240 = 336
$$

So,

$$
12 \times 28 = 336
$$

Therefore, Jessica runs a total of $336$ kilometers in $28$ days.
