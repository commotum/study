## Introduction

In this lesson, we'll get practice at multiplying five-digit and six-digit numbers by one-digit numbers using the standard algorithm.

As before, we multiply each place value by the one-digit number from right to left.

Without further ado, let's dive in!

## Multiplying Larger Numbers By One-Digit Numbers: Finding Missing Digits

**Example:** From left to right, what are the missing digits in the following multiplication problem?

$_(5)\begin{vmatrix}_(0) & _(0) & _(3) \\ 7 & 9 & 0\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}6; [math]\phantom{4}[/math]\begin{vmatrix}[math]\phantom{7}[/math] & 4 & [math]\phantom{0}[/math] & 3\end{vmatrix}6$

**Explanation**

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
_(3); 7\begin{vmatrix}9 & 0 & 0\end{vmatrix}6; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 6\end{vmatrix}6 \cdot 6 = 36; Carry:3; Write:6
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
_(0)\begin{vmatrix}_(3) \\ 7\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0\end{vmatrix}6; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 0 \cdot 6 = 0and3 + 0 = 3; Carry:0; Write:3
$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
_(0)\begin{vmatrix}_(0) & _(3) \\ 7 & 9\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 6 \\ \cdot & 6 \\ 0 & 3\end{vmatrix}6 \mid 0 \cdot 6 = 0and0 + 0 = 0; Carry:0; Write:0
$$

Next, we multiply the thousands:

$$
_(5)\begin{vmatrix}_(0) & _(0) & _(3) \\ 7 & 9 & 0\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}6; 4\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3 & 6\end{vmatrix}9 \cdot 6 = 54and0 + 54 = 54; Carry:5; Write:4
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens of thousands:

$$
_(5)\begin{vmatrix}_(0) & _(0) & _(3) \\ 7 & 9 & 0\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}6; [math]\color{red}4[/math]\begin{vmatrix}[math]\color{red}7[/math] & 4 & 0 & 3 & 6\end{vmatrix}7 \cdot 6 = 42and5 + 42 = 47; Carry:-; Write:47
$$

Therefore, the missing digits, from left-to-right, are $\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}4},\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}7}$, and $\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}0}$.

## Multiplying Larger Numbers By One-Digit Numbers

**Example:** Calculate the value of $43,532 \times 7$.

**Explanation**

We write it this way:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}3 & 5 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot \mid 7 \mid 24and1 + 24 = 2500000
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
_(1); 4\begin{vmatrix}3 & 5 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ 4\end{vmatrix}2 \cdot 7 = 1400000000000000000; Carry:1; Write:4
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
_(2)\begin{vmatrix}_(1) \\ 4\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}5 & 3\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 3 \cdot 7 = 21and1 + 21 = 22; Carry:2; Write:2
$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
_(3)\begin{vmatrix}_(2) & _(1) \\ 4 & 3\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}3 & 2 \\ \cdot & 7 \\ 7 & 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 5 \cdot 7 = 35and2 + 35 = 37; Carry:3; Write:7
$$

Next, we multiply the thousands:

$$
_(2)\begin{vmatrix}_(3) & _(2) & _(1) \\ 4 & 3 & 5\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}7; 4\begin{vmatrix}7 & 2 & 4\end{vmatrix}3 \cdot 7 = 21and3 + 21 = 24; Carry:2; Write:4
$$

Finally, we multiply the ten thousands:

$$
_(2)\begin{vmatrix}_(3) & _(2) & _(1) \\ 4 & 3 & 5\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}7; 3\begin{vmatrix}0 & 4 & 7 & 2 & 4\end{vmatrix}4 \cdot 7 = 28and2 + 28 = 30; Carry:-; Write:30
$$

Therefore,

$$
43,532 \times 7 = 304, 724
$$

## Word Problems

**Example:** A factory uses $836\,802$ kilowatt-hours of electricity each quarter. How much electricity will it use in a year, if a year contains $4$ quarters?

**Explanation**

To calculate the factory's energy consumption over one year, we need to multiply $836\,802$ by $4$.

We write it this way:

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}3 & 6 & 8 & 0\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot \mid 4
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
_(0); 8\begin{vmatrix}3 & 6 & 8 & 0\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}2 \cdot 4 = 8; Carry:0; Write:8
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
_(0)\begin{vmatrix}_(0) \\ 8\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}6 & 8 & 0\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ 0\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 0 \cdot 4 = 0and0 + 0 = 0; Carry:0; Write:0
$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
_(3)\begin{vmatrix}_(0) & _(0) \\ 8 & 3\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}8 & 0\end{vmatrix}2; \cdot\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 8 \mid 8 \cdot 4 = 32and0 + 32 = 32; Carry:3; Write:2
$$

Next, we multiply the thousands:

$$
_(2)\begin{vmatrix}_(3) & _(0) & _(0) \\ 8 & 3 & 6\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2 \\ \cdot & 4 \\ 7 & 2\end{vmatrix}0 \mid 8 \mid 6 \cdot 4 = 24and3 + 24 = 27; Carry:2; Write:7
$$

Next, we multiply the ten thousands:

$$
_(1)\begin{vmatrix}_(2) & _(3) & _(0) & _(0) \\ 8 & 3 & 6 & 8\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4; 4\begin{vmatrix}7 & 2 & 0 & 8\end{vmatrix}3 \cdot 4 = 12and2 + 12 = 14; Carry:1; Write:4
$$

Finally, we multiply the hundred thousands:

$$
_(1)\begin{vmatrix}_(2) & _(3) & _(0) & _(0) \\ 8 & 3 & 6 & 8\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}4; 3\begin{vmatrix}3 & 4 & 7 & 2 & 0 & 8\end{vmatrix}8 \cdot 4 = 32and1 + 32 = 33; Carry:-; Write:33
$$

Therefore,

$$
836\,802 \times 4 = 3 \, 347 \, 208
$$

Therefore, the factory's total energy consumption in a year is $3\,347\,208$ kilowatt-hours.
