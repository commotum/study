## Introduction

We can also use the standard algorithm to divide a four-digit number by a one-digit number.

To illustrate, let's use the algorithm to divide $4,236$ by $3$. We start by writing down the question using long division notation:

| $3$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{4 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$ |  |
| --- | ---: |

First, we consider the thousands:

| $\color{blue}1$$3$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{{\color{red}4} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$1$$\color{lightgray}2$ | Divide: ${\color{red}4} \div 3 = {\color{blue}1}\,\text{R}\,1$Write over thousands: $\color{blue}1$Multiply: $3 \times {\color{blue}1} = 3$Subtract: ${\color{red}4} - 3 = 1$Bring down: $2$ |
| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the hundreds:

| $1$$\color{blue}4$$3$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{4 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$\color{red}1$$\color{red}2$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$2$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$\color{lightgray}3$ | Divide: ${\color{red}12} \div 3 = {\color{blue}4}$Write over hundreds: $\color{blue}4$Multiply: $3 \times {\color{blue}4} = 12$Subtract: ${\color{red}12} - 12 = 0$Bring down: $3$ |
| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the tens:

| $1$$4$$\color{blue}1$$3$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{4 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$1$$2$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$2$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$\color{red}3$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$-\!\!\!$$3$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$\color{lightgray}6$ | Divide: ${\color{red}3} \div 3 = {\color{blue}1}$Write over tens: $\color{blue}1$Multiply: $3 \times {\color{blue}1} = 3$Subtract: ${\color{red}3} - 3 = 0$Bring down: $6$ |
| --- | ---: |

Finally, we consider the ones:

| $1$$4$$1$$\color{blue}2$$3$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{4 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$1$$2$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$2$$0$$3$$-\!\!\!$$3$$0$$\color{red}6$$-\!\!\!$$6$$\fbox{0}$ | Divide: ${\color{red}6} \div 3 = {\color{blue}2}$Write over ones: $\color{blue}2$Multiply: $3 \times {\color{blue}2} = 6$Subtract: ${\color{red}6} - 6 = \fbox{0}$ |
| --- | ---: |

We've gone through all the digits in the number $4,236$, so the division is done. The number we have written at the top is the answer. There is no remainder.

Therefore,

$$
4,236 \div 3 = 1,412
$$

## Dividing with No Remainder

**Example:** A rocket, flying at a constant speed, covered a distance of $2,088$ miles in a period of $6$ minutes. How many miles did the rocket cover in $1$ minute?

**Explanation**

To find out how many miles the rocket covered in $1$ minute, we need to divide $2,088$ by $6$.

We start by writing down the question using long division notation:

| $6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8}$ |  |
| --- | ---: |

Notice that $\color{red}2$ (the number of thousands in ${\color{red}2},088$) is less than the divisor $6$. Therefore, we should consider both the thousands and hundreds:

| $\color{blue}3$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{{\color{red}2} {\:\phantom{\mid}} {\color{red}0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$8$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$2$$\color{lightgray}8$ | Divide: ${\color{red}20} \div 6 = {\color{blue}3}\,\text{R}\,2$Write over hundreds: $\color{blue}3$Multiply: $6 \times {\color{blue}3} = 18$Subtract: ${\color{red}20} - 18 = 2$Bring down: $8$ |
| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the tens:

| $3$$\color{blue}4$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$8$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$\color{red}2$$\color{red}8$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$2$$4$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$4$$\color{lightgray}8$ | Divide: ${\color{red}28} \div 6 = {\color{blue}4}\,\text{R}\,4$Write over tens: $\color{blue}4$Multiply: $6 \times {\color{blue}4} = 24$Subtract: ${\color{red}28} - 24 = 4$Bring down: $8$ |
| --- | ---: |

Finally, we consider the ones:

| $3$$4$$\color{blue}8$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{2 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 8}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$1$$8$$2$$8$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$2$$4$$\color{red}4$$\color{red}8$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$4$$8$$\fbox{0}$ | Divide: ${\color{red}48} \div 6 = {\color{blue}8}$Write over ones: $\color{blue}8$Multiply: $6 \times {\color{blue}8} = 48$Subtract: ${\color{red}48} - 48 = \fbox{0}$ |
| --- | ---: |

We've gone through all the digits in the number $2,088$, so the division is done. The number we have written at the top is the answer. There is no remainder.

Therefore,

$$
2,088 \div 6 = 348
$$

So, the rocket covered $348$ miles every minute.

## Dividing With a Remainder

**Example:** What are the quotient and the remainder of dividing $3,634$ by $4$?

**Explanation**

We start by writing down the question using long division notation:

| $4$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$ |  |
| --- | ---: |

Notice that $\color{red}3$ (the number of thousands in ${\color{red}3},634$) is less than the divisor $4$. Therefore, we should consider both the thousands and hundreds:

| $\color{blue}9$$4$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{{\color{red}3} {\:\phantom{\mid}} {\color{red}6} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$6$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$\color{lightgray}3$ | Divide: ${\color{red}36} \div 4 = {\color{blue}9}$Write over hundreds: $\color{blue}9$Multiply: $4 \times {\color{blue}9} = 36$Subtract: ${\color{red}36} - 36 = 0$Bring down: $3$ |
| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the tens:

| $9$$\color{blue}0$$4$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$6$$0$$\color{red}3$ | Divide: ${\color{red}3} \div 4 = {\color{blue}0}\,\text{R}\,3$Write over tens: $\color{blue}0$ |
| --- | ---: |

Notice that $\color{red}3$ is less than the divisor $4$ but not all numbers of the dividend have yet been used. Therefore, we need to bring down one more digit:

| $9$$0$$4$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$6$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$3$$\color{lightgray}4$ | Bring down: $4$ |
| --- | ---: |

Finally, we consider the ones:

| $9$$0$$\color{blue}8$$4$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$6$$0$$\color{red}3$$\color{red}4$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$3$$2$$\fbox{2}$ | Divide: ${\color{red}34} \div 4 = {\color{blue}8}\,\text{R}\,\,2$Write over ones: $\color{blue}8$Multiply: $4 \times {\color{blue}8} = 32$Subtract: ${\color{red}34} - 32 = \fbox{2}$ |
| --- | ---: |

We've gone through all the digits in the number $3,634$, so the division is done. However, we still have a remainder of $2$, so we include this in our final answer.

Therefore, when we divide $3,634$ by $4$,

- the quotient is $908$, and
- the remainder is $2$.

## Bringing Down More Than One Digit at a Time

**Example:** Find the value of $6,096 \div 6$.

**Explanation**

We start by writing down the question using long division notation:

| $6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$ |  |
| --- | ---: |

First, we consider the thousands:

| $\color{blue}1$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{{\color{red}6} {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$\color{lightgray}0$ | Divide: ${\color{red}6} \div 6 = {\color{blue}1}$Write over thousands: $\color{blue}1$Multiply: $6 \times {\color{blue}1} = 6$Subtract: ${\color{red}6} - 6 = 0$Bring down: $0$ |
| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the hundreds:

| $1$$\color{blue}0$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$0$$\color{red}0$ | Divide: ${\color{red}0} \div 6 = {\color{blue}0}$Write over hundreds: $\color{blue}0$ |
| --- | ---: |

Notice that $\color{red}0$ is less than the divisor $6$ but not all numbers of the dividend have yet been used. Therefore, we need to bring down one more digit:

| $1$$0$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$0$$\color{lightgray}9$ | Bring down: $9$ |
| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the tens:

| $1$$0$$\color{blue}1$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$0$$0$$\color{red}9$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$-\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$\color{lightgray}\downarrow$$3$$\color{lightgray}6$ | Divide: ${\color{red}9} \div 6 = {\color{blue}1}\,\text{R}\,3$Write over ones: $\color{blue}1$Multiply: $6 \times {\color{blue}1} = 6$Subtract: ${\color{red}9} - 6 = \fbox{3}$Bring down: $6$ |
| --- | ---: |

Finally, we consider the ones:

| $1$$0$$1$$\color{blue}6$$6$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 9 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$6$$0$$0$$9$$-\!\!\!\!$$6$$\color{red}3$$\color{red}6$$-\!\!\!\!$$3$$6$$\fbox{0}$ | Divide: ${\color{red}36} \div 6 = {\color{blue}6}$Write over ones: $\color{blue}6$Multiply: $6 \times {\color{blue}6} = 36$Subtract: ${\color{red}36} - 36 = \fbox{0}$ |
| --- | ---: |

We've gone through all the digits in the number $6,096$ so the division is done. The number we have written at the top is the answer. There is no remainder.

Therefore,

$$
6,096 \div 6 = 1,016
$$

## Bringing Down More Than Two Digits at a Time

**Explanation**

We start by writing down the question using long division notation:

| $8$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7}$ |  |

| $1$$\color{blue}0$$8$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$8$$0$$\color{red}0$ | Divide: ${\color{red}0} \div 8 = {\color{blue}0}$Write over hundreds: $\color{blue}0$ |
| --- | ---: |

Notice that $\color{red}0$ is less than the divisor $8$ but not all numbers of the dividend have yet been used. Therefore, we need to bring down one more digit:

| --- | ---: |

Next, we consider the tens:

| $1$$0$$\color{blue}0$$8$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$$8$$0$$0$$\color{red}7$ | Divide: ${\color{red}7} \div 8 = {\color{blue}0}\,\text{R}\,7$Write over hundreds: $\color{blue}0$ |
