## Introduction

We can compare two patterns by comparing the corresponding terms.

For example, consider patterns A and B, shown in the table below.

| Pattern A: add $2$ | $\color{blue}0$ | $\color{blue}2$ | $\color{blue}4$ | $\color{blue}6$ | $\color{blue}8$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pattern B: add $4$ | $\color{red}0$ | $\color{red}4$ | $\color{red}8$ | $\color{red}12$ | $\color{red}16$ |

Note that we can multiply each term in pattern A by $2$ to get the corresponding term in pattern B:

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 \cdot 2 &= 0 \\
2 \cdot 2 &= 4 \\
4 \cdot 2 &= 8 \\
6 \cdot 2 &= 12 \\
8 \cdot 2 &= 16
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we can state the following:

$\qquad$ *"Each term in pattern B is **$2$ times larger** than the corresponding term in pattern A."*

<a id="forming-a-statement-of-comparison-given-two-patterns"></a>
## Forming a Statement of Comparison Given Two Patterns

**Example:** Consider patterns A and B, shown in the table below.

| Pattern A: subtract $2$ | $12$ | $10$ | $8$ | $6$ | $4$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pattern B: subtract $6$ | $36$ | $30$ | $24$ | $18$ | $12$ |

What is missing in the following sentence?

- Each term in pattern A is $\underline{\phantom{{}^{000000000000000000000}}}$ than the corresponding term in pattern B.

**Explanation**

Note that we can divide each term in pattern B by $3$ to get the corresponding term in pattern A:

$$
\begin{aligned}
36 \div 3 &= 12 \\
30 \div 3 &= 10 \\
24 \div 3 &= 8 \\
18 \div 3 &= 6 \\
12 \div 3 &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct statement is:

- Each term in pattern A is **$3$ times smaller** than the corresponding term in pattern B.

| Pattern A: add $3$ | $0$ |  |  |  |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pattern B: add $18$ | $0$ |  |  |  |

By finding the missing numbers in each pattern, complete the following sentence:

- Each term in pattern A is $\underline{\phantom{{}^{000000000000000000000}}}$ than the corresponding term in pattern B.

**Explanation**

First, we compute the missing numbers in the two patterns.

- The rule of pattern A is "add $3$", and the first number is $0$. So, we have the following pattern:![](<../Source/8.3.2. Comparing Patterns/Images/940dc7996210b3d08e4be2da704ed9ca.png>)

So we can fill in the table:

| Pattern A: add $3$ | $0$ | $3$ | $6$ | $9$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pattern B: add $18$ | $0$ | $18$ | $36$ | $54$ |

$$
\begin{aligned}

- Each term in pattern A is **$6$ times smaller** than the corresponding term in pattern B.

## A Quicker Comparison

When the two patterns both begin with $0$, we can compare them using just their rules.

So, say we know that:

- *Both patterns start from zero*
- *The rule for pattern A is "add $10$".*
- *The rule for pattern B is "add $5$".*

We can make comparison statements without computing any terms in either pattern.

Pattern A is "add $\color{red}10$" and pattern B is "add ${\color{blue}5}$." Therefore, pattern A is growing faster, by a factor of

$$
{\color{red}{10}}\div {\color{blue}{5}} = 2 \,
$$

Consequently, we have two equivalent statements:

- The terms of pattern A are $2$ times larger than the corresponding terms in pattern B.
- The terms of pattern B are $2$ times smaller than the corresponding terms in pattern A.

## Forming a Statement of Comparison Without Computing Missing Terms

**Example:** Stephanie wants to find the relationship between the terms in patterns A and B below. She wants to do this without calculating all of the values.

| Pattern A: add $7$ | $0$ |  |  |  |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pattern B: add $21$ | $0$ |  |  |  |

She writes the following down in her notebook.

- *Both patterns start from zero*
- *The rule for pattern A is "add $7$".*
- *The rule for pattern B is "add $21$".*
- *Therefore, the terms in pattern B are $\underline{\phantom{{}^{0000000000000}}}$ times larger than the corresponding terms in pattern A.*

What should be inserted into the blank space to make Stephanie's answer correct?

**Explanation**

Pattern A is "add $\color{red}7$" and pattern B is "add ${\color{blue}21}$." Therefore, pattern B is growing faster, by a factor of

$$
{\color{blue}{21}}\div {\color{red}{7}} =3 \,
$$

So the final point should be:

- Therefore, the terms in pattern B are $3$ times larger than the corresponding terms in pattern A.
