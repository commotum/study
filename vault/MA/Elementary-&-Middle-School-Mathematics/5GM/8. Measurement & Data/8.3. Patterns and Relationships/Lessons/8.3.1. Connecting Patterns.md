## Introduction

Let's find the next number in the pattern below.
$2,\quad 3,\quad 6,\quad 11,\quad 18,\quad 27$

First, we need to find the rule. We begin by inspecting what is happening at each step:

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/ddc7d191b9fae3ba696d7aa4fe26184d.png>)

In this case, the rule has its own rule!

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/72a18b6bf697c17be8a31c216782e34a.png>)

The amount we add starts at $1$ and increases by $2$ on each step.

So, to find the number after $27$, we find the size of the next step by adding

$$
9+2=11
$$

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/32108e9780ea7e64744dfc15b074a585.png>)

Therefore, the next number is

$$
27+11=38 \,
$$

## Identifying Patterns Within Patterns

**Example:** What is the next number in the sequence below?
$40,\, 35,\,35,\,30,\,30,\,25$

**Explanation**

We begin by inspecting what is happening at each step:

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/415794c8c92bbbacad8f7efec2c7b4a6.png>)

We see that on the odd-numbered steps, we should subtract $5$, while on the even-numbered steps, we should subtract $0$.

So, to find the number after $25$, we should subtract $0$.

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/3a9f531acde6ce06521e2a9e25202554.png>)

Therefore, the next number is
$25-0=25$.

## Identifying Patterns Within Patterns: Word Problems

**Example:** Daphne started a bake sale at her school. She sold $5$ cakes on the first day, $9$ on the second day, $15$ on the third day, and $23$ on the fourth day. How many cakes will Daphne sell on the fifth day if the same pattern continues?

**Explanation**

We have the following pattern:
$5,\,9,\,15,\,23$

Let's find out how much we should add to each element to get the next one.

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/73db662c475b5dbecf56595125914d89.png>)

The amount we add starts at $4$ and increases by $2$ on each step.

So, to find the number after $23$, we should add

$$
8+2=10
$$

![](<../Source/8.3.1. Connecting Patterns/Images/4c87db387290c858392f02be9e99b823.png>)

Therefore, the next number is

$$
23+10=33 \,
$$

We conclude that Daphne will sell $33$ cakes on the fifth day.

## Generating a Pattern Given Its Relationship With Another Pattern

**Example:** Each term in pattern B is $2$ larger than the corresponding term in pattern A. Pattern A is given below.

$3,\, 5,\,9,\,17$

What is pattern B?

**Explanation**

To find pattern B, we add $2$ to each term of pattern A:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3 + 2 &= 5 \\
5 + 2 &= 7 \\
9 + 2 &= 11 \\
17 + 2 &= 19
\end{aligned}
$$

So, pattern B is given by

$$
5,\, 7,\,11,\,19
$$
