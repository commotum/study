## Introduction

To multiply a decimal by a multi-digit whole number, such as $28 \times 6.2$, we can use the usual method of removing and replacing the decimal point.

**Step 1.** First, ignore the decimal point and multiply as if both numbers were whole numbers:

$$
[math]\color{blue}4[/math]; [math]\color{blue}1[/math]; 2\begin{vmatrix}8 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 6\end{vmatrix}.2; 5\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ +\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}6 & 8 & 0 \\ 1 & 7 & 3\end{vmatrix}6
$$

**Step 2.** Then, we insert the decimal point to have the same number of decimal places as the decimal factor.

Here, there is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $6.2$, so the product will also have ${\color{blue}{1}}$ decimal place. We take our value of $1736$ and insert a decimal point to make a number with $\color{blue}1$ decimal place:

$$
1
$$

Therefore,

$$
28 \times 6.2 = 173.6 \,
$$

## Multiplying a Decimal With One Decimal Place by a Two-Digit Whole Number

**Example:** Find the value of $9.4 \times 23$.

**Explanation**

First, we ignore the decimal point and multiply as if both numbers were whole numbers:

$$
[math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; [math]\color{blue}1[/math]; 9\begin{vmatrix}.4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 8 & 2 \\ + & 1 & 8\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 6 \mid 2
$$

We now count the total number of decimal places in the two factors.

There is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $9.4$, so the product will also have ${\color{blue}{1}}$ decimal place.

So, we take our value of $2162$ and insert a decimal point to make a number with $1$ decimal place:

$$
1
$$

Therefore,

$$
94 \times 2.3 = 216.2 \,
$$

## Multiplying a Decimal With One Decimal Place by a Three-Digit Whole Number

**Example:** What is $154$ multiplied by $3.9$?

**Explanation**

First, we ignore the decimal point and multiply as if both numbers were whole numbers:

$$
[math]\color{blue}1[/math]; [math]\color{blue}4[/math]\begin{vmatrix}[math]\color{blue}1[/math] \\ [math]\color{blue}3[/math] \\ 1\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 & 3 &.9 \\ 1 & 3 & 8 & 6 \\ + & 4 & 6 & 2\end{vmatrix}0; 6\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0\end{vmatrix}6
$$

We now count the total number of decimal places in the two factors.

There is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $3.9$, so the product will also have ${\color{blue}{1}}$ decimal place.

So, we take our value of $6006$ and insert a decimal point to make a number with $1$ decimal place:

$$
1
$$

Therefore,

$$
154 \times 3.9 = 600.6 \,
$$

## Multiplying a Decimal With Two Decimal Places by a Three-Digit Whole Number

**Example:** What is $7.09$ multiplied by $237$?

**Explanation**

First, we ignore the decimal point and multiply as if both numbers were whole numbers:

$$
[math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]; [math]\color{blue}\phantom{0}[/math]\begin{vmatrix}[math]\color{blue}1[/math] \\ [math]\color{blue}2[/math] \\ [math]\color{blue}6[/math] \\ 7\end{vmatrix}.0\begin{vmatrix}9 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 & 2 & 3\end{vmatrix}7; 4\begin{vmatrix}9 & 6 & 3 \\ + & 2 & 1\end{vmatrix}2\begin{vmatrix}7 & 0 \\ + & 1\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}1 & 8 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 6 & 8 & 0\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 3
$$

We now count the total number of decimal places in the two factors.

There are $\color{blue}2$ decimal places in $7.09$, so the product will also have ${\color{blue}{2}}$ decimal places.

So, we take our value of $168033$ and insert a decimal point to make a number with $\color{blue}2$ decimal places:

$$
2
$$

Therefore,

$$
7.09 \times 237 = 1680.33 \,
$$

## Multiplying a Decimal by a Whole Number: Word Problems

**Example:** A roll of adhesive tape is $7.50$ meters long. How many meters of adhesive tape are in $136$ rolls?

**Explanation**

First, notice that $7.50=7.5$.

To find out how many meters of adhesive tape are in $136$ rolls, we need to multiply $136$ by $7.5$.

First, we ignore the decimal point and multiply as if both numbers were whole numbers:

$$
[math]\color{blue}2[/math]; [math]\color{blue}1[/math]\begin{vmatrix}[math]\color{blue}4[/math] \\ [math]\color{blue}3[/math] \\ 1\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 0 & 7\end{vmatrix}.5; 6\begin{vmatrix}8 & 0 \\ + & 9\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}2 & 0 \\ 1 & 0\end{vmatrix}2 \mid 0 \mid 0
$$

We now count the total number of decimal places in the two factors.

There is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $7.5$, so the product will also have ${\color{blue}{1}}$ decimal place.

So, we take our value of $10200$ and insert a decimal point to make a number with $\color{blue}1$ decimal place:

$$
1
$$

So,

$$
136 \times 7.5 = 1,020.0 \,
$$

Therefore, $136$ rolls of adhesive tape contain $1,020$ meters of tape.
