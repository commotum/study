## Introduction

We can also use the standard algorithm for division to divide four and five-digit decimals by whole numbers.

For instance, to find $50.04 \div 12$, we start by writing down the problem using long division notation. We keep the decimal point in the dividend:

| $12$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\,\bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}\,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$ |
| --- |

We also bring the decimal point up into the quotient, placing it directly above the dot in the dividend:

| $\:\! \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} \phantom{0} \bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}$$12$$\! \require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\,\bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}\,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$ |
| --- |

Now, сarrying out the long division process as usual, we get:

| $4$$\!\!. 1$$7$$12$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{5 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0 {\,. \,} 0 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 4}$$-\!\!\!\!\!\!$$4$$8$$2$$0$$-\!\!\!\!$$1$$2$$8$$4$$-\!\!\!\!$$8$$4$$0$ |
| --- |

Therefore,

$$
50.04 \div 12 = 4.17\,
$$

## Dividing Decimals By Decimals Using the Standard Algorithm

To use the standard algorithm, the divisor must be a whole number.

So, to divide decimals by other decimals such as $0.832 \div 0.13$, we must find an equivalent division problem by multiplying both the dividend and divisor by an appropriate power of ten.

First, we write the division problem $0.832 \div 0.13$ as a fraction:

$$
\dfrac{0.832}{0.13}
$$

Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $100$, we get a whole number in the denominator:

$$
\dfrac{0.832 \times 100}{0.13 \times 100} = \dfrac{83.2}{13}
$$

So the problem is equivalent to $83.2 \div 13$.

Next, we write down the problem using long division notation, where we keep the decimal point in the dividend:

| $13$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\,\bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}\,} 2}$ |
| --- |

We also bring the decimal point up into the quotient, placing it directly above the dot in the dividend:

| $\:\:\: \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} \bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}$$13$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\,\bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}\,} 2}$ |
| --- |

Now, сarrying out the long division process as usual, we get:

| $6$$\!\!\!. 4$$13$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{8 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\,. \,} 2}$$-\!\!\!\!$$7$$8$$5$$2$$-\!\!\!\!$$5$$2$$\phantom{0}$$0$ |
| --- |

Therefore,

$$
0.832 \div 0.13 = 6.4\,
$$

## Dividing Decimals by Decimals

**Example:** What is the value of $73.71 \div 5.4$?

**Explanation**

We can write this division problem as a fraction:

$$
\dfrac{73.71}{5.4}
$$

Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $10$, we get a whole number in the denominator:

$$
\dfrac{73.71 \times 10}{5.4 \times 10} = \dfrac{737.1}{54}
$$

So the problem is equivalent to $737.1 \div 54$.

Next, we write down the problem using long division notation, where we keep the decimal point in the dividend:

| $54$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\,\bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}\,} 1}$ |
| --- |

We also bring the decimal point up into the quotient, placing it directly above the dot in the dividend:

| $\:\,\! \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid \mid}} \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}}\phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} \phantom{0} {\:\phantom{\mid}} \bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}$$54$$\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\,\bbox[2px, lightgray]{\color{blue}.}\,} 1}$ |
| --- |

Now, сarrying out the long division process as usual, we get:

| $1$$3$$\!\!\!. 6$$5$$54$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\,. \,} 1 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 0}$$-\!\!\!\!$$5$$4$$1$$9$$7$$-\!\!\!\!$$1$$6$$2$$3$$5$$1$$-\!\!\!\!$$3$$2$$4$$2$$7$$0$$-\!\!\!\!$$2$$7$$0$$0$ |

## Dividing Decimals by Decimals: Word Problems

**Example:** A pump delivered $367.5$ liters of water in $3.5$ hours. How many liters of water would the pump deliver every hour if it worked at a steady pace?

**Explanation**

To determine the number of liters of water the pump would deliver in $1$ hour, we need to calculate $367.5\div 3.5$.

We can write this division problem as a fraction:

$$
\dfrac{367.5}{3.5}
$$

Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $10$, we get a whole number in the denominator:

$$
\dfrac{367.5 \times 10}{3.5 \times 10} = \dfrac{3,675}{35}
$$

So the problem is equivalent to $3,675 \div 35$.

Next, we proceed to use long division:

| $1$$0$$5$$35\,$$\!\!\require{enclose}\enclose{longdiv}{3 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 6 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 7 {\:\phantom{\mid}} 5}$$-\!\!\!\!$$3$$5$$1$$7$$-\!\!\!\!$$0$$0$$1$$7$$5$$-\!\!\!\!$$1$$7$$5$$0$ |
| --- |

So,

$$
367.5\div 3.5 = 105
$$

We conclude that the pump would deliver $105$ liters of water every hour.
