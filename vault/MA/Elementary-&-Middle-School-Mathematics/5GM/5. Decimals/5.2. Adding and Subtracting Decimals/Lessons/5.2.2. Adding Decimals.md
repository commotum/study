## Introduction

Adding decimals is no harder than adding whole numbers.

For example, let's find $0.4 + 2.6$.

First, we write one number above the other, lining up the decimal points and placing a decimal point below the others in our answer:

$$
0\begin{vmatrix}. & 4 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}. \mid 6;
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers. After each addition, we write the ones place and carry the tens place of the result.

$$
1; 0\begin{vmatrix}. & 4 \\ + & 2\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}6 \\ 3\end{vmatrix}. \mid 0
$$

Therefore,

$$
0.4 + 2.6 = 3
$$

## Adding Two-Digit and Three-Digit Decimals

**Example:** What is $0.94 + 8.32$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
0\begin{vmatrix}. & 9 & 4 \\ + & 8 &.\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 2;
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
1; 0\begin{vmatrix}. & 9 & 4 \\ + & 8 &.\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 9\end{vmatrix}. \mid 2 \mid 6
$$

Therefore,

$$
0.94 + 8.32 = 9.26
$$

## Adding Decimals Containing Different Numbers of Whole Digits

**Example:** What is $2.43 + 50.41$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}. & 4 & 3 \\ + & 5 & 0\end{vmatrix}. \mid 4 \mid 1;
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}. & 4 & 3 \\ + & 5 & 0\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 5 & 2\end{vmatrix}. \mid 8 \mid 4
$$

Therefore,

$$
2.43 + 50.41 = 52.84
$$

## Adding Four-Digit, Five-Digit and Six-Digit Decimals

**Example:** What is $982.97 + 306.74$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
9\begin{vmatrix}8 & 2 &. & 9 & 7 \\ + & 3 & 0 & 6 &.\end{vmatrix}7 \mid 4;
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ 9\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}2 &. & 9 & 7 \\ + & 3 & 0 & 6\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}7 & 4 \\ 1 & 2\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}9 &. & 7\end{vmatrix}1
$$

Therefore, $1,289.71$.

<a id="word-problems"></a>
## Word Problems

**Example:** Brandon stirs $4.76$ liters of milk with $2.82$ liters of water in a pot. How much liquid is in the pot?

**Explanation**

To find how much liquid is in the pot, we need to calculate $4.76 + 2.82$.

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}. & 7 & 6 \\ + & 2 &.\end{vmatrix}8 \mid 2;
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
1; 4\begin{vmatrix}. & 7 & 6 \\ + & 2 &.\end{vmatrix}8\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ 7\end{vmatrix}. \mid 5 \mid 8
$$

Therefore, there are $7.58$ liters of liquid in the pot.
