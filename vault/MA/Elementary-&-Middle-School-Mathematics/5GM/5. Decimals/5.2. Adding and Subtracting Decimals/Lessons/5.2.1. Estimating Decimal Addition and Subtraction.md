## Introduction

We can find a good estimate for the sum of two decimals by first rounding each decimal to the nearest whole number.

To illustrate, let's find an estimate for the value of $16.6+1.3$.

First, we round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$16.6\begin{vmatrix}+ & 1.3 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 17 & +\end{vmatrix}1$

Then, we calculate the sum:

$$
{\color{blue}17} + {\color{blue}1} = 18
$$

Therefore, $16.6+1.3$ is about $18$.

The exact answer is $17.9$, so our estimate is pretty close.

<a id="estimating-decimal-addition"></a>
## Estimating Decimal Addition

**Example:** Estimate $15.123 + 8.8$ by rounding each number to the nearest whole number.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$15.123\begin{vmatrix}+ & 8.8 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 15 & +\end{vmatrix}9$

Next, we calculate the sum:

$$
{\color{blue}15} + {\color{blue}9} = 24
$$

Therefore, $15.123 + 8.8$ is about $24$.

## Estimating Decimal Addition: Word Problems

**Example:** Joe cuts a piece of wire into two lengths. One part is $8.15$ meters long, and the other part is $7.75$ meters long. By rounding each number to the nearest whole number, estimate the length of the wire before Joe cut it.

**Explanation**

To estimate the initial length of the wire, we need to estimate the sum $8.15+ 7.75$.

First, we round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$8.15\begin{vmatrix}+ & 7.75 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 8 & +\end{vmatrix}8$

Next, we calculate the sum:

$$
{\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}8} = 16
$$

Therefore, the length of the wire was approximately $16$ meters before Joe cut it.

## Estimating Decimal Subtraction

We also use rounding to estimate the difference of two decimals. To illustrate, let's find an estimate of $25.85- 12.32$.

First, we round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$25.85\begin{vmatrix}- & 12.32 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 26 & -\end{vmatrix}12$

Then, we calculate the difference:

$$
{\color{blue}26} - {\color{blue}12} = 14
$$

Therefore, $25.85-12.32$ is about $14$.

**Example:** By rounding each number to the nearest whole number, estimate the value of $55.1 - 24.76$.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$55.1\begin{vmatrix}- & 24.76 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 55 & -\end{vmatrix}25$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
{\color{blue}55} - {\color{blue}25} = 30

Therefore, $55.1 - 24.76$ is approximately $30$.

## Estimating Decimal Subtraction: Word Problems

**Example:** A meteorologist measures the total amount of rainfall that two cities, city $A$ and city $B$, received in a week. He found that both cities received a combined total of $14.7$ inches of rain. If city $A$ received $5.745$ inches of rain, then by rounding each number to the nearest whole number, estimate the amount of rainfall that city $B$ received.

**Explanation**

To estimate the total amount of rain that city $B$ received, we need to estimate the difference $14.7 - 5.745$.

First, we round both numbers to the nearest whole number:
$14.7\begin{vmatrix}- & 5.745 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 15 & -\end{vmatrix}6$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
{\color{blue}15} - {\color{blue}6} = 9
$$

Therefore, city $B$ received approximately $9$ inches of rainfall during the week.
