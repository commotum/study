## Introduction

We can use benchmarks to estimate decimal subtraction.

For example, suppose we want to estimate the following difference using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$ or $0.5{:}$

$$
12.53 - 7.17
$$

We first round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:

$$
12.53\begin{vmatrix}- & 7.17 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 12.5 & -\end{vmatrix}7.0
$$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}2 &. & 5 \\ - & 7 &.\end{vmatrix}0; 5 \mid. \mid 5
$$

Therefore, $12.53 - 7.17$ is approximately $5.5$, which we can write as follows:

$$
12.53 - 7.17 \approx 5.5
$$

## Estimating Decimal Subtraction

**Example:** Estimate the difference $7.6 - 2.18$ using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$ or $0.5$.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:
$7.6\begin{vmatrix}- & 2.18 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 7.5 & -\end{vmatrix}2.0$

Next, we calculate the difference:
$7\begin{vmatrix}. & 5 \\ - & 2\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}. \mid 5$

Therefore, $7.6 - 2.18$ is approximately $5.5$.

## Estimating Decimal Subtraction: Borrowing Once

**Example:** Estimate the difference $9.17 - 5.4$ using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$ or $0.5$.

**Explanation**

First, we round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:
$9.17\begin{vmatrix}- & 5.4 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 9.0 & -\end{vmatrix}5.5$

Next, we calculate the difference:
$8\begin{vmatrix}10 \\ 9\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ -\end{vmatrix}5\begin{vmatrix}. & 5 \\ 3 &.\end{vmatrix}5$

Therefore, $9.17 - 5.4$ is approximately $3.5$.

## Word Problems

**Example:** Three years ago, Rick was $4.43$ feet tall. He's now $5.11$ feet tall. Using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$, or $0.5$, estimate how much Rick grew during this period.

**Explanation**

To estimate how much Rick has grown during this period, we have to estimate the difference $5.11-4.43$.

First, we round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:
$5.11\begin{vmatrix}- & 4.43 \\ ↓ & ↓ \\ 5.0 & -\end{vmatrix}4.5$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}10 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ -\end{vmatrix}4\begin{vmatrix}. & 5 \\ 0 &.\end{vmatrix}5
$$

Therefore, Rick grew by approximately $0.5$ feet during this period.
