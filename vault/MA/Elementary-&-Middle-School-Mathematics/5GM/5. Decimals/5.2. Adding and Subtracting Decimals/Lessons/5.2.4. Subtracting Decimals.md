## Introduction

Decimal subtraction works in a similar way to whole number subtraction.

As an example, let's find the value of $0.8 - 0.5$.

First, we write one number above the other, lining up the decimal points and placing a decimal point below the others in our answer:

$$
0\begin{vmatrix}. & 8 \\ - & 0\end{vmatrix}. \mid 5;
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
0\begin{vmatrix}. & 8 \\ - & 0\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ 0\end{vmatrix}. \mid 3
$$

Therefore:

$$
0.8 - 0.5= 0.3
$$

This is just like $8-5=3$, but with the decimal point in a different position.

<a id="subtracting-decimals"></a>
## Subtracting Decimals

**Example:** Find the difference $8.17 - 0.14$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}. & 1 & 7 \\ - & 0 &.\end{vmatrix}1 \mid 4;
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
8\begin{vmatrix}. & 1 & 7 \\ - & 0 &.\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}4 \\ 8\end{vmatrix}. \mid 0 \mid 3
$$

Therefore:

$$
8.17 - 0.14 = 8.03
$$

## Subtracting Decimals: Borrowing Once

**Example:** What is $5.7 - 3.9$?

**Explanation**

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
5\begin{vmatrix}. & 7 \\ - & 3\end{vmatrix}. \mid 9;
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
4\begin{vmatrix}17 \\ 5\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}7 \\ -\end{vmatrix}3\begin{vmatrix}. & 9 \\ 1 &.\end{vmatrix}8
$$

Therefore:

$$
5.7 - 3.9 = 1.8
$$

## Subtracting Decimals: Borrowing More Than Once

**Example:** Find the value of $7.362 - 0.634$.

**Explanation**

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
7\begin{vmatrix}. & 3 & 6 & 2 \\ - & 0 &. & 6\end{vmatrix}3 \mid 4;
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
6\begin{vmatrix}13 & 5 & 12 \\ 7 &. & 3\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ -\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}. & 6 & 3 & 4 \\ 6 &. & 7 & 2\end{vmatrix}8
$$

Therefore:

$$
7.362 - 0.634 = 6.728
$$

## Subtracting Decimals: Word Problems

**Example:** A consignment of strawberries in a dispatch van weighs $208.5$ pounds. The van must deliver the load to two stores. If it unloads $124.7$ pounds of those strawberries at the first store, how much will it unload at the second store?

**Explanation**

To find out how much of the strawberries the van will unload at the second store, we need to calculate
$208.5-124.7 \,$.

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}0 & 8 &. & 5 \\ - & 1 & 2 & 4\end{vmatrix}. \mid 7;
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$
1\begin{vmatrix}10 & 7 & 15 \\ 2 & 0 & 8\end{vmatrix}.\begin{vmatrix}5 \\ -\end{vmatrix}1\begin{vmatrix}2 & 4 &.\end{vmatrix}7; 8\begin{vmatrix}3 &.\end{vmatrix}8
$$

Therefore, the van will unload $83.8$ pounds of strawberries at the second store.
