## Introduction

Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are discrete random variables, where the conditional expected value of $X$ given $Y=y$ is given by $\mu_{X \mid y}$. In other words,

$$
\textrm{E}\begin{bmatrix}X\, & \,Y=y\end{bmatrix} = \mu_{X \mid y}
$$

The **conditional variance of $X$ given $Y=y$** is defined as

$$
\textrm{Var}\begin{bmatrix}X\, & \,Y=y\end{bmatrix} = \textrm{E}\big\begin{bmatrix}(X - \mu_{X \mid y} \big)^2 \, & \, Y=y\end{bmatrix}
$$

This is similar to the definition of $\textrm{Var}[X]$ in that it represents the average (squared) distance of the random variable $X$ from the mean. The difference here is that the expected values are now dependent on the outcome of $Y$.

Similarly to the case of $\textrm{Var}[X]$, this often isn't the easiest formula to use in practice. However, it can be shown that the definition of conditional variance is equivalent to

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix} &= E\begin{bmatrix}X^{2} & Y = y\end{bmatrix} - μ_{X\begin{vmatrix}y}^{2} \\ = [∑_(x)x^{2}f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y)] - μ_{X\end{vmatrix}y}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

where $f_{X\mid Y}(x \, \mid \, y)$ is the conditional PMF of $X$ given $Y$. Note that these formulas are analogous to those for $\textrm{Var}[X]$.

Finally, we have an analogous definition for the conditional variance of $Y$ given $X=x$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = x\end{bmatrix} &= E\begin{bmatrix}(Y - μ_{Y \mid x})^{2} & X = x\end{bmatrix} \\
&= E\begin{bmatrix}Y^{2} & X = x\end{bmatrix} - μ_{Y\begin{vmatrix}x}^{2} \\ = [∑_(y)y^{2}f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)] - μ_{Y\end{vmatrix}x}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating a Conditional Variance Given a Conditional Mass Function

**Example:** Suppose $X$ and $Y$ are discrete random variables. Find $\textrm{Var}\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X=3\big\end{bmatrix}$ given that the conditional probability mass function $f_{Y\mid X}(y \, \mid \, 3)$ is shown in the table above and the corresponding conditional expected value is $\mu_{Y \mid 3} = \dfrac{8}{3}$.

| $y$ | $1$ | $2$ | $4$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $f_{Y\mid X}(y \, \mid \, 3)$ | $\dfrac{1}{3}$ | $\dfrac{1}{6}$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ |

**Explanation**

Recall that the conditional variance of $Y$ given $X=x$ is defined by

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = x\end{bmatrix} &= E\begin{bmatrix}(Y - μ_{Y \mid x})^{2} & X = x\end{bmatrix} \\
&= E\begin{bmatrix}Y^{2} & X = x\end{bmatrix} - μ_{Y\begin{vmatrix}x}^{2} \\ = [∑_(y)y^{2}f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)] - μ_{Y\end{vmatrix}x}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = 3\end{bmatrix} &= [∑_(y)y^{2}f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid 3)] - μ_{Y \mid 3}^{2} \\
&= [(1)^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3} + (2)^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{6} + (4)^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}] - (\frac{8}{3})^{2} \\
&= \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3} + 8 - \frac{64}{9} \\
&= \frac{17}{9}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating a Conditional Variance Using Row Totals

**Example:** Suppose $X$ and $Y$ are discrete random variables. Find $\textrm{Var}\big\begin{bmatrix}Y \, & \, X=1\big\end{bmatrix}$ given that $X$ and $Y$ have the joint probability mass function $f(x,y)$ shown in the table above and the corresponding conditional expected value is $\mu_{Y \mid 1} = \dfrac{9}{4}$.

| $f$ | $Y=1$ | $Y=2$ | $Y=3$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $X=1$ | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{5}$ |
| $X=2$ | $\dfrac{3}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{5}$ | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ |

**Explanation**

Recall that the conditional variance of $Y$ given $X=x$ is defined by

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = x\end{bmatrix} &= E\begin{bmatrix}(Y - μ_{Y \mid x})^{2} & X = x\end{bmatrix} \\
&= E\begin{bmatrix}Y^{2} & X = x\end{bmatrix} - μ_{Y\begin{vmatrix}x}^{2} \\ = [∑_(y)y^{2}f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)] - μ_{Y\end{vmatrix}x}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

In order to find $f_{Y \mid X}(y \, \mid \, x)$, let's first find the marginal distribution for $X$, corresponding to the row totals:

| $f$ | $Y=1$ | $Y=2$ | $Y=3$ | $f_X(x)$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $X=1$ | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{5}$ | $\dfrac{2}{5}$ |
| $X=2$ | $\dfrac{3}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{5}$ | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ | $\dfrac{3}{5}$ |

The conditional probability mass function of $Y$ given that $X=x$ is

$$
f_{Y \mid X}(y \, \mid \, x) = \dfrac{f(x,y)}{f_X(x)}
$$

Therefore, dividing each joint probability by the corresponding row total, we obtain the conditional probability mass function of $Y$ given that $X=x{:}$

| $f_{Y \mid X}(y \, \mid \, x)$ | $Y=1$ | $Y=2$ | $Y=3$ |  |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $X=1$ | $\dfrac{1}{4}$ | $\dfrac{1}{4}$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ | $1$ |
| $X=2$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ | $\dfrac{1}{3}$ | $\dfrac{1}{6}$ | $1$ |

Finally, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}Y & X = 1\end{bmatrix} &= [∑_(y)y^{2}f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid 1)] - μ_{Y \mid 1}^{2} \\
&= [1^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{4} + 2^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{4} + 3^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}] - (\frac{9}{4})^{2} \\
&= \frac{1}{4} + 1 + \frac{9}{2} - \frac{81}{16} \\
&= \frac{11}{16}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Calculating a Conditional Variance Using Column Totals

**Example:** Suppose $X$ and $Y$ are discrete random variables. Find $\textrm{Var}\big\begin{bmatrix}X \, & \, Y=2\big\end{bmatrix}$ given that the random variables $X$ and $Y$ have the joint probability mass function $f(x,y)$ shown in the table above and the corresponding conditional expected value is $\mu_{X \mid 2}=\dfrac{10}{9}$.

| $f$ | $Y=2$ | $Y=4$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $X=1$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ | $\dfrac{1}{4}$ |
| $X=2$ | $\dfrac{1}{16}$ | $\dfrac{3}{16}$ |

**Explanation**

Recall that the conditional variance of $X$ given $Y=y$ is defined by

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = y\end{bmatrix} &= E\begin{bmatrix}(X - μ_{X \mid y})^{2} & Y = y\end{bmatrix} \\
&= E\begin{bmatrix}X^{2} & Y = y\end{bmatrix} - μ_{X\begin{vmatrix}y}^{2} \\ = [∑_(x)x^{2}f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y)] - μ_{X\end{vmatrix}y}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

In order to find $f_{X \mid Y}(x \, \mid \, y)$, let's first find the marginal distribution of $Y$, corresponding to the column totals:

| $f$ | $Y=2$ | $Y=4$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $X=1$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ | $\dfrac{1}{4}$ |
| $X=2$ | $\dfrac{1}{16}$ | $\dfrac{3}{16}$ |
| $f_Y(y)$ | $\dfrac{9}{16}$ | $\dfrac{7}{16}$ |

The conditional probability mass function of $X$ given that $Y=y$ is

$$
f_{X \mid Y}(x \, \mid \, y) = \dfrac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}
$$

Therefore, dividing each joint probability by the corresponding column total, we obtain the conditional probability mass function of $X$ given that $Y=y{:}$

| $f_{X\mid Y}(x\, \mid \,y)$ | $Y=2$ | $Y=4$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $X=1$ | $\dfrac{8}{9}$ | $\dfrac{4}{7}$ |
| $X=2$ | $\dfrac{1}{9}$ | $\dfrac{3}{7}$ |
|  | $1$ | $1$ |

Finally, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
Var\begin{bmatrix}X & Y = 2\end{bmatrix} &= [∑_(x)x^{2}f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid 2)] - μ_{X \mid 2}^{2} \\
&= [1^{2} \cdot \frac{8}{9} + 2^{2} \cdot \frac{1}{9}] - (\frac{10}{9})^{2} \\
&= \frac{8}{9} + \frac{4}{9} - \frac{100}{81} \\
&= \frac{8}{81}
\end{aligned}
$$
