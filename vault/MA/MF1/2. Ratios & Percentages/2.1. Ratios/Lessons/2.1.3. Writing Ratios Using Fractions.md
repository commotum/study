## Introduction

We can write ratios as fractions by placing the first number over the second number.

So, what ratio describes the number of rectangles to triangles below?

![](<../Source/2.1.3. Writing Ratios Using Fractions/Images/52d7ed8acdb780645278f3f87580d816.png>)

First, we count the number of rectangles. We see that there are $\color{blue}4$ rectangles.

Then, we count the number of triangles. We see that there are $\color{red}3$ triangles.

So the ratio of rectangles to triangles is ${\color{blue}4}:{\color{red}3}$. As a fraction, this is

$$
\dfrac{\color{blue}4}{\color{red}3}
$$

We interpret this number to mean that there are

$$
\dfrac{\color{blue}4}{\color{red}3}
$$

rectangles for each triangle.

## Writing a Ratio as a Fraction

**Example:** What ratio describes the number of rhombuses to the number of triangles?

![](<../Source/2.1.3. Writing Ratios Using Fractions/Images/10d0563b3a6941e54f30a0056d97e28b.png>)

**Explanation**

First, we count the number of rhombuses. We see that there are $2$ rhombuses.

Then, we count the number of triangles. We see that there are $3$ triangles.

So the ratio of rhombuses to triangles is

$$
\dfrac{2}{3}
$$

## Completing a Statement Describing a Ratio as a Fraction

**Example:** What is missing in the following sentence?

> To make a fertilizer Esther mixed $2$ quarts of fertilizer concentrate and $3$ quarts of distilled water, so there are $0$ quarts of fertilizer concentrate for each quart of distilled water.

**Explanation**

The ratio of the number of quarts of fertilizer concentrate to the number of quarts of distilled water is ${\color{blue}{2}}:{\color{red}{3}}$.

We can rewrite this ratio as a fraction, as follows:

$$
\dfrac{{\color{blue}{2}}}{{\color{red}{3}}}
$$

This means that there is

$$
\dfrac{2}{3}
$$

quart of fertilizer concentrate for each quart of distilled water.

## Completing a Statement by Interpreting a Fraction as a Ratio

**Example:** From left to right, what are the missing numbers in the following sentence?

> A chef requires $\dfrac{1}{7}$ grams of chilies for each gram of turmeric for his recipe. Therefore, the recipe has a ratio of $0$ grams of chilies to $0$ grams of turmeric.

**Explanation**

Let's rewrite the fraction

$$
\dfrac{1}{7}
$$

as a ratio:

$$
1:7
$$

Therefore, the recipe has a ratio of $1$ gram of chilies to $7$ grams of turmeric.
