## Introduction

In a previous lesson, we saw how to apply a percentage decrease to a number to get a new, smaller number. In this lesson, we’ll work backwards, finding the *original value* when we know the percentage decrease and the new value.

For example, suppose that after a $5\%$ decrease, a conference registration fee is $\[math]228$. What was the original fee?

Decreasing a number by $5\%$ means that we must apply a new percentage of

$$
100\% - 5\% = 95\%
$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$
95\%=\frac{95}{100}=0.95
$$

Now, let the original fee be $x$. Then, after a $5\%$ decrease, the new value is

$$
0.95x
$$

We are told that the decreased value is $\[/math]228$. So, we set them equal and solve for $x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.95x &= 228 \\
x &= \frac{228}{0.95} \\
x &= 240
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the original fee was $\[math]240$.

We can check that this answer is correct by applying a $5\%$ decrease to $\[/math]240{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\[math]240 \cdot (100% - 5%) &= \[/math]240 \cdot 95% \\
&= \[math]240 \cdot 0.95 \\
&= \[/math]228✓
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding an Original Value Given a Percentage Decrease

**Example:** After decreasing a quantity $x$ by $16\%$, the result is $84$. What is the value of $x$?

**Explanation**

Decreasing a number by $16\%$ means that we must apply a percentage of

$$
100\% - 16\% = 84\%
$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$
84\% = \dfrac{84}{100} = 0.84
$$

We're told that by decreasing a number $x$ by $16\%$, we get the number $84$. So, we have the equation
$0.84x = 84$.

Now, we solve for $x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x &= \frac{84}{0.84} \\
&= 100
\end{aligned}
$$

So, we have $x = 100$.

## Finding an Original Value Given a Percentage Decrease: Word Problems

**Example:** After a $15\%$ discount, the price of a smartphone is $\[math]425$. What was the original price?

**Explanation**

Decreasing a number by $15\%$ means that we must apply a new percentage of

$$
100\% - 15\% = 85\%
$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$
85\% = \dfrac{85}{100} = 0.85
$$

Let the smartphone's original price be $x$. So, after a $15\%$ decrease, the new value is
$0.85x$.

We are told the discounted price is $\[math]425$. So, we have the equation

$$
0.85x = 425
$$

Now, we solve for $x$, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.85x &= 425 \\
x &= \frac{425}{0.85} \\
x &= 500
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the original price was $\[/math]500$.

## Finding a Total Decrease Given a Percentage Decrease

**Example:** A collector sold part of a marble collection. After $45\%$ of the original marbles were sold, $484$ marbles remained. How many marbles were sold?

**Explanation**

Decreasing a number by $45\%$ means that we must apply a new percentage of

$$
100\% - 45\% = 55\%
$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$
55\% = \dfrac{55}{100} = 0.55
$$

Let the original number of marbles be $x$. So, after a $45\%$ decrease, the new value is

$$
0.55x
$$

We are told that $484$ marbles remained. So, we have the equation

$$
0.55x = 484
$$

Now, we solve for $x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.55x &= 484 \\
x &= \frac{484}{0.55} \\
x &= 880
\end{aligned}
$$

Now that we know the original number of marbles was $880$, we find the number removed by taking $45\%$ of $880{:}$

$$
\dfrac{45}{100} \cdot 880 = 396
$$

So, $396$ marbles were sold.
