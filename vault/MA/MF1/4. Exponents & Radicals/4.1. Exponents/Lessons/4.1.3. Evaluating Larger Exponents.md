## Introduction

To evaluate a large exponent, we first write out the power using multiplication signs as usual. Then, we calculate the product by splitting it into groups of two-digit multiplications, before calculating the resulting product.

For example, let's evaluate $2^4$.

First, we write
$4$

Since

$$
2 \times 2 = 4
$$

we have

$$
\underbrace{2 \times 2}_{\large \color{blue}4} \times \underbrace{2 \times 2}_{\large \color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}4} \times {\color{blue}4}
$$

Finally, we work out $4\times 4$.
Since

$$
4\times 4=16
$$

we conclude that $2^4=16$.

## Squaring Two-Digit Numbers

**Example:** Evaluate $31^2$.

**Explanation**

To evaluate $31^2$, we start by writing it out with a multiplication sign:
$2$

Then, we work out $31 \times 31$.

$$
3\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 3\end{vmatrix}1; 3\begin{vmatrix}1 \\ +\end{vmatrix}9\begin{vmatrix}3 & 0 \\ 9 & 6\end{vmatrix}1
$$

Therefore,

$$
31 \times 31 = 961
$$

## Squaring Numbers Ending in Zeros

**Example:** Evaluate $220^2$.

**Explanation**

To evaluate $220^2$, we start by writing it out with a multiplication sign:
$2$

Then, we work out $220 \times 220$.

First, we figure out the non-zero part and the block of zeros for both numbers:
$\begin{bmatrix}220 & ⟶220 \\ 220 & ⟶220\end{bmatrix}$

We can see that there are $2$ zeros in total.

Next, we multiply the non-zero parts:

$$
2\begin{vmatrix}2 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 4 & 4 \\ + & 4 & 4\end{vmatrix}0; 4 \mid 8 \mid 4
$$

Finally, we join the above with the block of zeros:

$$
{\color{red}484}{\color{blue}00}= 48,400
$$

Therefore,

$$
220^2 = 48,400
$$

## Raising Numbers to Larger Exponents

**Example:** Evaluate $7^4$.

**Explanation**

According to the definition,
$4$

Since

$$
7 \times 7 = 49
$$

we have

$$
\underbrace{7 \times 7}_{\color{blue}49} \times \underbrace{7 \times 7}_{\color{blue}49} = {\color{blue}49} \times {\color{blue}49}
$$

Finally, we work out $49 \times 49$.

$$
[math]\color{blue}3[/math]; [math]\color{blue}8[/math]; 4\begin{vmatrix}9 \\ \cdot\end{vmatrix}0\begin{vmatrix}0 & 4 & 9 \\ 4 & 4 & 1 \\ + & 1 & 9\end{vmatrix}6\begin{vmatrix}0 \\ 2\end{vmatrix}4 \mid 0 \mid 1
$$

Therefore, $7^4=2,401$.
