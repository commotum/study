## Introduction

Let's remind ourselves of the difference of squares formula:

$$
a^2-b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)
$$

We can use this formula to factor the difference of two squared monomials.

For example, suppose that we want to factor the expression

$$
x^2 - 4
$$

This expression is a difference of squares:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 4 &=  \\
&= x^{2} - 2^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

So, we can factor the expression using the difference of squares formula with $a=x$ and $b=2$, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 4 &=  \\
x^{2} - 2^{2} &=  \\
&= (x + 2)(x - 2)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Factoring a Difference of Squares

**Example:** Factor $y^2 - 9$.

**Explanation**

First, we write the expression as a difference of squares:

$$
y^2 - 9 = y^2 - 3^2
$$

Then, we factor the expression using the difference of squares formula:

$$
y^2 - 3^2 = (y + 3)(y - 3)
$$

## Factoring a Difference of Squares with Leading Coefficients

**Example:** Factor the expression $4a^2 - 49$.

**Explanation**

First, we write the expression as a difference of squares:

$$
4a^2 - 49 = (2a)^2 - 7^2
$$

Then, we factor the expression using the difference of squares formula:

$$
(2a)^2 - 7^2 = (2a+7)(2a-7)
$$

## Factoring a Difference of Squares Whose Terms Have a Common Factor

**Example:** Factor the expression $3x^2-300$.

**Explanation**

Before we start our normal process of factoring, notice that all of the terms have a common factor of $3$. Therefore, we can simplify the expression by factoring out $3$, as follows:

$$
3x^2-300 = 3(x^2-100)
$$

Now, let's factor the expression inside parentheses using the difference of squares formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 100 &=  \\
x^{2} - (10)^{2} &=  \\
&= (x + 10)(x - 10)
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, our original expression factors as follows:

$$
3(x^2-100) = 3(x+10)(x-10)
$$

## Factoring a Difference of Squares Containing More Than One Variable

**Example:** Express $-49s^2 + 100r^2$ as a product of two binomials.

**Explanation**

We can write this as a difference of squares by switching the order of the terms. Then, we factor as usual.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-49s^{2} + 100r^{2} &=  \\
100r^{2} - 49s^{2} &=  \\
(10r)^{2} - (7s)^{2} &=  \\
&= (10r - 7s)(10r + 7s)
\end{aligned}
$$
