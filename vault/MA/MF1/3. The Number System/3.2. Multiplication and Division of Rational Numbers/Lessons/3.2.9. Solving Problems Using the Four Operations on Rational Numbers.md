## Introduction

We can solve some real-world problems using the four operations on rational numbers.

- We use addition when there is an increase in the data.
- We use subtraction to find a difference or when there is a decrease in the data.
- We use multiplication when a quantity is a multiple larger (or higher) than another. In particular, "double" means to multiply by $2$, and "triple" means to multiply by $3$.
- We use division when a quantity is a multiple smaller (or lower) than another.

Let's see some examples.

---

<a id="multiplying-and-dividing-negative-numbers"></a>
## Multiplying and Dividing Negative Numbers

**Example:** The average temperature in Helsinki, Finland, in January $2017$ was $-5.5^{\circ} \, \textrm{C}$. If the average temperature (measured in degrees Celsius) in Yakutsk, Russia, in the same month was eight times lower, what was the average temperature in Yakutsk in January $2017$?

**Explanation**

To determine the temperature in Yakutsk, we multiply the temperature in Helsinki by $8$. This gives

$$
(-5.5^{\circ}\, \textrm{C}) \times 8 = -44^{\circ} \, \textrm{C}
$$

Therefore, the average temperature in Yakutsk in January $2017$ was $-44^{\circ} \, \textrm{C}$.

## Multiplying Negative Fractions and Mixed Numbers

**Example:** The temperature in a chamber that holds chlorine is $-24\dfrac{1}{3}{^\circ}\textrm{F}$. It's known that chlorine boils at $\dfrac{6}{5}$ the chamber's temperature measured in degrees Fahrenheit. What is the boiling temperature of chlorine?

**Explanation**

To find out the boiling temperature of chlorine, we need to calculate

$$
-24\dfrac{1}{3} \times \dfrac{6}{5}
$$

First, we write

$$
-24\dfrac{1}{3}
$$

as an improper fraction:

$$
-24\dfrac{1}{3} = -\dfrac{24\times 3 + 1}{3} = -\dfrac{73}{3}
$$

Now, we can multiply the two numbers. We multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-\frac{73}{3}) \cdot \frac{6}{5} &= -\frac{73 \cdot 6}{3 \cdot 5} \\
&=-\frac{73 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}{3 \cdot 5} \\
&=-\frac{73 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}{3 \cdot 5} \\
&=-\frac{73 \cdot 2}{\cdot 5} \\
&=-\frac{146}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we write the resulting improper fraction as a mixed number:

$$
-\dfrac{146}{5} = -29\dfrac15
$$

Therefore, chlorine boils at

$$
-29\dfrac15{^\circ}\textrm{F}
$$

## Combining Operations on Negative Numbers

**Example:** Martin has an overdraft facility authorized by his bank. His bank balance at the beginning of the month was $-\[math] 550$.

To reduce his debt with the bank, Martin deposited some money into his account. After making the deposit, his new balance was $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the initial balance. Then, at the end of the month, Martin made a withdrawal of $\[/math] 40$.

What was Martin's bank balance at the end of the month?

**Explanation**

To determine Martin's bank balance after he made the deposit, we multiply the initial balance by

$$
\dfrac{2}{5}
$$

This gives

$$
(-\[math] 550) \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} =-\[/math] 220
$$

Martin's bank balance was updated again when he made a withdrawal. To determine the final balance, we subtract the amount of money he withdrew from $-\[math]220$. This gives

$$
-\[/math] 220 - \[math] 40 =-\[/math] 260
$$

Therefore, Martin's bank balance at the end of the month was $-\[math] 260$.
