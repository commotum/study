## Introduction

Suppose we want to find all of the values of $x$ that satisfy *either* $4x+2 < 6$ *or*

$$
2x \geq 8
$$

To do this, we solve each inequality individually and then compute the union of the two solutions.

We start by solving $4x+2 < 6$:
$\begin{bmatrix}4x + 2 & < 6 \\ 4x & < 4 \\ x & < 1\end{bmatrix}$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/52952fcc9431374e3b2f7dab2804a802.png>)

Then, we solve

$$
2x \geq 8
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2x & \ge 8 \\ x & \ge 4\end{bmatrix}
$$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/84a736826d245d96a936fe7f05039190.png>)

To graph the values of $x$ that satisfy either inequality, we overlay the two graphs on the same number line:

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/109fce543e939c85d2abcc472e3df7bb.png>)

Thus, the final solution is $x < 1$ or

$$
x \geq 4
$$

We can write this using interval notation as

$[MATH: (-\infty, 1) \cup [4,\infty).]$

## Finding the Union of Two Linear Inequalities

**Example:** Find the set of values of $x$ that satisfy $6x-1 \leq 2$ or $5x-1 > 9$. Express your answer in interval notation.

**Explanation**

We start by solving

$$
6x-1 \leq 2
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}6x - 1 & \le 2 \\ 6x & \le 3 \\ x & \le \frac{3}{6} \\ x & \le \frac{1}{2}\end{bmatrix}
$$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/dbb13dc3cc15fc3b659395d1ca343124.png>)

Then, we solve $5x -1 > 9$:
$\begin{bmatrix}5x - 1 & > 9 \\ 5x & > 10 \\ x & > 2\end{bmatrix}$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/310f094f64629ed10ff73851fdcf5a77.png>)

To graph the values of $x$ that satisfy either inequality, we overlay the two graphs on the same number line:

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/283864db43507a3a480513b68d4c39d5.png>)

Therefore, the final solution is

$$
x \leq \dfrac{1}{2}
$$

or $x >2$. We can write this using interval notation as

$[MATH: (- ∞, \frac{1}{2}]∪(2, ∞).]$

## Solving For the Union of Two Opposite-Sided Linear Inequalities that Overlap

**Example:** Find all the values of $x$ that satisfy $12x+8 > 44$ or $5x+3 \leq 28$.

**Explanation**

We start by solving $12x+8 > 44$:
$\begin{bmatrix}12x + 8 & > 44 \\ 12x & > 36 \\ x & > 3\end{bmatrix}$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/3c416e4e1b63a6cb7733b0c4013853af.png>)

Then, we solve

$$
5x+3 \leq 28
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}5x + 3 & \le 28 \\ 5x & \le 25 \\ x & \le 5\end{bmatrix}
$$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/e2b3056d4782fa16d5e921d27a17a05b.png>)

To graph the values of $x$ that satisfy either inequality, we overlay the two graphs on the same number line:

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/26058f671a9f80aa8bc66650df56fb49.png>)

Therefore, the final solution is $-\infty < x < \infty$. We can write this using interval notation as $(-\infty,\infty)$.

## Finding the Union of Two Linear Inequalities When One Inequality is Compound

**Example:** Find the set of values of $x$ that satisfy $1 < 5x+6 \leq 11$ or $5x-7 > 8$.

**Explanation**

We start by solving

$$
1 < 5x+6 \leq 11
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}1 & < 5x + 6 \le 11 \\ -5 & < 5x \le 5 \\ -1 & < x \le 1\end{bmatrix}
$$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/6acdf21842dede41a0639285d2e2bf64.png>)

Then, we solve $5x-7 > 8$:
$\begin{bmatrix}5x - 7 & > 8 \\ 5x & > 15 \\ x & > 3\end{bmatrix}$

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/03780cb98421f954a967a2f5980983d9.png>)

To graph the values of $x$ that satisfy either inequality, we overlay the two graphs on the same number line:

![](<../Source/5.6.13. Compound OR Inequalities/Images/2bbfdca50ab5f5c2e6064b56d8b57bf6.png>)

Thus, the final solution is

$$
-1 < x \leq 1
$$

or $x > 3$. We can write this using interval notation as

$[MATH: (-1,1] \cup (3,\infty).]$
