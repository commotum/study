## Introduction

To simplify an expression like $-(3+b)$, we need to **distribute** the negative sign across each of the terms inside the parentheses. This can be accomplished by treating the negative sign like multiplication by $(-1)$. For example:

$$
\begin{aligned}
- (3 + b) &=  \\
(-1)(3 + b) &=  \\
(-1) \cdot 3 + (-1) \cdot b &=  \\
- 3 + (-b) &=  \\
&= - 3 - b
\end{aligned}
$$

In these cases, the negative sign in front of the parentheses represents the $-1$. However, to save space, only the negative sign is shown.

<a id="distributing-the-negative-sign-over-the-sum-of-two-terms"></a>
## Distributing the Negative Sign over the Sum of Two Terms

**Example:** Simplify the expression $-\left(3n+\dfrac{1}{2}k \right)$.

**Explanation**

First, we rewrite the negative sign as multiplication by $(-1)\mathbin$:

$$
\begin{aligned} - \left(3n+\dfrac{1}{2}k \right) \\ &= (-1) \left(3n+\dfrac{1}{2}k \right) \end{aligned}
$$

Then, we distribute the $(-1)$ to each term in the parentheses, and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned} (-1) \left(3n+\dfrac{1}{2}k \right) \\ &= (-1) (3n)+(-1) \left(\dfrac{1}{2}k \right) \\ &= -3n+ \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}k \right) \\ &= -3n-\dfrac{1}{2}k \end{aligned}
$$

## Distributing the Negative Sign over the Difference of Two Terms

**Example:** Simplify the expression $-(2x-1.3)$.

**Explanation**

First, we rewrite the negative sign as multiplication by $(-1)\mathbin$:

$$
\begin{aligned} -(2x-1.3) \\ &= (-1)(2x-1.3) \end{aligned}
$$

Then, we distribute the $(-1)$ to each term in the parentheses, and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned} (-1)(2x-1.3) \\ &= (-1) (2x)-(-1) (1.3) \\ &= -2x-(-1.3) \\ &= -2x+1.3 \end{aligned}
$$

<a id="rule-of-thumb"></a>
## Rule of Thumb

By distributing the negative sign across each of the terms inside the parentheses, we're merely changing the sign of each term. In other words:

- If a term was *positive* inside the parentheses, it becomes *negative*.
- If a term was *negative* inside the parentheses, it becomes *positive*.

## Distributing the Negative over More Than Two Terms

**Example:** Simplify $-\left(-p + 2q - \dfrac{1}{3} \right)$.

**Explanation**

To distribute the negative sign to the terms inside the parentheses, all we have to do is change the sign of each term.

$$
\begin{aligned}
- (-p + 2q - \frac{1}{3}) &=  \\
&= p - 2q + \frac{1}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
