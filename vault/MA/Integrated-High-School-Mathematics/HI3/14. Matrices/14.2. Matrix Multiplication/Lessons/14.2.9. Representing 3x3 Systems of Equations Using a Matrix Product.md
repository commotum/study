## Introduction

Remember that we can represent a system of two linear equations in two variables using matrices. We can do the same thing for a system of three linear equations in three variables, such as the following:

$$
{3x + 2y-2z = 7; 2x-1y-4z = 2; 2x + 1y-3z = 3
$$

For a $3 \times 3$ system like the one above, we put the coefficients of the system into a $3 \times 3$ coefficient matrix:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 \\ 2 & 1 & -3\end{bmatrix}
$$

Each row has the coefficients of one of the equations in the system, and each column consists of the coefficients of one particular variable.
We also have the $3 \times 1$ column vectors

$$
\begin{bmatrix}7 \\ 2 \\ 3\end{bmatrix}
$$

where $\mathbf{b}$ consists of each constant on the right side of the system and $\mathbf{x}$ contains the variables.
With these matrices, we can write our system as

$$
A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}
$$

or

$$
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 \\ 2 & 1 & -3\end{bmatrix}
$$

We can write any system of three equations with three variables in this form. Just to check that the above equation is true, let's multiply out the left-hand side to see if it equals the right-hand side:

$$
\begin{aligned}
Ax &= \begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 \\ 2 & 1 & -3\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x \\ y \\ z\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}3x + 2y-2z \\ 2x-1y-4z \\ 2x + 1y-3z\end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix}7 \\ 2 \\ 3\end{bmatrix} \\
&= b✓
\end{aligned}
$$

## Identifying Entries of a Matrix Product Representing a 3x3 System of Equations

**Example:** The system of equations
${6x + 4y = 1; x - 8y - z =-2; 3x + 2y - 2z = 1$

can be represented using matrix notation as

$\begin{bmatrix}a & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & -8 & -1 \\ 3 & b & -2\end{bmatrix}$

What are the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$?

**Explanation**

First, let's write the system so that $x$, $y$, and $z$ appear in each equation:

$$
{6x + 4y + 0z = 1; x - 8y - z =-2; 3x + 2y - 2z = 1
$$

Now, we can write the system in the form

$$
A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}
$$

where

$$
\begin{bmatrix}6 & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & -8 & -1 \\ 3 & 2 & -2\end{bmatrix}
$$

is the coefficient matrix,

$$
\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ -2 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}
$$

is the column vector of constants, and

$$
\begin{bmatrix}x \\ y \\ z\end{bmatrix}
$$

is the column vector of variables.

So, the equation

$$
A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}
$$

becomes

$$
\begin{bmatrix}6 & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & -8 & -1 \\ 3 & 2 & -2\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore, $a=6$, $b=2$, and $c=-2$.

## Identifying Entries of a Matrix Product Representing a 3x3 System of Equations by Reorganizing

**Example:** The system of equations
${2x - y + z = 4; z - x + 2 =-1; 2y - 2x - 4z =-4$
can be represented using matrix notation as
$\begin{bmatrix}2 & -1 & 1 \\ -1 & a & 1 \\ b & 2 & -4\end{bmatrix}$
What are the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$?

**Explanation**

First, let's write the system so that $x$, $y$, and $z$ appear in each equation in the correct order and all constants are on the right-hand side of the system:

$$
{2x - y + z = 4; - x + 0y + z =-3; - 2x + 2y - 4z =-4
$$

Now, we can write the system in the form

$$
A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}
$$

where

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2 & -1 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \\ -2 & 2 & -4\end{bmatrix}
$$

is the coefficient matrix,

$$
\begin{bmatrix}4 \\ -3 \\ -4\end{bmatrix}
$$

is the column vector of constants, and

$$
\begin{bmatrix}x \\ y \\ z\end{bmatrix}
$$

is the column vector of variables.

So, the equation

$$
A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}
$$

becomes

$$
\begin{bmatrix}2 & -1 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \\ -2 & 2 & -4\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore $a=0$, $b=-2$, $c=-3$.
