## Introduction

The double-angle formula

$$
\sin{2x}=2\sin{x}\cos{x}
$$

can be used to simplify complicated trigonometric expressions. For instance, suppose we want to simplify the expression

$$
\dfrac{\sin{2x}}{2\cos^2{x}}
$$

Using the double-angle formula, we can simplify the given expression, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\sin 2x}{2\cos^{2} x} &= \frac{2\sin x\cos x}{2\cos x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{2\cos x \cdot \sin x}{2\cos x \cdot \cos x} \\
&= \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} \\
&= \tan x
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="simplifying-expressions-using-the-double-angle-formula-for-sine"></a>
## Simplifying Expressions Using the Double-Angle Formula for Sine

**Example:** Simplify the expression $\dfrac{\sin^2{x}}{\sin{2x}}$.

**Explanation**

First, we recall the double-angle formula for sine:

$$
\sin{2x} = 2\sin{x}\cos{x}
$$

Substituting the above into the given expression, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\sin^{2} x}{\sin 2x} &= \frac{\sin x\sin x}{2\sin x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{\sin x\sin x}{2\sin x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{\sin x}{2\cos x} \\
&= \frac{1}{2}\tan x
\end{aligned}
$$

## Simplifying Expressions Containing the Cosecant of a Double Angle

**Example:** Simplify the expression $2\tan x \csc 2x$.

**Explanation**

First, recall that

$$
\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}, \qquad \csc 2x = \dfrac{1}{\color{blue}\sin 2x}
$$

Now, using the double-angle formula for sine,

$$
\sin{2x} = 2\sin{x}\cos{x}
$$

we simplify the expression as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2\tan x\csc 2x &= 2 \cdot \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} \cdot \frac{1}{\sin 2x} \\
&= \frac{2\sin x}{\cos x} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sin x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{2\sin x}{\cos x} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sin x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{1}{\cos^{2} x} \\
&= \sec^{2} x
\end{aligned}
$$

## Simplifying Advanced Trigonometric Expressions Using the Double-Angle Formula for Sine

**Example:** Write $\cos{x}\csc{x}\sec^2{x}$ as a single trigonometric function.

**Explanation**

First, we simplify the given expression using the following identities:

$$
\csc x = \dfrac{1}{\sin x}, \qquad \sec x = \dfrac{1}{\cos x}
$$

Substituting the above into our expression, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos x\csc x\sec^{2} x &= \cos x \cdot \frac{1}{\sin x} \cdot \frac{1}{\cos^{2} x} \\
&= \frac{\cos x}{\sin x\cos^{2} x} \\
&= \frac{\cos x}{\sin x\cos x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{\cos x}{\sin x\cos x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{1}{\sin x\cos x}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we recall the double-angle formula for sine:

$$
2\sin{x}\cos{x} = \sin{2x}
$$

Substituting the above into our expression, we finally get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{\sin x\cos x} &= \frac{2}{2\sin x\cos x} \\
&= \frac{2}{\sin 2x} \\
&= 2\csc 2x
\end{aligned}
$$
