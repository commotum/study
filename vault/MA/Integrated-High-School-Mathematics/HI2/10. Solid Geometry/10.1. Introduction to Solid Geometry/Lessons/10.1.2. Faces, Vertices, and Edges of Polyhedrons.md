## Introduction

The **faces** of a polyhedron are the polygonal sides that construct it.

Let's consider the triangular prism below.

![](<../Source/10.1.2. Faces, Vertices, and Edges of Polyhedrons/Images/a0d7b36cb7c8a69efbbc01beb1638a52.png>)

Our triangular prism has $5$ faces:

![](<../Source/10.1.2. Faces, Vertices, and Edges of Polyhedrons/Images/9b04d849605bbe69f1307517a503d456.png>)

The **vertices** of a polyhedron are its corners. Our triangular prism has $6$ vertices:

![](<../Source/10.1.2. Faces, Vertices, and Edges of Polyhedrons/Images/ade61275401f006641b5b60d5c68f711.png>)

The **edges** of a polyhedron are the straight line segments that connect its vertices. Our triangular prism has $9$ edges:

![](<../Source/10.1.2. Faces, Vertices, and Edges of Polyhedrons/Images/9f65b8e71baf9f96ab67ec7ffc65889a.png>)
