## Evaluating an Absolute Value Function

**Example:** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = \mid x - 4 \mid$. What is the value of $f(-2)$?

**Explanation**

To find the value of $f(-2)$, we substitute $x=-2$ into the function's definition and evaluate:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= \mid x - 4 \mid \\
f(-2) &= \mid - 2 - 4 \mid \\
&= \mid - 6 \mid \\
&= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $f(-2)$ is $6$.

## Finding an Input of an Absolute Value Function Corresponding to an Output

**Example:** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = \mid x - 4x \mid$. What values of $a$ satisfy $f(5) - f(a) = -15$?

**Explanation**

First, we evaluate $f(5){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(5) &= \mid 5 - 4(5) \mid \\
&= \mid 5 - 20 \mid \\
&= \mid - 15 \mid \\
&= 15
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute $f(5)$ into the equation

$$
f(5) - f(a) = -15
$$

and solve it for $f(a)$, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(5) - f(a) &= -15 \\
15 - f(a) &= -15 \\
- f(a) &= -30 \\
f(a) &= 30
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, substitute

$$
f(a)= \mid a - 4a \mid
$$

into the above and solve for $a{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(a) &= 30 \\
\mid a - 4a \mid &= 30 \\
\begin{vmatrix}-3a & &= 30 \\ 3 & a\end{vmatrix}|= 30 \\
\mid a \mid &= 10 \\
a &= ± 10
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $a$ could be $10$ or $-10$.

## Determining an Unknown Constant Given Two Absolute Value Functions

**Example:** The functions $c$ and $d$ are defined by the given equations.
$c(m) = 8 + \left\begin{vmatrix}2m + m^2\right &, \qquad d(z) = \left & \dfrac{z - 5}{2z}\right\end{vmatrix} + z + 2$
If $c(-2) = c$, where $c$ is a constant, what is the value of $d(c)$?

**Explanation**

We begin by evaluating $c(-2){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
c(-2) &= 8 + \mid 2(-2) + (-2)^{2} \mid \\
&= 8 + \mid - 4 + 4 \mid \\
&= 8 + \mid 0 \mid \\
&= 8 + 0 \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

So, $c = 8$. Now, we evaluate

$$
d(c) = d(8){:}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
d(8) &= \begin{vmatrix}(8 - 5)/(2(8)) & + 8 + 2 \\ = & \frac{3}{16}\end{vmatrix}+ 10 \\
&= \frac{3}{16} + 10 \\
&= \frac{3 + 160}{16} \\
&= \frac{163}{16}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Determining an Unknown Input-Output Parameter for an Absolute Value Function

**Example:** Let $z(w) = \mid 25 - 5w \mid$. For which values of $k$ does $z(k) = 5k$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
z(w) = \mid 25 - 5w \mid
$$

and asked to find the value of $k$ for which $z(k) = 5k$. Therefore, we need to solve the equation

$$
\mid 25 - 5k \mid = 5k
$$

We now split into two cases based on the definition of absolute value.

- First, we consider the case where $25 - 5k \ge 0$. Then, we have
$\mid 25 - 5k \mid = 25 - 5k$.
Therefore, our absolute value equation becomes
$25 - 5k = 5k$.
Solving the above equation for $k$, we obtain
$25 - 5k|= 5k; 25|= 10k; k|= \frac{5}{2}$.
Now, we check whether this value of $k$ satisfies the condition for the first case:
$25 - 5(\frac{5}{2})|= 25 - \frac{25}{2}; = \frac{25}{2}; \ge 0✓$
So, $k = \dfrac{5}{2}$ is a valid solution.
- Now, we consider the case where $25 - 5k < 0$. Then, we have
$\mid 25 - 5k \mid = -(25 - 5k)$.
This equation becomes:
$-25 + 5k = 5k \Rightarrow -25 = 0$,
which is not true. Therefore, no solution arises from this case.

Thus, the only value that satisfies the equation $z(k) = 5k$ is

$$
k = \dfrac{5}{2}
$$
