## Introduction

This topic provides practice in analyzing exponential functions by evaluating them for given inputs, identifying y-intercepts, interpreting tables of values, and recognizing their graphical representations.

<a id="evaluating-an-exponential-function"></a>
## Evaluating an Exponential Function

**Example:** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = 5(3^{x})$. What is the value of $f(2)$?

**Explanation**

To find the value of $f(2)$, we substitute $x=2$ into the function's definition and evaluate:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= 5(3^{x}) \\
f(2) &= 5(3^{2}) \\
&= 5 \cdot 9 \\
&= 45
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $f(2)$ is $45$.

## Identifying an Exponential Function's Y-Intercept

**Example:** The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = 5(3)^x - 7$. What is the $y$-intercept of the graph of $y = g(x)$ in the $xy$-plane?

**Explanation**

To find the $y$-intercept of the graph of a function, we evaluate the function at $x = 0$.

Substituting $x = 0$ into the equation, we get:

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(x) &= 5(3)^{x} - 7 \\
g(0) &= 5(3)^{0} - 7 \\
&= 5(1) - 7 \\
&= 5 - 7 \\
&=-2
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the $y$-intercept is the point $(0, -2)$.

## Identifying Tables of Values of Exponential Functions

**Example:** Which table gives three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $p(x)$ at $x=-1,0,1$ for the function $p$ shown below?
$p(x) = 24(3^x)$

**Explanation**

We are given the exponential function

$$
p(x) = 24 \cdot 3^x
$$

We evaluate $p(x)$ at three different $x$-values.

- When $x = -1$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(-1) &= 24 \cdot 3^{-1} \\
&= 24 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

- When $x = 0$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(0) &= 24 \cdot 3^{0} \\
&= 24 \cdot 1 \\
&= 24
\end{aligned}
$$

- When $x = 1$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(1) &= 24 \cdot 3^{1} \\
&= 24 \cdot 3 \\
&= 72
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the table that matches the function is the following:

| $x$ | $p(x)$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $-1$ | $8$ |
| $0$ | $24$ |
| $1$ | $72$ |

## Identifying a Graph of an Exponential Function

**Example:** Which of the graphs above shows the function $y = 6\left(3^{x}\right)$?

![](<../Source/2.8.1. Exponential Functions/Images/46a1fa7dbafef50f2ea8cd5393eadc8b.png>)

**Explanation**

We are given the exponential function

$$
y = 6 \left(3^{x}\right)
$$

Let's analyze the properties of this function:

- Since the base of the exponential is $3 > 1$, the function is increasing and $y$ grows rapidly as $x \to \infty$.
- The coefficient $6$ vertically stretches the graph and makes it pass through the point $(0,6)$.
- As $x \to -\infty$, $y \to 0$, so the graph approaches the horizontal asymptote $y = 0$.

Additionally, note that from the given equation, we have:

$$
y(1) = 6 \cdot 3^1 = 18 \quad\text{and}\quad y(2) = 6 \cdot 3^2 = 54
$$

So, the graph contains the points $(1, 18)$ and $(2, 54)$.

Therefore, the graph that satisfies all of these properties is the following (top-left in the original picture).

![](<../Source/2.8.1. Exponential Functions/Images/a52cea92666974517bee7035d0a43a61.png>)

Please note that the following (bottom-right in the original picture) **cannot** be the correct answer, since it clearly does not pass through the point $(2,54)$.

![](<../Source/2.8.1. Exponential Functions/Images/5fc82d9848c54c0379a30316a48b81eb.png>)
