## Introduction

Questions about multiplying binomials test the fluency with the distributive property and the ability to organize terms correctly to avoid sign or arithmetic errors.

In this lesson, we’ll practice expanding products of binomials of different forms, simplifying the results, and matching coefficients or terms to solve for unknown values.

## Multiplying Linear Binomials With Integer Coefficients

**Example:** The expression $(6x - 1)(5x - 2)$ is equivalent to the expression $a x^{2} + b x + c$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are constants. What is the value of $b$?

**Explanation**

We need to expand the expression $(6x - 1)(5x - 2)$ to put it in the form $a x^{2} + b x + c$.

To do that, we need to apply the distributive property:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(6x - 1)(5x - 2) &= 6x \cdot 5x + 6x \cdot (-2) - 1 \cdot 5x - 1 \cdot (-2) \\
&= 30x^{2} - 12x - 5x + 2 \\
&= 30x^{2} - 17x + 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, by matching this with its given equivalent expression, $a x^{2} + b x + c$, we get

$$
b = \bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{-17}
$$

## Multiplying Binomials of Different Degrees

**Example:** Expand and simplify the expression $(r^2 - 2r)(r^3 + 3r)$.
Write your final answer in standard form, with terms ordered by descending degree.

**Explanation**

We apply the distributive property to multiply each term in the first binomial by each term in the second.

First, we multiply $r^2$ by both terms in the second binomial:

$$
r^2(r^3 + 3r) = r^5 + 3r^3
$$

Next, we multiply $2r$ by both terms in the second binomial:

$$
-2r(r^3 + 3r) = -2r^4 - 6r^2
$$

Now, we combine all the terms:

$$
r^5 + 3r^3 - 2r^4 - 6r^2
$$

We arrange the terms in descending order of degree to write the expression in standard form.

Therefore, the correct answer is $r^5 - 2r^4 + 3r^3 - 6r^2$.

## Multiplying Quadratic Binomials With Unknown Coefficients

**Example:** Consider the functions
$f(x) = x^{2} + b x, \qquad g(x) = 2 x^{2} - 4 x$
where $b$ is a constant. If
$f(x) \cdot g(x) = 2 x^{4} + 8 x^{3} - 24 x^{2}$,
what is the value of $b$?

**Explanation**

We are asked to find the value of $b$ so that the product of $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ matches the given quartic expression.

First, we expand:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x^{2} + bx)(2x^{2} - 4x) &= x^{2} \cdot 2x^{2} + x^{2} \cdot (-4x) + bx \cdot 2x^{2} + bx \cdot (-4x) \\
&= 2x^{4} - 4x^{3} + 2bx^{3} - 4bx^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we group like terms:

$$
2 x^{4} + \left(-4 + 2 b \right) x^{3} + \left(-4 b \right) x^{2}
$$

Now, we compare the above expression with

$$
2 x^{4} + 8 x^{3} - 24 x^{2}
$$

Comparing coefficients of $x^{3}$, we get the following:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-4 + 2b &= 8 \\
2b &= 12 \\
b &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Comparing coefficients of $x^{2}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-4b &= -24 \\
b &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

In both of the cases, the values of $b$ match. Therefore, the value of $b$ is $6$.

## Multiplying Three-Variable Binomials

**Example:** Expand and simplify the expression $(x^2 - y)(y^2 - z^3)$.

**Explanation**

We wish to expand the product of two binomials,
$(x^2 - y)(y^2 - z^3)$.

We multiply each term of the first binomial by the second binomial. Then, we simplify by collecting like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x^{2} - y)(y^{2} - z^{3}) &= x^{2}(y^{2} - z^{3}) - y(y^{2} - z^{3}) \\
&= x^{2}y^{2} - x^{2}z^{3} - y^{3} + yz^{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

No further simplification is possible, so this is the final form.
