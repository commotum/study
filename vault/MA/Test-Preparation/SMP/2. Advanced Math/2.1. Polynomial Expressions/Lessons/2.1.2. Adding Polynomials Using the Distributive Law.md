## Introduction

Questions involving distributive laws often appear on the math section of the SAT exam. These questions check whether we can correctly expand, combine, and simplify expressions.

Here, we’ll practice applying the distributive property to polynomial expressions, simplifying them, and identifying or comparing coefficients to find missing values.

---

<a id="the-sum-of-two-binomials-where-one-is-factored"></a>
## The Sum of Two Binomials Where One Is Factored

**Example:** The expression

$\left(2900 + 120 z^{2}\right) + 2 \left(50 z^{2} - 1500\right)$

can be written in the form $a z^{2} + b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. What is the value of $a + b$?

**Explanation**

We are asked to simplify

$$
2900 + 120 z^{2} + 2 \left(50 z^{2} - 1500\right)
$$

First, we expand the parentheses:
$2900 + 120 z^{2} + 100 z^{2} - 3000$

Next, we combine like terms and simplify as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2900 + 120z^{2} + 100z^{2} - 3000 &= (120z^{2} + 100z^{2}) + (2900 - 3000) \\
&= 220z^{2} - 100
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we compare the above expression with $az^2+b{:}$

$$
a = 220, \qquad b = -100
$$

Therefore, their sum is

$$
a + b = 220 + (-100) = \bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{120}
$$

## Applying the Distributive Law With an Unknown Parameter

**Example:** The expression $2y^2 + ay$ is equivalent to $y(2y - 9)$ for some constant $a$. What is the value of $a$?

**Explanation**

We are given that

$$
2y^2 + ay = y(2y - 9)
$$

First, we expand the right-hand side:

$$
y(2y - 9) = 2y^2 - 9y
$$

Now, we compare the two expressions to determine the value of $a$, as follows:

$$
2{\color{blue}y^2}-9{\color{red}y} = 2{\color{blue}y^2}+a{\color{red}y} \[5pt]
$$

This gives us

$$
a = -9
$$

Therefore, the answer is $-9$.

## The Sum or Difference of Two Polynomials Using the Distributive Law

**Example:** Consider the expression
$2\left(4x^2 - rx + 1\right) + 3x(r + 2)$
where $r$ is a constant. If this expression is equivalent to $8x^2 + 16x + 2$, what is the value of $r$?

**Explanation**

We begin by expanding both parts of the expression:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(4x^{2} - rx + 1) &= 8x^{2} - 2rx + 2 \\
3x(r + 2) &= 3rx + 6x
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we combine the like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
8x^{2} - 2rx + 2 + 3rx + 6x &= 8x^{2} + (-2r + 3r)x + 2 \\
&= 8x^{2} + (r + 6)x + 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we set the above expression equal to the given expression:

$$
8x^2 + (r + 6)x + 2 = 8x^2 + 16x + 2
$$

Comparing the coefficients of $x$, we get

$$
r + 6 = 16
$$

Solving this equation gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
r + 6 &= 16 \\
r &= 10
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $r$ is $10$.
