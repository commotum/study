## Evaluating a Cubic Function

**Example:** The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = 2x^{3} - 4$. What is the value of $g(-2)$?

**Explanation**

To find $g(-2)$, we substitute $x = -2$ into the function's definition and evaluate:

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(x) &= 2x^{3} - 4 \\
g(-2) &= 2(-2)^{3} - 4 \\
&= 2 \cdot (-8) - 4 \\
&=-16 - 4 \\
&=-20
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding the Input of a Cubic Function Corresponding to an Output

**Example:** Given the function $f(x) = 4x^3$, find the value of $x$ for which $f(x) = 108$.

**Explanation**

We set $f(x)$ equal to $108$ and solve the obtained equation for $x$, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= 108 \\
4x^{3} &= 108 \\
x^{3} &= 27 \\
x^{3} &= 3^{3} \\
x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the required value of $x$ is $3$.
