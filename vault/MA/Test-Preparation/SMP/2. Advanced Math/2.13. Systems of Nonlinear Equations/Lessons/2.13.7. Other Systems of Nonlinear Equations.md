## Solving Systems Containing Square Root Equations

**Example:** ${x = 144; y = \sqrt{x} - 5$

The graphs of the equations above intersect at the point $(x, y)$ in the $xy$-plane. What is the value of $y$?

**Explanation**

We are given the system of two equations

$$
{x = 144; y = \sqrt{x} - 5
$$

To find the point where the graphs intersect, we substitute $x = 144$ into the second equation. We obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= \sqrt{x} - 5 \\
&= \sqrt{144} - 5 \\
&= 12 - 5 \\
&= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $y$ is $7$.

## Solving Systems Containing Exponential Equations

**Example:** ${x = 9; y = 4 \cdot 2^{x - 2} + 1$

The graphs of the given equations intersect at the point $(x, y)$ in the $xy$-plane. What is the value of $y$?

**Explanation**

We are given the following system:

$$
{x = 9; y = 4 \cdot 2^{x - 2} + 1
$$

Substituting $x = 9$ into the second equation gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 4 \cdot 2^{7} + 1 \\
&= 4 \cdot 128 + 1 \\
&= 512 + 1 \\
&= 513
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we have that $y=513$.

## Solving Systems Containing Absolute Value Equations

**Example:** ${y = 13; y = \mid - 3x + 4 \mid$

The graphs of the given equations in the $xy$-plane intersect at the point $(x, y)$. What are the possible values of $x$?

**Explanation**

We are given the following system:

$$
{y = 13; y = \mid - 3x + 4 \mid
$$

From the first equation, we know that $y = 13$.

Substituting $y = 13$ into the second equation gives

$$
\mid -3x + 4 \mid = 13
$$

This absolute value equation has two cases:

- If $-3x + 4 > 0$, we have
$-3x + 4|= 13; - 3x|= 9; x|=-3$.
- If $-3x + 4 < 0$, we have
$-3x + 4|=-13; - 3x|=-17; x|= \frac{17}{3}$.

Therefore, $x = -3$ and

$$
x = \dfrac{17}{3}
$$

are the two solutions of the given system of equations.
