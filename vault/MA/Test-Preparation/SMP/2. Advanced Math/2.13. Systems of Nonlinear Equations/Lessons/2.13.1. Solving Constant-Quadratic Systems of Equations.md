## Solving Quadratic Systems Using Direct Substitution

**Example:** ${n = m^{2} + 6m - 7; m - 5 =-3$

The solution to the given system of equations is $(m, n)$. What is the value of $n$?

**Explanation**

We are given a system in which one equation is quadratic and the other is linear. We begin by solving the linear equation for $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m - 5 &= -3 \\
m &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute this value of $m$ into the first equation to find the corresponding value of $n{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= m^{2} + 6m - 7 \\
&= 2^{2} + 6(2) - 7 \\
&= 4 + 12 - 7 \\
&= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $n$ is $9$.

## Solving Quadratic Systems Using the Square Root Method

**Example:** ${y = x^{2} - 7; y = 18$

When the equations above are graphed in the $xy$-plane, what are the coordinates $(x, y)$ of the points of intersection of the two graphs?

**Explanation**

To find the points of intersection, we set the two expressions for $y$ equal to each other, isolate $x^2$, and then solve for $x$ by taking the square root of both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 7 &= 18 \\
x^{2} &= 25 \\
\sqrt{x^{2}} &= \sqrt{25} \\
\mid x \mid &= 5 \\
x &= ± 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute each $x$-value into either equation (we use $y = 18$) to find the corresponding points:

- If $x = 5$, then $y = 18$.
- If $x = -5$, then $y = 18$.

So the points of intersection are $(5, 18)$ and $(-5, 18)$.

## Solving Quadratic Systems by Factoring

**Example:** ${y = 49; y = x^{2} - 2x + 1$

The graphs of the given equations in the $xy$-plane intersect at the point $(x, y)$. What is the possible value of $x$?

**Explanation**

We are given the following system:

$$
{y = 49; y = x^{2} - 2x + 1
$$

Substituting $y = 49$ into the second equation gives:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 2x + 1 &= 49 \\
x^{2} - 2x + 1 - 49 &= 0 \\
x^{2} - 2x - 48 &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

We solve this quadratic equation by factoring. We begin by finding two numbers that multiply to $-48$ and add to $-2$. These are $-8$ and $6{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 2x - 48 &= 0 \\
x^{2}- 8x + 6x_(⏟)_(-2x) - 48 &= 0 \\
x(x - 8) + 6(x - 8) &= 0 \\
(x - 8)(x + 6) &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Applying the zero product rule, we obtain

$$
\begin{bmatrix}x - 8 = 0 & orx + 6 = 0 \\ x = 8 & orx =-6\end{bmatrix}
$$

Therefore, among the given options, the possible value of $x$ is $8$.
