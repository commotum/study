## Solving Multivariable Equations by Adding or Subtracting the Constant Term

**Example:** The variables $x$, $y$, and $z$ are related by the equation
$z + 29 = xy$.
Find the equation expresses $z$ in terms of $x$ and $y$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
z + 29 = xy
$$

and asked to solve for $z$ in terms of $x$ and $y$.

To isolate $z$, we subtract $29$ from *both sides*:

$$
\begin{aligned}
z + 29 - 29 &= xy - 29 \\
z &= xy - 29
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is

$$
z = xy - 29
$$

## Solving Multivariable Equations by Adding or Subtracting the Variable Term

**Example:** The variables $x$, $z$, and $k$ are related by the equation
$14x + z = k$.
Find the equation expresses the value of $k - z$ in terms of $x$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
14x + z = k
$$

and asked to express $k - z$ in terms of $x$.

To isolate $k - z$, we subtract $z$ from *both sides* of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
14x + z - z &= k - z \\
14x &= k - z
\end{aligned}
$$

Rewriting with $k - z$ on the left-hand side, we get

$$
k - z = 14x
$$

So, the correct answer is $14x$.

## Solving Multivariable Equations by Multiplying or Dividing by the Constant Factor

**Example:** The variables $a$, $b$, and $c$ are related by the equation
$-3a = 4(b + c)$.
Find the equation expresses $a$ in terms of $b$ and $c$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
-3a = 4(b + c)
$$

and asked to solve for $a$ in terms of $b$ and $c$.

To isolate $a$, we divide *both sides* of the equation by $-3{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{-3a}{-3} &= (4(b + c))/(-3) \\
a &= - (4(b + c))/(3)
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is

$$
a = -\dfrac{4(b + c)}{3}
$$

## Solving Multivariable Equations by Multiplying or Dividing by the Variable Factor

**Example:** The following equation relates the distinct positive numbers $x$, $a$, and $b{:}$
$x = -\dfrac{a}{15b}$
Find the equation that expresses $a$ in terms of $x$ and $b$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
x = -\dfrac{a}{15b}
$$

and asked to solve for $a$ in terms of $x$ and $b$.

To eliminate the denominator, we multiply *both sides* of the equation by $15b{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x \cdot 15b &= -\frac{a}{15b} \cdot 15b \\
15bx &= -a
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply *both sides* by $-1$ to isolate $a$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
15bx \cdot (-1) &= -a \cdot (-1) \\
- 15bx &= a
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is
$a = -15bx$.
