## Constructing Exponential Growth Models From Descriptions

**Example:** A certain bacteria culture starts with $5$ cells. Each hour, the number of cells triples. If $N$ represents the number of cells after $t$ hours, write the expression that gives $N$ in terms of $t$.

**Explanation**

We're told that the initial number of cells is $5$. Each hour, the number of cells triples.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth factor) is

$$
N = a \cdot b^t
$$

In this case, we have the following:

- $a=5$ is the initial number of cells.
- $b = 3$ is the growth factor (the population triples each hour).
- $t$ is the time, in hours.

Therefore, the correct expression for the number of cells $N$ after $t$ hours is

$$
N = 5 \cdot 3^t
$$

## Constructing Exponential Models From Percentage Increase Descriptions

**Example:** A small streaming platform was launched in March $2020$ with $850$ users. Each month for the next $18$ months, the number of users increased by $12\%$ compared to the previous month.

Write the equation that represents the total number of users $u(t)$ on the platform $t$ months after March $2020$, where $t \leq 18$.

**Explanation**

We are told that the number of users starts at $850$ and increases by $12\%$ each month for the next $18$ months. This means we have exponential growth, where the quantity increases by the same percentage every month.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate $r$) is

$$
u(t) = a(1+r)^t
$$

In our case, we have the following:

- $a=850$ is the initial number of users.
- $r = 0.12$ is the growth rate ($12\%$ expressed as a decimal).
- $t$ is the time, in months.

Therefore, the correct expression for $u(t)$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
u(t) &= 850 \cdot (1 + 0.12)^{t} \\
&= 850 \cdot 1.12^{t}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Constructing Exponential Models Given Percentage Increases Greater Than 100%

**Example:** During a charity livestream, the event had already received $\[math]60$ in donations when it went live. An exponential model estimates that at the end of each hour after the livestream began, the total amount of money donated increased by $140\%$ of the amount from the previous hour.

Write equation that best represents this model, where $D(t)$ is the estimated total amount of money, in dollars, donated $t$ hours after the livestream began and $t \leq 5$.

**Explanation**

We are told the initial amount of donations was $\[math]60$. The total amount donated increases by $140\%$ of the previous hour’s total each hour.

The formula for exponential growth is

$$
D(t) = a(1+r)^t
$$

In our case, we have the following:

- $a=60$ is the initial amount of money received when the livestream began.
- $r = 1.4$ is the growth rate ($140\%$ expressed as a decimal).
- $t$ is the time, in hours, after the livestream began.

Therefore, the equation that best represents this model is

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(t) &= 60 \cdot (1 + 1.4)^{t} \\
&= 60 \cdot (2.4)^{t}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Constructing Exponential Growth Models Using Fractional Exponents

**Example:** A colony of bacteria is placed in a petri dish. Initially, there are $800$ bacteria. The number of bacteria is expected to double every $6$ hours.

Find the equation that represents the number of bacteria $N(t)$, $t$ hours after the bacteria were first placed in the dish.

**Explanation**

We're told that a colony of bacteria starts with an initial count of $800$ bacteria, and the number of bacteria is expected to double every $6$ hours.

The formula for exponential growth, in terms of a doubling period $p$, is

$$
N(t) = a \cdot 2^{t/p}
$$

In our case, we have the following:

- $a=800$ is the initial number of bacteria.
- The number of bacteria doubles every $6$ hours. Hence, the doubling period is $p=6$.
- $t$ is the time, in hours, after the bacteria were first placed in the dish.

Therefore, the correct expression for the number of bacteria $N(t)$ is

$$
N(t) = 800 \cdot 2^{t/6}
$$

## Constructing and Evaluating Exponential Growth Models

**Example:** A bank account is opened with a balance of $\[math]5,000$. The balance doubles every $6$ years. How much money will be in the account after $18$ years?

**Explanation**

We're told that a bank account starts with $\[math]5,000$, and the balance doubles every $6$ years. We want to find out how much money will be in the account after $18$ years.

The formula for exponential growth, in terms of a doubling period $d$, is

$$
N(t) = a \cdot 2^{t/d}
$$

In our case, we have the following:

- $a=5,000$ is the initial balance.
- The balance doubles every $6$ years. Therefore, the doubling period is $d=6$.
- $t$ is the time, in years, after the account is opened.

Thus, the correct expression for the balance $N(t)$ is

$$
N(t) = 5,000 \cdot 2^{t/6}
$$

Finally, we need to find the balance after $t=18$ years. Substituting $t=18$ into the equation for $N(t)$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
N(18) &= 5, 000 \cdot 2^{18/6} \\
&= 5, 000 \cdot 2^{3} \\
&= 5, 000 \cdot 8 \\
&= 40, 000
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, there will be $\[/math]40,000$ in the account after $18$ years.
