## Introduction

This topic provides practice in interpreting exponential models. We'll analyze the meaning of parameters such as the $y$-intercept, initial value, and decay or growth rates, and determine how these relate to specific points or inequalities within a given context.

<a id="interpreting-the-y-intercept-of-an-exponential-function"></a>
## Interpreting the Y-Intercept of an Exponential Function

**Example:** An ecologist is modeling the population of a certain fish species in a lake. The function $Q(t) = 6,300(0.77)^t$ gives the estimated population of the species, where $t$ is the number of years since $2010$, and $0 \leq t \leq 8$. If $y = Q(t)$ is graphed in the $xy$-plane, which of the following is the best interpretation of the $y$-intercept of the graph in this context?

1. The estimated population of the fish species in $2010$ was $6,300$.
2. The estimated population of the fish species in $2018$ was $6,300$.
3. The minimum estimated population of the fish species between $2010$ and $2018$ was $6,300$.
4. The maximum estimated population of the fish species between $2010$ and $2018$ was $4,851$.

**Explanation**

The $y$-intercept of the graph of a function $y = Q(t)$ in the $xy$-plane is the value of the function at $t = 0$.

Evaluating the function at $t = 0$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q(0) &= 6, 300(0.77)^{0} \\
&= 6, 300(1) \\
&= 6, 300
\end{aligned}
$$

This means that the estimated fish population at $t = 0$ was $6,300$.

In the given context, $t$ represents the number of years since $2010$.
So, $t = 0$ corresponds to the year $2010$.

Therefore, the correct interpretation of the $y$-intercept is as follows:

> The estimated population of the fish species in $2010$ was $6,300$.

## Interpreting the Initial Value of an Exponential Function

**Example:** The function $Q(s) = 4,500 \cdot (0.92)^{s/3}$ represents the remaining charge of a phone battery, in milliamp-hours (mAh), after $s$ hours. What is the battery’s remaining charge at the start?

**Explanation**

We're told that the function

$$
Q(s) = 4,500 \cdot (0.92)^{s/3}
$$

represents the remaining charge, in mAh, after $s$ hours.

The remaining charge at the start corresponds to $s=0$ hours. Hence, we must calculate $Q(0)$.

Substituting $s = 0$ into the function, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q(0) &= 4, 500 \cdot (0.92)^{0/3} \\
&= 4, 500 \cdot (0.92)^{0} \\
&= 4, 500 \cdot 1 \\
&= 4, 500
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the battery’s remaining charge at the start is $4,500$ mAh.

## Interpreting a Value at a Point for an Exponential Function

**Example:** The function $f(t) = 500(0.97)^t$ models the value, in dollars, of a computer monitor from $2012$ through $2022$, where $t$ is the number of years after $2012$. Which of the following is the best interpretation of the statement "$f(5)$ is approximately equal to $429$" in this context?

1. The value of the computer monitor is estimated to be approximately $429$ dollars in $2017$.
2. The value of the computer monitor decreased by approximately $429$ dollars between $2012$ and $2017$.
3. The value of the computer monitor is estimated to be approximately $5$ times lower in $2017$ than in $2012$.
4. The value of the computer monitor is estimated to decrease by approximately $429$ dollars every $5$ years between $2012$ and $2022$.

**Explanation**

The function

$$
f(t) = 500(0.97)^t
$$

gives the estimated dollar value of a computer monitor $t$ years after the year $2012$.

We are told that

$$
f(5) \approx 429
$$

This means that when $t = 5$, the monitor’s value is approximately $429$ dollars.

Since $t = 5$ corresponds to the year $2017$, the best interpretation of the statement "$f(5)$ is approximately equal to $429$" is as follows:

> The value of the computer monitor is estimated to be approximately $429$ dollars in $2017$.

## Identifying an Inequality Corresponding to a Growth or Decay Rate

**Example:** The function
$D(t) = D_0(1 + r)^t$
models the download count of an app $t$ months after release, where $r$ is a constant and $D_0$ is the initial count. If the download count increases by a fixed percentage each month, which of the following must be true?

1. $r > 0$
2. $-1 < r < 0$
3. $r < -1$
4. $-1 < r < 1$

**Explanation**

The given model is an exponential function of the form

$$
D = D_0(1 + r)^t
$$

Here, $D_0$ is the initial count, $r$ is the percent change rate (expressed as a decimal), and $t$ is the number of months after release.

We are told that the download count increases by a fixed percentage every month. This corresponds to *exponential growth*.

In the exponential model

$$
D = D_0(1 + r)^t{:}
$$

- If $r > 0$, the quantity increases (growth).
- If $r = 0$, the quantity stays constant.
- If $-1 < r < 0$, the quantity decreases (decay).
- If $r \leq -1$, then $1 + r \leq 0$, and the expression $(1 + r)^t$ becomes undefined or negative for non-integer or fractional values of $t$. So, these values are not valid in this model.

Since the download count is increasing at a constant percent per month, we must have
$r > 0$.

## Interpreting a Decay Rate Corresponding to a Unit of Time

**Example:** The function $C(t) = 850(0.93)^t$ models the estimated concentration of airborne particles at an altitude of $t$ hundreds of meters above sea level. Which of the following does this model predict?

1. For every increase in altitude by $100$ meters, the concentration of airborne particles decreases by $7\%$.
2. For every increase in altitude by $100$ meters, the concentration of airborne particles decreases by $850$.
3. For every increase in altitude by $1$ meter, the concentration of airborne particles decreases by $7\%$.
4. For every increase in altitude by $1$ meter, the concentration of airborne particles decreases by $850$.

**Explanation**

The given function is in the form

$$
C(t) = a \cdot b^t
$$

which is the standard form of an exponential function. In this case, $a = 850$ and $b = 0.93$.

The constant $b$ determines the *growth or decay rate* of the function:

- If $b > 1$, the function models growth.
- If $0 < b < 1$, the function models decay.

Here, $b = 0.93$, so the function represents *exponential decay*.

To interpret the rate of decay, we subtract the base from $1{:}$

$$
1 - 0.93 = 0.07
$$

This tells us that the quantity *decreases by $7\%$* for every increase of $1$ unit in $t$.

Since $t$ is measured in *hundreds of meters*, an increase of $1$ in $t$ corresponds to an increase of $100$ meters in altitude.

Therefore, this model predicts that for every increase in altitude by $100$ meters, the concentration of airborne particles decreases by $7\%$.
