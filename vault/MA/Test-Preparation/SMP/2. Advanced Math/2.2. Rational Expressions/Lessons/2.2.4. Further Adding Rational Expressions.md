## Introduction

Questions that require adding or subtracting rational expressions with variables and common factors in their denominators test fluency in recognizing common factors. In this topic, we’ll practice combining rational expressions involving common monomial and binomial denominators, including cases where we must first factor some of the denominators.

<a id="adding-rational-expressions-with-common-monomial-factors-in-the-denominators"></a>
## Adding Rational Expressions With Common Monomial Factors in the Denominators

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to $\dfrac{7}{8x}-\dfrac{3}{4x}$, where $x \neq 0$?

1. $\dfrac{1}{8x}$
2. $\dfrac{10}{8x}$
3. $\dfrac{4}{8x}$
4. $-\dfrac{1}{4x}$

**Explanation**

Here, we wish to subtract

$$
\dfrac{7}{8x}-\dfrac{3}{4x}
$$

To do this, we must first obtain a common denominator. As

$$
\operatorname{LCM}(8x,4x)=8x
$$

our common denominator is $8x$. So, we multiply the top and bottom of our second fraction by $2{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{7}{8x} - \frac{3}{4x} &= \frac{7}{8x} - \frac{2}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{4x} \\
&= \frac{7}{8x} - \frac{6}{8x} \\
&= \frac{7 - 6}{8x} \\
&= \frac{1}{8x}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is expression "I."

## Adding Rational Expressions With Common Binomial Factors in the Denominators

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to $\dfrac{w}{w - 8} + \dfrac{w}{6(8 - w)}$, where $w\ne8$?

1. $\dfrac{4w}{6(w - 8)}$
2. $-\dfrac{5w}{6(w - 8)}$
3. $\dfrac{5w}{6(w - 8)}$
4. $\dfrac{2w}{3(w - 8)}$

**Explanation**

Notice that we may factor out a $-1$ from $8 - w$ to obtain

$$
8 - w = -(w - 8)
$$

Therefore, we rewrite the expression as

$$
\dfrac{w}{w - 8} + \dfrac{w}{6(8 - w)} = \dfrac{w}{w - 8} - \dfrac{w}{6(w - 8)}
$$

The least common denominator is $6(w - 8)$, so we multiply the numerator and denominator of the first fraction by $6$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{w}{w - 8} - (w)/(6(w - 8)) &= \frac{6}{6} \cdot \frac{w}{w - 8} - (w)/(6(w - 8)) \\
&= (6w)/(6(w - 8)) - (w)/(6(w - 8)) \\
&= (6w - w)/(6(w - 8)) \\
&= (5w)/(6(w - 8))
\end{aligned}
$$

So the expression simplifies to

$$
\dfrac{5w}{6(w - 8)}
$$

which is expression "III."

## Adding Rational Expressions With Common Binomial Factors Obtained After Factoring

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to $\dfrac{b - 2ub}{2u^2 - 2ub} - \dfrac{1 + b}{u - b}$?

1. $\dfrac{-2ub - 1}{2u^3 - 4u^2b - 2ub^2}$
2. $\dfrac{b - 2u - 4ub - 2u^2}{2u^3 - 4u^2b - 2ub^2}$
3. $\dfrac{b - 2u - ub - 2u^2}{2u^2 - 2ub}$
4. $\dfrac{b - 2u - 4ub}{2u^2 - 2ub}$

**Explanation**

First, notice that we have factorable terms in our expression.

- For $b - 2ub$ we have $\operatorname{GCF}(b, 2ub) = b$, and so $b - 2ub = b(1 - 2u)$.
- For $2u^2 - 2ub$ we have $\operatorname{GCF}(2u^2, 2ub) = 2u$, and so $2u^2 - 2ub = 2u(u - b)$.

Next, we rewrite our expression as follows:

$$
\dfrac{b(1 - 2u)}{2u(u - b)} - \dfrac{1 + b}{u - b}
$$

Now, our common denominator is $2u(u - b)$. Multiply the second term by

$$
\dfrac{2u}{2u}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(b(1 - 2u))/(2u(u - b)) - (2u(1 + b))/(2u(u - b)) &= (b(1 - 2u) - 2u(1 + b))/(2u(u - b)) \\
&= (b - 2ub - 2u - 2ub)/(2u(u - b)) \\
&= (b - 2u - 4ub)/(2u(u - b))
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, noticing that all our answer choices have an expanded denominator, we write

$$
\dfrac{b - 2u - 4ub}{2u(u - b)} = \dfrac{b - 2u - 4ub}{2u^2 - 2ub}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is expression "IV."
