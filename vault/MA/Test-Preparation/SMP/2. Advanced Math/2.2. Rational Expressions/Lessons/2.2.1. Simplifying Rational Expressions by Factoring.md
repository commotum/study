## Simplifying Rational Expressions With Common Constant Factors

**Example:** The expression $\dfrac{42}{6x + 48}$ is equivalent to $\dfrac{7}{x + a}$, where $a$ is a constant and $x > 0$. What is the value of $a$?

**Explanation**

We are told that the expression

$$
\dfrac{42}{6x + 48}
$$

is equivalent to

$$
\dfrac{7}{x + a}
$$

for some constant $a$.

First, we factor out the greatest common factor from the denominator and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{42}{6x + 48} &= (42)/(6(x + 8)) \\
&= (6 \cdot 7)/(6(x + 8)) \\
&= (6 \cdot 7)/(6(x + 8)) \\
&= \frac{7}{x + 8}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since, we are told that this expression is equivalent to

$$
\dfrac{7}{x + a}
$$

therefore by matching the denominators, we get $a = 8$.

## Simplifying Rational Expressions With Common Monomial Factors

**Example:** The expression $\dfrac{24x}{16x^2 + 8x}$ is equivalent to $\dfrac{3}{k(2x + 1)}$, where $k$ is a constant and $x \neq 0$. What is the value of $k$?

**Explanation**

We wish to simplify

$$
\dfrac{24x}{16x^2 + 8x}
$$

First, since

$$
\operatorname{GCF}(16x^2,8x)=8x
$$

we factor the denominator:

$$
\dfrac{24x}{16x^2 + 8x}=\dfrac{24x}{8x(2x + 1)}
$$

Now, since

$$
x\neq0
$$

we can simplify by cancelling the factors of $8x$ in the numerator and denominator:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(24x)/(8x(2x + 1)) &= (8x \cdot 3)/(8x(2x + 1)) \\
&= (8x \cdot 3)/(8x(2x + 1)) \\
&= \frac{3}{2x + 1} \\
&= (3)/(1(2x + 1))
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $k=1$.

## Simplifying Rational Expressions With Common Binomial Factors

**Example:** Simplify
$\dfrac{-7x(x + 1) - 4(x + 1)}{x + 1}$,

where $x > -1$?

**Explanation**

We begin by observing that the numerator has a common factor of $(x + 1)$. So, we factor it out and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-7x(x + 1) - 4(x + 1))/(x + 1) &= ((x + 1)(-7x - 4))/(x + 1) \\
&= ((x + 1)(-7x - 4))/(x + 1) \\
&=-7x - 4
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $x > -1$, that means

$$
x + 1 \ne 0
$$

so we are allowed to cancel the common factor $(x + 1)$.

Therefore,

$$
\dfrac{-7x(x + 1) - 4(x + 1)}{x + 1} = -7x - 4
$$
