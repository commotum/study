## Introduction

On the math section of the SAT exam, we may encounter questions that ask to add or subtract rational expressions, often with unlike denominators.

In this topic, we’ll practice turning sums and differences into a single simplified fraction by creating least common denominators, as well as distributing and canceling common factors.

## Adding a Fraction to a Rational Expression

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to $\dfrac{3}{m}+\dfrac{5}{4}$, where $m\neq0$?

1. $\dfrac{12+5m}{4m}$
2. $\dfrac{3+20m}{4m}$
3. $\dfrac{12+5m}{4}$
4. $\dfrac{15+4m}{4m}$

**Explanation**

Here, we wish to add

$$
\dfrac{3}{m}+\dfrac{5}{4}
$$

To do this, we must first obtain a common denominator. As $m$ is a variable, our common denominator is

$$
m\cdot 4=4m
$$

and so we multiply the top and bottom of our first fraction by $4$, and multiply the top and bottom of our second fraction by $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3}{m} + \frac{5}{4} &= \frac{4}{4} \cdot \frac{3}{m} + \frac{m}{m} \cdot \frac{5}{4} \\
&= \frac{12}{4m} + \frac{5m}{4m} \\
&= \frac{12 + 5m}{4m}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since the result is already simplified, this is our final answer which is expression "I."

## Adding Rational Expressions and Monomials

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to $3x - \dfrac{x + y}{x}$, where $x > 0$?

1. $\dfrac{2x + y}{x}$
2. $\dfrac{3x^2 + x - y}{x}$
3. $\dfrac{3x^2 - x - y}{x}$
4. $\dfrac{3x^2 - x + y}{x}$

**Explanation**

Notice that all the answer choices are single fractions, so we start by obtaining a common denominator for our expression. Since

$$
\operatorname{LCM}(1, x) = x
$$

our common denominator is $x$. We may rewrite our expression by multiplying the numerator and denominator of our first term by $x$ and simplifying as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x - \frac{x + y}{x} &= \frac{x}{x} \cdot 3x - \frac{x + y}{x} \\
&= \frac{3x^{2}}{x} - \frac{x + y}{x} \\
&= (3x^{2} - (x + y))/(x) \\
&= \frac{3x^{2} - x - y}{x}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is expression "III."

## Adding Rational Expressions

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to $\dfrac{4}{3y+1}+\dfrac{5}{y-2}$?

1. $\dfrac{19y + 3}{(3y+1)(y-2)}$
2. $-\dfrac{19y - 3}{(3y+1)(y-2)}$
3. $\dfrac{3y - 19}{(3y+1)(y-2)}$
4. $\dfrac{19y - 3}{(3y+1)(y-2)}$

**Explanation**

First, we observe that the denominators are not factorable, and the expressions are already in lowest terms. To simplify this sum, we must obtain a common denominator. Since

$$
\operatorname{GCF}(3y+1, y-2) = 1
$$

our common denominator is $(3y+1)(y-2)$. We rewrite the expression as:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{4}{3y + 1} + \frac{5}{y - 2} &= (4(y - 2))/((3y + 1)(y - 2)) + (5(3y + 1))/((3y + 1)(y - 2)) \\
&= (4y - 8 + 15y + 5)/((3y + 1)(y - 2)) \\
&= (19y - 3)/((3y + 1)(y - 2))
\end{aligned}
$$

So our equivalent expression is

$$
\dfrac{19y - 3}{(3y+1)(y-2)}
$$

which is expression "IV."

## Adding Rational Expressions After Simplifying

**Example:** Which expression is equivalent to
$\dfrac{4s + 8}{r - 4} + \dfrac{s(r - 4)}{r^2 s - 4 r s}$,
where $r > 4$?

1. $\dfrac{4rs + 9r + 4}{r(r - 4)}$
2. $\dfrac{4rs + 9r - 4}{r(r - 4)}$
3. $\dfrac{4s + 8 + r - 4}{r(r - 4)}$
4. $\dfrac{4s(r - 4) + 1}{r(r - 4)}$

**Explanation**

We are asked to simplify the expression

$$
\dfrac{4s + 8}{r - 4} + \dfrac{s(r - 4)}{r^2 s - 4 r s}
$$

First, we simplify the first term by factoring out $4$ from the numerator:

$$
\dfrac{4s + 8}{r - 4} = \dfrac{4(s + 2)}{r - 4}
$$

Next, we simplify the second term by factoring out $rs$ from the denominator:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(s(r - 4))/(r^{2}s - 4rs) &= (s(r - 4))/(rs(r - 4)) \\
&= (s(r - 4))/(rs(r - 4)) \\
&= \frac{1}{r}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $r > 4$, we know

$$
r-4 \ne 0
$$

, we can cancel the common factor $(r - 4)$.

Now we add the final form of the two terms:

$$
\dfrac{4(s + 2)}{r - 4} + \dfrac{1}{r}
$$

To add the rational expressions, we first find a common denominator, which is the product of the two denominators $x(r - 4)$.

Now we rewrite each fraction with the common denominator, then combine the expressions by adding the numerators over the common denominator and simplifying:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4(s + 2))/(r - 4) + \frac{1}{r} &= (4r(s + 2))/(r(r - 4)) + (r - 4)/(r(r - 4)) \\
&= (4r(s + 2) + (r - 4))/(r(r - 4)) \\
&= (4rs + 8r + r - 4)/(r(r - 4)) \\
&= (4rs + 9r - 4)/(r(r - 4))
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\dfrac{4s + 8}{r - 4} + \dfrac{s(r - 4)}{r^2 s - 4 r s} = \dfrac{4rs + 9r - 4}{r(r - 4)}
$$

So, the correct answer is expression "II."
