## Interpreting a Quadratic Function's Initial Value

**Example:** A basketball is thrown upward from a platform. The equation $h = -5.3 t^{2} + 16 t + 24$ represents this situation, where $h$ is the height of the basketball above the ground, in meters, $t$ seconds after it is thrown. Which number represents the height, in meters, from which the basketball was thrown?

**Explanation**

We are told that the equation

$$
h = -5.3t^{2} + 16t + 24
$$

gives the height of the basketball $t$ seconds after it is thrown.

The initial height from which the basketball was thrown is its height at time $t = 0$.

Thus, after substituting this value into the given equation, we find that the initial height is

$$
\begin{aligned}
h &= -5.3t^{2} + 16t + 24 \\
&=-5.3(0)^{2} + 16(0) + 24 \\
&= 24
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the basketball was thrown from a height of $24$ meters.

## Interpreting the Value of a Quadratic Function at a Point

**Example:** The height, in meters, of a drone as it ascends during flight can be modeled by the function $h(t) = -4t^{2} + 52t + 1$. Which of the following statements best interprets the equation $h(12) = 49$?

1. The height of the drone $12$ seconds after it begins flying is $49$ meters.
2. The height of the drone $49$ seconds after it begins flying is $12$ meters.
3. The drone reaches a maximum height of $49$ meters at $12$ seconds.
4. The drone’s average speed over the first $12$ seconds is $49$ meters per second.

**Explanation**

The function

$$
h(t) = -4t^{2} + 52t + 1
$$

models the height of a drone, in meters, $t$ seconds after it begins flying.

Substituting $t = 12$ into the formula gives $h(12)$, which represents the height of the drone $12$ seconds after it begins flying.

Since $h(12) = 49$, this means that the drone is $49$ meters above the ground $12$ seconds after it begins flying.

Therefore, the best interpretation is as follows:

> The height of the drone $12$ seconds after it begins flying is $49$ meters.

## Interpreting the Independent Variable at a Quadratic Function's Vertex

**Example:** $D(t) = -1.4(t - 18)^2 + 210$

A wildlife researcher found that the function above models the population density $D(t)$, in deer per square kilometer, as a function of average temperature $t$ in degrees Celsius. According to this model, what temperature gives the maximum population density?

**Explanation**

We are given a quadratic function that models population density, and we are asked to find the temperature that maximizes it.

The function is a quadratic in vertex form:

$$
D(t) = a(t - h)^2 + k
$$

Here, we have

$$
a = -1.4, \quad h = 18, \quad k = 210
$$

Since the coefficient $a = -1.4$ is negative, the parabola opens downward, and the vertex represents the maximum population density.

Therefore, the temperature that gives the maximum population density is

$$
\begin{aligned}
t &= h \\
&= 18^{∘}Celsius
\end{aligned}
$$

## Interpreting a Quadratic Function's Vertex

**Example:** A drone’s elevation above the ground, in meters, is modeled by the function
$v(t) = -0.4(t - 5)^2 + 20$,
where $t$ is the time in seconds since it took off, for $0 \leq t \leq 10$. Which of the following best describes the vertex of the graph of $y = v(t)$ in the $ty$-plane?

1. The drone’s maximum elevation was $20$ meters above the ground.
2. The drone’s maximum elevation was $5$ meters above the ground.
3. The drone was $20$ meters above the ground at launch.
4. The drone was $5$ meters above the ground at launch.

**Explanation**

The elevation function is a quadratic function with vertex at $(h, k)$, written in vertex form:

$$
v(t) = a(t - h)^2 + k
$$

Here, we have the vertex is at

$$
(h, k) = (5, 20)
$$

Since the coefficient $a = -0.4$ is negative, the parabola opens downward, and the vertex represents the maximum elevation of the drone.

Therefore, the vertex tells us that "The drone’s maximum elevation was $20$ meters above the ground."

## Interpreting a Quadratic Function's Roots

**Example:** A mining drone is used to monitor tunnel conditions. The function
$m(t) = -\dfrac{1}{55}(t - 19)(t + 11)$
gives the height above the mine floor, in meters, of the drone $t$ minutes after it starts a scan, where $t > 0$. How many minutes after starting the scan did it take for the drone to return to the mine floor?

**Explanation**

The drone reaches the mine floor when its height is $m(t) = 0$. So we have

$$
m(t) = -\dfrac{1}{55}(t - 19)(t + 11) = 0
$$

This product equals $0$ when either factor is $0$. That gives

$$
(t - 19) = 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad t = 19
$$

or

$$
(t + 11) = 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad t = -11
$$

We discard $t = -11$, because $t > 0$.

Therefore, the drone reaches the mine floor after

$$
t = 19 \text{minutes}
$$
