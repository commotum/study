Example: Interpreting the Slope of a Best-Fit Line
?

The scatterplot above shows the heights and number of tomatoes harvested by 10 tomato plants. A line of best fit for the data is also shown. Which of the following statements best describes the association between the height and the number of tomatoes harvested?

  1. As the height increases, the crop yield tends to increase.
  2. As the height increases, the crop yield tends to decrease.
  3. As the height decreases, the crop yield tends to increase.
  4. There is no association between the height and the crop yield.
EXPLANATION

We are given a scatterplot that shows how the number of tomatoes harvested varies with the plant's height, along with a line of best fit. Each point represents a measurement of the crop yield of a tomato plant with a specific height.

From the graph, we observe that as height increases along the horizontal axis, the crop yield increases along the vertical axis. This upward trend is confirmed by the line of best fit, which slopes upward.
