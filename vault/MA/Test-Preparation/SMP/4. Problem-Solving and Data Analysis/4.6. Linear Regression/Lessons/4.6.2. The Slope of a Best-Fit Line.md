## Interpreting the Slope of a Best-Fit Line

**Example:** The scatterplot above shows the heights and number of tomatoes harvested by 10 tomato plants. A line of best fit for the data is also shown. Which of the following statements best describes the association between the height and the number of tomatoes harvested?

![](<../Source/4.6.2. The Slope of a Best-Fit Line/Images/a664673be5644e13d327e6be50ce15e0.png>)

1. As the height increases, the crop yield tends to increase.
2. As the height increases, the crop yield tends to decrease.
3. As the height decreases, the crop yield tends to increase.
4. There is no association between the height and the crop yield.

**Explanation**

We are given a scatterplot that shows how the number of tomatoes harvested varies with the plant's height, along with a line of best fit. Each point represents a measurement of the crop yield of a tomato plant with a specific height.

From the graph, we observe that as height increases along the horizontal axis, the crop yield increases along the vertical axis. This upward trend is confirmed by the line of best fit, which slopes upward.

An upward-sloping trend line shows a positive association, meaning that as the explanatory variable height increases, the response variable crop yield tends to increase.

Therefore, the correct interpretation is
$\text{As the height increases, the crop yield tends to increase.}$

## Determining a Possible Slope of a Best-Fit Line

**Example:** Which of the following is closest to the slope of this line of best fit?

![](<../Source/4.6.2. The Slope of a Best-Fit Line/Images/bc19b20a3116e4a211a2bacae51448ad.png>)

1. $0.8$
2. $0.5$
3. $0.3$
4. $-0.5$

**Explanation**

We want to find the slope of the line of best fit.

First, we observe from the graph shown that the line of best fit passes through the points $(0, 9)$ and $(5, 13)$.

So, we use these two points to find the slope $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{13 - 9}{5 - 0} \\
&= \frac{4}{5} \\
&= 0.8
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the line of best fit is $0.8$.

## Estimating the Slope of a Best-Fit Line

**Example:** The scatterplot above shows the relationship between variables $x$ and $y$, along with a line of best fit. Which of the following is closest to the slope of the line of best fit shown?

![](<../Source/4.6.2. The Slope of a Best-Fit Line/Images/c133196def9400c2abb472bb214dd4a6.png>)

1. $-0.98$
2. $-0.50$
3. $0.90$
4. $1.90$

**Explanation**

To estimate the slope of the line of best fit, we pick two points on the line (not from the scattered data).

First, we observe from the graph shown that the line of best fit passes approximately through the points

$$
(x_1, y_1) = (0, 12)
$$

and

$$
(x_2, y_2) = (8, 4)
$$

So, we use these two points to find the slope $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} \\
&= \frac{4 - 12}{8 - 0} \\
&=-\frac{8}{8} \\
&=-1
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, among the choices, the slope closest to our estimated value of $-1$ for the line of best fit is $-0.98$.
