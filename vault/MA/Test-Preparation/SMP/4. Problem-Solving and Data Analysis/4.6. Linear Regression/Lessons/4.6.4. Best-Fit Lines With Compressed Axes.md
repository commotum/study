## Finding the Equation of a Best-Fit Line

**Example:** The scatterplot shows the gas mileage and the average speed for $7$ fuel-efficient vehicles, where $v$ represents the average speed, in miles per hour (mph), and $g$ represents the gas mileage, in miles per gallon (mpg). A line of best fit for the data is also shown. Which of the following could be an equation of the line of best fit?

![](<../Source/4.6.4. Best-Fit Lines With Compressed Axes/Images/8c3c95432d49bc34df81093c4ff43fb4.png>)

1. $g = -0.12v + 48$
2. $g = -0.12v - 48$
3. $g = 0.12v + 48$
4. $g = 0.12v - 48$

**Explanation**

We are asked to identify an equation that represents the line of best fit. This line has the form:

$$
g = mv + b
$$

where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $d$-intercept.

From the graph, we note that the line passes approximately through the points

$$
(v_1, g_1) = (75, 39)
$$

and

$$
(v_2, g_2) = (100, 36)
$$

So, we use these two points to find the slope $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{g_{2} - g_{1}}{v_{2} - v_{1}} \\
&= \frac{36 - 39}{100 - 75} \\
&=-\frac{3}{25} \\
&\approx - 0.12
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we substitute a point on the line, such as $(100, 36)$, into the equation to solve for $b{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
g &= mv + b \\
36 &= -0.12(100) + b \\
36 &= -12 + b \\
b &= 48
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation that represents the line of best fit is

$$
g = -0.12v + 48
$$

## Finding the Equation of a Best-Fit Line From a Scatter Plot

**Example:** The scatterplot shows the miles driven $d$ (in hundreds) and fuel remaining $F$ (in liters). Which of the following could be an equation for a line of best fit for the data?

![](<../Source/4.6.4. Best-Fit Lines With Compressed Axes/Images/2e03ccfcd06894ddfef36f3bca55ecbd.png>)

1. $F=-5d+60$
2. $F=-5d+40$
3. $F=-2d+60$
4. $F=5d-60$

**Explanation**

We are asked to identify the equation whose graph most closely represents the line of best fit for the data shown in the form

$$
F = md + b
$$

where $d$ is the slope and $b$ is the $F$-intercept.

From the shape of the data points, the line of best fit should pass roughly through the points

$$
(d_1, F_1) = (2, 50)
$$

and

$$
(d_2, F_2) = (4, 40)
$$

Thus, these two points can be used to find an approximate slope for the line of best fit:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{F_{2} - F_{1}}{d_{2} - d_{1}} \\
&= \frac{40 - 50}{4 - 2} \\
&=-\frac{10}{2} \\
&=-5
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, to estimate the $F$-intercept, we substitute a point on the line, such as $(2, 50)$, into the equation to solve for $b{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
F &= md + b \\
50 &= -5(2) + b \\
50 &= -10 + b \\
b &= 60
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, among the options, the most appropriate model for the line of best fit of the data shown is

$$
F=-5d+60
$$
