## Introduction

Often, questions about best-fit lines test the ability to interpret and model data using linear relationships.

In this topic, we’ll practice identifying equations of best-fit lines by estimating both the slope and the intercept.

## Identifying the Equation of a Line of Best Fit

**Example:** The scatterplot shows the relationship between two variables, $x$ and $y$. A line of best fit is also shown. Which of the following equations best represents the line of best fit?

![](<../Source/4.6.3. Identifying Best-Fit Lines/Images/a6bd6891d68681c16e9a8f6f83d7f33e.png>)

1. $y = 0.6x + 2.5$
2. $y = -0.6x + 2.5$
3. $y = 0.6x - 2.5$
4. $y = -0.6x - 2.5$

**Explanation**

We are asked to identify an equation that represents the line of best fit.

From the graph:

- The line goes upward from left to right, so the slope is positive.
- The line appears to cross the $y$-axis at approximately $2.5$, so the $y$-intercept is $2.5$.

Therefore, among the choices, the equation that best represents the line of best fit for the data points is

$$
y = 0.6x + 2.5
$$

## Identifying the Equation of a Line of Best Fit: Word Problems

**Example:** The scatterplot above shows the impact of staffing on response time, where $k$ is the number of support agents on shift and $r$ is the average resolution time in minutes. A line of best fit is also shown. Which of the following could be an equation of the line of best fit shown?

![](<../Source/4.6.3. Identifying Best-Fit Lines/Images/80976171efcfa4775cd6ef53c7725e1b.png>)

1. $r = 87.5 - 2.48\,k$
2. $r = 87.5 + 2.48\,k$
3. $r = -87.5 - 2.48\,k$
4. $r = -87.5 + 2.48\,k$

**Explanation**

We are asked to identify an equation that represents the line of best fit.

From the graph, we observe that:

- The line slopes downward from left to right, decreasing by almost $30$ minutes in $r$ for every $12$-agent increase in $k$. That means
$m=-\frac{30}{12}=-2.5$.
Thus, the slope of the line of best fit should be slightly above $-2.5$.
- The line crosses the $r$-axis at about $90$, so a reasonable $r$-intercept is slightly below $90$.

Therefore, among the choices, the model that best represents the line of best fit is

$$
r = 87.5 - 2.48\,k
$$

## Identifying a Possible Line of Best Fit From a Scatter Plot

**Example:** The scatterplot shows the relationship between two variables, $x$ and $y$. Which of the following equations is the most appropriate linear model for the data shown?

![](<../Source/4.6.3. Identifying Best-Fit Lines/Images/b62125da57c0e2fbbb577e8ec38f8c7b.png>)

1. $y=13.1-0.67x$
2. $y=13.1+0.67x$
3. $y=0.67-13.1x$
4. $y=0.67+13.1x$

**Explanation**

We are asked to identify an equation that represents a line of best fit for the data in the scatterplot.

From the graph:

- The data points in the scatterplot follow a downward trend, meaning the slope must be negative.
- Also, the first data point is near $(0, 13)$, so the $y$-intercept should be close to $13$.

Therefore, among the choices, the most appropriate linear model that best represents the line of best fit for the data points is

$$
y=13.1-0.67x
$$

## Identifying a Possible Line of Best Fit From a Scatter Plot: Word Problems

**Example:** Each dot in the scatterplot above represents the weeks in physical therapy $x$, and the pain score $y$, in points, for each patient visit. The graph of which of the following equations is a line that most closely fits the data?

![](<../Source/4.6.3. Identifying Best-Fit Lines/Images/ac52e9c5b9a978650515cb41a1b8501a.png>)

1. $y = -3.3x + 36$
2. $y = -3.3x - 36$
3. $y = -3.3x + 28$
4. $y = 3.3x + 36$

**Explanation**

We are asked to identify the equation whose graph most closely represents the line of best fit for the data shown.

From the plot:

- The data points appear to have a line of best fit with a positive $y$-intercept, so we can eliminate the choices with negative intercepts, namely $y = -3.3x - 36$ and $y = 1.5x - 6.0$.
- The data points slope downward from left to right, so the slope of the line of best fit should be negative, and we can accordingly eliminate the choice with a positive slope, namely $y = 3.3x + 36$.

Now, we are left with only two choices,

$$
y = -3.3x + 36
$$

and

$$
y = -3.3x + 28
$$

Since the line of best fit appears to cross the $y$-axis near at a larger positive value (closer to $36$ than to $28$), we eliminate

$$
y = -3.3x + 28
$$

We can see that in the following graph.

![](<../Source/4.6.3. Identifying Best-Fit Lines/Images/3d69dd7f4cb53b674683c56e5733774b.png>)

Therefore, the most appropriate model for the line of best fit of the data shown is

$$
y = -3.3x + 36
$$
