## Finding a Probability Given a Frequency Table and the Total

**Example:** Each of the $40$ pets at a shelter was classified as either dog, cat, or rabbit, as shown in the frequency table.

| Pet Type | Frequency |
| --- | ---: |
| Dog | $21$ |
| Cat | $14$ |
| Rabbit | $5$ |

If one of these pets is selected at random, what is the probability of selecting a rabbit?

**Explanation**

We want to find the probability of selecting a rabbit from the shelter.

We are told that there are $40$ pets in total, and from the table, we get that there are $5$ rabbits.

Since the probability of an event is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the number of total possible outcomes, the probability of selecting a rabbit is

$$
P(\textrm{rabbit}) = \dfrac{5}{40}
$$

## Finding a Probability Given a Frequency Table

**Example:** A club recorded which games were played during a tournament. The table shows how many times each game was played. If one game is selected at random from the games played, what is the probability that it is a game of checkers?

| Game | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Chess | $15$ |
| Checkers | $18$ |
| Cards | $17$ |
| Total | $50$ |

**Explanation**

We want to find the probability of selecting checkers from the list of games played.

From the table, we get that there are $50$ games played in total, of which $18$ are games of checkers.

Since the probability of an event is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the number of total possible outcomes, the probability of selecting a game of checkers is

$$
P(\textrm{checkers}) = \dfrac{18}{50}
$$

## Finding a Conditional Probability From a Frequency Table

**Example:** The table above shows how many members of a community fall within each age group. Of those members who are at least $20$ years old, what fraction are at least $40$ years old?

| Age Group | Number of Members |
| --- | ---: |
| $0{-}9$ | $4$ |
| $10{-}19$ | $6$ |
| $20{-}29$ | $9$ |
| $30{-}39$ | $10$ |
| $40{-}49$ | $7$ |
| $50{-}59$ | $3$ |

**Explanation**

We want to find the fraction of members who are at least $40$ years old among those who are at least $20$ years old.

The total number of members who are at least $20$ years old is the sum of the members in the $20{-}29$, $30{-}39$, $40{-}49$, and $50{-}59$ age groups:

$$
9 + 10 + 7 + 3 = 29
$$

On the other hand, the number of members who are at least $40$ years old is the sum of the members in the $40{-}49$ and $50{-}59$ age groups:

$$
7 + 3 = 10
$$

Therefore, the fraction of members who are at least $40$ years old out of those who are at least $20$ years old is

$$
\dfrac{10}{29}
$$
