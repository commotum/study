## Finding a Probability Given a Dataset

**Example:** Consider the following data set of three numbers:
$-4, \: 2, \: 5$
If a number from this data set is selected at random, what is the probability of selecting a positive number?

**Explanation**

The probability of an event is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of possible outcomes.

We are told that a data set contains three numbers: $-4, 2$, and $5$, of which $2$ and $5$ are positive.

Therefore, since the number of favorable outcomes (positive numbers) is $2$, and the total number of possible outcomes (all numbers in the set) is $3$, the desired probability is

$$
P(\textrm{The selected number is positive}) = \dfrac{2}{3}
$$

## Finding a Probability Given Frequencies

**Example:** A bag contains $5$ yellow marbles, $4$ red marbles, and $9$ blue marbles. If one marble is selected at random from the bag, what is the probability of selecting a red marble?

**Explanation**

The probability of an event is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of possible outcomes.

We are told that a bag contains $5$ yellow, $4$ red, and $9$ blue marbles. So, the total number of marbles in the bag is

$$
5 + 4 + 9 = 18
$$

Therefore, since the number of favorable outcomes (red marbles) is $4$, and the total number of possible outcomes (all marbles) is $18$, the desired probability is

$$
P(\textrm{The selected marble is red}) = \dfrac{4}{18} = \dfrac{2}{9}
$$

## Finding a Probability of Complementary Events Given Frequencies

**Example:** A cup contains $6$ black pens, $11$ blue pens, $9$ red pens, and $4$ green pens. If one pen is randomly selected from the cup, what is the probability that it is not black or red?

**Explanation**

The probability of an event is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of possible outcomes.

We are told that a cup contains $6$ black, $11$ blue, $9$ red, and $4$ green pens. The total number of pens in the cup is

$$
6 + 11 + 9 + 4 = 30
$$

We are also told that the randomly selected pen is neither black nor red, which means it must be either blue or green. The number of pens that are either blue or green is

$$
11 + 4 = 15
$$

Therefore, since the number of favorable outcomes (blue or green pens) is $15$, and the total number of possible outcomes (all pens) is $30$, the desired probability is

$$
P(\textrm{The selected pen is not black or red}) = \dfrac{15}{30} = \dfrac{1}{2}
$$
