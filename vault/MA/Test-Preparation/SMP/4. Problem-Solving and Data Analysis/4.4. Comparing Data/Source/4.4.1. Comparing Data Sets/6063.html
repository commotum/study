Employees' Daily Emails Sent
Group Sample size Mean ()[math] Standard deviation ()[/math]

Which statement is true based on the table?

  1. Group included the employee with the highest daily emails sent.
  2. The Group data set was identical to the Group data set.
  3. The daily emails sent in Group had a larger spread than those in Group
  4. The median daily emails sent of Group is greater than the median daily emails sent of Group
EXPLANATION

From the table, we notice that the two groups surveyed have the same sample size and mean value but different standard deviations.

Standard deviation tells us how spread out the data are. A bigger standard deviation means the data are more spread out.

Let’s check which statement correctly interprets this difference in standard deviation:

  • Statement I is false because the table only gives the mean and the standard deviation. It does not show the maximum value for each group.

  • Statement II is false because if two data sets were identical, they would have the same mean and the same standard deviation. While both groups share the same mean, their standard deviations are different.

  • Statement III is true because the daily emails sent in Group have a standard deviation of which is larger than Group ’s This means that the daily emails sent in Group had a larger spread than those in Group

  • Statement IV is false because, without more information about the distribution of the data (for example, whether it is skewed), we cannot compare the medians.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only".
