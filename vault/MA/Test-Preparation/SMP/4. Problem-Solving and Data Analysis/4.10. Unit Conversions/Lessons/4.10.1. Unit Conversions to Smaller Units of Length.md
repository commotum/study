<a id="converting-from-fathoms-to-feet"></a>
## Converting From Fathoms to Feet

**Example:** A cable is being laid underwater to a depth of $20$ fathoms $(\textrm{ftm})$. What is the cable’s depth in feet?

Hint: $1 \, \textrm{ftm} = 6 \, \textrm{ft}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the depth from fathoms to feet.

We are given that there are $6$ feet per fathom, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{6 \, \textrm{ft}}{\textrm{ftm}}
$$

This is our conversion factor.

Now, multiplying $20\,\textrm{ftm}$ by our conversion factor, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
20ftm &= 20ftm \cdot \frac{6ft}{ftm} \\
&= 20ftm \cdot \frac{6ft}{ftm} \\
&= 20 \cdot 6ft \\
&= 120ft
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the cable’s depth is $\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{120}\,\textrm{ft}$.

## Converting From Rods to Yards

**Example:** A property boundary is listed in old survey records as $18$ rods $(\textrm{rd})$. What is this distance in yards?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{rd} = 5.5\,\textrm{yd}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the boundary length from rods to yards.

We are given that there are $5.5$ yards per rod, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{5.5 \, \textrm{yd}}{\textrm{rd}}
$$

This is our conversion factor.

Now, multiplying $18\,\textrm{rd}$ by our conversion factor, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
18rd &= 18rd \cdot \frac{5.5yd}{rd} \\
&= 18rd \cdot \frac{5.5yd}{rd} \\
&= 18 \cdot 5.5yd \\
&= 99yd
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the boundary measures $\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{99}\,\textrm{yd}$.

## Converting From Yards to Feet

**Example:** A parade float travels $46$ yards along the main street. What is this distance in feet?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{yd} = 3\,\textrm{ft}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the distance from yards to feet.

We are given that there are $3$ feet per yard, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{3 \, \textrm{ft}}{\textrm{yd}}
$$

This is our conversion factor.

Now, multiplying $46\,\textrm{yd}$ by our conversion factor, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
46yd &= 46yd \cdot \frac{3ft}{yd} \\
&= 46yd \cdot \frac{3ft}{yd} \\
&= 46 \cdot 3ft \\
&= 138ft
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the distance is $\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{138}\,\textrm{ft}$.

## Converting From Meters to Centimeters

**Example:** A marine biologist uses sonar to measure the depth of a coral reef at $36\,\textrm{m}$. What is this depth in centimeters?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{m} = 100 \, \textrm{cm}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the depth from meters to centimeters.

We are given that there are $100$ centimeters per meter, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{100 \, \textrm{cm}}{\textrm{m}}
$$

This is our conversion factor.

Now, multiplying $36\,\textrm{m}$ by our conversion factor, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
36m &= 36m \cdot \frac{100cm}{m} \\
&= 36m \cdot \frac{100cm}{m} \\
&= 36 \cdot 100cm \\
&= 3, 600cm
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the depth of the coral reef is $3,600\,\textrm{cm}$.
