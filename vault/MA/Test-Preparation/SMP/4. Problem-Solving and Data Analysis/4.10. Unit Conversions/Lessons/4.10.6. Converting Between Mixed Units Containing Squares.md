## Converting Units of Area to Smaller Units

**Example:** An archaeological site spans $1.1\,\textrm{mi}^2$. What is the total area of the site in square yards?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{mi} = 1,760\,\textrm{yd}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the area of the site from square miles to square yards.

We are told that there are $1,760\,\textrm{yd}$ per $\textrm{mi}$, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{1,760\,\textrm{yd}}{\textrm{mi}}
$$

We need to convert this into area units. So, by squaring this quantity and applying the rules of exponents, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\frac{1, 760yd}{mi})^{2} &= \frac{1, 760^{2}yd^{2}}{mi^{2}} \\
&= \frac{3, 097, 600yd^{2}}{mi^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is our conversion factor.

Now, multiplying $1.1\,\textrm{mi}^2$ by our conversion factor, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
1.1mi^{2} &= 1.1mi^{2} \cdot \frac{3, 097, 600yd^{2}}{mi^{2}} \\
&= 1.1mi^{2} \cdot \frac{3, 097, 600yd^{2}}{mi^{2}} \\
&= 1.1 \cdot 3, 097, 600yd^{2} \\
&= 3, 407, 360yd^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the total area of the site is $3,407,360\,\textrm{yd}^2$.

## Converting Units of Area to Larger Units

**Example:** A professional sports complex covers $3,872,000 \, \textrm{yd}^2$. What is the area of the complex in square miles? Round your answer to two decimal places.

Hint: $1\,\textrm{mi} = 1,760\,\textrm{yd}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the area from square yards to square miles.

We are given that there are $1,760$ yards per mile, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{1,760 \, \textrm{yd}}{\textrm{mi}}
$$

We need to convert this into area units. So, by squaring this quantity and applying the rules of exponents, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\frac{1, 760yd}{mi})^{2} &= \frac{1, 760^{2}yd^{2}}{mi^{2}} \\
&= \frac{3, 097, 600yd^{2}}{mi^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

However, since we're converting from a smaller unit of area $(\textrm{yd})^2$ to a larger one $(\textrm{mi})^2$, we need to multiply $3,872,000 \, \textrm{yd}^2$ by the reciprocal of the above:

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{mi}^2}{3,097,600 \, \textrm{yd}^2}
$$

This is our conversion factor.

Now, multiplying $3,872,000 \, \textrm{yd}^2$ by our conversion factor, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
3, 872, 000yd^{2} &= 3, 872, 000yd^{2} \cdot \frac{mi^{2}}{3, 097, 600yd^{2}} \\
&= 3, 872, 000yd^{2} \cdot \frac{mi^{2}}{3, 097, 600yd^{2}} \\
&= \frac{3, 872, 000}{3, 097, 600}mi^{2} \\
&= 1.25mi^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

rounded to two decimal places.

Therefore, the area is approximately $1.25\,\textrm{mi}^2$.

## Converting Between Mixed Units Containing Squared Units of Time

**Example:** A maglev train accelerates at a rate of $5.6 \, \dfrac{\textrm{m}}{\textrm{s}^2}$. What is this acceleration rate, in miles per minute squared, rounded to the nearest tenth?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{mi} = 1,609\,\textrm{m}$

**Explanation**

We are given the units

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{m}}{\textrm{s}^2}
$$

and we want to convert this into

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{min}^2}
$$

We're converting two units instead of just one, so we're going to have to multiply by two conversion factors:

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{m}}{\textrm{s}^2}\cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{min}^2}
$$

One of the conversion factors should change $\textrm{m}$ to $\textrm{mi}$.

$$
\dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{m}}}{\textrm{s}^2}\cdot \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\cancel{\textrm{m}}} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{min}^2}
$$

The other conversion factor should change $\textrm{s}^2$ into $\textrm{min}^2$.

$$
\dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{m}}}{\cancel{\textrm{s}^2}}\cdot \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\cancel{\textrm{m}}} \cdot \dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{s}^2}}{\textrm{min}^2} = \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{min}^2}
$$

So, our conversion formula is

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{m}}{\textrm{s}^2}\cdot \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{m}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{s}^2}{\textrm{min}^2} = \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{min}^2}
$$

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use and apply them.

- The acceleration rate given in the problem statement is $5.6 \, \dfrac{\textrm{m}}{\textrm{s}^2}$.
- We are given that there are $1,609$ meters per mile. Taking the reciprocal, we find that there are $\dfrac{1}{1,609}$ miles per meter.
- There are $60\,\textrm{s}$ per $\textrm{min}$. We can write this as follows:
$\dfrac{60\,\textrm{s}}{\textrm{min}}$
To determine the number of seconds squared in one minute squared, we square this quantity and apply the rules of exponents, as follows:
$(\frac{60s}{min})^{2}|= \frac{60^{2}s^{2}}{min^{2}}; = \frac{3, 600s^{2}}{min^{2}}$

Now, applying our conversion formula, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
5.6\frac{m}{s^{2}} &= 5.6\frac{m}{s^{2}} \cdot \frac{1}{1, 609}\frac{mi}{m} \cdot 3, 600\frac{s^{2}}{min^{2}} \\
&= (5.6 \cdot \frac{1}{1, 609} \cdot 3, 600)\frac{m}{s^{2}} \cdot \frac{mi}{m} \cdot \frac{s^{2}}{min^{2}} \\
&= \frac{5.6 \cdot 3, 600}{1, 609}\frac{mi}{min^{2}} \\
&= \frac{20, 160}{1, 609}\frac{mi}{min^{2}} \\
&\approx 12.5\frac{mi}{min^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

rounded to the nearest tenth.

Therefore, the maglev train is accelerating at a speed of approximately

$$
12.5\,\dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{min}^2}
$$

<a id="converting-between-mixed-units-containing-other-squared-units"></a>
## Converting Between Mixed Units Containing Other Squared Units

**Example:** A painting machine is covering walls at a rate of $480,000\,\dfrac{\textrm{ft}^2}{\textrm{h}}$. Express this rate in square meters per minute. Round your answer to the nearest integer.

Hint: $1\,\textrm{m} = 3.28\,\textrm{ft}$

**Explanation**

We're given the rate in

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{ft}^2}{\textrm{h}}
$$

and we want to convert it to

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{min}}
$$

We'll need to multiply by two conversion factors to change both the area unit ($\textrm{ft}^2$ to $\textrm{m}^2$) and the time unit ($\textrm{h}$ to $\textrm{min}$):

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{ft}^2}{\textrm{h}}\cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{min}}
$$

One of the conversion factors should change $\textrm{ft}^2$ to $\textrm{m}^2$.

$$
\dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{ft}^2}}{\textrm{h}}\cdot \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\cancel{\textrm{ft}^2}} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{min}}
$$

The other conversion factor should change $\textrm{h}$ to $\textrm{min}$.

$$
\dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{ft}^2}}{\cancel{\textrm{h}}}\cdot \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\cancel{\textrm{ft}^2}} \cdot \dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{h}}}{\textrm{min}} = \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{min}}
$$

So, our conversion formula is

$$
\dfrac{\textrm{ft}^2}{\textrm{h}}\cdot \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{ft}^2} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{h}}{\textrm{min}} = \dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{min}}
$$

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use and apply them.

- The painting rate is given as $480,000 \, \dfrac{\textrm{ft}^2}{\textrm{h}}$.
- We are given that there are $3.28$ feet per meter. Taking the reciprocal, we find that there are $\dfrac{1}{3.28}$ meters per foot. We can write this as follows:
$\dfrac{1}{3.28} \dfrac{\textrm{m}}{\textrm{ft}}$
To determine the number of square meters in one square foot, we square this quantity and apply the rules of exponents, as follows:
$(\frac{1}{3.28}\frac{m}{ft})^{2}|= \frac{1^{2}}{3.28^{2}}\frac{m^{2}}{ft^{2}}; = \frac{1}{10.7584}\frac{m^{2}}{ft^{2}}$
- There are $60$ minutes per hour, or $\dfrac{1}{60}$ hours per minute. We can write this as follows:
$\dfrac{1}{60} \dfrac{\textrm{h}}{\textrm{min}}$

Now, applying our conversion formula, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
480, 000\frac{ft^{2}}{h} &= 480, 000\frac{ft^{2}}{h} \cdot \frac{1}{10.7584}\frac{m^{2}}{ft^{2}} \cdot \frac{1}{60}\frac{h}{min} \\
&= (480, 000 \cdot \frac{1}{10.7584} \cdot \frac{1}{60})\frac{ft^{2}}{h} \cdot \frac{m^{2}}{ft^{2}} \cdot \frac{h}{min} \\
&= \frac{480, 000}{10.7584 \cdot 60}\frac{m^{2}}{min} \\
&= \frac{480, 000}{645.504}\frac{m^{2}}{min} \\
&\approx 744\frac{m^{2}}{min}
\end{aligned}
$$

rounded to the nearest integer.

Therefore, the machine is painting at a rate of approximately

$$
744\,\dfrac{\textrm{m}^2}{\textrm{min}}
$$
