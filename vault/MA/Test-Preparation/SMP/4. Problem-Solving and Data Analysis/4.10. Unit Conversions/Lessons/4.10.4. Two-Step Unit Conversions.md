## Converting Units of Volume to Smaller Units

**Example:** How many fluid ounces are equivalent to $25\,\textrm{qt}$?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{cup} = 8\,\textrm{fl oz}$ and $1\,\textrm{qt} = 4 \, \textrm{cups}$

**Explanation**

We want to convert the volume from quarts to fluid ounces.

We are also given that there are $8$ fluid ounces per cup, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{8 \, \textrm{fl oz}}{\textrm{cup}}
$$

In the same way, we are given that there are $4$ cups per quart, which we can write as follows:

$$
\dfrac{4 \, \textrm{cups}}{\textrm{qt}}
$$

To convert quarts to fluid ounces, we multiply the given value by the conversion factors above. So, multiplying $25\,\textrm{qt}$ by our conversion factors, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
25qt &= 25qt \cdot \frac{8fl oz}{cup} \cdot \frac{4cups}{qt} \\
&= 25qt \cdot \frac{8fl oz}{cup} \cdot \frac{4cups}{qt} \\
&= 25 \cdot 8 \cdot 4fl oz \\
&= 800fl oz
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $25\,\textrm{qt}$ is equivalent to $\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{800}\,\textrm{fl oz}$.

## Converting Units of Length to Smaller Units

**Example:** A race is $44$ furlongs $(\textrm{fur})$ long. How many feet is this distance?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{fur} = 220\,\textrm{yd}$ and $1\,\textrm{yd} = 3\,\textrm{ft}$

**Explanation**

We are given the units $\textrm{fur}$, and we want to convert this into $\textrm{ft}$. We're converting two units instead of just one, so we're going to have to multiply by two conversion factors:

$$
\textrm{fur}\cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \textrm{ft}
$$

One of the conversion factors should change $\textrm{fur}$ to $\textrm{yd}$.

$$
\cancel{\textrm{fur}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{yd}}{\cancel{\textrm{fur}}} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \textrm{ft}
$$

The other conversion factor should change $\textrm{yd}$ into $\textrm{ft}$.

$$
\cancel{\textrm{fur}} \cdot \dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{yd}}}{\cancel{\textrm{fur}}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{ft}}{\cancel{\textrm{yd}}} = \textrm{ft}
$$

So, our conversion formula is

$$
\textrm{fur} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{yd}}{\textrm{fur}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{ft}}{\textrm{yd}} = \textrm{ft}
$$

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use and apply them.

- The length given in the problem statement is $44 \, \textrm{fur}$.
- We are given that there are $220$ yards per furlong.
- We are also given that there are $3$ feet per yard.

Applying our conversion formula, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
44fur &= 44fur \cdot 220\frac{yd}{fur} \cdot 3\frac{ft}{yd} \\
&= (44 \cdot 220 \cdot 3)fur \cdot \frac{yd}{fur} \cdot \frac{ft}{yd} \\
&= 29, 040ft
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $44\,\textrm{fur}$ is equivalent to $29,040\,\textrm{ft}$.

## Converting Units of Length to Larger Units

**Example:** A group of friends is hiking a trail. Its steepest section is $880\,\textrm{ft}$ long. How long is this section in miles?

Hint: $1\,\textrm{yd} = 3\,\textrm{ft}$ and $1\,\textrm{mi} = 1,760\,\textrm{yd}$

**Explanation**

We are given the units $\textrm{ft}$, and we want to convert this into $\textrm{mi}$. We're converting two units instead of just one, so we're going to have to multiply by two conversion factors:

$$
\textrm{ft}\cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \textrm{mi}
$$

One of the conversion factors should change $\textrm{ft}$ to $\textrm{yd}$.

$$
\cancel{\textrm{ft}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{yd}}{\cancel{\textrm{ft}}} \cdot \,\dfrac{?}{?} = \textrm{mi}
$$

The other conversion factor should change $\textrm{yd}$ into $\textrm{mi}$.

$$
\cancel{\textrm{ft}} \cdot \dfrac{\cancel{\textrm{yd}}}{\cancel{\textrm{ft}}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\cancel{\textrm{yd}}} = \textrm{mi}
$$

So, our conversion formula is

$$
\textrm{ft} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{yd}}{\textrm{ft}} \cdot \dfrac{\textrm{mi}}{\textrm{yd}} = \textrm{mi}
$$

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use and apply them.

- The length given in the problem statement is $880 \, \textrm{ft}$.
- We are given that there are $3$ feet per yard. Taking the reciprocal, we find that there are $\dfrac{1}{3}$ yards per foot.
- We are also given that there are $1,760$ yards per mile. Taking the reciprocal, we find that there are $\dfrac{1}{1,760}$ miles per yard.

Applying our conversion formula, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
880ft &= 880ft \cdot \frac{1}{3}\frac{yd}{ft} \cdot \frac{1}{1, 760}\frac{mi}{yd} \\
&= (880 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{1, 760})ft \cdot \frac{yd}{ft} \cdot \frac{mi}{yd} \\
&= (\frac{880}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{1, 760})mi \\
&= \frac{1}{6}mi
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the steepest section is

$$
\dfrac{1}{6}\,\textrm{mi}
$$

long.

## Converting Between Currencies and Finding a Markdown

**Example:** Mateo has $310$ British pounds $(\textrm{£}310)$ that he wants to exchange for US dollars at a currency kiosk. The exchange rate is $\textrm{£}1 = \[math]1.34$. The kiosk charges a $2.5\%$ fee on the converted US dollar amount. How many US dollars will Mateo receive after the $2.5\%$ fee?

**Explanation**

We are told that $\textrm{£}1$ is equivalent to $\[math]1.34$. So, to convert $\textrm{£}310$ to US dollars, we multiply it by the exchange rate:

$$
310 \cdot 1.34 = 415.4
$$

Therefore, $\textrm{£}310$ is equivalent to $\[/math]415.4$.

Now, because Mateo pays a $2.5\%$ fee on the converted amount, he receives

$$
(100 - 2.5)\% = 97.5\%
$$

of the converted US dollars.

Finally, since

$$
415.4 \cdot 97.5\% = 415.4 \cdot 0.975 = 405.015
$$

we conclude that Mateo receives a total of $\[math]405.015$ after the conversion.
