## Finding the Median From a Box Plot

**Example:** The box plot above shows the distribution of the number of hours of sleep per night for $21$ adults. What is the median number of hours of sleep?

![](<../Source/4.3.4. Box Plots/Images/c0ec6e53204df8bb3f8c1688aa4894f2.png>)

**Explanation**

In a box plot, the median of the data set is represented by the thick line inside the box.

In this case, the median is located at $7$.

Therefore, the median number of hours of sleep is $7$.

1. $119$
2. $55$
3. $141$
4. $175$

**Explanation**

## Comparing Medians Given Two Box Plots

**Example:** The box plots summarize the lengths, in centimeters, of two groups of river fish.

![](<../Source/4.3.4. Box Plots/Images/e58e9ceb9dea11ae00119a8505ba6bd9.png>)

Which of the following statements must be true?

1. The median length of Group $B$ is greater than the median length of Group $A$.
2. The median length of Group $A$ is greater than the median length of Group $B$.
3. The mean length of Group $A$ is greater than the mean length of Group $B$.
4. The mean length of Group $B$ is greater than the mean length of Group $A$.

**Explanation**

In a box plot:

- The median is the thick line inside the box, so medians can be read directly.
- The mean cannot be determined from a box plot alone.

From the plots:

- Group $A$’s median is at $33 \text{cm}$.
- Group $B$’s median is at $37 \text{cm}$.

Therefore, the median for Group $B$ is greater than the median for Group $A$.

## Finding the Difference in Ranges Given Two Box Plots

**Example:** The two box plots show the distribution of fuel efficiencies (in miles per gallon) measured for two car models.

What is the positive difference between the ranges of the fuel efficiencies for the two models?

![](<../Source/4.3.4. Box Plots/Images/04698e160f698aa41d6a6d63f3dfc063.png>)

**Explanation**

The range of a data set equals the difference between the maximum and the minimum.

- For Model $A$, the maximum is $42$ and the minimum is $22$, so the range is
$\text{Range}(A) = 42 - 22 = 20$.
- For Model $B$, the maximum is $38$ and the minimum is $18$, so the range is
$\text{Range}(B) = 38 - 18 = 20$.

Subtracting, we obtain

$$
\bigl\begin{vmatrix}\text{Range}(A) - \text{Range}(B)\bigr & = & 20 - 20\end{vmatrix} = 0
$$

Therefore, the positive difference is $\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{0}$.
