## Calculating a Percentage Increase Between Integers

**Example:** The number of employees at a startup increased from $25$ to $125$. What was the percent increase in employees?

**Explanation**

To find the percent increase, we first calculate the absolute increase in quantity:

$$
125 - 25 = 100
$$

Now, we compute the percent increase by dividing the increase by the original amount:

$$
\dfrac{100}{25} = 4
$$

Finally, we convert to a percentage:

$$
4 \times 100 = 400\%
$$

So, the number of employees increased by $400\%$.

## Calculating a Percentage Increase Between Decimals

**Example:** An hour ago, the download speed of an Internet connection was $24.0\text{Mbps}$. Now the download speed is $30.0\text{Mbps}$. To the nearest tenth of a percent, by what percent did the speed increase?

**Explanation**

To find the percent increase, we start by computing the absolute increase in price:

$$
30.0-24.0=6.0
$$

Now, we compute the percent increase by dividing the increase by the original value:

$$
\frac{6.0}{24.0}=0.25
$$

Finally, we convert to a percentage:

$$
0.25\times 100\%=25.0\%
$$

So, the speed increased by $25.0\%$.

## Calculating a Percentage Increase From a Table

**Example:** A table shows the average annual rainfall in a region for six different years.

| Year | Average rainfall (cm) |
| --- | ---: |
| $1985$ | $100$ |
| $1995$ | $120$ |
| $2005$ | $150$ |
| $2010$ | $180$ |
| $2015$ | $200$ |
| $2025$ | $220$ |

What was the percentage increase in rainfall from $1995$ to $2010$?

**Explanation**

To find the percentage increase, we use the formula

$$
\text{Percentage Increase} = \frac{\text{New Value} - \text{Original Value}}{\text{Original Value}} \times 100\%
$$

The original value is $120$ (in $1995$), and the new value is $180$ (in $2010$). Substituting these values, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
Percentage Increase &= \frac{180 - 120}{120} \cdot 100% \\
&= \frac{60}{120} \cdot 100% \\
&= 0.50 \cdot 100% \\
&= 50%
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the rainfall increased by $50\%$ from $1995$ to $2010$.

## Calculating a Percentage Increase Over 100%

**Example:** $90$ is $k\%$ greater than $20$. What is the value of $k$?

**Explanation**

We are told that $90$ is $k\%$ greater than $20$, so the total increase is

$$
20 + \dfrac{k}{100} \cdot 20
$$

That gives us the equation:

$$
20 + \dfrac{k}{100} \cdot 20 = 90
$$

Now, we solve for $k{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
20 + \frac{k}{100} \cdot 20 &= 90 \\
\frac{k}{100} \cdot 20 &= 90 - 20 \\
\frac{k}{100} \cdot 20 &= 70 \\
\frac{k}{100} &= \frac{70}{20} \\
\frac{k}{100} &= 3.5 \\
k &= 350
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $k$ is $350$.
