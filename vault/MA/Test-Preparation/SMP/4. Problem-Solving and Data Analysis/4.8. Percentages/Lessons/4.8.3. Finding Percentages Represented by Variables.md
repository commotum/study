## Finding a Ratio of Two Numbers as a Percentage

**Example:** An artist earned $\[math]20$ from a print sale. This amount represented $p\%$ of the total print price of $\[/math]25$. What is the value of $p$?

**Explanation**

We are told that an earning of $\[math]20$ is $p\%$ of the print’s $\[/math]25$ price.

First, we express $p\%$ of $25$ as a fraction and set it equal to $20$:

$$
\frac{p}{100} \cdot 25 = 20
$$

Now, we solve for $p{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{p}{100} \cdot 25 &= 20 \\
\frac{p}{100} &= \frac{20}{25} \\
\frac{p}{100} &= 0.8 \\
p &= 0.8 \cdot 100 \\
p &= 80
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $p$ is ${80}$.

## Finding a Percentage Greater Than 100%

**Example:** An athlete increased the weight lifted during training from $160$ pounds to $192$ pounds. The new weight was $p\%$ of the original weight. What is the value of $p$?

**Explanation**

We are told that $192$ pounds was $p\%$ of $160$ pounds.

First, we express $p\%$ of $160$ as a fraction and set it equal to $192$:

$$
\frac{p}{100} \cdot 160 = 192
$$

Now, we solve for $p{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{p}{100} \cdot 160 &= 192 \\
\frac{p}{100} &= \frac{192}{160} \\
\frac{p}{100} &= 1.2 \\
p &= 1.2 \cdot 100 \\
p &= 120
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $p$ is $120$.

## Finding a Reciprocal Percentage Given an Associated Growth Factor

**Example:** The total rainfall recorded after $8$ weeks was $1.42$ times the total rainfall recorded after $4$ weeks. If the rainfall after $4$ weeks is $z\%$ of the rainfall after $8$ weeks, what is the value of $z$, to the nearest whole number?

**Explanation**

Let $R_{4}$ be the total rainfall after $4$ weeks, and $R_{8}$ be the total rainfall after $8$ weeks.

We are told that the rainfall after $8$ weeks was $1.42$ times the rainfall after $4$ weeks. This can be expressed as

$$
R_{8} = 1.42 \cdot R_{4}
$$

or equivalently,

$$
R_{4} = {\color{blue}\dfrac{1}{1.42}} \cdot R_{8}
$$

Now, if the rainfall after $4$ weeks is $z\%$ of the rainfall after $8$ weeks, we obtain the equation

$$
R_{4} = {\color{blue}\dfrac{z}{100}} \cdot R_{8}
$$

Comparing both equations for $R_{4}$, we obtain

$$
{\color{blue}\dfrac{z}{100}} = {\color{blue}\dfrac{1}{1.42}}
$$

Finally, solving for $z$ in the last equation, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
z &= \frac{100}{1.42} \\
&= 70.422… \\
&\approx 70
\end{aligned}
$$

rounded to the nearest whole number.
