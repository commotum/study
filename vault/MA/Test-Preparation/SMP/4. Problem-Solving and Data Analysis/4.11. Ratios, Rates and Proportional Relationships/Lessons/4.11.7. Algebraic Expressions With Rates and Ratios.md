## Introduction

Questions involving algebraic expressions with rates or ratios often require translating a real-world rate into symbolic form and manipulating it correctly.

In this topic, we’ll practice expressing rates and ratios algebraically, forming equivalent expressions, and setting up equations that describe relationships between variables in real contexts.

## Identifying Equivalent Expressions: Word Problems

**Example:** A farm harvests $18.75$ baskets of fruit each hour. If the farm operates for $y$ hours, it harvests $18.75y$ baskets. Which of the following expressions shows how many baskets the farm harvests in $4y$ hours?

1. $18.75(y + 4)$
2. $4(18.75y)$
3. $4 + 18.75y$
4. $4y + 18.75y$

**Explanation**

We are given that, for $y$ hours, the number of baskets harvested is $18.75y$.

Since the harvesting rate is constant, if the farm operates for $4y$ hours, it harvests $4(18.75y)$ baskets.

## Finding a Ratio of Equivalent Expressions: Word Problems

**Example:** A truck carries $50$ bricks per trip. The total number of bricks $B$ carried after $t$ trips can be calculated using $B = 50t$. For any positive constant $a$, the number of bricks carried after $20a$ trips is how many times the number of bricks carried after $4a$ trips?

**Explanation**

The number of bricks carried after $20a$ trips is

$$
B = 50(20a) = 1000a
$$

The number of bricks carried after $4a$ trips is

$$
B = 50(4a) = 200a
$$

Now, we compute how many times larger the first brick count is compared to the second:

$$
\dfrac{1000a}{200a} = 5
$$

Therefore, the number of bricks carried after $20a$ trips is $5$ times the number carried after $4a$ trips.

---

## Finding an Expression for a Value Given a Rate

**Example:** A gardener plants $a$ saplings in $b$ minutes. Which expression represents the number of saplings the gardener plants in $3b$ minutes?

1. $\dfrac{a}{3}$
2. $a + 3$
3. $3a$
4. $a - 3$

**Explanation**

We are given that the gardener plants $a$ saplings every $b$ minutes. This can be represented by the rate

$$
\dfrac{a}{b}
$$

Therefore, for $3b$ minutes, the number of saplings planted is

$$
\dfrac{a}{b} \times 3b = 3a
$$

So, the correct expression is $3a$.

## Finding an Expression for a Value Given a More Complex Rate

**Example:** A printer prints $150t$ pages in $10u$ minutes. Which expression represents the number of pages the printer prints in $25u$ minutes at this rate?

1. $\dfrac{25t}{150}$
2. $375t$
3. $\dfrac{150t}{25}$
4. $150t$

**Explanation**

First, we determine the rate of pages per minute. We are given that the printer prints $150t$ pages in $10u$ minutes. So, the rate is

$$
\dfrac{150t}{10u} = \dfrac{15t}{u}
$$

Next, we find how many pages are printed in $25u$ minutes. To do this, we multiply the rate by the time:

$$
\dfrac{15t}{u} \times 25u = 375t
$$

Therefore, the number of pages printed in $25u$ minutes is $375t$.

## Finding an Equation Given a Rate

**Example:** Leo is managing his home heating more efficiently. For every $10$ cubic meters of natural gas he saves, he reduces his heating bill by $\[math]2$. If he wants to reduce his bill by $\[/math]8$ this month, which equation could he use to determine how many cubic meters, $n$, he needs to save?

**Explanation**

Let $n$ represent the number of cubic meters of natural gas Leo saves.

Each $10$ cubic meters saved reduces his bill by $\[math]2$. That means saving $\[/math]1$ requires saving $5$ cubic meters.

To save $\[math]8$, he needs to save

$$
n = 5 \times 8
$$

To express this relationship as an equation, we write

$$
\dfrac{n}{5} = 8
$$

So, the correct equation is *$\dfrac{n}{5} = 8$*.

---
