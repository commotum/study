## Introduction

Rate problems appear in a variety of contexts (speed, density, cost, and work), often requiring translating a real-world description into a simple division or multiplication of quantities.

In this topic, we’ll practice identifying the correct rate, setting up proportional relationships, and applying them to solve problems.

---

<a id="finding-a-rate"></a>
## Finding a Rate

**Example:** A Ferris wheel completes $1800$ revolutions in $45$ minutes. At this rate, how many revolutions per minute does the Ferris wheel complete?

**Explanation**

We're told that the Ferris wheel completes $1800$ revolutions in $45$ minutes.

To calculate the number of revolutions per minute, we divide the total number of revolutions by the number of minutes:

$$
\dfrac{1800}{45} = 40
$$

Therefore, the Ferris wheel completes $40$ revolutions per minute.

## Problem-Solving by Multiplying by a Rate

**Example:** The table below shows the distribution, by location and power capacity (maximum rate of power generation), of the twenty largest geothermal plants in the United States in $2022$.

| State | Power capacity |  |  |  |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low | Medium | High | Total |  |
| Nevada | $2$ | $3$ | $1$ | $6$ |
| California | $1$ | $1$ | $2$ | $4$ |
| Utah | $1$ | $2$ | $0$ | $3$ |
| Idaho | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ | $2$ |
| Oregon | $1$ | $1$ | $0$ | $2$ |
| New Mexico | $1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ |
| Arizona | $0$ | $1$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| Total | $6$ | $9$ | $5$ | $20$ |

The total power capacity of the three plants located in Utah was $1350$ megawatts, and the total power capacity of the twenty plants was $8450$ megawatts in $2022$. The amount of energy produced in one hour at a rate of one megawatt is one megawatt-hour.

If each of the three Utah plants in $2022$ had operated continuously for $24$ hours at the maximum rate of power generation, approximately how many megawatt-hours of energy would the three plants have produced?

1. $35,000$
2. $32,000$
3. $30,000$

**Explanation**

We are given that the total power capacity of the three plants located in Utah was $1350$ megawatts.

If all three Utah plants operated continuously for $1$ hour, the amount of energy produced would be $1350$ megawatt-hours. So, if all three Utah plants operated continuously for $24$ hours, the amount of energy produced, in megawatt-hours, would be

$$
1350 \cdot 24 = 32,400
$$

Therefore, the three Utah plants would produce $32,400$ megawatt-hours in $24$ hours. Thus, among the given answer options, the value closest to the found value is $32,000$.

## Problem-Solving by Dividing by a Rate

**Example:** The population density of Fairview is $400$ people per square mile. Fairview has a population of $320,000$ people. What is the area, in square miles, of Fairview?

**Explanation**

We are told that Fairview's population density is $400$ people per square mile, and it has a population of $320,000$ people.

To calculate the area of Fairview, we divide the population by the population density:

$$
\dfrac{320,000}{400} = 800
$$

Therefore, the area of Fairview is $800$ square miles.

## Calculating a Rate Given Two Other Rates

**Example:** A type of shampoo has a density of $0.85$ ounce per cubic inch. An online retailer sells this shampoo at $\[math]8.20$ per ounce. What is the selling price, in dollars per cubic inch, for this shampoo purchased from the retailer?

**Explanation**

We are given that the shampoo has a density of $0.85$ ounce per cubic inch and is sold at $\[math]8.20$ per ounce.

To calculate the selling price per cubic inch, we multiply the density (in ounces per cubic inch) by the price per ounce:

$$
0.85 \cdot 8.20 = 6.97
$$

Therefore, the selling price of the shampoo is $6.97$ dollars per cubic inch.
