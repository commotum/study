## Using Equal Proportions to Compute a Coefficient

**Example:** If $\dfrac{a}{b} = 10$ and $\dfrac{50a}{m b} = 5$, what is the value of $m$?

**Explanation**

We are given the relations

- $\dfrac{a}{b} = 10$,
- $\dfrac{50a}{m b} = 5$.

Let's substitute

$$
\dfrac{a}{b}=10
$$

into the second equation and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{50a}{mb} &= 5 \\
\frac{50}{m} \cdot \frac{a}{b} &= 5 \\
\frac{50}{m} \cdot 10 &= 5 \\
\frac{500}{m} &= 5 \\
500 &= 5m \\
m &= \frac{500}{5} \\
m &= 100
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $m$ is $100$.

## Using Unequal Proportions to Compute a Coefficient

**Example:** If $\dfrac{2.7\,m}{n} = 5.4$ and $\dfrac{m}{nt} = 16$, what is the value of $t$?

**Explanation**

We are given two equations

$$
\dfrac{2.7\,m}{n}=5.4 \quad \text{and} \quad \dfrac{m}{nt}=16
$$

First, we solve the first equation for

$$
\dfrac{m}{n}{:}
$$

$$
\dfrac{m}{n}=\dfrac{5.4}{2.7}=2
$$

Now, we substitute

$$
\dfrac{m}{n}=2
$$

into the second equation and solve for $t{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{m}{nt} &= 16 \\
\frac{m}{n} \cdot \frac{1}{t} &= 16 \\
2 \cdot \frac{1}{t} &= 16 \\
\frac{2}{t} &= 16 \\
2 &= 16t \\
t &= \frac{2}{16} \\
t &= 0.125
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $t$ is $0.125$.
