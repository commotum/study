<a id="finding-the-median-of-a-dataset-with-an-odd-number-of-values"></a>
## Finding the Median of a Dataset With an Odd Number of Values

**Example:** $95,\: 97,\: 99,\: 100,\: 101,\: 102,\: 104,\: 106,\: 108$

What is the median of the data values given above?

**Explanation**

The median is the middle value of a data set when the values are arranged in order from smallest to greatest.

To find the median, we first check that the data is in increasing order. In this case, it is already sorted:

$$
95,\: 97,\: 99,\: 100,\: 101,\: 102,\: 104,\: 106,\: 108
$$

There are $9$ data values, which is an odd number. When the number of values is odd, the median is the value in the exact middle. That means we take the $5$th value:

$$
95,\: 97,\: 99,\: 100,\: \bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{101},\: 102,\: 104,\: 106,\: 108
$$

Therefore, the median is $101$.

## Finding the Median of a Dataset With an Even Number of Values

**Example:** $7,\: 14,\: 21,\: 18,\: 35,\: 2,\: 10,\: 27$
What is the median of the data values given above?

**Explanation**

The median is the middle value of a data set when the values are arranged in order from smallest to greatest.

To find the median, we must first order the numbers from least to greatest:

$$
2, \: 7, \: 10, \: 14, \: 18, \: 21, \: 27, \: 35
$$

There are $8$ numbers in the data set. Since $8$ is even, we find the average of the two middle numbers.

$$
2, \: 7, \: 10, \: \bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{14}, \: \bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{18}, \: 21, \: 27, \: 35
$$

The two middle numbers are $14$ and $18$, so the median is

$$
\frac{14 + 18}{2} = \frac{32}{2} = 16
$$

## Identifying Possible Medians Given Value Distributions

**Example:** A city tracks the water usage of $31$ households:

- Group $A$ includes $8$ households that used more than $400$ gallons of water.
- Group $B$ includes $15$ households that used between $150$ and $400$ gallons (inclusive).
- Group $C$ includes $8$ households that used less than $150$ gallons.

Which of the following could be the median water usage?

1. $200$ gallons
2. $300$ gallons
3. $400$ gallons

**Explanation**

There are a total of $31$ households. So, the median is the $16$th value when all usage amounts are listed in increasing order.

Now, let’s look at how the values are distributed.

- The first $8$ values are from Group $C$. These are all less than $150$ gallons. These values occupy positions $1$ through $8$.
- The next $15$ values are from Group $B$, which includes usage between $150$ and $400$ gallons (inclusive). These values occupy positions $9$ through $23$.
- The final $8$ values are from Group $A$, which are all more than $400$ gallons. These values occupy positions $24$ through $31$.

Hence, the $16$th value falls within Group $B$'s usage range and is between $150$ and $400$ gallons.

Therefore, of the given options, $200$ gallons, $300$ gallons, and $400$ gallons are all possible values for the median, and the correct answer is "I, II, and III".
