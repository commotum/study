## Introduction

Similar-figure questions often ask us to use a given scale factor (or infer one from corresponding side pairs) to find a missing length or perimeter.

In this topic, we’ll practice reading the correspondence from descriptions or diagrams and setting up proportions to find unknown lengths.

## Finding Lengths in Similar Triangles Given a Scale Factor

**Example:** Triangles $EFH$ and $JKM$ are similar, with $\overline{EF}$ corresponding to $\overline{JK}$. The perimeter of triangle $EFH$ is $12$ and $JK = 4 EF$. Find the perimeter of triangle $JKM$.

**Explanation**

Recall that similar triangles have proportional corresponding side lengths.

We are told that
$JK = 4 EF$.

Thus, $\triangle JKM$ is an enlargement of $\triangle EFH$ by a scale factor of $4$.

When a triangle is scaled by a factor of $k$, the perimeter also scales by the same factor.

Therefore, the perimeter of $\triangle JKM$ is

$$
p = 4 \cdot 12 = 48
$$

## Finding a Ratio of Side Lengths in Similar Right Triangles

**Example:** Triangles $J K L$ and $U V W$ are shown above. Which of the following is equal to the ratio $\dfrac{K L}{J K}$?

![](<../Source/3.8.3. Finding Corresponding Lengths in Similar Shapes/Images/424924a6557779ceec3bfc24e627897e.png>)

1. $\dfrac{V W}{U V}$
2. $\dfrac{U V}{V W}$
3. $\dfrac{U W}{V W}$

**Explanation**

To solve this problem, we use angle measures to show that triangles $JKL$ and $UVW$ are similar by the AA criterion. This lets us identify corresponding sides and determine the ratio

$$
\dfrac{KL}{JK}
$$

We are given that $\angle J$ is a right angle and

$$
m\angle L = 33^\circ
$$

so we can find $m\angle K$ using the fact that the sum of the interior angles in a triangle is $180^\circ{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m∠K &= 180^{∘} - (m∠J + m∠L) \\
&= 180^{∘} - (90^{∘} + 33^{∘}) \\
&= 180^{∘} - 123^{∘} \\
&= 57^{∘}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, in the triangles $\triangle JKL$ and $\triangle UVW$, there are two pairs of congruent angles:

- $m\angle J = m\angle U = 90^\circ$, and
- $m\angle K = m\angle V = 57^\circ$.

Thus, $\triangle JKL \sim \triangle UVW$ by the *AA similarity criterion*.

Therefore, the ratio

$$
\dfrac{KL}{JK}
$$

corresponds to

$$
\dfrac{V W}{U V}
$$

## Finding Sides in Similar Triangles Given Two Perimeters

**Example:** Triangles $XYZ$ and $ABC$ are similar, with $\overline{BC}$ corresponding to $\overline{YZ}$. The perimeters of the triangles are given in the table above. If $BC = 8$, what is the length of $\overline{YZ}$?

| Triangle | Perimeter |
| --- | ---: |
| $\triangle ABC$ | $36$ |
| $\triangle XYZ$ | $72$ |

**Explanation**

Recall that similar triangles have proportional corresponding side lengths and perimeters.

We are given the following:

- The perimeter of $\triangle XYZ$ is $72$.
- The perimeter of $\triangle ABC$ is $36$.
- Side $\overline{BC}$ corresponds to side $\overline{YZ}$ with $BC = 8$.

The scale factor from $\triangle ABC$ to $\triangle XYZ$ is

$$
k = \frac{72}{36} = 2
$$

Therefore, the length of $\overline{YZ}$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
YZ &= k \cdot BC \\
&= 2 \cdot 8 \\
&= 16
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding Sides in Similar Triangles Given Other Sides

**Example:** Triangle $J K L$ is similar to triangle $R S T$, where $J$, $K$, and $L$ correspond to $R$, $S$, and $T$, respectively. In triangle $J K L$, the length of $\overline{J K}$ is $56$ and the length of $\overline{K L}$ is $84$. In triangle $R S T$, the length of $\overline{R S}$ is $28$. What is the length of $\overline{S T}$?

**Explanation**

Since $\triangle JKL \sim \triangle RST$, it follows that

$$
\frac{KL}{JK} = \frac{ST}{RS}
$$

Therefore, since $JK = 56$, $KL = 84$, and $RS = 28$, we substitute these values and solve for $ST{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{84}{56} &= \frac{ST}{28} \\
\frac{3}{2} &= \frac{ST}{28} \\
ST &= \frac{3}{2} \cdot 28 \\
&= 42
\end{aligned}
$$

## Using Corresponding Angles in Similar Trapezoids

**Example:** In the diagram above, $\overline{AD}$, $\overline{BE}$, and $\overline{CF}$ are parallel. Points $B$ and $E$ lie on $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{DF}$, respectively. If $AB = 10$, $BC = 20$, and $EF = 9$, what is the length of $\overline{DE}$?

![](<../Source/3.8.3. Finding Corresponding Lengths in Similar Shapes/Images/febd0682987704656d240b1ecb695a4b.png>)

**Explanation**

Since $\overline{AD}$, $\overline{BE}$, and $\overline{CF}$ are parallel, we have four pairs of corresponding angles formed by the transversals $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{DF}{:}$

- The angles $\angle{DAB}$ and $\angle{EB C}$ are corresponding, formed by the transveral $\overline{AC}$.
- The angles $\angle{ABE}$ and $\angle{BCF}$ are corresponding, formed by the transveral $\overline{AC}$.
- The angles $\angle{BED}$ and $\angle{CFE}$ are corresponding, formed by the transveral $\overline{DF}$.
- The angles $\angle{E DA}$ and $\angle{F EB}$ are corresponding, formed by the transveral $\overline{DF}$.

Corresponding angles are congruent.

![](<../Source/3.8.3. Finding Corresponding Lengths in Similar Shapes/Images/f48a29ec1f3d0ac04e4457c0a3546328.png>)

As a result, trapezoids $DABE$ and $EBCF$ are similar polygons.

In similar polygons, corresponding sides are proportional. In particular, we have

$$
\dfrac{AB}{BC} = \dfrac{DE}{EF}
$$

Substituting the known values and solving for $DE$, we conclude that

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{10}{20} &= \frac{DE}{9} \\
DE &= \frac{9 \cdot 10}{20} \\
DE &= \frac{90}{20} \\
DE &= 4.5
\end{aligned}
$$
