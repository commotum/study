## Finding the Surface Area of a Right Rectangular Prism Given Its Dimensions

**Example:** A right rectangular prism has dimensions of $4$ inches by $6$ inches by $10$ inches. What is the surface area, in square inches, of the prism?

**Explanation**

To find the surface area $A$ of a right rectangular prism, we use the formula

$$
A = 2(\ell w + \ell h + w h)
$$

where $\ell$, $w$, and $h$ are the length, width, and height of the prism.

Substituting the given values $\ell = 4$, $w = 6$, and $h = 10$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= 2(4 \cdot 6 + 4 \cdot 10 + 6 \cdot 10) \\
&= 2(24 + 40 + 60) \\
&= 2 \cdot 124 \\
&= 248
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the surface area of the prism is $248$ square inches.

## Finding the Surface Area of a Right Rectangular Prism From a Description

**Example:** A metal toolbox is shaped like a right rectangular prism. It has a length of $18~\mathrm{cm}$, a width of $9~\mathrm{cm}$, and a height of $7~\mathrm{cm}$. What is the surface area, in square centimeters $(\mathrm{cm}^2)$, of the toolbox?

**Explanation**

To find the surface area $A$ of a right rectangular prism, we use the formula

$$
A = 2(\ell w + \ell h + w h)
$$

where $\ell$ is the length, $w$ is the width, and $h$ is the height.

Substituting the given values $\ell = 18$, $w = 9$, and $h = 7$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= 2(18 \cdot 9 + 18 \cdot 7 + 9 \cdot 7) \\
&= 2(162 + 126 + 63) \\
&= 2 \cdot 351 \\
&= 702
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the surface area of the toolbox is $702 \, \mathrm{cm}^2$.
