## Introduction

On the math section of the SAT exam, you may be asked to model real-world situations using systems of inequalities. To prepare, we'll practice constructing these models to represent problems in different contexts.

---

<a id="modeling-with-systems-of-two-linear-inequalities"></a>
## Modeling With Systems of Two Linear Inequalities

**Example:** A science teacher is buying $x$ microscopes and $y$ lab kits for student experiments. The teacher needs to purchase at least $16$ items in total, but wants to spend no more than $\[math]600$. If each microscope costs $\[/math]50$ and each lab kit costs $\[math]25$, what is the system of inequalities that best models this situation?

**Explanation**

First, we translate each part of the problem into an inequality:

- There must be at least $16$ in total, so we have
$x + y \geq 16$.
- We're told that the total cost cannot exceed $\[math]600$. Since each microscope costs $\[/math]50$ and each lab kit costs $\[math]25$, we have
$50x + 25y \leq 600$.

Therefore, putting it all together, the system of inequalities that models this situation is

$$
{x + y \ge 16; 50x + 25y \le 600
$$

## Modeling With Systems of Multiple Linear Inequalities

**Example:** A farmer is purchasing $x$ bags of grain and $y$ water tanks for livestock. The farmer must purchase at least $25$ items in total, but wants to spend no more than $\[math]7,000$. Each bag of grain costs $\[/math]120$ and each water tank costs $\[math]350$, and the farmer must buy at least $10$ bags of grain and at least $5$ water tanks. What is the system of inequalities representing this situation?

**Explanation**

First, we translate each condition into an inequality:

- We're told that the total cost cannot exceed $\[math]7,000$. Since each bag of grain costs $\[/math]120$ and each water tank costs $\[math]350$, we have
$120x + 350y \leq 7,000$.
- The total number of items must be at least $25$, so we have
$x + y \geq 25$.
- The farmer must buy at least $10$ bags of grain. Then, we can write
$x \geq 10$.
- The farmer must also buy at least $5$ water tanks, so we have
$y \geq 5$.

Thus, putting it all together, the system of inequalities that represents this situation is

$$
{120x + 350y \le 7, 000; x + y \ge 25; x \ge 10; y \ge 5
$$

## Modeling With Inequalities Containing Variables on Both Sides

**Example:** A company is packing laptops and monitors into shipping crates. Each crate can carry no more than $180$ pounds. Each laptop weighs $5.2$ pounds, and each monitor weighs $12.3$ pounds. The number of monitors must be no more than twice the number of laptops. Let $l$ represent the number of laptops and $m$ represent the number of monitors, where $l$ and $m$ are nonnegative integers. Which of the following systems of inequalities best represents this situation?

**Explanation**

We translate each condition into an inequality:

- The total weight cannot exceed $180$ pounds. Since each laptop weighs $5.2$ pounds and each monitor weighs $12.3$ pounds, we have
$5.2l + 12.3m \leq 180$.
- The number of monitors must be no more than twice the number of laptops, so we write:
$m \leq 2l$.

Putting both constraints together, the system of inequalities is

$$
{5.2l + 12.3m \le 180; m \le 2l
$$

Let $f$ represent the number of bags of flour and $s$ represent the number of bags of sugar, where $f$ and $s$ are nonnegative integers. - [ ] A. $4.5f + 6.8s \ge 250$

- [ ] D. ${4.5f + 6.8s \ge 250; f \le 3s$
