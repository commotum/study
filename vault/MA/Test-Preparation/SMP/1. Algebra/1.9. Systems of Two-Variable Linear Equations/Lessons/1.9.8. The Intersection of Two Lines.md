## Finding Unknown Parameters Given a System and Its Solution

**Example:** The equations $ax + 4by = 100$ and $3x - 2by = -10$ intersect at the point $(8, y)$. What is the value of $a$?

**Explanation**

Coordinates of the point of intersection $(8, y)$ represent the solution to the following system of linear equations:

$$
{ax + 4by = 100; 3x - 2by =-10
$$

Since $(8,y)$ is the solution of this system of equations, we first substitute $x = 8$ into the second equation to solve for $by{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(8) - 2by &= -10 \\
24 - 2by &= -10 \\
- 2by &= -34 \\
by &= 17
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute $x = 8$ and $by = 17$ into the first equation to solve for $a{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
a(8) + 4(17) &= 100 \\
8a + 68 &= 100 \\
8a &= 32 \\
a &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

So the value of $a$ is $4$.
