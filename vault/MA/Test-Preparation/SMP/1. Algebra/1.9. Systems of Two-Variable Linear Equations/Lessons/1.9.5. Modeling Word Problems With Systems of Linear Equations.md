## Modeling Using Systems of Equations in Standard Form: One Equation Represents a Sum

**Example:** A bookstore bundled together paperback novels and hardcover reference books for a promotion. Each paperback novel cost $\[math]20$, and each hardcover book cost $\[/math]45$. The store assembled a total of $25$ bundles and the total value of the bundles was $\[math]975$. Given that $n$ represents the number of paperback novels and $h$ represents the number of hardcover books, determine the system of equations that can be solved to find $n$ and $h$.

**Explanation**

Let's establish the linear equations that model the given situation.

- The store assembled a total of $25$ bundles, so
$n + h = 25$.
- The total value of the bundles was $\[math]975$. Since paperback novels cost $\[/math]20$ and hardcover books cost $\[math]45$, we have
$20n + 45h = 975$.

Putting these equations together, we have the following system of equations:

$$
{n + h = 25; 20n + 45h = 975
$$

## Modeling Speed, Distance, and Time Problems Using Linear Systems

**Example:** A hiker traveled through a forest and then along a mountain trail, covering a total of $48$ kilometers. The entire hike took $12$ hours. The hiker’s average speed was $4\,\text{kph}$ in the forest and $2\,\text{kph}$ on the mountain trail. Given that $t$ is the time, in hours, spent hiking through the forest and $m$ is the time, in hours, spent on the mountain trail, determine the system of equations representing this situation.

**Explanation**

Let's establish the linear equations that model the given situation.

- The total time elapsed was $12$ hours, so
$t + m = 12$.
- Now, we know that $\text{distance} = \text{speed} \times \text{time}$. Therefore,the distance the hiker traveled through the forest was $4t$, andthe distance traveled along the mountain trail was $2m$.

Since the total distance traveled was $48$ kilometers, we have

$$
4t + 2m = 48
$$

Putting these equations together, we have the following system of equations:

$$
{t + m = 12; 4t + 2m = 48
$$

## Modeling Using Systems of Equations in Standard Form

**Example:** Ella and Noah bought pencils and markers at an art store. The cost of each pencil was the same, as was the cost of each marker. Ella bought $4$ pencils and $5$ markers for $\[math]19$. In contrast, Noah bought $7$ pencils and $2$ markers for $\[/math]13$.

Determine the system of linear equations representing this situation if $x$ is the price, in dollars, of each pencil and $y$ is the price, in dollars, of each marker.

**Explanation**

Let's establish the linear equations that model the given situation.

- Ella bought $4$ pencils at $x$ dollars each, and $5$ markers at $y$ dollars each, for a total of $\[math]19$. Thus, we have
$4x + 5y = 19$.
- Noah bought $7$ pencils at $x$ dollars each, and $2$ markers at $y$ dollars each, for a total of $\[/math]13$. Hence, we have
$7x + 2y = 13$.

Putting these equations together, we have the following system of equations:

$$
{4x + 5y = 19; 7x + 2y = 13
$$

## Modeling a Commerce Problem Using Systems of Equations and Percentages

**Example:** Two sections of a test contribute to a final score. The original total possible score was $200$ points. Due to a grading adjustment, Section A lost $15\%$ of its weight and Section B lost $20\%$. The adjusted combined score is now $165$ points. Determine the system of equations giving the original score value $a$ of Section A and $b$ of Section B.

**Explanation**

Let's establish the linear equations that model the given situation.

- The combined original score was $200$ points, so
$a + b = 200$.
- After the reductions, the combined score was $165$ points. Since Section A retained $85\%$ of its original weight, and Section B retained $80\%$, we have
$0.85a + 0.80b = 165$.

Putting these equations together, we have the following system of equations:

$$
{a + b = 200; 0.85a + 0.80b = 165
$$
