## Introduction

This topic provides practice in solving systems of linear equations using the elimination method. Typically, this involves adding or subtracting equations to eliminate a variable. Also, premultiplying an equation by $-1$ to simplify a system is a strategy that's often useful.

## Solving Systems of Equations Using Elimination

**Example:** ${4y = 6x + 5; - 4y = 2x - 11$

The equations form a system. The solution is $(x, y)$. What is the value of $40x$?

**Explanation**

We will solve the system using elimination.

Since the left-hand sides are $4y$ and $-4y$, we can add the equations to eliminate $y{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
4y &= 6x + 5 \\
- 4y &= 2x - 11 \\
0 &= (6x + 2x) + (5 - 11) \\
0 &= 8x - 6 \\
8x &= 6 \\
x &= \frac{6}{8} \\
x &= \frac{3}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we compute $40x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
40x &= 40(\frac{3}{4}) = 30
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving Systems of Equations Containing Decimals Using Elimination

**Example:** ${-5u + v = 3.6; 5u + 3v = 20.4$

The solution to the given system of equations is $(u, v)$. What is the value of $v$?

**Explanation**

Notice that when we add the two equations, the $5u$ terms will cancel.

Therefore, we add the two equations and solve for $v{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
-5u + v &= 3.6 \\
5u + 3v &= 20.4 \\
(v + 3v) &= (3.6 + 20.4) \\
4v &= 24 \\
v &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving a System of Equations By Premultiplying by -1 and Eliminating

**Example:** ${10x - 5y = 20; 4x - 5y = 8$

In the solution $(x, y)$ to the system of equations above, what is the value of $x$?

**Explanation**

We will solve the system using elimination.

Notice that if we multiply the second equation by $-1$, the $-5y$ terms will cancel when adding the two equations:

$$
{10x - 5y = 20; 4x - 5y = 8 \mid \cdot (-1)
$$

Now we add the two equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
10x + - 5y &= 20 \\
- 4x+ 5y &= -8 \\
(10x - 4x) &= (20 - 8) \\
6x &= 12 \\
x &= \frac{12}{6} \\
x &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$
