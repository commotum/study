## Identifying the Graph Given a Slope-Intercept Form

**Example:** Which of the following is the graph of the equation $y = -\dfrac{4}{3}x + 5$ in the $xy$-plane?

![](<../Source/1.6.3. Graphs of Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form/Images/cc40f32c2ff127cf2938d9d8ae700df0.png>)

**Explanation**

The given equation is already in the slope-intercept form

$$
y = mx + b
$$

where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

From the equation

$$
y = -\dfrac{4}{3}x + 5
$$

, we can identify:

$$
m = -\dfrac{4}{3} \qquad \text{and} \qquad b = 5
$$

When looking at the options, we expect:

- The line intersects the $y$-axis at $(0, 5)$.
- The line slopes downward from left to right, decreasing by $4$ units in $y$ for every $3$ unit increase in $x$.

Therefore, the graph that represents the equation

$$
y = -\dfrac{4}{3}x + 5
$$

is:

![](<../Source/1.6.3. Graphs of Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form/Images/cd887fbafaae71f20374d1a50a20a9d6.png>)

## Finding the Slope-Intercept Form Given a Graph

**Example:** What is the equation of the line shown in the $xy$-plane above?

![](<../Source/1.6.3. Graphs of Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form/Images/b39fc73176317d66c6314139d7708f87.png>)

**Explanation**

We want to determine the equation of the line in the form

$$
y = mx + b
$$

where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

Looking at the graph, we see that the line crosses the $y$-axis at $-3$. Thus, the $y$-intercept is
$b = -3$.

Next, we determine the slope. Looking at the graph, we see that the line passes through the points

$$
(x_1, y_1) = (0, -3)
$$

and

$$
(x_2, y_2) = (2, -4)
$$

Thus, the slope is

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} \\
&= (-4 - (-3))/(2 - 0) \\
&= \frac{-1}{2} \\
&=-\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation of the line is

$$
y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x - 3
$$

## Finding the Slope-Intercept Form Given a Graph: In Context

**Example:** The graph above shows the amount saved $s$, in dollars, after working for $t$ minutes. What equation relates $s$ and $t$?

![](<../Source/1.6.3. Graphs of Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form/Images/840ba750c3eed9955d626bf763636fa9.png>)

**Explanation**

We want to determine the equation that relates the amount saved $s$ to the time $t$.

The graph is a line that can be expressed in the form

$$
s = mt + b
$$

where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

Looking at the graph, we see the line passes through the origin, $(0,0)$. Thus, the $y$-intercept is
$b = 0$.

Next, we determine the slope. Looking at the graph, we see that the line passes through the points

$$
(t_1, s_1) = (0, 0)
$$

and

$$
(t_2, s_2) = (2, 4)
$$

Thus, the slope is

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{s_{2} - s_{1}}{t_{2} - t_{1}} \\
&= \frac{4 - 0}{2 - 0} \\
&= \frac{4}{2} \\
&= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation that relates the amount saved $s$ to the time $t$ is
$s = 2t$.

## Finding the Standard Form Given a Graph: In Context

**Example:** The graph shows the relationship between the books purchased, $x$, and the magazines purchased, $y$, that Chloe can buy. Find the equation representing this relationship.

![](<../Source/1.6.3. Graphs of Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form/Images/f9727594ae576dc18c5436a1d7d6d99e.png>)

**Explanation**

We want to determine an equation that represents the relationship between the books purchased, $x$, and magazines purchased, $y$.

The graph is a line that can be expressed in the form

$$
y = mx + b
$$

where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

Looking at the graph, we see that it crosses the $y$-axis at

$$
(x_1, y_1) = (0, 120)
$$

Thus, the $y$-intercept is
$b = 120$.

The line also crosses the $x$-axis at

$$
(x_2, y_2) = (120, 0)
$$

Thus, the slope is

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} \\
&= \frac{0 - 120}{120 - 0} \\
&=-\frac{120}{120} \\
&=-1
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, the equation of the line is

$$
y = -x + 120
$$

Now, to convert this to standard form:

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= -x + 120 \\
x + y &= 120
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation that represents this relationship is

$$
x + y = 120
$$
