## Solving for an Unknown Parameter

**Example:** Consider the equation in the variable $y$, given by
$4(r - 2)y = -8$
where $r$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $r$?

**Explanation**

Suppose we have an equation of the form
$ax = b$
where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and

$$
b\neq 0
$$

If $a = 0$, then the equation has no solution.

Notice that this equation is in the form $ay = b$. In this case,

$$
a = 4(r - 2), \qquad b = -8
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if

$$
4(r - 2) = 0
$$

Solving this for $r$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
4(r - 2) &= 0 \\
(4(r - 2))/(4) &= \frac{0}{4} \\
r - 2 &= 0 \\
r &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving for an Unknown Parameter - Constants on Both Sides

**Example:** Consider the equation in the variable $z$, given by
$(r - 9)z + 2 = -1$
where $r$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $r$?

**Explanation**

Suppose we have an equation of the form
$ax = b$
where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and

$$
b\neq 0
$$

If $a = 0$, then the equation has no solution.

First, let's write our equation in the form $az = b{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(r - 9)z + 2 &= -1 \\
(r - 9)z + 2 - 2 &= -1 - 2 \\
(r - 9)_(⏟)_(a)z &= -3_(⏟)_(b)
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if
$r - 9 = 0$.

Solving this for $r$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
r - 9 &= 0 \\
r &= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving for an Unknown Parameter - Variables and Constants on Both Sides

**Example:** Consider the equation in the variable $x$, given by
$2nx + 7 = -4x + 5$
where $n$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $n$?

**Explanation**

Suppose we have an equation of the form
$ax = b$
where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and

$$
b\neq 0
$$

If $a = 0$, then the equation has no solution.

First, we rewrite the equation in the form $ax = b$.

We start by adding $4x$ to both sides of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2nx + 7 &= -4x + 5 \\
2nx + 4x + 7 &= -4x + 5 + 4x \\
2nx + 4x + 7 &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we subtract $7$ from both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2nx + 4x + 7 &= 5 \\
2nx + 4x + 7 - 7 &= 5 - 7 \\
2nx + 4x &= -2
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we factor $x$ on the left-hand side. This gives

$$
(2n + 4)x = -2
$$

Notice that this equation is now in the form $ax = b{:}$

$$
\underbrace{(2n + 4)}_{a}x = \underbrace{-2}_{b}
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if

$$
2n + 4= 0
$$

Solving this for $n$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
2n + 4 &= 0 \\
2n + 4 - 4 &= 0 - 4 \\
2n &= -4 \\
\frac{2n}{2} &= \frac{-4}{2} \\
n &= -2
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving Linear Equations With No Solutions for an Unknown Parameter Using the Distributive Law

**Example:** $4x + m(3 - 2x) = 5 - 6x$

In the equation above, $m$ is a constant. If the equation has no solution, what is the value of $m$?

**Explanation**

We're given the equation

$$
4x + m(3 - 2x) = 5 - 6x
$$

and told that it has no solution. This means it must be false for all values of $x$.

Let us expand the product on the left-hand side and collect the like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x + 3m - 2mx &= 5 - 6x \\
4x - 2mx + 6x &= 5 - 3m \\
10x - 2mx &= 5 - 3m \\
(10 - 2m)x &= 5 - 3m
\end{aligned}
$$

This equation has no solutions if the following two conditions are satisfied:

$$
10 - 2m = 0and5 - 3m \ne 0
$$

Solving the equality for $m$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
10 - 2m &= 0 \\
2m &= 10 \\
m &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Since

$$
5 - 3(5) = -10 \neq 0
$$

$m = 5$ indeed yields an equation with no solutions.
