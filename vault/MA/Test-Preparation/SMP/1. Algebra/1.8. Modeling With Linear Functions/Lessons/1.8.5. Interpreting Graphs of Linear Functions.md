## Interpreting a Unit Rate From a Graph

**Example:** The graph of the function $s$, where $y = s(x)$, shows the total cost $y$, in dollars, for park entry and renting $x$ skateboards for a park event. What is the best interpretation of the slope of this graph in this situation?

![](<../Source/1.8.5. Interpreting Graphs of Linear Functions/Images/a2b04982ef0646eb9608970a1449bc8e.png>)

1. The total cost is $\[math]25$ for all skateboards.
2. Each skateboard costs $\[/math]25$ to rent.
3. The total cost for the trip is $\[math]75$.
4. Each skateboard costs $\[/math]75$ to rent.

**Explanation**

First, we find the slope of the line. We identify the points

$$
(x_1,y_1) = (0,75)
$$

and

$$
(x_2,y_2) = (6,225)
$$

on the line. So, we use the slope formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} \\
&= \frac{225 - 75}{6 - 0} \\
&= \frac{150}{6} \\
&= 25
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, using the $y$-intercept point $(0,75)$, we have that the equation of our line is

$$
y = 25x + 75
$$

This equation shows that the total cost, $y$, grows linearly with the number of skateboards rented, $x$. The slope, $25$, is multiplied by the number of skateboards, so it represents the cost per skateboard.

So, the slope of the graph shows that each skateboard costs $\[math]25$ to rent.

Therefore, the correct answer is **only II.**

## Interpreting a Graph Modeling a Total

**Example:** The graph above represents the possible number of museum tickets and train tickets a customer can purchase to spend exactly $\[math]72.00$ total. The price of each museum ticket is the same, and the price of each train ticket is the same. What is the price of one train ticket?

![](<../Source/1.8.5. Interpreting Graphs of Linear Functions/Images/b4a03988245b79814ae39f60d05770ef.png>)

**Explanation**

Let $t$ be the number of train tickets and $m$ be the number of museum tickets bought for $\[math]72$.

We see that the graph passes through the point $(9,0)$. This means that the cost of $9$ train tickets and $0$ museum tickets is $\[/math]72$, which gives
$9 d = 72$,

Solving this for $d$, we get
$d = 8$.

Therefore, one train ticket costs $\[math]8.00$.

## Interpreting an X-Intercept in Context

**Example:** After a drone begins descending, its height above the ground, in meters, is modeled by the function $h(t) = 120 - 15t$, where $t$ is the number of seconds since it started descending. If the graph of $y = h(t)$ is drawn in the $xy$-plane, which of the following best describes the meaning of the $x$-coordinate of the line’s $x$-intercept in this situation?

1. The total vertical distance traveled by the drone
2. The speed of the drone in meters per second as it descends
3. The height the drone began descending from
4. The number of seconds it takes for the drone to reach the ground

**Explanation**

The function

$$
h(t) = 120 - 15t
$$

shows how the height $h$ (in meters) decreases over time $t$ (in seconds).

The $x$-intercept of the graph $y = h(t)$ is the point where the graph intersects the $x$-axis. At this point, $y = 0$, and so it corresponds to the moment when the drone reaches the ground.

To find the $x$-intercept, we set $y = 0$ and solve:

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &= h(t) \\
0 &= 120 - 15t \\
15t &= 120 \\
t &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

This means the drone takes $8$ seconds to reach the ground after it starts descending.

So, the $x$-coordinate of the $x$-intercept represents **the number of seconds it takes for the drone to reach the ground.**

Therefore, the correct answer is **only IV.**
