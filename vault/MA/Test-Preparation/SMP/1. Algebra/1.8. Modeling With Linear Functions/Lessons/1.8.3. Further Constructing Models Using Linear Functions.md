## Determining an Increase or Decrease of a Dependent Variable

**Example:** The function

$F(x) = \frac{9}{5}(x - 273.15) + 32$

gives the temperature, in degrees Fahrenheit $(^\circ \text{F})$, that corresponds to a temperature of $x$ Kelvins $(K)$. If a temperature increased by $7.2$ Kelvins, by how much did the temperature increase in degrees Fahrenheit?

**Explanation**

Because the function is linear, an increase of $7.2$ Kelvins results in an increase in degrees Fahrenheit of

$$
\frac{9}{5} \times 7.2
$$

Evaluating, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{9}{5} \cdot 7.2 &= 1.8 \cdot 7.2 \\
&= 12.96^{∘}F
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the temperature increased by $12.96\, ^\circ \text{F}$.

## Constructing a Linear Function Over the Positive Integers

**Example:** The cost of renting a chainsaw is $\[math]50$ for the first day and $\[/math]25$ for each additional day. What is the function that give the cost $C$, in dollars, of renting the chainsaw for $x$ days, where $x$ is a positive integer?

**Explanation**

The total cost is made up of two parts.

- For the first day, the renting costs $50$ dollars.
- For the extra time beyond the first day, the renting costs, in dollars,
$25(x - 1)$,
where $x\geq 1$ is the total time of rent, in days.

Therefore, the total renting cost $C$, in dollars, for $x$ days, is

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 50 + 25(x - 1) \\
&= 50 + 25x - 25 \\
&= 25x + 25
\end{aligned}
$$

## Constructing a Linear Function

**Example:** The cost of renting a large party tent for up to $7$ days is $\[math]200$ for the first day and $\[/math]85$ for each additional day. What is the equation that give the cost $C$, in dollars, of renting the tent for $d$ days, where $d$ is a positive integer and $d \leq 7$?

**Explanation**

The total cost is made up of two parts.

- For the first day, the renting costs $200$ dollars.
- For the extra time beyond the first day, the renting costs, in dollars,
$85(d - 1)$,
where $d\geq 1$ is the total time of rent, in days.

Therefore, the tent renting cost $C$, in dollars, for $d$ days, is

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 200 + 85(d - 1) \\
&= 200 + 85d - 85 \\
&= 85d + 115
\end{aligned}
$$
