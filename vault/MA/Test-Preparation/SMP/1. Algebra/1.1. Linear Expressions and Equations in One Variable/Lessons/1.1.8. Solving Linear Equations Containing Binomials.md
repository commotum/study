## Simplifying an Equation Containing a Binomial by Dividing

**Example:** $2(5b - 6) = 28$
Find the solution of the above equation.

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
2(5b - 6) = 28
$$

and are asked to determine which equation has the same solution.

To simplify, we divide both sides of the equation by $2{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
5b - 6 &= \frac{28}{2} \\
5b - 6 + 6 &= 14 + 6 \\
5b &= 20 \\
\frac{5b}{5} &= \frac{20}{5} \\
b &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving an Equation Containing Parentheses

**Example:** $(w + 11) + 7 = 29$
What value of $w$ is the solution to the given equation?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
(w + 11) + 7 = 29
$$

and are asked to solve for $w$.

Let's simplify and solve the equation for $w{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(w + 11) + 7 &= 29 \\
w + 18 &= 29 \\
w &= 29 - 18 \\
w &= 11
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the solution to the given equation is $w=11$.

## Binomial on the Left; Simplifying Before Solving By Applying the Distributive Law

**Example:** What value of $q$ is the solution to the equation $5q + 4(q + 3) = 84$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
5q + 4(q + 3) = 84
$$

and are asked to find the value of $q$ that satisfies it.

First, we expand the expression on the left-hand side by distributing the $4{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
5q + 4(q + 3) &= 5q + 4q + 12 \\
&= 9q + 12
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the equation becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
9q + 12 &= 84
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we subtract $12$ from both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
9q &= 72
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we divide both sides by $9{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{72}{9} \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $q$ that satisfies the equation is $8$.

## Solving Equations With Variables on Both Sides

**Example:** $4(x + 3) + 2 = 7 - 3(2x - 5) - 1$
What value of $x$ is a solution of the above equation?

**Explanation**

First, we begin by expanding both sides of the equation and simplifying by collecting like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
4(x + 3) + 2 &= 7 - 3(2x - 5) - 1 \\
4x + 12 + 2 &= 7 - 6x + 15 - 1 \\
4x + 14 &= 21 - 6x
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we solve the equation for $x$, as usual:

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x + 14 &= 21 - 6x \\
4x + 14 - 14 &= 21 - 6x - 14 \\
4x &= 7 - 6x \\
4x + 6x &= 7 - 6x + 6x \\
10x &= 7 \\
\frac{10x}{10} &= \frac{7}{10} \\
x &= \frac{7}{10}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the answer is

$$
x=\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{\dfrac{7}{10}}
$$

## Solving Equations With Fractional or Decimal Coefficients

**Example:** What value of $x$ is the solution to the equation $3.3x + 0.7(x + 4) = 22.8$?

**Explanation**

First, we expand the expression on the left-hand side and simplify by collecting like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3.3x + 0.7(x + 4) &= 3.3x + 0.7x + 2.8 \\
&= 4.0x + 2.8
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the equation becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
4.0x + 2.8 &= 22.8
\end{aligned}
$$

We multiply both sides of the equation by $10$ to get rid of the decimals:

$$
\begin{aligned}
10 \cdot (4.0x + 2.8) &= 10 \cdot (22.8) \\
40x + 28 &= 228
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we solve for $x$, as usual:

$$
\begin{aligned}
40x + 28 &= 228 \\
40x + 28 - 28 &= 228 - 28 \\
40x &= 200 \\
\frac{40x}{40} &= \frac{200}{40} \\
x &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the answer is $x=5$.
