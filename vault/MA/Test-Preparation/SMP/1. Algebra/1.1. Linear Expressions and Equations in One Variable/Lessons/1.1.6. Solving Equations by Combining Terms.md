## Solving Two-Step Equations With Prior Simplification

**Example:** $6v - 2v + 18 = 66$
What value of $v$ is the solution to the given equation?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
6v - 2v + 18 = 66
$$

and are asked to solve for $v$.

First, we simplify the left-hand side by combining like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6v - 2v + 18 &= 66 \\
4v + 18 &= 66
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we solve the equation for $v{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
4v + 18 &= 66 \\
4v &= 48 \\
v &= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving Equations With Variables on Both Sides

**Example:** What value of $m$ is the solution to the equation $8m = 72 - m$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation $8m = 72 - m$ and are asked to find the value of $m$ that satisfies it.

First, we isolate the variable on one side of the equation by adding $m$ to both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
8m + m &= 72 - m + m \\
9m &= 72
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we divide both sides of the equation by $9$ to solve for $m$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$
