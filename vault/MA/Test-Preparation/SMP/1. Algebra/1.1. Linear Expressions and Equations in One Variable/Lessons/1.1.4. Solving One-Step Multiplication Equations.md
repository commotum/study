Make sure you double-check your arithmetic before submitting your answer! This is especially important for free-response questions.

## Solving One-Step Multiplication Equations

**Example:** $2v = 38$
What value of $v$ is the solution to the given equation?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation $2v = 38$ and are asked to solve for $v$.

Let's solve the equation for $v{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2v &= 38 \\
v &= \frac{38}{2} \\
v &= 19
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $v$ that satisfies the equation is $19$.

## Solving a One-Step Multiplication Equation and Evaluating an Expression

**Example:** If $11k = 77$, what is the value of $6k$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation $11k = 77$ and are asked to find the value of $6k$.

First, we solve the equation for $k{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
11k &= 77 \\
k &= \frac{77}{11} \\
k &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute $k = 7$ into the expression $6k$ and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6k &= 6 \cdot 7 \\
&= 42
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $6k$ is $42$.

## Solving a One-Step Multiplication Equation and Evaluating an Expression (Free-Response)

**Example:** Calculate $m + 4$ given that $8m = 64$.

**Explanation**

We are given the equation $8m = 64$ and are asked to find the value of $m + 4$.

To isolate the variable $m$, we divide both sides of the equation by $8$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{64}{8} \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute $m = 8$ into $m + 4$ and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m + 4 &= 8 + 4 \\
&= 12
\end{aligned}
$$
