## Finding an Equivalent Equation by Adding or Subtracting

**Example:** Which of the following has the same solutions as $5m - 35 = 90$?

1. $5m=25$
2. $5m=55$
3. $5m=125$
4. $5m=450$

**Explanation**

Notice that the given answer choices are of the form
$5m = k$
where $k$ is a constant.

To write our equation in this form, we apply the addition principle, adding $35$ to both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
5m - 35 &= 90 \\
5m - 35 + 35 &= 90 + 35 \\
5m + 0 &= 125 \\
5m &= 125
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is $5m=125$.

## Finding an Equivalent Equation by Multiplying

**Example:** If $2x + 1 = 9$, then what is the value of $8x + 4$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
2x + 1 = 9
$$

and are asked to find the value of $8x + 4$.

Since we are already told that

$$
2x + 1 = 9
$$

we can multiply both sides of the equation by $4{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + 1 &= 9 \\
4 \cdot (2x) + 4 \cdot 1 &= 4 \cdot 9 \\
8x + 4 &= 36
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding an Equivalent Equation by Dividing

**Example:** Which of the following is equivalent to the equation $9z + 15 = 24$?

1. $z + 5 = 6$
2. $3z + 5 = 8$
3. $z + 12 = 8$
4. $3z + 12 = 16$

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
9z + 15 = 24
$$

and are asked to determine which equation is equivalent to it.

The greatest common factor of all terms is $3$, so we divide both sides of the equation by $3$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{9z + 15}{3} &= \frac{24}{3} \\
3z + 5 &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation

$$
3z + 5 = 8
$$

is equivalent to the original equation.

It can be verified that none of the other answer choices are equivalent to

$$
9z + 15 = 24
$$

## Finding an Equivalent Equation Using Multiple Steps

**Example:** If $6y + \dfrac{2}{3} = 4$, what is the value of $12y + 1$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
6y + \dfrac{2}{3} = 4
$$

and are asked to find the value of $12y + 1$.

To find $12y + 1$ from the given expression, we multiply both sides of the equation by $2$ and solve for $12y{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(6y + \frac{2}{3}) &= 2 \cdot 4 \\
12y + \frac{4}{3} &= 8 \\
12y &= 8 - \frac{4}{3} \\
12y &= \frac{20}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, to obtain $12y + 1$, we add $1$ to both sides and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
12y &= \frac{20}{3} \\
12y + 1 &= \frac{20}{3} + 1 \\
12y + 1 &= \frac{23}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $12y + 1$ is

$$
\dfrac{23}{3}
$$

## Finding Equivalent Quadratic Expressions

**Example:** If $3x^2 - 6x - 24 = 0$, what is the value of $x^2 - 2x$?

**Explanation**

Notice that all the coefficients in the given equation are multiples of $3$. Therefore, multiplying both sides by

$$
\dfrac{1}{3}
$$

distributing and simplifying, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x^{2} - 6x - 24 &= 0 \\
\frac{1}{3} \cdot (3x^{2} - 6x - 24) &= \frac{1}{3} \cdot 0 \\
\frac{1}{3} \cdot 3x^{2} - \frac{1}{3} \cdot 6x - \frac{1}{3} \cdot 24 &= 0 \\
x^{2} - 2x - 8 &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we proceed to isolate the expression $(x^2 - 2x)$. Adding $8$ to both sides in the last equation, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2} - 2x - 8 &= 0 \\
x^{2} - 2x - 8 + 8 &= 0 + 8 \\
x^{2} - 2x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $x^2 - 2x$ is $\bbox[4pt,border: 1px solid lightgray]{8}$.
