## Solving Equations With Fractional Coefficients

**Example:** $\dfrac{2v}{5} = 14$
In the equation above, what is the value of $v$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
\dfrac{2v}{5} = 14
$$

and are asked to solve for $v$.

Let's solve the equation for $v{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{2v}{5} &= 14 \\
2v &= 14 \cdot 5 \\
2v &= 70 \\
v &= \frac{70}{2} \\
v &= 35
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $v$ that satisfies the equation is $35$.

## Solving Equations With Fractional Coefficients and Evaluating an Expression

**Example:** Calculate $8x$ given that $\dfrac{5}{8}x - 1 = 9$.

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
\dfrac{5}{8}x - 1 = 9
$$

and are asked to find the value of $8x$.

First, we solve the equation for $x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{5}{8}x - 1 &= 9 \\
\frac{5}{8}x &= 9 + 1 \\
\frac{5}{8}x &= 10 \\
5x &= 80 \\
x &= \frac{80}{5} \\
x &= 16
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute $x = 16$ into $8x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
8x &= 8 \cdot 16 \\
&= 128
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving Equations With Multiple Fractional Coefficients

**Example:** If $\dfrac{5}{8}y - \dfrac{2}{8}y = 6$, what is the value of $y$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
\dfrac{5}{8}y - \dfrac{2}{8}y = 6
$$

and are asked to solve for $y$.

First, we simplify the left-hand side by combining like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{5}{8}y - \frac{2}{8}y &= (\frac{5}{8} - \frac{2}{8})y \\
&= \frac{3}{8}y
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the equation becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3}{8}y &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we solve for $y$ by multiplying both sides of the equation by

$$
\dfrac{8}{3}
$$

:

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 6 \cdot \frac{8}{3} \\
&= 16
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $y$ that satisfies the equation is $16$.
