## Equations With a Binomial on One Side and a Variable on Both Sides

**Example:** For what value of $w$ does $w + 7 = 4(w - 2)$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
w + 7 = 4(w - 2)
$$

and are asked to determine the value of $w$ that satisfies the equation.

First, we expand the right-hand side:

$$
\begin{aligned}
w + 7 &= 4(w - 2) \\
&= 4w - 8
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we isolate the variable $w$ by subtracting $w$ from both sides and then adding $8$ to both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
w + 7 &= 4w - 8 \\
w + 7 - w &= 4w - 8 - w \\
7 &= 3w - 8 \\
15 &= 3w \\
\frac{15}{3} &= w \\
5 &= w
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $w$ that satisfies the equation is $5$.

## Equations With Two Binomials and a Variable on Both Sides

**Example:** What value of $y$ is the solution to the equation $9(y + 4) + 2(2y - 7) = 7y$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
9(y + 4) + 2(2y - 7) = 7y
$$

and are asked to find the value of $y$ that satisfies it.

We begin by distributing on the left-hand side and combining like terms

$$
\begin{aligned}
9(y + 4) + 2(2y - 7) &= 7y \\
9y + 36 + 4y - 14 &= 7y \\
13y + 22 &= 7y
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we combine like terms on both sides of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
13y - 7y + 22 &= 7y - 7y \\
6y + 22 &= 0 \\
6y &= -22
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we divide both sides by $6$ to isolate $y{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= \frac{-22}{6} =-11 3
\end{aligned}
$$

## Equations With Binomials on Both Sides

**Example:** If $3(2s + 2) - 1 = 2(5s + 1) + 3$, what is the value of $s + 9$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
3(2s + 2) - 1 = 2(5s + 1) + 3
$$

and are asked to find the value of $s + 9$.

First, we begin by expanding both sides of the equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(2s + 2) - 1 &= 2(5s + 1) + 3 \\
6s + 6 - 1 &= 10s + 2 + 3 \\
6s + 5 &= 10s + 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we solve the equation for $s{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
6s - 10s + 5 &= 10s - 10s + 5 \\
- 4s + 5 &= 5 \\
- 4s &= 5 - 5 \\
- 4s &= 0 \\
s &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, substitute $s = 0$ into $s + 9$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
s + 9 &= 0 + 9 \\
&= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $s + 9$ is $9$.

## Determining a Condition for Two Fractions to be Equal

**Example:** If $\dfrac{8k + 1}{5} = \dfrac{8k + 1}{9}$, then, find the value of $8k + 2$.

**Explanation**

We are given that

$$
\dfrac{8k + 1}{5} = \dfrac{8k + 1}{9}
$$

The numerators on both sides are the same, but the denominators are different. The only way this equation can be true is if the numerator is $0$.

First, we set

$$
8k + 1 = 0
$$

and solve for $k{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
8k + 1 &= 0 \\
8k &= -1 \\
k &= -\frac{1}{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute

$$
k = -\dfrac{1}{8}
$$

into the expression $8k + 2{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
8k + 2 &= 8 \cdot (-\frac{1}{8}) + 2 \\
&=-1 + 2 \\
&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

## Expanding the Product of Binomials and Reducing to a Linear Equation

**Example:** Which of the following is a solution to the equation
$(x + 2)(x + 3) = (x - 2)(x - 3) + 10$?

**Explanation**

By expanding the products on both the left-hand side and the right-hand side, and then simplifying, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 2)(x + 3) &= (x - 2)(x - 3) + 10 \\
x^{2} + 5x + 6 &= x^{2} - 5x + 6 + 10 \\
x^{2} + 5x + 6 - x^{2} - 6 &= x^{2} - 5x + 6 + 10 - x^{2} - 6 \\
5x &= -5x + 10 \\
5x + 5x &= -5x + 10 + 5x \\
10x &= 10 \\
\frac{10x}{10} &= \frac{10}{10} \\
x &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the solution to the given equation is $x = 1$.
