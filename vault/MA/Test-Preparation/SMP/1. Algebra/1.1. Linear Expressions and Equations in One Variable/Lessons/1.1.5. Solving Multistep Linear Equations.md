## Solving Two-Step Equations Containing Larger Numbers

**Example:** What value of $b$ satisfies the equation $9b - 81 = 108$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
9b - 81 = 108
$$

and are asked to solve for $b$.

To isolate the term $9b$, we add $81$ to both sides of the equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
9b &= 108 + 81 \\
&= 189
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we divide both sides by $9$

$$
\begin{aligned}
b &= \frac{189}{9} \\
&= 21
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $b$ that satisfies the equation is $21$.

## Solving Two-Step Equations (Free-Response)

**Example:** $6r - 18 = 180$
What is the solution to the given equation?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
6r - 18 = 180
$$

and are asked to solve for $r$.

Let's solve the equation for $r{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
6r - 18 &= 180 \\
6r &= 180 + 18 \\
6r &= 198 \\
r &= \frac{198}{6} \\
r &= 33
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving a Two-Step Equation and Evaluating an Expression

**Example:** If $6p + 8 = 26$, what is the value of $2p - 3$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
6p + 8 = 26
$$

and are asked to find the value of $2p - 3$.

First, we solve the equation for $p{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
6p + 8 &= 26 \\
6p &= 18 \\
p &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute $p = 3$ into $2p - 3{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2p - 3 &= 2(3) - 3 \\
&= 6 - 3 \\
&= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the value of $2p - 3$ is $3$.

## Solving a Two-Step Equation and Evaluating an Expression (Free-Response)

**Example:** Calculate $2d - 7$ given that $4d + 20 = 0$.

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
4d + 20 = 0
$$

and are asked to find the value of $2d - 7$.

First, we solve the equation for $d{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
4d + 20 &= 0 \\
4d &= -20 \\
d &= -5
\end{aligned}
$$

Now that we know $d = -5$, we substitute this value into the expression $2d - 7$ and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2d - 7 &= 2(-5) - 7 \\
&=-10 - 7 \\
&=-17
\end{aligned}
$$
