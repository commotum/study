## Evaluating an Expression Given X

**Example:** If $z = 8$, what is the value of $z + 11$?

**Explanation**

We are given that $z = 8$ and are asked to find the value of $z + 11$.

To do this, we substitute $8$ for $z$ in the expression and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
z + 11 &= 8 + 11 \\
&= 19
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $z + 11$ is $19$.

## Evaluating an Expression Given kX

**Example:** If $7x = 63$, what is the value of $7x + 6$?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation $7x = 63$ and are asked to find the value of $7x + 6$.

Since we are already told that $7x = 63$, we can substitute this value into the expression $7x + 6$ and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
7x + 6 &= 63 + 6 \\
&= 69
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the value of $7x + 6$ is $69$.

## Taking an Equation's Reciprocal

**Example:** If $\dfrac{a}{2} = 3$, then what is the value of $\dfrac{2}{a}$?

**Explanation**

We are given that

$$
\dfrac{a}{2} = 3
$$

and are asked to find the value of

$$
\dfrac{2}{a}
$$

It is sufficient to take the reciprocal on both sides of the equation, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a}{2} &= 3 \\
\frac{a}{2} &= \frac{3}{1} \\
\frac{2}{a} &= 1 3
\end{aligned}
$$
