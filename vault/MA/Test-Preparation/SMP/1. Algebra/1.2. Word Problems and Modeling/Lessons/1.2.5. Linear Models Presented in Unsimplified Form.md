## Introduction

Some answers in the math section of the SAT may be left in unsimplified form. In this topic, we'll practice recognizing when an unsimplified answer is still correct.

## Constructing an Unsimplified Expression to Model a Situation

**Example:** A painting crew is assigned to complete $7$ walls in a gallery. Each wall will be painted using either latex paint or oil paint, but not both. A latex-painted wall requires $2$ gallons of paint, and an oil-painted wall requires $5$ gallons. If $l$ walls will use latex paint and the remaining walls will use oil paint, determine the expression representing the total number of gallons of paint needed.

**Explanation**

Let $l$ be the number of walls that will use latex paint.

There are $7$ total walls. If $l$ walls use latex paint, then the remaining $7 - l$ walls use oil paint.

Each latex-painted wall requires $2$ gallons of paint, so all latex-painted walls require
$2l \text{gallons.}$

Each oil-painted wall requires $5$ gallons of paint, so all oil-painted walls require
$5(7 - l) \text{gallons.}$

Therefore, the total number of gallons of paint needed is
$2l + 5(7 - l)$.

This expression represents the total number of gallons of paint needed.

**Question 1:** A chef is preparing $6$ different pasta stations for a food festival. Each station will serve either Recipe X or Recipe Y, but not both. Recipe X uses $5$ ounces of cheese per station, and Recipe Y uses $3$ ounces of cheese per station. If $r$ stations will be set up for Recipe X and the remaining stations will be set up for Recipe Y, which of the following expressions represents the total number of ounces of cheese the chef will need?

- [ ] A. $5r + 18$
- [ ] B. $6(5 + 3r)$
- [ ] C. $6r + 3$
- [ ] D. $5r + 3(6 - r)$
- [ ] E. $5r + 3r$

## Constructing an Equation to Model a Situation

**Example:** A jewelry box contains $120$ pieces that are either rings, necklaces, or bracelets. The number of rings is $2$ times the number $n$ of necklaces. There are $40$ bracelets. Determine the equation that can be used to find the number of necklaces, $n$.

**Explanation**

Let $n$ represent the number of necklaces.

The number of rings is $2n$, and there are $40$ bracelets.

To find the total number of pieces, we add together the necklaces, rings, and bracelets:

$$
n + 2n + 40 = 120
$$

This equation represents the relationship between the number of each type of jewelry and the total.

---

**Question 3:** A factory produces $10$-inch, $8$-inch, and $6$-inch steel rods. On a certain day, the number of $10$-inch rods produced is $4$ times the number $r$ of $8$-inch rods, and the number of $6$-inch rods produced is $30$. On that day, the factory produces a total of $110$ rods. Which equation represents this situation?

- [ ] A. $5r + 30 = 110$
- [ ] B. $4r + 30 = 5r$
- [ ] C. $10r + 8r + 6(30) = 110$
- [ ] D. $4r + 8r + 6r = 110$
- [ ] E. $4r + 30 = 110$

---

**Question 4:** A game board is made up of $40$ tiles, each of which is either red, blue, or green. The number of red tiles is $4$ times the number $g$ of green tiles. There are $12$ blue tiles. Which of the following equations can be used to find the number of green tiles, $g$?

- [ ] A. $g + 12 = 40$
- [ ] B. $5g + 10 = 40$
- [ ] C. $4g + 12 = 40$
- [ ] D. $g + 4g + 12 = 40$
- [ ] E. $g + 4g + 40 = 12$

## Constructing an Unsimplified Equation With Multiple Terms

**Example:** A designer toy company spends $\[math]38$ to manufacture a collectible figure. A reseller earns a commission of $22\%$ of the sale price of each figure. The company’s profit on each figure is the sale price minus both the manufacturing cost and the commission. Given that the sale price is $\[/math]75$, find the equation representing the number of figures, $n$, the company must sell to earn a profit of $\[math]10{,}494$.

**Explanation**

Let $n$ be the number of figures sold.

We are told the production cost per unit is $\[math]38$, and the reseller earns $22\%$ of the sales price. The sales price is $\[/math]75$, so the commission per unit is
$0.22 \times 75$.

To find the profit per unit, we subtract both the manufacturing cost and the commission from the sales price:
$75 - 38 - (0.22 \times 75)$

This simplifies to
$(75 - 38 - 0.22 \times 75)$.

To find the total profit, we multiply the profit per unit (given above) by the number of units $n{:}$
$(75 - 38 - 0.22 \times 75)n$

Since the company wishes to earn a profit of $\[math]10{,}494$, this gives the equation

$$
(75 - 38 - 0.22 \times 75)n = 10{,}494
$$

Therefore, the correct equation is *$(75 - 38 - 0.22 \times 75)n = 10{,}494$*.

---

**Question 5:** A company pays $$75$ to manufacture a smartphone. A sales agent earns a commission equal to $15%$ of the sales price of the smartphone. The company’s profit on each phone is the sales price minus both the production cost and the commission. If the sales price of the phone is $$120$, which of the following equations gives the number of units, $n$, the company sold to earn a profit of $$8,250$?

- [ ] A. $(120 - 75 - 0.15 \cdot 120)n = 8,250$
- [ ] B. $(0.15 \cdot 120 + 75)n = 8,250$
- [ ] C. $0.85(120) - 75n = 8,250$
- [ ] D. $(120 - 75)(1 - 0.15)n = 8,250$
- [ ] E. $(120(1 - 0.15) - 75)n = 8,250$

---

**Question 6:** A designer toy company spends $$38$ to manufacture a collectible figure. A reseller earns a commission of $22%$ of the sale price of each figure. The company’s profit on each figure is the sale price minus both the manufacturing cost and the commission. If the sale price is $$75$, which of the following equations gives the number of figures, $n$, the company must sell to earn a profit of $$10,494$?

- [ ] A. $(0.22 \cdot 75 + 38)n = 10,494$
- [ ] B. $(75(1 - 0.22) - 38)n = 10,494$
- [ ] C. $0.78(75) - 38n = 10,494$
- [ ] D. $(75 - 38)(1 - 0.22)n = 10,494$
- [ ] E. $(75 - 38 - 0.22 \cdot 75)n = 10,494$
