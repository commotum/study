## Modeling Perimeter Problems Using One-Variable Equations

**Example:** The width of a rectangular patio is $a$ feet. The length is $8$ feet less than the width. Determine the expression representing the perimeter, in feet, of the patio in terms of $a$.

**Explanation**

We are told that the width is $a$ feet, and the length is $8$ feet less than that, so the length is $a - 8$.

The formula for the perimeter of a rectangle is

$$
\text{Perimeter} = 2(\text{length}) + 2(\text{width})
$$

Substituting the expressions gives
$2(a - 8) + 2(a)$.

Now we simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(a - 8) + 2a &= 2a - 16 + 2a \\
&= 4a - 16
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the expression for the perimeter is *$4a - 16$ feet*.

## Modeling Perimeter Problems Using Two-Variable Equations

**Example:** A manufacturer produces $7$ octagonal frames, each with side length $x$, and $3$ metal trays, each with a length of $4y$ and a width of $y$. None of these objects share any sides. The total perimeter of all the frames and trays is $476$. Determine the equation representing this situation.

**Explanation**

To model this situation, we will add together the perimeters of all the shapes.

- Each *regular octagon* has $8$ sides of length $x$, so its perimeter is $8x$.
With $7$ octagons, the total perimeter from the frames is
$7 \cdot 8x = 56x$.
- Each *rectangle* has perimeter $2(\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2(4y + y) = 10y$.
With $3$ trays, the total perimeter from the rectangles is
$3 \cdot 10y = 30y$.

Adding both contributions together, we get the total perimeter:

$$
56x + 30y = 476
$$

Thus, the correct equation is

$$
56x + 30y = 476
$$

## Problems Involving Isosceles Triangles

**Example:** The function $m(x) = 2x + 1{,}200$ gives the perimeter, in millimeters ($\text{mm}$), of an isosceles triangle with two equal sides of length $x$ mm and a fixed base. What is the length of the base of the triangle?

**Explanation**

For an isosceles triangle with two equal sides of length $x\text{mm}$ and a base of length $b\text{mm}$, the perimeter is given by

$$
P = x + x + b = 2x + b
$$

From the problem, we know the perimeter function is

$$
m(x) = 2x + 1{,}200
$$

By comparing, we get

$$
2x + b = 2x + 1{,}200
$$

Therefore, after subtracting $2x$ from both sides, the length of the base of the triangle is

$$
b = 2x + 1{,}200 - 2x = 1{,}200 \text{mm}
$$

## Interpreting a Geometric Model

**Example:** In quadrilateral $EFGH$, sides $EF$ and $FG$ each have a length of $k$ meters, and side $GH$ has a length of $\ell$ meters. The equation $2k + \ell = 96$ represents this situation. What is the interpretation of $96$ in this context?

**Explanation**

The equation

$$
2k + \ell = 96
$$

models the total length of the three sides described. The terms $2k$ and $\ell$ represent the total combined length of sides $EF$, $FG$ and the length of side $GH$, respectively.

Therefore, the constant $96$ represents the sum of the lengths, in meters, of sides $EF$, $FG$, and $GH$.
