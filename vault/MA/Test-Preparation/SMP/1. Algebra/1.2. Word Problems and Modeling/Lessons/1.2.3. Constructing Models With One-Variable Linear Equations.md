<a id="creating-a-linear-expression"></a>
## Creating a Linear Expression

**Example:** Bella has $y$ pens. Ryan has $10$ more than six times the number of pens that Bella has. In terms of $y$, how many pens does Ryan have?

**Explanation**

Six times the number of pens that Bella has is $6y$. Since Ryan has $10$ more than that, we add $10{:}$
$6y + 10$

So, Ryan has $6y + 10$ pens.

---

## Modeling Word Problems Using Linear Equations

**Example:** Jared bought a flash drive for $\[math]6.75$ and several styluses that cost $\[/math]3.50$ each. If he spent a total of $\[math]17.25$, which equation can be used to find $s$, the number of styluses he purchased?

**Explanation**

Jared’s total cost includes $\[math]6.75$ for the flash drive and $s$ styluses at $\[/math]3.50$ each. So the total spent on styluses is $3.50s$, and the total purchase cost was $\[math]17.25$.

Therefore, the equation is

$$
3.50s + 6.75 = 17.25
$$

This equation models the total cost in terms of the number of styluses purchased.

---

**Question 3:** A guitar costs $$189$. Dana paid a deposit of $$45$ and agreed to pay $p$ equal monthly payments of $$24$ each. Which equation models this situation?

- [ ] A. $45 + 189 = 24p$
- [ ] B. $24p - 45 = 189$
- [ ] C. $24p + 45 = 189$
- [ ] D. $189p + 45 = 24$
- [ ] E. $45p + 24 = 189$

---

**Question 4:** Riya bought a notebook for $$3$ and several folders that cost $$2.75$ each. If she spent a total of $$13.25$, which equation can be used to find $f$, the number of folders she purchased?

- [ ] A. $2.75f + 3 = 13.25$
- [ ] B. $2.75f - 3 = 13.25$
- [ ] C. $2.75 + 3f = 13.25$
- [ ] D. $13.25f + 3 = 2.75$
- [ ] E. $3 + 13.25 = 2.75f$

## Modeling Mathematical Problems Using Linear Equations

**Example:** Nine more than twelve times a number $a$ is equal to $81$. Find the equation representing this situation.

**Explanation**

The phrase “twelve times a number $a$” translates to $12a$, and “nine more than” means we add $9$ to that expression. So the entire expression is $12a + 9$.

Since this is equal to $81$, the full equation is

$$
12a + 9 = 81
$$

This equation represents the situation described in the problem.

---

**Question 5:** Seven more than five times a number $n$ is equal to $62$. Which equation represents this situation?

- [ ] A. $5n + 7 = 62$
- [ ] B. $(7)(5)n = 62$
- [ ] C. $5n - 7 = 62$
- [ ] D. $5n = 62 + 7$
- [ ] E. $7n + 5 = 62$

---

- [ ] A. $12a - 9 = 81$
- [ ] B. $12a + 9 = 81$
- [ ] C. $12a = 81 + 9$
- [ ] D. $(9)(12)a = 81$
- [ ] E. $9a + 12 = 81$
