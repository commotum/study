## Evaluating a Function

**Example:** For the function $f$, if $f(4x) = x - 2$ for all values of $x$, what is the value of $f(8)$?

**Explanation**

First, note that

$$
8 = 4 \cdot 2
$$

Now, substituting $x = 2$ into the function definition and simplifying, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(4x) &= x - 2 \\
f(4 \cdot 2) &= 2 - 2 \\
f(8) &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

## Determining an Unknown Parameter

**Example:** The linear function $h$ is defined by
$h(x) = k - 3x$,
where $k$ is a constant. If $h(m + 2) = \dfrac{m}{2}$, where $m$ is a constant, write an expressions that represents the value of $k$.

**Explanation**

We must find an expression for $k$ in terms of $m$.

We are given that

$$
h(m + 2) = \dfrac{m}{2}
$$

Therefore, substituting

$$
x = m+2
$$

into the function definition and solving for $k$, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(x) &= k - 3x \\
h(m + 2) &= k - 3(m + 2) \\
\frac{m}{2} &= k - 3m - 6 \\
k &= \frac{m}{2} + 3m + 6 \\
k &= \frac{m + 6m}{2} + 6 \\
k &= \frac{7m}{2} + 6
\end{aligned}
$$
