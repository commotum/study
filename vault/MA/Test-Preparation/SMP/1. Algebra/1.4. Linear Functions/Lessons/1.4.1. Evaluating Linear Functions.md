## Evaluating a Function

**Example:** The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = 9x - 3$. What is the value of $g(-2)$?

**Explanation**

To find $g(-2)$, we substitute $x = -2$ into the function definition and then simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(x) &= 9x - 3 \\
g(-2) &= 9(-2) - 3 \\
&=-18 - 3 \\
&=-21
\end{aligned}
$$

## Evaluating a Function Containing Fractions

**Example:** The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = \dfrac{5}{9}(x - 4)$. What is the value of $f(22)$?

**Explanation**

To find $f(22)$, we substitute $x = 22$ into the function definition and then simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= \frac{5}{9}(x - 4) \\
f(22) &= \frac{5}{9}(22 - 4) \\
&= \frac{5}{9}(18) \\
&= 10
\end{aligned}
$$

## Determining a Difference of Two Outputs

**Example:** The function $h$ is defined by
$h(x) = \dfrac{x + 6}{6}$.
What is the value of $h(6) - h(-6)$?

**Explanation**

First, we calculate $h(6)$. To do this, we substitute $x = 6$ into the function definition and then simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(x) &= \frac{x + 6}{6} \\
h(6) &= \frac{6 + 6}{6} \\
&= \frac{12}{6} \\
&= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate $h(-6)$. To do this, we substitute $x = -6$ into the function definition and then simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(x) &= \frac{x + 6}{6} \\
h(-6) &= \frac{-6 + 6}{6} \\
&= \frac{0}{6} \\
&= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we calculate $h(6) - h(-6)$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(6) - h(-6) &= 2 - 0 \\
&= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

## Determining a Linear Combination of Outputs

**Example:** If $p(x) = x - 1$ and $q(x) = 3x$, what is the value of $4p(2) - q(2)$?

**Explanation**

First, we calculate $p(2)$. To do this, we substitute $x = 2$ into the function definition $p(x)$ and then simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(x) &= x - 1 \\
p(2) &= 2 - 1 \\
&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate $q(2)$. To do this, we substitute $x = 2$ into the function definition $q(x)$ and then simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
q(x) &= 3x \\
q(2) &= 3(2) \\
&= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we calculate $4p(2) - q(2)$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
4p(2) - q(2) &= 4(1) - 6 \\
&= 4 - 6 \\
&=-2
\end{aligned}
$$
