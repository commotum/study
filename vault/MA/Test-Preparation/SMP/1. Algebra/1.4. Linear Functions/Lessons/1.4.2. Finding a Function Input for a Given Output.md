## Solving a One-Step Equation

**Example:** The function $p$ is defined by $p(x) = 6x$. For what value of $x$ does $p(x) = 42$?

**Explanation**

We want to find the value of $x$ such that $p(x) = 42$.

Substituting the expression for the function $p$ into the equation above and solving for $x$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(x) &= 42 \\
6x &= 42 \\
x &= \frac{42}{6} \\
x &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving a Two-Step Equation

**Example:** The function $g$ is defined by $g(x) = 6x - 5$. For what value of $x$ does $g(x) = 49$?

**Explanation**

We want to find the value of $x$ such that $g(x) = 49$.

Substituting the expression for the function $g$ into the equation above and solving for $x$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(x) &= 49 \\
6x - 5 &= 49 \\
6x &= 54 \\
x &= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving an Equation by Clearing a Rational Expression

**Example:** The function $f$ is defined by
$f(x) = \dfrac{x + 2}{2}$,
and $f(b) = 6$, where $b$ is a constant. What is the value of $b$?

**Explanation**

Substituting $x=b$ into the expression for $f(x)$ and using the fact that

$$
f(b) = 6
$$

we can solve for $b$, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= \frac{x + 2}{2} \\
f(b) &= \frac{b + 2}{2} \\
6 &= \frac{b + 2}{2} \\
6 \cdot 2 &= b + 2 \\
12 &= b + 2 \\
12 - 2 &= b \\
b &= 10
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving an Equation Containing Fractional Coefficients

**Example:** The function $g$ is defined by
$g(x) = \dfrac{2}{3}x + \dfrac{7}{3}$.
For what value of $x$ does $g(x) = 5$?

**Explanation**

We want to find the value of $x$ such that $g(x) = 5$.

Substituting the expression for the function $g$ into the equation above and solving for $x$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(x) &= 5 \\
\frac{2}{3}x + \frac{7}{3} &= 5 \\
\frac{2}{3}x &= 5 - \frac{7}{3} \\
\frac{2}{3}x &= \frac{15}{3} - \frac{7}{3} \\
\frac{2}{3}x &= \frac{8}{3} \\
x &= \frac{8}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \\
x &= \frac{8 \cdot 3}{3 \cdot 2} \\
x &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$
