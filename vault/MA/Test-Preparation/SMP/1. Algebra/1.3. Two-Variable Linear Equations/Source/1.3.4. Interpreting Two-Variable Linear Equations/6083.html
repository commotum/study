Introduction

It's essential to understand the meaning of each part of a two-variable linear equation. In this topic, we'll practice interpreting different parts of two-variable models, including constants, variables, and terms.

Example: Interpreting a Sum
?

Noah bought apples that cost dollars each and bananas that cost dollars each. If the given equation represents this situation, what is the interpretation of in this context?

EXPLANATION

In the equation the term represents the total cost of the apples, and the term represents the total cost of the bananas. Adding them gives the total amount Noah spent.

Therefore, the number represents the total cost, in dollars, for all apples and bananas purchased.

Lena delivered liters of milk. She used small jugs and large jugs. The equation represents this situation. Which is the interpretation of in this context?

EXPLANATION

The equation models the total amount of milk Lena delivered, in liters.

In this equation, is the number of small jugs and is the number of large jugs. Correspondingly, and are the capacities, in liters, of the small and large jugs, respectively.

Consequently, the term represents the total number of liters of milk in the large jugs.
