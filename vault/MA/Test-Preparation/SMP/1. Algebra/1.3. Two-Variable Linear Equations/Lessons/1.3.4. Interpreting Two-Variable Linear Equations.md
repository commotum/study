## Introduction

It's essential to understand the meaning of each part of a two-variable linear equation. In this topic, we'll practice interpreting different parts of two-variable models, including constants, variables, and terms.

---

<a id="interpreting-a-sum"></a>
## Interpreting a Sum

**Example:** $6a + 3b = 45$

Noah bought $6$ apples that cost $a$ dollars each and $3$ bananas that cost $b$ dollars each. If the given equation represents this situation, what is the interpretation of $45$ in this context?

**Explanation**

In the equation

$$
6a + 3b = 45
$$

the term $6a$ represents the total cost of the apples, and the term $3b$ represents the total cost of the bananas. Adding them gives the total amount Noah spent.

Therefore, the number $45$ represents the total cost, in dollars, for all apples and bananas purchased.

## Interpreting a Variable

**Example:** Two teams at a bakery decorated cakes for a large event. Team 1 decorated $5$ cakes per day, and Team 2 decorated $8$ cakes per day. Over the course of several days, the total number of cakes decorated was $2{,}900$. The equation $5x + 8y = 2{,}900$ represents this situation. What is the interpretation of $y$ in this context?

**Explanation**

The equation

$$
5x + 8y = 2{,}900
$$

models the total number of cakes decorated. The terms $5x$ and $8y$ represent the number of cakes decorated by Team 1 and Team 2, respectively.

Since the factor of $8$ in $8y$ corresponds to Team 2’s rate, $y$ represents the number of days Team 2 worked.

## Interpreting a Term

**Example:** Lena delivered $60$ liters of milk. She used $x$ small jugs and $y$ large jugs. The equation $4x + 6y = 60$ represents this situation. Which is the interpretation of $6y$ in this context?

**Explanation**

The equation

$$
4x + 6y = 60
$$

models the total amount of milk Lena delivered, in liters.

In this equation, $x$ is the number of small jugs and $y$ is the number of large jugs. Correspondingly, $4$ and $6$ are the capacities, in liters, of the small and large jugs, respectively.

Consequently, the term $6y$ represents the total number of liters of milk in the large jugs.
