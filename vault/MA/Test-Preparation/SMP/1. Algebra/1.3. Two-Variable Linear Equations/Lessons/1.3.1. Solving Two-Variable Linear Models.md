## Solving a Proportional Relationship

**Example:** The equation $d = 0.09t$ models the relationship between the distance $d$, in kilometers, that a person travels from the stable on horseback, and the time $t$, in minutes, spent riding. How far did the person travel from the stable if the ride lasted $50$ minutes?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
d = 0.09t
$$

which shows that the distance $d$ in kilometers is equal to $0.09$ times the number of minutes $t$.

We are told that $t = 50$, so we substitute:

$$
d = 0.09(50)
$$

Now, we simplify:

$$
d = 4.5
$$

Therefore, the person traveled $4.5$ kilometers from the stable.

## Solving a Two-Variable Equation

**Example:** The equation $x + y = 2,400$ represents the number of calories from protein, $x$, and the number of calories from other sources, $y$, in a daily diet totaling $2,400$ calories. If the diet includes $680$ calories from protein, how many calories are from other sources?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
x + y = 2,400
$$

where $x$ represents calories from protein and $y$ represents calories from other sources.

We are told that the diet includes $x = 680$ calories from protein. So, we substitute this value into the equation:

$$
680 + y = 2,400
$$

To isolate $y$, we subtract $680$ from both sides:
$y = 1,720$

Therefore, the number of calories from other sources is $1,720$.

## Solving a Two-Variable Equation Containing Decimals

**Example:** A catering service calculates the total cost $C$ (in dollars) of preparing $t$ trays of food and using $h$ hours of staff labor using the following equation:
$7t + 8h = 118$
If the service used $6$ hours of staff labor for an event, how many trays of food did it prepare?

**Explanation**

We are given the equation

$$
7t + 8h = 118
$$

and told that $h = 6$.

First, we substitute $6$ for $h$ in the equation:

$$
7t + 8(6) = 118
$$

Next, we compute the product:

$$
7t + 48 = 118
$$

Then, we subtract $48$ from both sides:
$7t = 70$

Finally, we divide both sides by $7{:}$

$$
t = \frac{70}{7} = 10
$$

Therefore, the service prepared $10$ trays of food.
