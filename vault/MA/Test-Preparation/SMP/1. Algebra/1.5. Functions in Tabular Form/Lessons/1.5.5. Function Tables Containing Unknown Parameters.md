## Determining a Slope

**Example:** The table shows three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $y$, where $s$ is a constant, for the linear relationship between $x$ and $y$. What is the slope of the line that represents this relationship in the $xy$-plane?

| $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $-1$ | $s$ |
| $1$ | $s + 14$ |
| $3$ | $s + 28$ |

**Explanation**

To find the slope, we use any two points from the table.

Note that

- When $x = -1$, $y = s$
- When $x = 3$, $y = s + 28$

Thus, using the points $(-1, s)$ and $(3, s + 28)$, we compute the slope $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= ((s + 28) - (s))/(3 - (-1)) \\
&= \frac{28}{4} \\
&= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope is $7$.

## Constructing an Equation and Determining an Intercept

**Example:** The table shows two points on a line in the $xy$-plane. The $y$-intercept of the line is $(k - 1, b)$, where $k$ and $b$ are constants. What is the value of $b$?

| $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $k$ | $5$ |
| $k+3$ | $17$ |

**Explanation**

Since the relationship is linear, it must be of the form

$$
y = mx + b
$$

where

- $m$ is the slope, and
- $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

We are given that the points $(k, 5)$ and $(k+3, 17)$ lie on the line and that the line’s $y$-intercept is $(k - 1, b)$. So, since the $y$-intercept lies on the $y$-axis, its $x$-coordinate must be $0$. This gives

$$
k - 1 = 0 \quad\implies\quad k = 1
$$

Now, we substitute this value to find the coordinates of the points:

- $(k, 5) = (1, 5)$
- $(k+3, 17) = (4, 17)$

Then, we use these points to find the slope $m$ of our line:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{17 - 5}{4 - 1} \\
&= \frac{12}{3} \\
&= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we find the $y$-intercept using the point $(1, 5){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 &= 4(1) + b \\
5 &= 4 + b \\
b &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

## Constructing a Line in Standard Form in Terms of a Parameter

**Example:** The table shows three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $y$, where $t$ is a constant. There is a linear relationship between $x$ and $y$. What equation represents this relationship?

| $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $-4t$ | $8$ |
| $t$ | $18$ |
| $6t$ | $28$ |

**Explanation**

We use two points to find the slope and set up the equation.

First, we find the slope using the points $(-4t, 8)$ and $(t, 18){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= (18 - 8)/(t - (-4t)) \\
&= \frac{2}{t}
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we write the line in point-slope form using the point $(t, 18){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
y - 18 &= m(x - t) \\
y - 18 &= \frac{2}{t}(x - t)
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we multiply by $t$ to clear fractions and bring all terms together:

$$
\begin{aligned}
t(y - 18) &= 2(x - t) \\
ty - 18t &= 2x - 2t \\
ty - 2x &= 18t - 2t \\
ty - 2x &= 16t
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation is

$$
ty - 2x = 16t
$$
