## Introduction

On the math section of the SAT exam, you may be asked to analyze functions presented in tables rather than as equations or graphs.

In this topic, you'll practice identifying linear relationships given in tables, and, in some cases, determining an unknown value from the data.

<a id="constructing-a-function-then-determining-a-difference-of-parameters"></a>
## Constructing a Function Then Determining a Difference of Parameters

**Example:** For the linear function $f$, the table shows three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $f(x)$. Function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = ax + b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. What is the value of $a - b$?

| $x$ | $f(x)$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $-2$ | $3$ |
| $0$ | $7$ |
| $2$ | $11$ |

**Explanation**

First, we find $a$ using any two points. Let's use $(-2, 3)$ and $(0, 7){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
a &= (7 - 3)/(0 - (-2)) \\
&= \frac{4}{2} \\
&= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus,

$$
f(x) = 2x + b
$$

Next, we use that

$$
f(0) = 7
$$

to find $b{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(0) &= 2(0) + b \\
7 &= 0 + b \\
b &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we obtain

$$
a - b = 2 - 7 = -5
$$

## Constructing a Function Then Evaluating at an Unknown Point

**Example:** Some values of the linear function $f$ are shown in the table above. What is the value of $f(4)$?

| $x$ | $f(x)$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $1$ | $8$ |
| $3$ | $2$ |
| $5$ | $-4$ |

**Explanation**

Since the relationship is linear, it must be of the form

$$
f(x) = mx + b
$$

where

- $m$ is the slope, and
- $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

First, we find the slope using the points $(1,8)$ and $(3,2){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{2 - 8}{3 - 1} \\
&= \frac{-6}{2} \\
&=-3
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we find the $y$-intercept using the point $(1,8){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
8 &= -3(1) + b \\
8 &= -3 + b \\
b &= 11
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation is

$$
f(x) = -3x + 11
$$

Finally, we find $f(4){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(4) &= -3(4) + 11 \\
&=-12 + 11 \\
&=-1
\end{aligned}
$$

## Constructing a Function Then Determining an Unknown Point

**Example:** The table shows two values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $y$. In the $xy$-plane, the graph of the linear equation representing this relationship passes through the point $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}, a\right)$. What is the value of $a$?

| $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $-15$ | $-5$ |
| $5$ | $15$ |

**Explanation**

First, we find the slope using the two given points $(-15, -5)$ and $(5, 15)$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= (15 - (-5))/(5 - (-15)) \\
&= \frac{20}{20} \\
&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we use point-slope form with the point $(5, 15){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
y - 15 &= m(x - 5) \\
y - 15 &= (x - 5)
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we substitute

$$
x = \dfrac{1}{3}
$$

to find $a{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
a - 15 &= \frac{1}{3} - 5 \\
a - 15 &= -\frac{14}{3} \\
a &= 15 - \frac{14}{3} \\
a &= \frac{31}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Constructing a Function Then Finding an Intercept

**Example:** For line $m$, the table shows three $x$-values and their corresponding $y$-values. Line $n$ is the result of translating line $m$ up $2$ units in the $xy$-plane. What is the $x$-intercept of line $n$?

| $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $2$ | $12$ |
| $4$ | $18$ |
| $6$ | $24$ |

**Explanation**

First, let’s find the equation of line $m$ in slope-intercept form,

$$
y = mx + b
$$

We’ll start by calculating the slope $m$ using the points $(2,\ 12)$ and $(4,\ 18){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{18 - 12}{4 - 2} \\
&= \frac{6}{2} \\
&= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we find the $y$-intercept $b$ using the point $(2, 12){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
12 &= 3(2) + b \\
b &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, the equation of line $m$ is

$$
y = 3x + 6
$$

Since line $n$ is the result of translating line $m$ by $2$ units up, its equation is

$$
y = 3x + 8
$$

Finally, to find the $x$-intercept of line $n$, we set $y = 0$ in the above equation and solve for $x{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &= 3x + 8 \\
3x &= -8 \\
x &= -\frac{8}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the $x$-intercept of line $n$ is

$$
\left(-\dfrac{8}{3}, 0\right)
$$

## Identifying a Linear Function Given a Corresponding Nonlinear Function in Tabular Form

**Example:** The table above shows three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $d(x)$, where $d(x)=\dfrac{r(x)}{x + 1}$ and $r$ is a linear function. What is the $y$-intercept of the graph of $y=r(x)$ in the $xy$-plane?

| $x$ | $d(x)$ |
| --- | ---: |
| $-3$ | $-6$ |
| $1$ | $2$ |
| $3$ | $0$ |

**Explanation**

We are told that

$$
d(x) = \dfrac{r(x)}{x + 1}
$$

and that $r$ is a linear function. To isolate $r(x)$, we multiply both sides of the equation by $(x + 1){:}$

$$
r(x) = d(x) \cdot (x + 1)
$$

Using the values from the table, we compute $r(x)$ at three $x$-values.

- When $x = -3$, we have
$r(-3)|= d(-3) \cdot (-3 + 1); = (-6) \cdot (-2); = 12$.
- When $x = 1$, we have
$r(1)|= d(1) \cdot (1 + 1); = 2 \cdot 2; = 4$.
- When $x = 3$, we have
$r(3)|= d(3) \cdot (3 + 1); = 0 \cdot 4; = 0$.

Now, we have three points on the graph of $y=r(x)$, namely, $(-3, 12)$, $(1, 4)$, and $(3, 0)$.

Since $r$ is linear, we want to find the equation representing the line that passes through these points in the form

$$
y = mx + b
$$

where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

We use the two given points, say

$$
(x_1, y_1) = (-3, 12)
$$

and

$$
(x_2, y_2) = (1, 4)
$$

to find the slope $m{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} \\
&= (4 - 12)/(1 - (-3)) \\
&= \frac{-8}{4} \\
&=-2
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we substitute any point the line passes through, such as $(3, 0)$, into the equation to solve for $b{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= -2x + b \\
0 &= -2(3) + b \\
b &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the equation of the line $y=r(x)$ is

$$
y = -2x + 6
$$

The $y$-intercept is the value of $r(0)$, which we evaluate by substituting $x=0$ into the obtained equation:

$$
r(0) = -2(0) + 6 = 6
$$

So, the $y$-intercept of the graph of $y = r(x)$ is $(0, 6)$.
