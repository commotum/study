## Introduction

We'll now practice working with perpendicular lines. Here, we'll find slopes of lines that are perpendicular to given equations, whether written in slope-intercept form, general form, or represented as vertical lines.

---

<a id="finding-the-slope-of-a-line-perpendicular-to-a-line-given-in-slope-intercept-form"></a>
## Finding the Slope of a Line Perpendicular to a Line Given in Slope-Intercept Form

**Example:** Line $p$ is defined by $y = -\dfrac{7}{12}x + 5$. Line $q$ is perpendicular to line $p$ in the $xy$-plane. What is the slope of line $q$?

**Explanation**

The slope of line $p$ is

$$
-\dfrac{7}{12}
$$

Since the slopes of perpendicular lines are negative reciprocals of each other, we start by taking the reciprocal of

$$
-\dfrac{7}{12}
$$

which is

$$
-\dfrac{12}{7}
$$

and then change the sign. This gives us the slope of line $q{:}$

$$
m_q = \dfrac{12}{7}
$$

Therefore, the slope of line $q$ is

$$
\dfrac{12}{7}
$$

## Finding the Slope of the Line Perpendicular to a Line Given in General Form

**Example:** Line $f$ is defined by $\dfrac{4}{7}x - \dfrac{2}{3}y + 6 = 0$. Line $g$ is perpendicular to line $f$ in the $xy$-plane. What is the slope of line $g$?

**Explanation**

First, we rewrite the equation in slope-intercept form to find the slope of line $f{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{4}{7}x - \frac{2}{3}y + 6 &= 0 \\
- \frac{2}{3}y &= -\frac{4}{7}x - 6 \\
y &= \frac{6}{7}x + 9
\end{aligned}
$$

So, the slope of line $f$ is

$$
\dfrac{6}{7}
$$

A line perpendicular to this line has a slope that is the negative reciprocal. That means we flip the fraction and change the sign.

The reciprocal of

$$
\dfrac{6}{7}
$$

is

$$
\dfrac{7}{6}
$$

and taking the opposite sign gives

$$
m_g = -\dfrac{7}{6}
$$

Therefore, the slope of line $g$ is

$$
-\dfrac{7}{6}
$$

## Finding the Slope of the Line Perpendicular to a Vertical Line

**Example:** Line $b$ in the $xy$-plane is perpendicular to the line with equation $x = -8$. What is the slope of line $b$?

**Explanation**

The line given by $x = -8$ is a vertical line, which has an undefined slope.

A line perpendicular to a vertical line is horizontal, and the slope of any horizontal line is $0$. Therefore, the slope of line $b$ is $0$.
