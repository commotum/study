This topic will provide practice in analyzing and constructing parallel lines. We'll determine slopes from equations or graphs and write equations for lines parallel to those given.

## Finding the Slope of a Line Parallel to a Line Given in Slope-Intercept Form

**Example:** Consider the linear function $g(x) = -\dfrac{2}{3}x + 1$. If the graph of $y = g(x)$ is parallel to line $k$ in the coordinate plane, what is the slope of line $k$?

**Explanation**

First, observe that the function $g$ is written in slope-intercept form, which is

$$
y = mx + b
$$

where $m$ represents the slope.

So, the slope of the graph of $y = g(x)$ is

$$
-\dfrac{2}{3}
$$

Since parallel lines have the same slope, the slope of line $k$ is also

$$
-\dfrac{2}{3}
$$

## Finding the Slope of a Line Parallel to a Line Shown on a Graph

**Example:** Line $f$ is shown in the $xy$-plane. If line $g$ is parallel to line $f$, what is the slope $m$ of line $g$?

![](<../Source/1.7.1. Parallel Lines/Images/afc9f96eee461ca8bb20194a44b15444.png>)

**Explanation**

To find the slope of line $g$, we first determine the slope of line $f$, since parallel lines share the same slope.

From the graph, we identify two clearly marked points, $(0, -3)$ and $(4, 3)$, on line $f$ and use them to calculate the slope.

The slope formula is

$$
m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}
$$

Substituting the coordinates, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= (3 - (-3))/(4 - 0) \\
&= \frac{6}{4} \\
&= \frac{3}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of line $g$ is

$$
\dfrac 3 2
$$

## Finding the Line Passing Through a Point and Parallel to a Line Given in Slope-Intercept Form

**Example:** In the $xy$-plane, line $g$ passes through the point $(0, 2)$ and is parallel to the line represented by the equation $y = -4x + 1$. If line $g$ also passes through the point $(3, d)$, what is the value of $d$?

**Explanation**

Because line $g$ is parallel to

$$
y = -4x + 1
$$

it must have the same slope, which is $-4$.

Since line $g$ passes through the point $(0, 2)$, its equation is

$$
y = -4x + 2
$$

Then, we substitute $x = 3$ to find $d{:}$

$$
d = -4(3) + 2 = -12 + 2 = -10
$$

Therefore, the value of $d$ is $-10$.
