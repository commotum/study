## Introduction

We categorize angles that are not right, straight, or full into three groups.

- An **acute angle** has a measure between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$:![](<../Source/5.2.6. Acute, Obtuse, and Reflex Angles/Images/da96ee34aafe8dd3f89bff2f146ddcb2.png>)
- An **obtuse angle** has a measure between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$:![](<../Source/5.2.6. Acute, Obtuse, and Reflex Angles/Images/4b258aab2483422654b33317693edc24.png>)

- Angles whose measure is between $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ are called **reflex angles**.![](<../Source/5.2.6. Acute, Obtuse, and Reflex Angles/Images/d52887cae6395f2c49e1538ab2f08cb6.png>)
