## Introduction

The distance between two points in the coordinate plane is the number of units between them. Let's explore two methods for calculating the horizontal distance between two points with the points $(1,1)$ and $(5,1)$.

**Method 1**

The distance between two points is the length of the line segment that connects them.

First, we plot the given points in the coordinate plane.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/08f08da4bf92a93545e51e0c0838e0cf.png>)

From the diagram, we see that the length of the segment is $4$ units, so the distance equals $4$.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/64e452c4227adfa8cc18f504d550d973.png>)

**Method 2**

We can find the distance between the two points by finding the distance of each point from the $y$-axis and then subtracting.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/25bb5c835012f844e078d32d76e8fac1.png>)

The point $({\color{blue}{1}},1)$ is at a distance of $\color{blue}{1}$ from the $y$-axis, and the point $({\color{red}{5}},1)$ is at a distance of $\color{red}{5}$ from the $y$-axis. Therefore, the distance between the two points is

$$
{\color{red}{5}} - {\color{blue}{1}} = 4
$$

From the diagram, we see that the length of the segment is $3$ units, so the distance equals $3$.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/75992fea9d6c8aa0ed7da4db8fb089b3.png>)

The distance between two points is the length of the line segment that connects them.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/aad49ecf7b3399610cc07e33b5c9d9f2.png>)

## The Distance Between Two Points in Different Quadrants

When we want to find the distance between two points in different quadrants, method 1 works exactly the same, but method 2 is slightly different.

We can find the distance between the two points by finding the distance of each point from the $y$-axis for a horizontal distance, or $x$-axis for a vertical distance, and then *adding*.

So, what's the distance between $(-3,2)$ and $(1,2)$?

**Method 1**

We plot the given points in the coordinate plane.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/6ead0542500fe43d991f0603b149ec16.png>)

The distance between two points is the length of the line segment that connects them.

From the diagram, we see that the length of the segment is $4$ units, so the distance equals $4$.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/03d4884b05f30977d29a31e43621d9de.png>)

**Method 2**

We can find the distance between the two points by finding the distance of each point from the $y$-axis and then adding.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/a51519bbf204fed70fac371543dfdfc3.png>)

The point $({\color{blue}{1}},2)$ is at a distance of $\color{blue}{1}$ from the $y$-axis, and the point $({\color{red}{-3}},2)$ is at a distance of $\color{red}{3}$ from the $y$-axis. Therefore, the distance between the two points is

$$
{\color{red}{3}} + {\color{blue}{1}} = 4
$$

## Finding the Distance Between Two Points: Crossing the Axis

**Example:** What is the distance between the points $(3,4)$ and $(3,-3)$?

**Explanation**

**Method 1**

We plot the given points in the coordinate plane.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/7e39dd61d8e3737eec0bbbff80bebdc9.png>)

The distance between two points is the length of the line segment that connects them.

From the diagram, we see that the length of the segment is $7$ units, so the distance equals $7$.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/ac0e4ef684527a4154cfc95c968e2077.png>)

**Method 2**

We can find the distance between the two points by finding the distance of each point from the $x$-axis and then adding.

![](<../Source/3.1.2. Distances Between Points in the Coordinate Plane/Images/6998d232dc8459ca83fc52c951e408bc.png>)

The point $(3,{\color{blue}{4}})$ is at a distance of $\color{blue}{4}$ from the $x$-axis, and the point $(3,{\color{red}{-3}})$ is at a distance of $\color{red}{3}$ from the $x$-axis. Therefore, the distance between the two points is

$$
{\color{red}{3}} + {\color{blue}{4}} = 7
$$
