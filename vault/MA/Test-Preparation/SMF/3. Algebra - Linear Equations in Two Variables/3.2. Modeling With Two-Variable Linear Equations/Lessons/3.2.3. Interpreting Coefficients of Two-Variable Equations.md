## Introduction

In this lesson, we'll learn how to *interpret* the meaning of numbers, variables, and coefficients in two-variable equations. Understanding these interpretations is important because it helps connect an equation to the situation it represents.

For example, the equation below shows the number of kilometers, $p$, that a bus travels in the morning and the number of kilometers, $q$, that it travels in the evening.

$$
p + q = 110
$$

How can we interpret the number $110$ in this context?

In this equation,

- the term $p$ represents the number of kilometers traveled in the morning, and
- the term $q$ represents the number of kilometers traveled in the evening.

So, the left-hand side, $p+q$, represents the total number of kilometers the bus travels in the morning and evening.

Therefore, the right-hand side, $110$, also represents the total number of kilometers the bus travels in the morning and evening.

Let's see some more examples.

## Interpreting the Total

**Example:** $5w + 3j = 90$
Lucas filled $5$ water tanks that held $w$ liters each and $3$ jugs that held $j$ liters each. If the given equation represents this situation, which of the following is the best interpretation of $90$ in this context?

**Explanation**

In the given equation,

- the term $5w$ represents the total water in the tanks, wherethe coefficient $5$ represents the number of tanks, and the variable $w$ denotes the capacity of each tank;
- the term $3j$ represents the total water in the jugs, wherethe coefficient $3$ represents the number of jugs, and the variable $j$ denotes the capacity of each jug.

So, the left-hand side, $5w + 3j$, gives the total water Lucas filled, in liters.

Therefore, the right-hand side, $90$, also represents *the total water filled, in liters, in all tanks and jugs.*

## Interpreting Variables

**Example:** Two friends are reading books. Alice read $5$ books, and Bob read $15$ books. Together they read $1{,}200$ pages. The equation
$5p + 15q = 1{,}200$
represents this situation. What is the best interpretation of $q$ in this context?

**Explanation**

In the given equation,

- the term $5p$ represents the total pages read by Alice, and
- the term $15q$ represents the total pages read by Bob.

We know that Bob read $15$ books.

Therefore, the variable $q$ gives *the* ***average*** *number of pages per book read by Bob.*

## Interpreting Coefficients

**Example:** A reading club completed a total of $3{,}900$ pages last week. The equation
$210a + 120b = 3{,}900$
represents this situation, where $a$ is the number of long sessions and $b$ is the number of short sessions held. How many more pages does each long session take than each short session?

**Explanation**

In the given equation,

- the term $210a$ represents the total number of pages read in long sessions, and
- the term $120b$ represents the total number of pages read in short sessions.

We know that there were $a$ long sessions and $b$ short sessions last week.

Therefore,

- the coefficient $210$ gives the number of pages in each long session, and
- the coefficient $120$ gives the number of pages in each short session.

To find how many more pages each long session has than each short session, we subtract $120$ from $210{:}$

$$
210 - 120 = 90
$$

Therefore, each long session has $90$ more pages than each short session.
