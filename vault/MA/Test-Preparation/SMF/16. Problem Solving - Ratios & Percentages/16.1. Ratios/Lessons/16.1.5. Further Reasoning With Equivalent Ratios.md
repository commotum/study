## Introduction

Sometimes, we can use ratios to find quantities that aren't explicitly given in the ratio.

For instance, suppose that the ratio of boys to girls in the fifth grade is $3: 4$. If there are $15$ boys, how many students are there in total?

Notice that the "total number of students" doesn't appear in the ratio. However, we can find the number of students by adding the total number of boys and the number of girls.

We start with the ratio ${\color{blue}3}:{\color{red}4}$ and write it as a fraction:

$$
\dfrac{{\color{blue}3}}{{\color{red}4}}
$$

In this fraction, the numerator represents the number of boys and the denominator represents the number of girls.

We know that there are $15$ boys, so we want to make a numerator of $15$. We can do this by multiplying the numerator and denominator by $5$:

$$
\dfrac{{\color{blue}3}\times 5}{{\color{red}4}\times 5} = \dfrac{{\color{blue}15}}{{\color{red}20}}
$$

Therefore, since there are $15$ boys in the fifth grade, there must also be $20$ girls. So the total number of students is

$$
{\color{blue}15} + {\color{red}20} = 35\,
$$

## Calculating an Amount Given a Ratio

**Example:** In a ballet school, the ratio of the number of students with black hair to the total number of students is $5: 8$. If there are $25$ students with black hair, how many students are there in ballet school who don't have black hair?

**Explanation**

We start with the ratio ${\color{blue}5}:{\color{red}8}$ and write it as a fraction:

$$
\dfrac{{\color{blue}5}}{{\color{red}8}}
$$

In this fraction, the numerator represents the number of students with black hair and the denominator represents the total number of students.

We know that there are $25$ students with black hair, so we want to make a numerator of $25$. We can do this by multiplying the numerator and denominator by $5$:

$$
\dfrac{{\color{blue}5}\times 5}{{\color{red}8}\times 5} = \dfrac{{\color{blue}25}}{{\color{red}40}}
$$

Therefore, since there are $25$ students with black hair, there must be $40$ students at the ballet school. Of this amount, the number of students who do not have black hair is

$$
40 - 25 = 15
$$

## Calculating an Amount Given a Ratio as a Fraction

**Example:** In a Chicago hospital, the ratio of the number of boys born to the total number of babies born is $\dfrac{4}{9}$. If $100$ boys were born in that hospital in a particular month, how many girls were born that month?

**Explanation**

We are given that the ratio of the number of boys born to the total number of babies born is

$$
\dfrac{4}{9}
$$

In this fraction, the numerator represents the number of boys born and the denominator represents the total number of babies born.

We know that $100$ boys were born, so we want to make a numerator of $100$. We can do this by multiplying the numerator and denominator by $25$:

$$
\dfrac{{\color{blue}4}\times 25}{{\color{red}9}\times 25} = \dfrac{{\color{blue}100}}{{\color{red}225}}
$$

Therefore, since $100$ boys were born, $225$ babies were born during that month. Of this amount, the number of girls was

$$
225 - 100 = 125
$$
