## Introduction

Recall that the absolute value of a number represents the distance of that number from the origin.

Let's take a look at the number line below.

![](<../Source/12.1.2. Rules of Absolute Value/Images/1d951cedd50f792b48ebac86ff6d6fe2.png>)

Since $-3$ and $3$ are at the same distance from the origin, we have

$$
\begin{vmatrix}-3 & = & 3\end{vmatrix} = 3
$$

In general, for any real number $a$,

$$
\begin{vmatrix}-a & = & a\end{vmatrix}
$$

We can use this fact to simplify expressions containing absolute value. Let's see an example.

---

<a id="simplifying-the-absolute-value-of-a-negative-term"></a>
## Simplifying the Absolute Value of a Negative Term

**Example:** Simplify the expression $\mid -2x \mid$.

**Explanation**

Since

$$
\begin{vmatrix}-a & = & a\end{vmatrix}
$$

we can rewrite our expression as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{vmatrix}-2x & &= & 2x\end{vmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="the-distributive-properties"></a>
## The Distributive Properties

The absolute value operation is **distributive with respect to multiplication.** This means that the absolute value of a product equals the product of the absolute values.

To demonstrate, consider the following expression:

$$
\mid (-6) \cdot 2 \mid
$$

We can evaluate this expression in two separate ways:

- The first is to find the absolute value of the product. In doing this, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{vmatrix}(-6) \cdot 2 & &= & -12\end{vmatrix} \\
&= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

- The second is to find the product of the absolute values. In doing this, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{vmatrix}(-6) \cdot 2 & &= & -6 & \cdot & 2\end{vmatrix} \\
&= 6 \cdot 2 \\
&= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

In general

$$
\begin{vmatrix}a \cdot b & = & a & \cdot & b\end{vmatrix}
$$

The absolute value operation is also distributive with respect to division. In general,

$$
\left\begin{vmatrix}\dfrac{a}{b}\right & = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix}a &}{\end{vmatrix}b\end{vmatrix}}
$$

---

<a id="distributing-the-absolute-value-over-multiplication"></a>
## Distributing the Absolute Value Over Multiplication

**Example:** Simplify the expression $\mid -18a \mid$.

**Explanation**

Firstly, since

$$
\begin{vmatrix}-a & = & a\end{vmatrix}
$$

we can write our expression as

$$
\mid 18a \mid
$$

Then, we distribute the absolute value over the multiplication, and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mid 18a \mid &=  \\
\mid 18 \cdot a \mid &=  \\
\begin{vmatrix}18 & \cdot & a\end{vmatrix} &=  \\
18 \cdot\begin{vmatrix}a & &= \\ 18 & a\end{vmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

<a id="distributing-the-absolute-value-over-division"></a>
## Distributing the Absolute Value Over Division

**Example:** Simplify the expression $\left|-\dfrac{9}{5c}\right|$.

**Explanation**

Firstly, since

$$
\begin{vmatrix}-a & = & a\end{vmatrix}
$$

we can write our expression as

$$
\left|\dfrac{9}{5c}\right|
$$

Then, we distribute the absolute value over the division, and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mid \frac{9}{5c} \mid &=  \\
\frac{\begin{vmatrix}9 &}{\mid 5c\end{vmatrix}} &=  \\
\frac{9}{\mid 5c\begin{vmatrix}} &= \\ \frac{9}{\begin{vmatrix}5 & \cdot & c\end{vmatrix}}|= \\ \frac{9}{5 \mid c\end{vmatrix}}
\end{aligned}
$$
