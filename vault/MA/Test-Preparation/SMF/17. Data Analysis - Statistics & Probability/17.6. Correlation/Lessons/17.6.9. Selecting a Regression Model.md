## Introduction

A common problem in statistics is recognizing whether data follows a trend. Thankfully, we can often identify trends in data simply by looking at a scatter plot. We list a few common examples below:

- **Positive linear correlation**: The points seem to follow a straight line with a positive slope.

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/8f06248a8b4fcc30f1e0c1d025fee1ed.png>)

- **Negative linear correlation**: The points seem to follow a straight line with a negative slope.

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/7029bc141a003e212fdc15d5f35903e8.png>)

- **Quadratic correlation**: The points on the plot are scattered along a parabola.

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/4d80fd0a5ac01626e1a5cb9a4f42eb42.png>)

- **Exponential correlation**: The points on the plot are scattered along an exponential curve.

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/2d113e5386015670a6a480ea7127473b.png>)

- **No correlation**: The points seem to be scattered randomly.

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/3e8f2bb3de6e692642dfacb7ab3dafb7.png>)

The process of fitting data to a linear, quadratic, exponential, or another type of curve is called **regression**.

## Identifying an Appropriate Regression Model

**Example:** A country keeps a record of its population growth. The scatter plot above shows the correspondence between the population (in millions) and the time (in years) since records began.
Which of the following is the most appropriate regression model for this data?

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/8ee70e80b9a843d85fa6631144e192e4.png>)

1. Linear
2. Quadratic
3. Exponential

**Explanation**

As values of $x$ increase, the corresponding values of $y$ increase rapidly. It seems like the points on the plot are scattered along an exponential curve, as shown below.

![](<../Source/17.6.9. Selecting a Regression Model/Images/ce69557a403a035fef77b964524fb2c5.png>)

Therefore, the most appropriate regression model is *exponential.*
