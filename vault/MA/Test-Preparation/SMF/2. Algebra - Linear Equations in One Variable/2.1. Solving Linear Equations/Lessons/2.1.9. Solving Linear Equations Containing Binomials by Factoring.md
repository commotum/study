## Introduction

Consider the following equation:

$$
7(n - 2) + 5(n - 2) = 72
$$

It's tempting to begin by expanding the binomials using the distributive law. However, there is an easier, faster way in this case!

Notice that the binomial expression $(n-2)$ appears in both terms on the left-hand side:

$$
7{\color{blue}(n - 2)} + 5{\color{blue}(n - 2)} = 72
$$

This means $(n-2)$ is a *common factor* that we can factor out. This works just like the distributive property, but *in reverse*:

$$
\begin{aligned}
7(n - 2) + 5(n - 2) &= 72 \\
(7 + 5)(n - 2) &= 72
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, if we simplify the coefficient $7+5$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
12(n - 2) &= 72
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, to isolate $(n-2)$, we divide both sides by $12$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
12(n - 2) &= 72 \\
(12(n - 2))/(12) &= \frac{72}{12} \\
n - 2 &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

This equation tells us that the value of $(n-2)$ is $6$. If needed, we can continue by solving for $n$ itself, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
n - 2 &= 6 \\
n - 2 + 2 &= 6 + 2 \\
n &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

The key idea is that whenever the same binomial factor appears in multiple terms, we can factor it out first, simplify the coefficient, and then apply the usual equation-solving skills to determine the unknown value.

Let's see some more examples.

## Solving Equations by Factoring Linear Expressions

**Example:** If $5(6b+2) - 8(6b+2) = 9$, then what is the value of $b$?

**Explanation**

Notice that $(6b+2)$ is a common factor in both terms on the left-hand side.

We begin by factoring out $(6b+2){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
5(6b + 2) - 8(6b + 2) &= 9 \\
(5 - 8)(6b + 2) &= 9 \\
- 3(6b + 2) &= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we divide both sides by $-3$ to isolate $(6b+2){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
-3(6b + 2) &= 9 \\
(-3(6b + 2))/(-3) &= \frac{9}{-3} \\
6b + 2 &= -3
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we solve the obtained linear equation for $b$, as usual:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6b + 2 &= -3 \\
6b + 2 - 2 &= -3 - 2 \\
6b &= -5 \\
\frac{6b}{6} &= \frac{-5}{6} \\
b &= -\frac{5}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the solution is

$$
b=-\dfrac{5}{6}
$$

## Solving Equations Containing Fractional and Decimals Coefficients

**Example:** $0.85(2v + 3) - 0.35(2v + 3) = 2.5$
What value of $v$ is the solution to the given equation?

**Explanation**

Notice that $(2v+3)$ is a common factor in both terms on the left-hand side.

We begin by factoring out $(2v+3){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.85(2v + 3) - 0.35(2v + 3) &= 2.5 \\
(0.85 - 0.35)(2v + 3) &= 2.5 \\
0.50(2v + 3) &= 2.5
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply both sides by $10$ to remove the decimals. This gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
10 \cdot 0.50(2v + 3) &= 10 \cdot 2.5 \\
5(2v + 3) &= 25
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, divide both sides by $5$ to isolate $(2v+3){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2v + 3 &= \frac{25}{5} \\
2v + 3 &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, solve for $v{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2v + 3 &= 5 \\
2v &= 2 \\
v &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the solution is $v=1$.

## Solving Linear Equations With Common Binomials on Both Sides

**Example:** If $5 - 2(x - 1) = 25 - 6(x - 1)$, then what is the value of $x$?

**Explanation**

Notice that $(x-1)$ is a common factor on both sides of the equation.

We must isolate $(x-1)$ on one side of the equation. We start by adding $6(x-1)$ to both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 2(x - 1) &= 25 - 6(x - 1) \\
5 - 2(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) &= 25 - 6(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) \\
5 - 2(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) &= 25 - 6(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) \\
5 - 2(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) &= 25
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we subtract $5$ from both sides, and factor $(x-1)$ on the left-hand side:

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 2(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) - 5 &= 25 - 5 \\
- 2(x - 1) + 6(x - 1) &= 20 \\
(-2 + 6)(x - 1) &= 20 \\
4(x - 1) &= 20
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we divide both sides by $4$ and isolate $(x - 1){:}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
4(x - 1) &= 20 \\
(4(x - 1))/(4) &= \frac{20}{4} \\
x - 1 &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we solve the obtained linear equation for $x$, as usual:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x - 1 &= 5 \\
x - 1 + 1 &= 5 + 1 \\
x &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the solution is $x=6$.
