## Problem 1

Decide whether the statement is true or false:

If

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a_kx^k
$$

converges at $x=-7$, then it converges at $x=6$.

## Problem 2

If

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a_kx^k
$$

converges at $x=4$, then it diverges at $x=6$.

## Problem 3

Find the interval of convergence.

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x+3)^k}{8^k}
$$

Give the answer in interval notation.

---
## Problem 4

Find the interval of convergence.

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(x-11)^k}{k^2}
$$

Give the answer in interval notation. Use $\varnothing$ if the interval is empty.

---
## Problem 5

Consider the power series

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^kx^k}{6^k(k+5)}.
$$

1. Use the Ratio Test to find its radius and interval of convergence.
2. Use the Root Test to find its radius and interval of convergence.

Give exact values and use interval notation.

## Problem 10

The function $f$ is defined by

$$
f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^k}{10^k}.
$$

1. Find the domain of $f$ in interval notation.
2. Evaluate $f(9)$ and $f(-1)$ exactly.
3. Sum the series to obtain a formula for $f(x)$.

---
## Problem 11

The power series

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a_kx^k
$$

converges at $x=6$ and diverges at $x=-18$. Select every statement that must be true.

- The series converges at $x=-6$.
- The series diverges at $x=20$.
- The series converges at $x=3$.
- The series converges absolutely at $x=4$.
- The series converges absolutely at $x=6$.
- The series diverges at $x=7$.

## Problem 12

## Problem 14

Suppose $R$ is the radius of convergence of

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_kx^k.
$$

Express in terms of $R$ the radius of convergence of

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_kx^{3k}.
$$
