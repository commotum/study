## Introduction

To simplify an expression containing parentheses, we expand the parentheses and then collect like terms.

For example, let's simplify the following expression:

$$
3(x + 2) + 4x
$$

Our first step is to distribute the $3$ over each of the terms in the parentheses:

$$
\begin{aligned} 3(x + 2) + 4x \\[5pt] &= 3\cdot x + 3\cdot 2 + 4x \\[5pt] &= (3x) + (6) + 4x \\[5pt] &= 3x +6 + 4x & \end{aligned}
$$

The second and final step is to collect the like terms:

$$
\begin{aligned} 3x + 6 + 4x \\ &= (3x + 4x) + 6 \\ &= 7x + 6 \end{aligned}
$$

## Simplifying Linear Expressions With Positive Numbers in Parentheses

**Example:** Simplify $(4+c)\cdot 3-8$.

**Explanation**

First, we distribute the $3$ over each of the terms in parentheses.

$$
\begin{aligned} (4+c)\cdot 3-8 \\[5pt] &= 4\cdot3+c\cdot 3-8 \\[5pt] &= (12)+(3c)-8 \\[5pt] &= 12+3c-8 & \end{aligned}
$$

Then, we collect like terms.

$$
\begin{aligned} 12+3c-8 \\[5pt] &= 3c +(12-8) \\[5pt] &= 3c+4 \end{aligned}
$$

## Simplifying Linear Expressions With Positive Numbers in Parentheses: Distributing a Negative Number

**Example:** $(-4)(8x+7y) +30y=$

**Explanation**

First, we distribute the $(-4)$ over each of the terms in parentheses.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-4)(8x + 7y) + 30y &=  \\
(-4) \cdot 8x + (-4) \cdot 7y + 30y &=  \\
(-32x) + (-28y) + 30y &=  \\
&= - 32x - 28y + 30y
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we collect like terms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-32x - 28y + 30y &=  \\
- 32x + (-28y + 30y) &=  \\
&= - 32x + 2y
\end{aligned}
$$

## Simplifying Linear Expressions With Negative Numbers in Parentheses

**Example:** Simplify $(5 - y) \cdot 3 - 10$

**Explanation**

First, we distribute the $3$ over each of the terms in parentheses.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(5 - y) \cdot 3 - 10 &=  \\
5 \cdot 3 + (-y) \cdot 3 - 10 &=  \\
(15) + (-3y) - 10 &=  \\
&= 15 - 3y - 10
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we collect like terms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
15 - 3y - 10 &=  \\
(15 - 10) - 3y &=  \\
&= 5 - 3y
\end{aligned}
$$
