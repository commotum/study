## Problem 1

Determine if the following series converges or diverges. Clearly state which test you use and show that the series meets the requirements to use this test.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{11^{n-1}}{12^n+5 n}
$$

---
## Problem 2

Determine if the following series converges or diverges. Clearly state which test you use and show that the series meets the requirements to use this test.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-3)^{n-1} \cdot n^4}{5^{2 n+1}}
$$

---
## Problem 3

Determine if the following series converges or diverges. Clearly state which test you use and show that the series meets the requirements to use this test.

$$
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{\ln (n+1)}{2 n+2}
$$

---
## Problem 4

Determine if the following series converges or diverges. Clearly state which test you use and show that the series meets the requirements to use this test.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{3 n}{n^2+2 n+3}
$$

## Problem 5

Determine if the following series is absolutely convergent, conditionally convergent, or divergent. Clearly state which test you use and show that the series meets the requirements to use this test.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n \frac{2+n}{9+n^2}
$$
