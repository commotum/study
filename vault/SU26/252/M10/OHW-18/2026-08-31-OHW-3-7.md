## Problem 1

Decide whether the integral is improper and select a reason.

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-8x}\,dx
$$

Classification choices:

- Not improper
- Improper

Reason choices:

- The upper limit of integration is $\infty$.
- The function is continuous on $[0,\infty)$.
- The function is undefined at $x=0$.

## Problem 12

Decide whether the integral is improper and select a reason.

$$
\int_{-3}^3\frac{x}{x^2+3}\,dx
$$

Classification choices:

- Not improper
- Improper

Reason choices:

- The function is continuous on the closed interval $[-3,3]$.
- The function is undefined at $x=-3$.
- The function is continuous and defined at $x=-3$.
- The function is undefined at $x=0$.
