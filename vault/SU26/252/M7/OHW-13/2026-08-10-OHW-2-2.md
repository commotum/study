## Problem 1

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-1
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |
  **Question 1**

  A solid has a circular base of radius $a=4$. Every cross section perpendicular to the base is a square. Find the exact volume.

  ![[Source/2026-08-10-OHW-2-2/Images/circular-base-square-cross-sections.png]]

  $$
  V= ==1024/3==
  $$
feedback: |
  At position $x$, the circle gives a square side length $2\sqrt{16-x^2}$. Thus

  $$
  V=\int_{-4}^{4}\left(2\sqrt{16-x^2}\right)^2dx
   =4\int_{-4}^{4}(16-x^2)dx
   =\frac{1024}{3}.
  $$
```

```quiz
  **Question 2**

  The base is enclosed by $y=5x^2$, $x=1$, and $y=0$. Cross sections perpendicular to the $x$-axis are semicircles. Find the exact volume.

  $$
  V= ==5pi/8==
  $$
feedback: |
  The semicircle diameter is $d=5x^2$, so its area is $A(x)=\frac{\pi}{8}d^2=\frac{25\pi}{8}x^4$. Therefore

  $$
  V=\frac{25\pi}{8}\int_0^1x^4dx=\frac{5\pi}{8}.
  $$
```

## Problem 5

Let $R$ be bounded by

$$
y=\sin(\pi x)+1,\qquad y=3-\frac{x^2}{2},\qquad x=-1.
$$

The curves meet at $x=2$.

![[Source/2026-08-10-OHW-2-2/Images/sine-parabola-region.png]]

### Part A — Cross-sectional slicing

```quiz
type: radio
id: q-5a
content: |
  **Question 5a**

  Which integral gives the volume when perpendicular cross sections are semicircles whose diameters lie in $R$?
options:
- id: a
  content: |
    $$\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{2}\pi\left(\frac{3-\frac{x^2}{2}-\sin(\pi x)-1}{2}\right)^2dx$$
  feedback: |
    This is the area of a full circle. Each slice is only a semicircle, so a factor of $1/2$ is missing.
- id: b
  content: |
    $$\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{2}\left[\pi\left(3-\frac{x^2}{2}\right)^2-\pi\left(\sin(\pi x)+1\right)^2\right]dx$$
  feedback: |
    This subtracts two circular areas, which is a washer setup rather than a semicircular cross section.
- id: c
  content: |
    $$\displaystyle \int_{0}^{1}\frac{\pi}{2}\left(\frac{3-\frac{x^2}{2}-\sin(\pi x)-1}{2}\right)^2dx$$
  feedback: |
    The slice area is correct, but the region runs from $x=-1$ to $x=2$, not from $0$ to $1$.
- id: d
  content: |
    $$\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{2}\pi\left(3-\frac{x^2}{2}-\sin(\pi x)-1\right)^2dx$$
  feedback: |
    The vertical difference is the diameter, not the radius, and the cross section is only half a circle.
- id: e
  content: |
    $$\displaystyle \int_{-1}^{2}\frac{\pi}{2}\left(\frac{3-\frac{x^2}{2}-\sin(\pi x)-1}{2}\right)^2dx$$
  correct: true
  feedback: |
    Correct. The diameter is the upper curve minus the lower curve, the radius is half that value, and a semicircle has area $\frac12\pi r^2$.
```
