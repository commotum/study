## Optimized Study Order

### 1. Spring–Mass Simple Harmonic Motion

1. [ ] [Recover Spring Force, Stiffness, and Work from Extension Data](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-1.md>) — choose a sign convention for Hooke's law and distinguish force from spring work.
2. [ ] [Find Spring Speed and the Energy Split at a Displacement](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-2.md>) — compare the current spring energy with the amplitude energy before solving for speed.
3. [ ] [Infer and Scale a Mass–Spring Frequency](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-3.md>) — use $f=(2\pi)^{-1}\sqrt{k/m}$ and its square-root dependencies.
4. [ ] [Translate an SHM State into $x(t)$, $v(t)$, and $a(t)$](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-4.md>) — choose the phase from the initial state and keep the derivative signs consistent.

### 2. Simple and Physical Pendula

5. [ ] [Solve a Simple-Pendulum Target from Period Data](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-5.md>) — isolate length, gravity, or period while respecting the small-angle model.
6. [ ] [Assemble $I_p$ and Center of Mass for a Physical Pendulum](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-6.md>) — build pivot inertia and center-of-mass distance separately before using the period equation.

### 3. Traveling Waves and Strings

7. [ ] [Read a Wave Graph Before Using $v=f\lambda$](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-7.md>) — distinguish amplitude, wavelength, period, frequency, and propagation speed from the graph's axes.
8. [ ] [Turn a String Description into Wave Speed](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-8.md>) — calculate tension and linear density before applying $v=\sqrt{T/\mu}$.
9. [ ] [Chain Energy, Power, Area, and Intensity](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-9.md>) — keep energy-per-time separate from power-per-area.
10. [ ] [Rescale Wave Intensity or Amplitude with Distance](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-10.md>) — apply inverse-square intensity and the corresponding inverse-distance amplitude scaling.

### 4. Refraction and Sound Level

11. [ ] [Convert Light Speed and Wavelength with Refractive Index](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-11.md>) — hold frequency fixed across a boundary while speed and wavelength change.
12. [ ] [Carry a Ray through One or More Refracting Boundaries](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-12.md>) — measure every angle from the normal and apply Snell's law at each interface.
13. [ ] [Convert Between Sound Intensity and Decibels](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-13.md>) — use the logarithm in the forward direction and an exponential in reverse.
14. [ ] [Update a Sound Level after the Listener Moves](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-14.md>) — combine inverse-square distance scaling with a decibel difference.

### 5. Doppler Shift and Beats

15. [ ] [Choose Doppler Signs from Toward/Away Motion](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-15.md>) — decide whether observer and source motions increase or decrease the observed frequency before substituting.
16. [ ] [Resolve Beat-Frequency Ambiguity](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-16.md>) — use $f_b=|f_1-f_2|$ and an added observation to select the higher or lower unknown frequency.

### 6. Standing Waves

17. [ ] [Translate a Fixed-End Standing-Wave Mode](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-17.md>) — read nodes, antinodes, harmonic number, wavelength, and overtone language from the boundary pattern.
18. [ ] [Chain String Material Data into a Resonant Mode](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-18.md>) — move from material data to $\mu$, tension, wave speed, and the requested harmonic.
19. [ ] [Map Pipe Boundaries, Harmonics, and Overtones](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-19.md>) — distinguish open–open from open–closed mode families before calculating a frequency.
