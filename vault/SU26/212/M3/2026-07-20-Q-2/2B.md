# Quiz 2B

## Problem 1

Two stars of masses $M$ and $m$ orbit their common center of mass in circular orbits. Their separation is $d$, and $M=\tfrac32m$, as shown.

![](<Source/2026-07-20-Q-2/Images/problem-5-binary-star-system.png>)

Show all work and solve completely symbolically in terms of the given variables.

#### Question 1 — Part A

What is the center of mass of the system, measured from M? 
Show all work and solve completely symbolically in terms of given variables.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{\mathrm{cm}}
&=\frac{\sum_i m_i x_i}{\sum_i m_i} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{M(0)+m(d)}{M+m} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{md}{M+m} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{md}{\frac{3}{2}m+\frac{2}{2}m} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{md}{\frac{5}{2}m} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{2d}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\boxed{x_{\mathrm{cm}}=\frac{2d}{5}}
$$

#### Question 1 — Part B

Find the orbital period of the system. 
Show all work and solve completely symbolically in terms of given variables.

$$
\begin{aligned} T_{\text {Orbit }} & =2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{r^3}{G M}} \\ & =2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{2 d^3}{5 G_m}}\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\boxed{T_{\mathrm{orbit}} =2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2d^3}{5Gm}}}
$$

#### Question 1 — Part C

What is the total energy of the system? 
Show all work and solve completely symbolically in terms of given variables.

$$
\begin{aligned} E_{\mathrm{orbit}} &=-\frac{GMm}{2d} \\[6pt] &=-\frac{3Gm^2}{4d} \end{aligned}
$$$$
\boxed{E_{\mathrm{orbit}}=-\frac{3Gm^2}{4d}}
$$
