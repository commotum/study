  **Question 1**

  A light ray is traveling through air and refracts as it enters glass with an index of refraction of $1.5$, as shown.

  ![](<Images/air-glass-refraction-angle-diagram.png>)

  What is the angle with respect to the normal of the refracted light ray in the glass? Enter your answer in degrees as a number only: ==34==
feedback: |-
  The diagram's $32^\circ$ angle is measured from the surface, so the incident angle measured from the normal is

  $$
  \theta_1=90^\circ-32^\circ=58^\circ.
  $$

  Snell's law gives

  $$
  (1.00)\sin 58^\circ=(1.5)\sin\theta_2,
  $$

  so $\theta_2=34.43\ldots^\circ$. The givens support two significant figures, so the refracted angle is $34^\circ$, entered as `34`.
