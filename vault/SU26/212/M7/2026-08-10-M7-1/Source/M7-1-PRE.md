  **Question 1**

  A light ray is incident on a plane mirror. How is the angle of reflection related to the angle of incidence?
options:
- id: a
  content: |-
    The angle of reflection is less than the angle of incidence.
  feedback: |-
    For a plane mirror, the angles of incidence and reflection are both measured from the normal and are equal. The reflected angle is therefore not smaller than the incident angle.
- id: b
  content: |-
    The angle of reflection is equal to the angle of incidence.
  correct: true
  feedback: |-
    The law of reflection states that the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence when both are measured from the normal to the mirror.
- id: c
  content: |-
    The angle of reflection is greater than the angle of incidence.
  feedback: |-
    Reflection from a plane mirror preserves the ray's angle with the normal. The reflected angle is therefore equal to, not greater than, the incident angle.
