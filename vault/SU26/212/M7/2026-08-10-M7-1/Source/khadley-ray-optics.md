## The ray model of light

- Light can be modeled as a particle (photoelectric effect)
- Light can be modeled as a wave (interference)
- Light can be modeled as a ray

Assumption: Light travels in straight lines

- Valid when apertures are large compared to the wavelength of light

![[Images/rays.jpg]]

[Image source](http://hdwallpaperbook.com/backgrounds-for-pictures-free.html)

A light ray is a line in the direction along which the light energy is flowing.

![[Images/rays2.jpg]]

- A light ray continues forever unless it interacts with matter
	- At an interface it can reflect or refract
		- Within a material it can be scattered or absorbed
