```quiz

  **Question 1**

  A pipe wrench balances on a triangular base as shown. Which statement is true?

  ![](<Source/2026-08-13-Q-4/Images/balanced-pipe-wrench.png>)
options:
- id: a
  content: The side to the left of the base weighs more.
  feedback: |-
    The left side's center of mass is farther from the support, so its weight already has the longer lever arm. If the left side also weighed more, its torque would dominate and the wrench would not balance.
- id: b
  content: The side to the right of the base weighs more.
  correct: true
  feedback: |-
    Balance requires equal and opposite gravitational torques about the support: $W_Ld_L=W_Rd_R$. The left side acts through the longer effective lever arm, so the shorter right side must have the greater weight.
- id: c
  content: The sides weigh the same.
  feedback: |-
    Equal weights balance only when their centers of mass are equally far from the support. Here the left side has the longer effective lever arm, so equal side weights would not produce equal torques.
```

## Problem 4

Flute A is at $(-x_1,0)$ and flute B is at $(x_2,0)$. They emit the same frequency $f$ and begin exactly $\pi$ radians out of phase. Point P is at $(0,y)$.

![](<Source/2026-08-13-Q-4/Images/two-flute-source-geometry.png>)

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-4a
input_mode: math
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Question 4 — Part A**

  Derive the phase difference at P in terms of the given variables and the sound speed $c_s$. Show all work.

  Enter a keyboard-form expression using `x1`, `x2`, `y`, `f`, and `cs`, then reveal the reference result: ==pi+(2*pi*f/cs)*(sqrt(x2^2+y^2)-sqrt(x1^2+y^2))==
feedback: |-
  The source-to-detector distances are

  $$
  r_A=\sqrt{x_1^2+y^2},
  \qquad
  r_B=\sqrt{x_2^2+y^2}.
  $$

  Propagation adds $k(r_B-r_A)$ to the initial $\pi$-radian offset, with $k=2\pi f/c_s$. Therefore,

  $$
  \boxed{
  \Delta\phi
  =\pi+\frac{2\pi f}{c_s}
  \left(\sqrt{x_2^2+y^2}-\sqrt{x_1^2+y^2}\right)
  }.
  $$

  Reversing the subtraction changes the signed convention but represents the same relative phase after reduction modulo $2\pi$.
```


## Problem 5

A $630\ \mathrm{nm}$ laser illuminates a diffraction grating with $380\ \mathrm{slits/mm}$. A screen of width $4.5\ \mathrm{m}$ is centered a distance $D=0.72\ \mathrm{m}$ from the grating.

![](<Source/2026-08-13-Q-4/Images/diffraction-grating-screen-geometry.png>)

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-5a
input_mode: math
require_exact: true
content: |-
  **Question 5 — Part A**

  What is the angle in degrees between the central maximum and the center of the first bright fringe? Show all work.

  Enter the angle in degrees as a number only: ==14==
feedback: |-
  The grating spacing is

  $$
  d=\frac{1}{380\times10^3\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}}
  =2.6316\ldots\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m}.
  $$

  For the first bright order, $d\sin\theta_1=\lambda$, so

  $$
  \theta_1
  =\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{630\times10^{-9}}{2.6316\ldots\times10^{-6}}\right)
  =13.851\ldots^\circ
  \approx\boxed{14^\circ}.
  $$
```

```quiz
type: blank
id: q-5b
input_mode: math
require_exact: true
content: |-
  **Part B**

  How far from the central maximum is the center of the second bright fringe? Show all work.

  Enter the distance in meters as a number only: ==0.39==
feedback: |-
  For $m=2$,

  $$
  \theta_2
  =\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{2\lambda}{d}\right)
  =28.607\ldots^\circ.
  $$

  Exact screen geometry gives

  $$
  y_2=D\tan\theta_2
  =(0.72)\tan(28.607\ldots^\circ)
  =0.39267\ldots\ \mathrm{m}
  \approx\boxed{0.39\ \mathrm{m}}.
  $$

  The final value has two significant figures, matching $D=0.72\ \mathrm{m}$.
```
