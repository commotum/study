## Problem 1

Consider the following sequence:

$$
\left\{-\frac{3}{5}, \frac{9}{7},-\frac{27}{9}, \frac{81}{11},-\frac{243}{13} \ldots\right\}
$$


Write a general formula $a_n$ with $n \geq 1$. Then, determine the end behavior of this sequence.

---
## Problem 2

Let $a_1=1$ and $a_{n+1}=4-\frac{1}{a_n}, n \geq 1$. List out the first five terms of the sequence and determine the end behavior.

$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} a_{n+1}
$$

---
## Problem 3

Determine if the following series is convergent or divergent. If it is convergent, find the sum.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2 \cdot 5^{n-1}}{6^n}
$$

---
## Problem 4

Determine if the following series is convergent or divergent. If it is convergent, find the sum.

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}
$$

---
## Problem 5

Find the partial sum equation, $s_n$, for the series below. Use this to determine if the series is convergent or divergent and find its sum if it exists.

$$
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{n-1}-\frac{1}{n}\right)
$$
