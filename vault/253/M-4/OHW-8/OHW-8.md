## Problem 1

Choose the power series that gives the Taylor expansion of $f$ about $c$.

- $\displaystyle f(c)+f(c)(x-c)+\frac{f(c)(x-c)^2}{2}+\cdots+\frac{f(c)(x-c)^n}{n}+\cdots$
- $\displaystyle f(c)-f'(c)(x-c)+\frac{f''(c)(x-c)^2}{2!}-\cdots+(-1)^n\frac{f^{(n)}(c)(x-c)^n}{n!}+\cdots$
- $\displaystyle f(c)+f'(c)x+\frac{f''(c)}{2!}x^2+\cdots+\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}x^n+\cdots$
- $\displaystyle f(c)+f'(c)(x-c)+\frac{f''(c)}{2!}(x-c)^2+\cdots+\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}(x-c)^n+\cdots$

## Problem 2

Choose the correct relationship between Taylor and Maclaurin expansions.

- A Taylor expansion is a Maclaurin expansion when $c=0$.
- A Taylor expansion is a Maclaurin expansion when $x=0$.
- A Maclaurin expansion is a Taylor expansion when $x=0$.
- A Maclaurin expansion is a Taylor expansion when $c=0$.
- The two expansions are unrelated.
