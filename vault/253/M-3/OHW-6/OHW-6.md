## Problem 1

For the Ratio Test, suppose

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|=1.
$$

State what the test indicates about $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_k$.

## Problem 3

Use the Ratio Test to determine whether the series converges or diverges.

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(2k)!}{5^k8^{k-1}}
$$

Choose the appropriate conclusion: the ratio limit is $1$ and the test is inconclusive; the limit is greater than $1$ and the series diverges; the limit is less than $1$ and the series converges; or the limit is $\infty$ and the series diverges.
