## Problem 1

Select every correct statement about infinite series.

- If $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(a_k+b_k)$ converges, then both $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_k$ and $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}b_k$ converge.
- If two infinite series have different first $100$ terms but identical terms beginning with the $101$st term, then either both converge or both diverge.
- If $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_k=S$ converges and $c\neq 0$, then $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(ca_k)=cS$.
- The series $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^3$ diverges.
- For a positive-term series, an unbounded sequence of partial sums means the series could either converge or diverge.
