## Problem 1

Use the Comparison Test with

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^4}
$$

to select every valid statement below.

- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^3(k+10)}$ converges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^3(k+10)}$ diverges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^3+3}$ converges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^3+3}$ diverges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{3}{k^2+10}$ converges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{3}{k^2+10}$ diverges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^2(k+10)(k+3)}$ converges.
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^2(k+10)(k+3)}$ diverges.

## Problem 2

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{4}{9\sqrt{k}}.
$$

- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{4}{9k^{3/2}+k}$
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{4}{9\sqrt{k}+4}$
- $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{4}{9k\sqrt{k}+4}$

Then use the Comparison Test to decide whether the selected series converges or diverges.
