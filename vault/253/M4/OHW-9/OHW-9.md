## Problem 1

Find all real $x$ for which the power series converges.

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(x-7)^k}{k}
$$

Give the answer in interval notation.

---
## Problem 2

Find all real $x$ for which the power series converges.

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(x-2)^{k^2}
$$

Give the answer in interval notation.

---
## Problem 3

Find all real $x$ for which the power series converges.

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(k!)^2}{(2k)!}(x-4)^k
$$

Give the answer in interval notation.

---
## Problem 4

1. Obtain a power-series representation for

$$
\frac{1}{1-x^3}.
$$

Write it in the form $\displaystyle \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(\cdots)$.

2. Find its interval of convergence.

---
## Problem 5

Use

$$
\frac12\ln\left|\frac{1+x}{1-x}\right|
=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{2k+1}}{2k+1}
$$

to write the first three terms of an approximation for $\ln(5)$. Give the terms as reduced fractions.

---
## Problem 6

Suppose $R$ is the radius of convergence of

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_kx^k.
$$

Express in terms of $R$ the radius of convergence of

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_kx^{3k}.
$$
