- display-math:

  image_slot_id: section-001
- paragraph:
  text: On the overlap interval, multiply the two signal values point by point. This creates the product signal $x(\tau)$$h(t-\tau)$. The signed area under that product, measured along the $\tau$-axis, is the single output value for the chosen time.
- display-math:
  latex: \text{fixed }t:\quad y(t)=\text{signed area under }x(\tau)h(t-\tau)\text{ over }\tau
- paragraph:
  text: If the product is above the axis, its area adds to $y(t)$. If the product is below the axis, its area subtracts from $y(t)$. If there is no overlap, the product is zero everywhere, so that output value is zero.
- paragraph:
