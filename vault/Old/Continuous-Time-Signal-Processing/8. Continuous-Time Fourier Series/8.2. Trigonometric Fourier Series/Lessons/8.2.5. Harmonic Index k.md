## Introduction to Harmonic Index $k$

In the trigonometric Fourier series, $k$ is the positive integer harmonic index. It is the label that tells you which harmonic pair you are reading, not a separate quantity to solve for.

$$
x(t)=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\big[a_k\cos(k\omega_0 t)+b_k\sin(k\omega_0 t)\big]
$$

The template starts with the DC term $a_0/2$ and then uses each positive integer $k$ to label a cosine-sine pair. The same $k$ appears in the subscripts $a_k$ and $b_k$ and in the frequency $k\omega_0$.

$$
a_1\cos(\omega_0 t)+b_1\sin(\omega_0 t)
$$

Start with $k=1$: the first harmonic pair is $a_1\cos(\omega_0 t)+b_1\sin(\omega_0 t)$. The same index $1$ appears in both subscripts and in the frequency multiplier $\omega_0$.

$$
a_2\cos(2\omega_0 t)+b_2\sin(2\omega_0 t)
$$

Then $k=2$ gives $a_2\cos(2\omega_0 t)+b_2\sin(2\omega_0 t)$, and the pattern continues for $k=3,4,\dots$. Read each positive integer $k$ as the label on both coefficients and the matching frequency.

## Matching $k$ to the Harmonic Frequency $k\omega_0$

**Example:** For the harmonic index $k=4$, write the harmonic frequency that appears in the corresponding cosine and sine terms.

**Explanation**

The cue is the positive integer harmonic index. Here the index is $k=4$, so the task is to keep that same index and read the matching frequency label.

$$
k\omega_0
$$

Substitute $k=4$ into the pattern $k\omega_0$. The fourth harmonic frequency is $4\omega_0$, and that same multiplier appears in both the cosine and sine terms.

$$
a_4\cos(4\omega_0 t)+b_4\sin(4\omega_0 t)
$$

So the harmonic frequency paired with $k=4$ is $4\omega_0$. The index stays fixed while you read the harmonic terms.

## Pairing $a_k$ and $b_k$ with the Same Harmonic Index

**Example:** For $k=3$, write the cosine term and the sine term that share that harmonic index.

**Explanation**

The cue is $k=3$. Keep the same harmonic index on both coefficients and on the frequency factor.

$$
a_3\cos(3\omega_0 t)
$$

The cosine term uses coefficient $a_3$ and the harmonic frequency factor $3\omega_0 t$.

$$
b_3\sin(3\omega_0 t)
$$

The sine term uses coefficient $b_3$ and the same harmonic frequency factor $3\omega_0 t$. The paired terms are $a_3\cos(3\omega_0 t)$ and $b_3\sin(3\omega_0 t)$.

## Identifying the Harmonic Index from a Trigonometric Term

**Example:** A term appears as $b_6\sin(6\omega_0 t)$. What harmonic index $k$ labels it?

**Explanation**

The cue is the shared integer in the subscript and the frequency multiplier. Read that integer directly as the harmonic index.

$$
b_6\sin(6\omega_0 t)
$$

The coefficient subscript is $6$, and the frequency factor is also $6\omega_0 t$. Those two labels match, so the harmonic index is $k=6$.

$$
k=6
$$

This term is labeled by $k=6$ whether it is written with a sine or cosine factor.

## Reading Harmonic Indexes in a Partial Fourier Series

**Example:** Given $x(t)=\frac{a_0}{2}+a_1\cos(\omega_0 t)+b_1\sin(\omega_0 t)+a_4\cos(4\omega_0 t)+b_4\sin(4\omega_0 t)+a_6\cos(6\omega_0 t)+b_6\sin(6\omega_0 t)$, which positive harmonic indexes appear?

**Explanation**

Scan the series term by term. Ignore the DC term $a_0/2$ and read the integer label from each harmonic pair.

$$
x(t)=\frac{a_0}{2}+a_1\cos(\omega_0 t)+b_1\sin(\omega_0 t)+a_4\cos(4\omega_0 t)+b_4\sin(4\omega_0 t)+a_6\cos(6\omega_0 t)+b_6\sin(6\omega_0 t)
$$

The first harmonic pair gives $k=1$, the next gives $k=4$, and the last gives $k=6$.

$$
\{1,4,6\}
$$

The distinct positive harmonic indexes that appear are $1$, $4$, and $6$.
