
$$
v=\frac{d x}{d t}
$$
$$
a=\frac{d v}{d t}=\frac{d^2 x}{d t^2}
$$
$$
v(t)=v\left(t_0\right)+\int_{t_0}^t a(\tau) d \tau
$$
$$
x(t)=x\left(t_0\right)+\int_{t_0}^t v(\tau) d \tau
$$
$$
\vec{\omega}=\frac{d \vec{\theta}}{d t}
$$
$$
\vec{\alpha}=\frac{d \vec{\omega}}{d t}
$$
$$
\vec{\omega}(t)=\vec{\omega}\left(t_0\right)+\int_{t_0}^t \vec{\alpha}(\tau) d \tau
$$
$$
\vec{\theta}(t)=\vec{\theta}\left(t_0\right)+\int_{t_0}^t \vec{\omega}(\tau) d \tau
$$

---

$$s=r \theta$$

---

$$
\vec{\omega}(t)=\vec{\omega}_0+\vec{\alpha} t
$$
$$
\vec{\theta}(t)=\vec{\theta}_0+\vec{\omega}_0 t+\frac{1}{2} \vec{\alpha} t^2
$$

$$
a \rightarrow v \rightarrow x
$$
$$\alpha \rightarrow \omega \rightarrow \theta$$

$$
v(t)=v_0+a t
$$
$$x(t)=x_0+v_0 t+\frac{1}{2} a t^2$$

---
$$
\begin{aligned} & \alpha=\text { constant } \\ & \omega(t)=\int_0^t \alpha d t=\alpha t+\omega_0 \\ & \theta(t)=\int_0^t \omega d t=\frac{1}{2} \alpha t^2+\omega_0 t+\theta_0\end{aligned}
$$


## Translational kinematics
$$
a=\text{constant}
$$
$$
v_f=v_0+at
$$
$$
x_f=x_0+v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2
$$
$$
v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x
$$
## Rotational kinematics
$$
\alpha=\text{constant}
$$
$$
\omega_f=\omega_0+\alpha t
$$
$$
\theta_f=\theta_0+\omega_0t+\frac{1}{2}\alpha t^2
$$
$$
\omega_f^2=\omega_0^2+2\alpha\Delta\theta
$$
## Translation--rotation connections
$$
s=r\theta
$$
$$
v=r\omega
$$
$$
a_t=r\alpha
$$
$$
a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}=r\omega^2
$$
