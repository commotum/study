## 4. Diffraction Gratings

7. [ ] [Finding Wavelength from a Diffraction-Grating Pattern](<../../M6/2026-08-05-M6-1/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **Written-problem candidate:** turn screen position into an exact angle and use $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$ without forcing a small-angle shortcut.
8. [ ] [Finding Diffraction-Grating Line Density from Symmetric Fringes](<../../M6/2026-08-06-M6-2/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **Distinct grating form:** halve an opposite-order fringe separation, find the angle, and convert reciprocal slit spacing into lines per millimeter.
9. [ ] [Counting Visible Diffraction-Grating Maxima on a Finite Screen](<../../M6/2026-08-06-M6-2/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Constraint-and-counting form:** combine the physical order bound with the screen-edge bound, round down, and count symmetric orders including $m=0$.

## 5. Single-Slit Diffraction

10. [ ] [Finding Slit Width from Adjacent Single-Slit Minima](<../../M6/2026-08-06-M6-2/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Core single-slit form:** turn adjacent dark-minimum spacing into $a\approx\lambda L/\Delta y$ while keeping the full central width separate.
11. [ ] [Finding Slit Width from the Central Diffraction Maximum](<../../M6/2026-08-09-HW-9/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **Factor-of-two safeguard:** use $W_{\mathrm{central}}\approx2\lambda L/a$ and distinguish the full central width from the one-sided first-minimum distance.
