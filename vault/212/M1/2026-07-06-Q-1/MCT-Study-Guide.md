## Optimized Study Order (5 Lessons, 6 Parts)

### 1. Describe Angular Motion

1. [ ] [Angular Position, Displacement, and Average Angular Velocity](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-1.md>) — use final-minus-initial and divide the signed angular displacement by elapsed time.

### 2. Connect Angular and Linear Motion

2. [ ] [Connecting Angular and Linear Motion at a Radius](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-2.md>) — choose $s=r\theta$ or $v=r\omega$, prepare the radius, and distinguish shared angular speed from radius-dependent tangential speed.

### 3. Translate Cycle-Based Rates

3. [ ] [Period, Frequency, and Angular Speed](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-3.md>) — turn words and units into $T$, $f$, or $\omega$ and convert among Hz, RPM, and radians per second.

### 4. Resolve Circular-Motion Acceleration

4. [ ] [Radial, Tangential, and Net Acceleration](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-4.md>) — decide which perpendicular components are present, calculate them, and combine them as vectors.

### 5. Select Constant-Angular-Acceleration Equations

5. [ ] [Constant Angular Acceleration When Time Is Known](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-5A.md>) — list the known quantities and target, then choose the time-containing constant-$\alpha$ equation without introducing an extra unknown.
6. [ ] [Constant Angular Acceleration When Time Is Missing](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-5B.md>) — recognize the missing-time cue, recover $\alpha$ from rim data when necessary, and use the no-time equation.
