## Optimized Study Order

### 1. Describe and Convert Rotational Motion

1. [ ] [Angular Position, Displacement, and Average Angular Velocity](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-1.md>) — distinguish angular position from signed angular change, then divide by elapsed time.
2. [ ] [Connecting Angular and Linear Motion at a Radius](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-2.md>) — choose $s=r\theta$ or $v=r\omega$ and prepare the radius before substituting.
3. [ ] [Period, Frequency, and Angular Speed](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-3.md>) — translate cycle language and convert among period, hertz, RPM, and radians per second.

### 2. Solve Rotational and Circular Kinematics

4. [ ] [Radial, Tangential, and Net Acceleration](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-4.md>) — decide which perpendicular acceleration components exist and combine them as vectors.
5. [ ] [Constant Angular Acceleration When Time Is Known](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-5A.md>) — confirm constant $\alpha$, list knowns and the target, and select a time-containing equation.
6. [ ] [Constant Angular Acceleration When Time Is Missing](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-5B.md>) — use the no-time cue, recover $\alpha$ from rim data when needed, and solve before converting units.

### 3. Turn Circular Motion into Force Equations

7. [ ] [Turning Circular Motion into a Radial Force Equation](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-7.md>) — draw real forces, choose inward positive, and set their signed radial sum equal to $mv^2/r$.
8. [ ] [Enforcing the Zero-Normal-Force Contact Threshold](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-8.md>) — set $N=0$ at impending contact loss and enforce the correct physical inequality.
9. [ ] [Solving Static-Friction Thresholds in Circular Motion](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-9.md>) — identify the impending slip and use $f_s=\mu_sN$ only at the limiting condition.
10. [ ] [Resolving a Tilted Support Force in Horizontal Circular Motion](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-10.md>) — resolve a bank's normal force or a tetherball's tension into vertical and inward components.
11. [ ] [Writing Tension Equations at the Top, Bottom, and Side of a Vertical Circle](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-11.md>) — rebuild the inward equation at each location rather than reusing one tension formula.

### 4. Switch Models at Release

12. [ ] [Switching from Circular Motion to Projectile Motion at Release](<Lessons/MCT-Problem-12.md>) — carry the instantaneous tangent velocity into projectile components when the constraint disappears.
