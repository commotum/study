# Quiz 2 Scope

Quiz 2 combined two course-content modules. Its location under M3 records the final module taught before the quiz; it does not mean the quiz covered M3 alone.

## M2: Rotational Mechanics

- Center of mass and density
- Moment of inertia
- Torque and rotational dynamics
- Static equilibrium and massive pulleys
- Rolling motion and angular momentum
- Sources: [[212/M2/2026-07-07-M2-1/2026-07-07-M2-1|M2-1]] through [[212/M2/2026-07-14-M2-6/2026-07-14-M2-6|M2-6]], [[212/M2/2026-07-12-HW-3/2026-07-12-HW-3|HW 3]], and [[212/M2/2026-07-15-HW-4/2026-07-15-HW-4|HW 4]]

## M3: Gravitation and Orbits

- Newtonian gravitation and gravitational potential energy
- Circular orbits and Kepler's laws
- Binary-star systems
- Multi-body gravitational force, energy, and orbital motion
- Sources: [[212/M3/2026-07-15-M3-1/2026-07-15-M3-1|M3-1]], [[212/M3/2026-07-16-M3-2/2026-07-16-M3-2|M3-2]], [[212/M3/2026-07-17-HW-5/2026-07-17-HW-5|HW 5]], and [[212/M3/2026-07-19-PQ-2/2026-07-19-PQ-2|Practice Quiz 2]]

## Retrospective Gap

The lecture-example collection and `General-Form.md` were organized around M2 only. That excluded the worked gravitation and orbital models from M3 even though those models appeared on Quiz 2. The canonical cheat sheet eventually contained many M3 equations, but the supporting study path did not provide enough recognition and execution practice for them.
