# Physics 212 — Quiz 2 Study Guide

This is a 20-lesson rebuild of Quiz 2 preparation, ordered from prerequisite moves to integrated exam-style problems. It covers M2 rotational mechanics and M3 gravitation/orbits.

Progress: [Home](<../../Home.md>) | [Table of Contents](<../../0. Table of Contents/TOC.md>) | [Quiz 2 scope](<Reference/Scope.md>)

The five **PQ2 anchors** preserve every practice-quiz form. The other fifteen **beyond-PQ2** lessons cover recurring lecture and homework families, including the major families that appeared on the actual quiz without being directly represented on the practice quiz. Work in order and complete each lesson's quiz blocks before advancing.

## 1. System Geometry and Inertia

1. [Finding the Center of Mass of Two Point Masses](<../../M2/2026-07-07-M2-1/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** build a signed mass-position ledger.
2. [Finding a Two-Object Center of Mass in the Plane](<../../M2/2026-07-15-HW-4/Lessons/Problem-10.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** locate component centers first, then the system center and relative radii.
3. [Comparing Moment of Inertia From Mass Distribution](<../../M2/2026-07-08-M2-2/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** rank inertia by how far the mass lies from the axis.

**Bridge drill:** after finding a composite system's center of mass, measure the chosen mass's radius from that new point—not from the rod's end. For a mass at $x=L$, use $r=L-x_{\mathrm{cm}}$, then $v=\omega r$.

## 2. Torque, Pulleys, and Equilibrium

4. [Finding Torque from a Force at an Angle](<../../M2/2026-07-09-M2-3/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** identify the pivot, lever arm, sine factor, and torque sign.
5. [Finding Atwood-Machine Acceleration With a Massive Pulley](<../../M2/2026-07-13-M2-4/Lessons/Problem-4.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** coordinate two force equations with the pulley torque equation.
6. [Comparing Atwood-Machine Accelerations With Pulley Inertia](<../../M2/2026-07-14-M2-5/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** interpret pulley inertia as added resistance and keep the two tensions distinct.
7. [Comparing Descent Times with a Rotating Flywheel](<../2026-07-19-PQ-2/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **PQ2 anchor:** convert a rotational-inertia comparison into an acceleration and time comparison.
8. [Finding the Tipping Threshold With Torque Balance](<../2026-07-19-PQ-2/Lessons/Problem-4.md>) — **PQ2 anchor:** recognize the contact-loss pivot and impose $\sum\tau=0$ at the threshold.

**Bridge drill:** for $m_2>m_1$ accelerating through a massive pulley,

$$
m_2g>T_2>T_1>m_1g.
$$

Derive the four-part ranking from $m_2g-T_2=m_2a$, $T_1-m_1g=m_1a$, and $(T_2-T_1)R=I\alpha$; do not memorize it without the signs.

## 3. Rolling and Angular Momentum

9. [Speed of a Rolling Solid Cylinder From Energy](<../../M2/2026-07-15-HW-4/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** combine translation, rotation, $v=\omega R$, and energy conservation.
10. [Comparing Translational and Rotational Kinetic Energy in Rolling](<../2026-07-19-PQ-2/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **PQ2 anchor:** use $I=\beta MR^2$ to see what cancels and what depends on shape.
11. [Finding Angular Speed After a Bullet Embeds in a Rotor](<../../M2/2026-07-14-M2-5/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** conserve angular momentum across sticking while rejecting kinetic-energy conservation.
12. [Comparing Angular Momentum at Equal Angular Speed](<../2026-07-19-PQ-2/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **PQ2 anchor:** reduce $L=I\omega$ to an inertia ranking when $\omega$ is shared.

## 4. Gravity and Orbits

13. [Scaling Force With an Inverse-Square Law](<../2026-07-15-M3-1/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** establish the force-versus-distance dependency.
14. [Net Gravitational Force from a Right-Triangle Arrangement](<../2026-07-17-HW-5/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** add gravitational forces as vectors rather than magnitudes.
15. [Gravitational Potential Energy of a Three-Body System](<../2026-07-17-HW-5/Lessons/Problem-8.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** count every unordered pair once and use the $1/r$ energy law.
16. [Finding a Two-Body Center of Mass as a Fraction of Separation](<../2026-07-16-M3-2/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** place a binary system about its shared center of mass.
17. [Finding the Orbital Period of a Binary Star System](<../2026-07-16-M3-2/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** use full separation and total mass in the binary-period relation.
18. [Scaling Kinetic Energy in a Circular Orbit](<../2026-07-17-HW-5/Lessons/Problem-6.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** derive $K=GMm/(2r)$ before combining it with potential energy.
19. [Total Mechanical Energy of a Three-Body Orbit](<../2026-07-16-M3-2/Lessons/Problem-6.md>) — **Beyond PQ2:** keep body counts, pair counts, and energy signs separate.
20. [Four Stars Orbiting at the Corners of a Square](<../2026-07-19-PQ-2/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **PQ2 anchor:** integrate geometry, vector force, centripetal motion, and pair-counted energy.
