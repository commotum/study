### Question 2 

```quiz
type: radio
id: q2a-q2
shuffle: true
content: |-
  **Question 2**

  Two uniform solid cylinders have the same mass, but cylinder B has twice the radius of cylinder A. They start from rest at the same height on a ramp and roll to the bottom without slipping. Assume rolling friction is negligible and each cylinder has constant density. Which cylinder has the greater center-of-mass speed at the bottom?
options:
- id: a
  content: |-
    Cylinder A is moving faster.
- id: b
  content: |-
    Cylinder B is moving faster.
- id: c
  content: |-
    They have the same speed.
  correct: true
  feedback: |-
    For a uniform solid cylinder, $I=\tfrac12 mR^2$. Applying energy conservation with $v=\omega R$ gives

    $$
    mgh=\frac12mv^2+\frac12I\omega^2
    =\frac12mv^2+\frac14mv^2.
    $$

    The radius cancels, so both cylinders have the same speed after descending through the same height.
```

### Question 3

```quiz
type: radio
id: q2a-q3
shuffle: true
content: |-
  **Question 3**

  In System I, one planet of mass $m$ orbits a star of mass $M$ at radius $r$. System II has two planets of mass $m$ orbiting the same star at the same radius, on opposite sides as shown. What can be said about the total gravitational potential energy of the two systems?

  ![](<Source/2026-07-20-Q-2/Images/problem-3-one-vs-two-planet-systems.png>)
options:
- id: a
  content: |-
    $U_{\mathrm I}<U_{\mathrm{II}}$
- id: b
  content: |-
    $U_{\mathrm I}=U_{\mathrm{II}}$
- id: c
  content: |-
    $U_{\mathrm I}>U_{\mathrm{II}}$
  correct: true
  feedback: |-
    System I has one interacting pair:

    $$
    U_{\mathrm I}=-\frac{GMm}{r}.
    $$

    System II has two star–planet pairs and one planet–planet pair separated by $2r$:

    $$
    U_{\mathrm{II}}
    =-\frac{2GMm}{r}-\frac{Gm^2}{2r}.
    $$

    Therefore, $U_{\mathrm{II}}$ is more negative and $U_{\mathrm I}>U_{\mathrm{II}}$.
```

### Question 4

A uniform pole of length $L$ and mass $m_p$ has point masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ attached as shown. In this top-down view, the system rotates in the plane of the page about its center of mass with angular speed $\omega$.

![](<Source/2026-07-20-Q-2/Images/problem-4-pole-point-masses.png>)

Show all work and express each answer in terms of the variables shown in the diagram.

```quiz
type: blank
id: q2a-q4a
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Q4A**

  Find the center of mass of the system, measured from the left end of the pole.

  Enter a keyboard-form expression using `mp`, `m1`, `m2`, and `L`, then reveal the reference result: ==(mp*L/2+2*m1*L/3+m2*L)/(mp+m1+m2)==
feedback: |-
  Treat the uniform pole as a point mass at $L/2$. The attached masses are at $2L/3$ and $L$, so

  $$
  x_{\mathrm{cm}}
  =\frac{m_p(L/2)+m_1(2L/3)+m_2L}{m_p+m_1+m_2}.
  $$
```

```quiz
type: blank
id: q2a-q4b
require_exact: false
content: |-
  **Q4B**

  What is the tangential speed of $m_2$?

  Enter a keyboard-form expression using `omega`, `mp`, `m1`, `m2`, and `L`, then reveal the reference result: ==omega*L*(mp/2+m1/3)/(mp+m1+m2)==
feedback: |-
  The rotation radius of $m_2$ is its distance from the system's center of mass:

  $$
  r_2=L-x_{\mathrm{cm}}.
  $$

  Therefore,

  $$
  v_2=\omega r_2
  =\omega\left(L-x_{\mathrm{cm}}\right)
  =\frac{\omega L\left(m_p/2+m_1/3\right)}{m_p+m_1+m_2}.
  $$
```

### Question 1

Two stars of masses $M$ and $m$ orbit their common center of mass in circular orbits. Their separation is $d$, and $M=\tfrac32m$, as shown.

![](<Source/2026-07-20-Q-2/Images/problem-5-binary-star-system.png>)

Show all work and solve completely symbolically in terms of the given variables.

```quiz
type: blank
id: q2b-q1a
require_exact: true
content: |-
  **Q1A**

  Find the center of mass of the system, measured from $M$.

  Enter the simplified expression in terms of $d$: ==2d/5==
feedback: |-
  Place $M$ at $x=0$ and $m$ at $x=d$:

  $$
  x_{\mathrm{cm}}=\frac{M(0)+m(d)}{M+m}.
  $$

  With $M=\tfrac32m$,

  $$
  x_{\mathrm{cm}}=\frac{md}{(3/2)m+m}=\frac{2d}{5}.
  $$
```

```quiz
type: blank
id: q2b-q1b
require_exact: true
content: |-
  **Q1B**

  Find the orbital period of the system.

  Enter the expression using `pi`, `sqrt(...)`, `^`, and `*` for multiplication: ==2*pi*sqrt(2*d^3/(5*G*m))==
feedback: |-
  For a circular two-body orbit with separation $d$,

  $$
  \omega^2=\frac{G(M+m)}{d^3}.
  $$

  Using $T=2\pi/\omega$ and $M+m=\tfrac52m$ gives

  $$
  T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{d^3}{G(M+m)}}
  =2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2d^3}{5Gm}}.
  $$
```

```quiz
type: blank
id: q2b-q1c
require_exact: true
content: |-
  **Q1C**

  Find the total mechanical energy of the system.

  Enter the expression using `^` and `*` for multiplication: ==-3*G*m^2/(4*d)==
feedback: |-
  The gravitational potential energy is $U=-GMm/d$. For a circular orbit, the total kinetic energy is $K=GMm/(2d)$. Thus,

  $$
  E=K+U=-\frac{GMm}{2d}.
  $$

  Substituting $M=\tfrac32m$ gives

  $$
  E=-\frac{3Gm^2}{4d}.
  $$
```
