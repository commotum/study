# Missing Core Formulas

These formulas and retrieval rules appear in the Quiz 3 Study Guide lessons but are absent from, or not stated explicitly in, Cheat-Sheet-Reduced.md.

## Add First

### Period, Frequency, and Angular Frequency

$$
T=\frac{\Delta t}{N}
\qquad
f=\frac{N}{\Delta t}=\frac1T
$$

$$
\omega=2\pi f=\frac{2\pi}{T}
$$

### Simple Pendulum

For small angles,

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac Lg}
\qquad
f=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac gL}
$$

### SHM Speeding Up or Slowing Down

$$
xv<0\Rightarrow\text{speeding up toward equilibrium}
$$

$$
xv>0\Rightarrow\text{slowing down while moving away from equilibrium}
$$

At a turning point, $v=0$ momentarily. At equilibrium, $x=0$ and $a=0$ momentarily.

### Traveling-Wave Particle Motion

$$
y=F(x\mp v_wt)
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
v_y=\frac{\partial y}{\partial t}
=\mp v_w\frac{\partial y}{\partial x}
$$

Thus, for a right-moving wave, particle motion has the sign opposite the snapshot slope. For a left-moving wave, particle motion has the same sign as the snapshot slope.

$$
|v_y|_{\max}=A\omega=2\pi fA
$$

$$
|v_y|_{\max}=\frac{2\pi A}{\lambda}v_w
$$

### Static Equilibrium and Torque

$$
\text{static:}\qquad
\sum\vec F=0
\qquad
\sum\tau=0
$$

$$
\tau=rF_\perp=rF\sin\phi
$$

If $\theta$ is measured from the direction labeled parallel,

$$
F_\parallel=F\cos\theta
\qquad
F_\perp=F\sin\theta
$$

### Tension from a Stationary Hanging Mass

$$
a=0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad F_T=Mg
$$

### Rotating-Source Speed

$$
f_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac{\mathrm{rpm}}{60}
$$

$$
v_s=2\pi Rf_{\mathrm{rot}}
$$

Use this $v_s$ in the moving-source Doppler row.

### Pointwise Superposition

$$
y_{\mathrm{net}}=\sum_i y_i
$$

For two pulses or waves,

$$
y_{\mathrm{net}}=y_1+y_2
$$

Add signed displacements at the same position and time.

## High-Value Shortcuts

### Decibel Changes and Combined Intensities

$$
\Delta\beta
=(10\,\mathrm{dB})\log_{10}\!\frac{I_2}{I_1}
$$

$$
I_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sum_i I_i
$$

$$
2I\Rightarrow+3.01\,\mathrm{dB}
\qquad
10I\Rightarrow+10\,\mathrm{dB}
$$

### PQ3 Shelf–Wire Bridge

$$
F_T\sin\theta
=\left(\frac{m_1}{2}+m_2\right)g
$$

$$
F_T=\frac{(m_1+2m_2)g}{2\sin\theta}
$$

$$
L_w=\frac L{\cos\theta}
\qquad
\mu=\frac{m_w\cos\theta}{L}
$$

Together with $v_w=\sqrt{F_T/\mu}$,

$$
v_w
=\sqrt{
\frac{(m_1+2m_2)gL}
{2m_w\sin\theta\cos\theta}
}
$$

### Extracting an Offset SHM Graph

$$
x_{\mathrm{eq}}
=\frac{x_{\max}+x_{\min}}2
$$

$$
A=\frac{x_{\max}-x_{\min}}2
$$

### Counting Wavelengths in a Material

$$
N_\lambda=\frac d\lambda
$$

Using $n=c_0/v$ and $\lambda=c_0/(nf)$,

$$
N_\lambda=\frac{ndf}{c_0}
$$
