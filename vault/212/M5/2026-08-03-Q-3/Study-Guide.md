# Physics 212 — Quiz 3 Study Guide

This is a 20-lesson exam path through M4 oscillations and M5 waves. It is ordered as a learnable progression: read a model, practice its component moves, then finish with the closest exam-style synthesis.

Progress: [Home](<../../Home.md>) | [Table of Contents](<../../0. Table of Contents/TOC.md>) | [Practice Quiz 3](<../2026-08-02-PQ-3/2026-08-02-PQ-3.md>)

The five **PQ3 anchors** are high-confidence forms, but they occupy only five slots. The other fifteen **beyond-PQ3** lessons deliberately cover repeated lecture and homework families that could replace or extend those forms on the actual quiz. Damping, driven oscillations, and beats are omitted because the instructor explicitly excluded them from Quiz 3.

## 1. Read and Model Simple Harmonic Motion

1. [Finding Maximum SHM Speed From a Position-Time Graph](<../../M4/2026-07-21-M4-1/Lessons/Problem-6.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** extract $A$, $T$, $\omega$, and $v_{\max}$ from one graph.
2. [Finding Instantaneous SHM Velocity From Cycle Data](<../../M4/2026-07-21-M4-1/Lessons/Problem-8.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** build $x(t)$, differentiate it, and interpret the sign of $v(t)$.
3. [Ranking Acceleration Magnitudes from an SHM Position Graph](<../../M4/2026-07-23-HW-6/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** use $a=-\omega^2x$ instead of confusing acceleration with graph slope.
4. [Speed of a Spring Oscillator at a Given Position](<../../M4/2026-07-23-HW-6/Lessons/Problem-7.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** move between amplitude, position, and speed with oscillator energy.

## 2. Simple and Physical Pendula

5. [Deciding What Changes a Pendulum's Frequency](<../2026-08-02-PQ-3/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **PQ3 anchor:** distinguish real dependencies on $L$ and $g$ from the false mass dependency.
6. [Period of a Uniform Rod as a Physical Pendulum](<../../M4/2026-07-22-M4-2/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** establish $T=2\pi\sqrt{I_{\mathrm{pivot}}/(Mgd)}$ with one rigid body.
7. [Finding the Period of a Rod–Disk Physical Pendulum](<../../M4/2026-07-22-M4-2/Lessons/Problem-6.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** construct a composite pivot inertia and a separate gravitational-torque ledger.

## 3. Traveling Waves and Tensioned Wires

8. [Inferring Particle Motion From a Traveling-Wave Snapshot](<../2026-07-23-M5-1/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** separate the wave's propagation direction from each particle's transverse motion.
9. [Finding Wave Speed from a Traveling-Wave Equation](<../2026-07-28-HW-7/Lessons/Problem-10.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** read $k$ and $\omega$ from phase and use $v=\omega/k$.
10. [Wave Speed in a Wire Tensioned by a Hanging Mass](<../2026-07-27-M5-2/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** form $T$, $\mu$, and $v=\sqrt{T/\mu}$ in the basic case.
11. [Deriving Wave Speed on a Load-Bearing Wire](<../2026-08-02-PQ-3/Lessons/Problem-4.md>) — **PQ3 anchor:** chain free-body diagrams and torque equilibrium into wire geometry, linear density, and wave speed.

## 4. Intensity, Decibels, and Doppler Shift

12. [Finding Distance From Sound Intensity](<../2026-07-28-M5-3/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** use inverse-square scaling and solve for the unknown distance.
13. [Convert an Intensity Ratio into a Decibel Change](<../2026-08-02-PQ-3/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **PQ3 anchor:** combine intensities first, then apply the logarithmic level change.
14. [Doppler Shift for a Moving Observer](<../2026-07-28-M5-3/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** distinguish observer motion in the numerator from source motion in the denominator.
15. [Extreme Doppler Frequencies from a Rotating Source](<../2026-08-02-PQ-3/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **PQ3 anchor:** convert rpm to tangential source speed and choose the approaching/receding extremes.

## 5. Standing Waves and Resonance

16. [Third-Harmonic Frequency of a Wire Tensioned by a Hanging Mass](<../2026-07-29-M5-4/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** join tension, linear density, wave speed, boundary conditions, and harmonic number.
17. [Matching Fundamental Frequencies of Open and Closed Tubes](<../2026-07-31-HW-8/Lessons/Problem-2.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** keep the half-wave and quarter-wave boundary patterns distinct.

## 6. Superposition and Two-Source Interference

18. [Largest Displacement When Two Pulses Overlap](<../2026-07-30-M5-5/Lessons/Problem-1.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** apply point-by-point signed superposition before using phase language.
19. [Classifying Two-Source Interference from Path and Starting Phase](<../2026-08-02-PQ-3/Lessons/Problem-3.md>) — **PQ3 anchor:** add path phase and initial source phase before classifying the result.
20. [Finding the First Constructive-Interference Point](<../2026-07-30-M5-5/Lessons/Problem-5.md>) — **Beyond PQ3:** convert the first allowed path difference into a geometry equation.

## Coverage Audit

- Practice-quiz anchors: **5/5**.
- Beyond-practice coverage: **15/20 lessons**.
- Major non-PQ3 insurance: SHM graph reading, time functions, acceleration and energy; simple-to-composite physical pendula; traveling-wave motion and equations; inverse-square intensity; moving-observer Doppler shift; string and pipe harmonics; pulse superposition; and interference geometry.
- Most likely long-problem chains: **statics $\rightarrow T\rightarrow\mu\rightarrow v$** and **component geometry $\rightarrow I_{\mathrm{pivot}}$ plus restoring torque $\rightarrow$ physical-pendulum period**.
