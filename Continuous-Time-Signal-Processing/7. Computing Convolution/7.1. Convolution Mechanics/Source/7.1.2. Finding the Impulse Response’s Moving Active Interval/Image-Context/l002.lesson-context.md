Answer explanation blocks:
- paragraph:
  text: For $h(t-\tau)$, the right endpoint of the original support maps to the left endpoint: $u=4$ gives $\tau=t-4$. The left endpoint maps to the right endpoint: $u=1$ gives $\tau=t-1$. So the moving support is $t-4 \le \tau \le t-1$.
