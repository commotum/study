## Introduction to DC Adjustment from Vertical Shifting

Start from a periodic signal with known trigonometric Fourier coefficients. In this form, $a_0$ is the constant or DC term, so it is the coefficient to watch when the signal is shifted vertically.

$$
x(t)=a_0+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}[a_n\cos(n\omega_0 t)+b_n\sin(n\omega_0 t)]
$$

Now form the vertically shifted signal $x_{\text{new}}(t)=x(t)+c$. The added constant $c$ folds into the constant term, while the harmonic terms stay attached to the same cosine and sine components.

$$
x_{\text{new}}(t)=x(t)+c
$$

So the update rule is: add $c$ to the DC coefficient and leave every coefficient with $n\ge 1$ unchanged. Later examples can reuse this exact notation as $a_0^{\text{new}}=a_0+c$, $a_n^{\text{new}}=a_n$, and $b_n^{\text{new}}=b_n$.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=a_0+c,\qquad a_n^{\text{new}}=a_n,\qquad b_n^{\text{new}}=b_n \quad (n\ge 1)
$$

## Adding a Positive Offset to the DC Coefficient

**Example:** A periodic signal has trigonometric Fourier coefficients $a_0=2$, $a_1=-1$, $b_1=4$, $a_2=0$, and $b_2=-3$. The signal is shifted upward by $5$. Update the coefficient set.

**Explanation**

The cue is a vertical shift upward by $5$, so use the DC-update rule $a_0^{\text{new}}=a_0+c$. Read the original coefficient list first, then apply the offset only to $a_0$.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=2+5=7
$$

The harmonic coefficients stay fixed: $a_1^{\text{new}}=-1$, $b_1^{\text{new}}=4$, $a_2^{\text{new}}=0$, and $b_2^{\text{new}}=-3$. So the updated coefficient set is the original list with only the DC term changed.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=7,\qquad a_1^{\text{new}}=-1,\qquad b_1^{\text{new}}=4,\qquad a_2^{\text{new}}=0,\qquad b_2^{\text{new}}=-3
$$

## Applying a Negative Offset to the DC Coefficient

**Example:** A periodic signal has coefficients $a_0=9$, $a_1=2$, $b_1=-1$, $a_2=-4$, and $b_2=6$. The signal is shifted downward by $3$. Update the coefficient set.

**Explanation**

The cue is a vertical shift downward by $3$, so treat the shift as $c=-3$ and use the same DC-update rule $a_0^{\text{new}}=a_0+c$. Only the constant term should move.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=9+(-3)=6
$$

The harmonic coefficients stay fixed: $a_1^{\text{new}}=2$, $b_1^{\text{new}}=-1$, $a_2^{\text{new}}=-4$, and $b_2^{\text{new}}=6$. So the updated coefficient set keeps every nonzero harmonic term unchanged.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=6,\qquad a_1^{\text{new}}=2,\qquad b_1^{\text{new}}=-1,\qquad a_2^{\text{new}}=-4,\qquad b_2^{\text{new}}=6
$$

## Keeping Nonzero Harmonic Coefficients Unchanged

**Example:** A periodic signal is shifted upward by $2$. The original coefficients are $a_0=-1$, $a_1=5$, $b_1=0$, $a_2=-2$, and $b_2=4$. Identify which coefficients change and state the updated set.

**Explanation**

The cue is a vertical shift upward by $2$, so treat it as $c=2$ and use the same DC-update rule $a_0^{\text{new}}=a_0+c$. The only coefficient that changes is the constant term.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=-1+2=1
$$

Copy the harmonic coefficients exactly as written: $a_1^{\text{new}}=5$, $b_1^{\text{new}}=0$, $a_2^{\text{new}}=-2$, and $b_2^{\text{new}}=4$. The zero harmonic term stays zero, and every nonzero harmonic term stays unchanged.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=1,\qquad a_1^{\text{new}}=5,\qquad b_1^{\text{new}}=0,\qquad a_2^{\text{new}}=-2,\qquad b_2^{\text{new}}=4
$$

## Updating a Complete Fourier Coefficient Set After a Vertical Shift

**Example:** A periodic signal has coefficients $a_0=\tfrac{3}{2}$, $a_1=-2$, $b_1=1$, $a_2=0$, $b_2=-\tfrac{5}{2}$, $a_3=4$, and $b_3=3$. The signal is shifted upward by $\tfrac{7}{2}$. Update the full coefficient set and rewrite the trigonometric Fourier series.

**Explanation**

The cue is a vertical shift upward by $\tfrac{7}{2}$, so use the DC-update rule $a_0^{\text{new}}=a_0+c$. Apply the offset to the constant term first, then carry every harmonic coefficient forward unchanged.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=\tfrac{3}{2}+\tfrac{7}{2}=5
$$

Now copy the harmonic coefficients exactly: $a_1^{\text{new}}=-2$, $b_1^{\text{new}}=1$, $a_2^{\text{new}}=0$, $b_2^{\text{new}}=-\tfrac{5}{2}$, $a_3^{\text{new}}=4$, and $b_3^{\text{new}}=3$. Since the series uses $a_0^{\text{new}}/2$, the new constant term in the rewritten series is $\tfrac{5}{2}$.

$$
a_0^{\text{new}}=5,\qquad a_1^{\text{new}}=-2,\qquad b_1^{\text{new}}=1,\qquad a_2^{\text{new}}=0,\qquad b_2^{\text{new}}=-\tfrac{5}{2},\qquad a_3^{\text{new}}=4,\qquad b_3^{\text{new}}=3
$$

$$
x_{\text{shifted}}(t)=\tfrac{5}{2}-2\cos(\omega_0 t)+\sin(\omega_0 t)+0\cos(2\omega_0 t)-\tfrac{5}{2}\sin(2\omega_0 t)+4\cos(3\omega_0 t)+3\sin(3\omega_0 t)
$$
